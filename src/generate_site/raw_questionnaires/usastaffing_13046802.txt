Eligibilities
1.

Pathways Program Participant Eligible for Conversion
This authority allows for current Pathways Program participants, that have completed all Pathways program requirements to be non-competitively converted to a permanent or Term position.

To be eligible for non-competitive conversion from a Pathways Intern position, you must:

Be a U.S. Citizen or national (residents of American Samoa and Swains Island);
Be within the 180-day conversion timeframe from the date your degree, career, or technical education program requirements are completed;
Completed at least 480 hours of work experience acquired through the Internship Program while enrolled at least a part-time basis;
Up to 160 of the 480-hour service requirement may be waived when you complete 320 hours of career related experience or other experience that may be credited in accordance with 5 CFR 362.204(c) or 5 CFR 362.204(d). You must provide supporting documentation to support this provision (i.e. written contract, intern agreement, or recommendation letter from an instructor or program administrator).
Successfully completed your degree, career, or technical education program requirements;
Meet the qualification standards for the position to which you will be converted;
Receive favorable recommendation for conversion from your supervisor and maintain acceptable performance; and
Meet agency-specific requirements as specified in the Participant's Agreement.
If you are an Indefinite Intern, you must have a completed Individual Development Plan (IDP).
Pathways Recent Graduate - You may be eligible for conversion if you are a Pathways Program Recent Graduate and meet the following criteria:
Successfully completed at least 1-year of continuous service in addition to all the requirements of the Recent Graduates Program;
Demonstrated successful job performance consistent with the applicable performance appraisal program established under the agency's approved performance appraisal system that results in a rating of record (or summary rating) of at least Fully Successful or equivalent and a recommendation for conversion by the first-level supervisor; and
Met the OPM Qualification Standard for the competitive service position to which you will be converted.

To claim eligibility for non-competitive conversion, you must submit the following documentation with your application:

Copies of the appropriate documentation including:

Transcripts with degree date, unofficial will be accepted, however official transcripts will be required prior to a final job offer;
Current Pathways Program agreement;
Copy of favorable recommendation for conversion from the work experience supervisor;
Most Recent Performance Appraisal; and
Current Notification of Personnel Action, Standard Form 50 showing Pathways status.
Additional supporting documentation requirements are listed in the Required Documents section of this announcement.

Career Transition Assistance Plan (CTAP)

To claim CTAP eligibility, please confirm:
You are a current or former federal employee displaced from the Department of the Interior (or one of the associated bureaus) AND
You are located in the same local commuting area of the vacancy; AND
You are applying to a position that is at or below the grade level of the position from which you were or will be separated; AND
You are applying to a position that does not have greater promotion potential than the position from which you were or will be separated; AND
Your last performance rating of record is at least fully successful or the equivalent.
To verify CTAP eligibility, you must submit supporting documentation with your application to include:
Your agency notice, such as a Reduction in Force (RIF) separation notice or a Proposed Removal/Separation notice; AND
Your most recent annual performance appraisal rating of record; AND
Your most recent SF-50, or equivalent personnel action, noting current position, grade level, and duty location.
To verify your eligibility, you must submit supporting documentation such as a Reduction in Force (RIF) separation notice to include your latest performance rating (if the notice does not have this information included, you must submit a copy of your most recent performance appraisal) and a copy of your latest Notification of Personnel Action, Standard Form 50. Additional supporting documentation requirements are listed in the Required Documents section of this announcement. For more information, visit the USAJOBS Help Center - Career Transition Programs (CTAP, ICTAP, RPL).
