A WG-10 is expected to: 1) operate heavy engineering equipment such as crawler tractors, bulldozers, excavators, motor graders, or loaders to excavate, move, and backfill earth; grade and level surfaces; reshape unpaved roads or dikes; and spread soil, gravel, or other surfacing materials; 2) perform mechanical excavation and drainage maintenance by operating heavy equipment to clean or reshape ditches and canals, remove accumulated sediment, excavate for culvert installation or maintenance, or perform similar work to maintain drainage infrastructure; 3) operate heavy equipment on uneven, sloped, soft, or wet ground and near obstacles such as trees, culverts, water-control structures, buildings, impoundments, or drop-offs while performing earthmoving, grading, or excavation work without damaging surrounding infrastructure; 4) perform routine operator maintenance and inspections on heavy engineering equipment by checking fluid levels, inspecting tires or tracks and attachments, identifying mechanical defects, and completing routine servicing or minor repairs as required; 5) transport heavy equipment and materials by operating trucks with trailers or other equipment-hauling vehicles, securing equipment and materials for transport, and loading or unloading equipment in accordance with applicable safety procedures.
