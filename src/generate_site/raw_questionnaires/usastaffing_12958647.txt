Eligibilities
1.
Are you currently employed with Peace Corps?
this includes US Direct Hires, Experts, and Temporary Employees,
this DOES NOT include Peace Corps Volunteers, Returned Peace Corps Volunteers and US Citizen Personnel Service Contractors.
Please note: If you are a US Direct Hire, Expert, or Temporary Employee, you MUST submit a recent Standard Form-50 (SF-50) that verifies you meet the time-in-grade requirement for the grade at which you are applying.  See the "Qualifications" section of the job announcement for a description of time-in-grade requirements.  If you are applying for a higher grade and your SF-50 has an effective date within the past year, it may not clearly demonstrate you meet the one-year time-in-grade requirement.  If you do not submit an SF-50 with your application materials, your application will be disqualified from further consideration.

Some examples of appropriate SF-50s include:
Within grade increases at the highest grade held; or
Promotions with an effective date more than one year old; or
SF-50s at the highest grade held with an effective date more than one year old.
DO NOT submit the following SF-50s, since they will not demonstrate that you meet time-in-grade:
Cash or time-off award SF-50s; or,
General Adjustments with an effective date less than a year old.

Preferences
1.
Has your background or work history involved a connection to intelligence activities?

"Intelligence activity" includes any activities or specialized training involving or related to the clandestine collection of information, or the analysis or dissemination of such information, intended for use by the United States Government or any foreign government in formulating or implementing political or military policy in regard to other countries. This includes, but is not limited to, any involvement in covert actions designed to influence events in foreign countries.

Some examples include employment with:
An intelligence agency; or,
A division of an agency involved in intelligence activities; or,
An instrumentality of the United States Government or any foreign government, a substantial part of whose mission involves intelligence activities. This includes government contracting companies and other non-governmental organizations.
Please note: Applicants who have a background or work history involving a connection to intelligence activities will be required to submit a completed Intelligence Background Questionnaire upon request. Failure to meet this requirement will result in disqualification from further consideration. An applicant's Intelligence Background Questionnaire will be reviewed by the Peace Corps Office of General Counsel and the applicant may be deemed ineligible for employment for a minimum of 10 years from the last day of disqualifying employment. Please see the "Additional Information" section for more information on Peace Corps Manual Section 611 requirements and restrictions.
