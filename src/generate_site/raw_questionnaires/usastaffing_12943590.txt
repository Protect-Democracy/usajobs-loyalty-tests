1.
From the descriptions below, select the appropriate response based on your level of experience, education, and/or training, that you fully possess and that demonstrates your ability to perform Heavy Mobile Equipment Mechanic duties without more than normal supervision.
 I have the ability to perform the most difficult and complex tasks of rebuilding, overhauling, and repairing gasoline and diesel engine powered heavy mobile equipment and vehicles for correction of all mechanical problems; repairing automatic and non-automatic transmissions and brake systems; and repairing hydraulic operating systems and controls. I am skilled in testing completed repair and modification work to ensure equipment and specification requirements are met and using diagnostic tools such as micrometers, pressure gauges, precision clearance gauges, dial indicators, engine analyzers, and dynamometers to determine performance or malfunctions. I am considered an expert and work with a high degree of independence.
 I have the ability to perform the full range of tasks repairing gasoline and diesel engine powered heavy mobile equipment and vehicles for correction of most mechanical problems; repairing automatic and non-automatic transmissions and brake systems; repairing hydraulic operating systems and controls; and limited use of diagnostic tools such as micrometers, pressure gauges, precision clearance gauges, dial indicators, engine analyzers, and dynamometers to determine performance or malfunctions. I am skilled in submitting notices to supervisor of completion repair work for testing to ensure equipment and specification requirements are met. I work on my own initiative under general supervision subject to occasional inspection.
 I have the ability to perform common tasks including performing preventive maintenance for gasoline and diesel engine powered heavy mobile equipment and vehicles for prevention mechanical problems, such as replacing spark plugs and changing or replenishing engine fluids and filters; changing or replenishing transmission fluid and replacing brake pads; and adding hydraulic fluid to hydraulic operating systems. I can use some diagnostic tools such as micrometers, and pressure gauges to determine minimal engine malfunctions for repairs. I work under normal supervision of a work leader or journey-level employee who observes tasks in progress and upon completion to make sure they are properly performed.
 I have the ability to perform simple operation of checking heavy mobile equipment and vehicle components or I have received training on the operation of heavy mobile equipment.
 I do not possess the abilities described above.
