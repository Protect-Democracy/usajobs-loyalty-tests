1.
From the descriptions below, select the appropriate response based on your level of experience, education, and/or training, that you fully possess and that demonstrates your ability to perform Engineering Equipment Operator duties without more than normal supervision.
 I have the ability to perform the most difficult and complex Engineering Equipment Operator duties including operating a wide variety of engineer equipment to fine grade slopes, inclines, ramps, curves, and excavations by scraping, scooping, rolling, or leveling on rough and rocky terrain or in dense forest areas. I am skilled in working to fine specifications from grade stakes set by engineers; performing work in various weather conditions which may alter soil composition; and applying a variety of electric resistance and manual welding processes to weld various types of metals and alloys, including dissimilar metals such as copper to steel. I am considered an expert and work with a high degree of independence.
 I have the ability to perform the full range of Engineering Equipment Operator duties including operating various types of heavy equipment to excavate, backfill, or grade earth to rough specifications. I am skilled in moving earth on mountains and steep slopes, graded curves and shoulders; and welding various sizes, shapes, and thicknesses of metals and materials together. I work on my own initiative under general supervision subject to occasional inspection.
 I have the ability to perform the common duties of an Engineering Equipment Operator including operating a variety of heavy equipment to push, pull, pile, or load materials such as sand, gravel, earth, or refuse; digging trenches, clearing brush, tree stumps, and rocks. I can grade surfaces on generally flat, rolling or simple terrain. I work under normal supervision of a work leader or journey-level employee who observes tasks in progress and upon completion to make sure they are properly performed.
 I have the ability to perform simple Engineering Equipment Operator duties such as assisting higher-graded journeyman level operators by understanding and selecting proper attachments for the work to be done or I have received training on adjusting and repairing generators; and operating motor vehicles with a GVWR up to 26,000 lbs to haul materials.
