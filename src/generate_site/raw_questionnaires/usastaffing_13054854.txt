I am able to check parts and components of systems, assemblies, and subassemblies and their related components in order to ensure no deficiencies/discrepancies exist in accordance with proper interpretation and application of ordnance technical data, Time Compliance Technical Orders actions, and/or schematics. I have knowledge of munitions maintenance methods and procedures to determine the degree of disassembly or necessary rework required in order to troubleshoot, repair and/or modify munitions support and test equipment, and associated handling equipment or devices.
