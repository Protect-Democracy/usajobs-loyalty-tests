1.
Ability to do the work of Engineering Equipment Operator without more than normal supervision (screen-out). Engineering Equipment Operators at this level are able to demonstrate skill in operating heavy equipment to push, pull, pile and load materials. Operate a variety of heavy equipment and road work machinery including, but not limited to, bulldozers, backhoes, loaders, graders, excavators, motorized brooms, compactors, and rollers.

 I have the ability to operate all of the following heavy engineering equipment: snowplows, remove snowplow, snowplow trucks, graders, bulldozers, skid steers, loaders, excavators/backhoes, dump trucks, tractor-trailer combinations, and road compactors. Operation includes work on steep grades and difficult road conditions and terrain. I am often called upon for difficult assignments or to assist others.
 I have the ability to operate all of the following heavy engineering equipment independently: snowplows, remove snowplow, snowplow trucks, graders, bulldozers, skid steers, loaders, excavators/backhoes, dump trucks, tractor-trailer combinations, and road compactors. Operation includes work on steep grades and difficult road conditions and terrain. I can independently operate heavy equipment.
 I have the ability to do the work of an Engineering Equipment Operator without more than normal supervision. I can operate heavy equipment to push, pull, pile, and load materials. Operate a variety of heavy equipment and road work machinery including, but not limited to, bulldozers, backhoes, loaders, graders, excavators, motorized brooms, and water/sewer tankers.
 I have been trained to operate heavy equipment, but have not performed this work or have been closely supervised when performing this work.
 I have no experience in operating heavy engineering equipment as described above.
