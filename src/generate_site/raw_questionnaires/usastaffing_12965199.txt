SPECIALIZED EXPERIENCE GS-13: You must have one year of specialized experience at a level of difficulty and responsibility equivalent to the GS-12 grade level in the Federal service. Specialized experience for this position must include the following:
Using IRS functions, programs, policies, regulations, procedures, and operations to evaluate programs plans and determine impact of changes on program goals;
Using cross-functional programs and their interrelationship with assigned program to conduct reviews, studies/projects, and coordinate systems and resource utilization of program operations;
Recognizing significant tax issues and enforcement changes to revise or recommend changes in basic implementation regulations;
Using a wide range of analytical and evaluative methods to develop new approaches to existing programs such as compliance, accounts management and/or taxpayer education and communication function;

Delivering oral communication to present plans and proposals to individuals and groups, including top-level managers and field employees;
