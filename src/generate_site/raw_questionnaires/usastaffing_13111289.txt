Specialized experience for this position must include one full year, equivalent to the GS-13, performing at least 4 of 5 of the following duties: 1. Managing and directing comprehensive law library operations and services. Experience overseeing the administration and delivery of law library programs, including legal research and reference services, information management, resource accessibility, and the development and implementation of policies, procedures, and service standards to support the legal research and information needs of attorneys, judges, legal professionals, and other stakeholders. 2. Providing advanced legal research, reference, and information services. Experience conducting, directing, or overseeing complex legal research involving primary and secondary legal authorities, including statutes, regulations, case law, legislative history, administrative materials, and other legal resources; and providing authoritative research guidance and recommendations to support legal analysis, decision-making, and the interpretation and application of the law. 3. Managing legal research collections, electronic resources, and information technologies. Experience evaluating law library operations, identifying organizational and resource needs, developing program objectives and performance measures, and managing resources, budgets, contracts, and vendor relationships to improve operational efficiency, service delivery, and the effective use of personnel and financial resources. 5. Providing leadership and direction to professional library staff and teams. Experience leading, coordinating, or directing the work of professional librarians and library support personnel, including assigning and reviewing work, establishing priorities, providing technical guidance, mentoring staff, facilitating collaboration, and ensuring the timely and effective delivery of library services.
