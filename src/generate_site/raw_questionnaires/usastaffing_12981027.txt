2.
To minimally qualify for the Print Plant Worker (Leader) KA 3566-00 level, you must meet the SCREEN OUT/JOB ELEMENT (Job Element #73): Ability to Lead or Supervise. Typical duties of this position include: 1) possessing skill in setting the pace by personal performance, instructing and guiding workers at all stages by setting an example for others to follow, while displaying good morale, attention to work, compliance, safety, and good housekeeping rules and practices. 2) Possessing the ability to handle and control light and heavy-weight powered equipment such as vacuum cleaners, high-speed buffers, polishers, floor scrubbers, etc., and make minor repairs when necessary, and 3) Settting the pace for Fork Truck Lift operations to lead in operating an electrically powered industrial fork truck or a power hand jack to load/unload trash hoppers, skids, pallets, bales or rolls. 

From the descriptions below, select the response that best demonstrates your ability, experience, or training to perform the duties of this position.
 I am able to perform the most difficult and complex Printing Plant Worker (Leader) duties. As part of my regular duties, I independently lead Custodial Workers and Fork Lift Operators by setting the pace through personal performance, assigning and adjusting workloads, instructing employees on work methods, safety practices, housekeeping requirements, and equipment operation. I ensure needed materials, tools, and stock are available; resolve routine and non-routine work problems; conduct spot checks to ensure quality standards are met; operate and direct the operation of powered custodial equipment such as vacuum cleaners, high-speed buffers, polishers, and floor scrubbers; and lead fork lift operations involving loading, unloading, and moving trash hoppers, skids, pallets, bales, and rolls. I am frequently consulted by others regarding work procedures and equipment operation and perform my duties with a high degree of independence.
 I am able to perform the full range of Printing Plant Worker (Leader) duties on my own initiative with only general supervision. As part of my regular duties, I assign work, provide guidance and instruction to employees, ensure needed materials and supplies are available, monitor recurring assignments, resolve routine work problems, conduct spot checks to ensure quality standards are achieved, operate powered custodial equipment, and perform or assist with fork lift operations. I work independently under general supervision and am subject to occasional review.
