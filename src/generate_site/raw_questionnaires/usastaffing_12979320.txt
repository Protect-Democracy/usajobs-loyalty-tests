Must maintain a thorough working knowledge and understandings of principles, concepts, policies, laws, regulations, and objectives applicable to an extensive variety of multiple complex and interacting VA and benefit programs including but not limited to eligibility determination, health benefit counseling, financial assessment processes, co-payment responsibilities, Beneficiary Travel, Long Term Care eligibility, Non-VA Care, sharing Agreements such as Tricare and CHAMPVA.

Must demonstrate working knowledge and technical proficiency in administration of multiple Beneficiary Travel programs; eligibility criteria and regulatory authorities for each; to appropriately administer the program benefits to eligible veterans.
