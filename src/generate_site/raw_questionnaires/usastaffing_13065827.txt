Ability to Do the Work of the Position without More Than Normal Supervision (Screen-out)
1.
Select the one response that best describes your work experience performing the work of a Model Maker Worker without more than normal supervision. Your response to this question must clearly be reflected in your resume or your application will be marked as not qualified. The Model Maker worker assists higher graded model makers in planning, laying out and constructing large hydraulic models and related equipment. The working models simulate the hydraulic and mechanical features of water resource structures and systems. The position involves a variety of trade skills including those using hand tools for wood, metal, plastic and concrete construction; as well as operation of construction equipment such forklifts, manlifts, hoist and skid-steers.
 I have experience with highly complex hands-on construction using hand tools and power tools for wood, metal, and plastic construction which demonstrates my skill to perform the functions of a Model Maker Worker under normal supervision. I have experience in at least two of the following trade functions: heavy carpentry, woodworking, plastic working, metal working.
 I have experience with hands-on construction using hand tools and power tools for wood, metal, and plastic construction which demonstrates my skill to perform the functions of a Model Maker Worker under normal supervision. I have experience in at least two of the following trade functions: heavy carpentry, woodworking, plastic working, metal working.
 I have experience with hands-on construction using hand tools and power tools for wood, metal, and plastic construction which demonstrates my skill to perform the functions of a Model Maker Worker under close supervision. I have experience in at least two of the following trade functions: heavy carpentry, woodworking, plastic working, metal working.
