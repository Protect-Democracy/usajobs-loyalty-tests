Specialized Experience for this position is defined as:
Drafting pleadings, briefs, and/or legal opinions in the area of trademark law; and 
Conducting legal research and drafting written work product analyzing complex and difficult legal, factual, or policy issues in the area of trademark law.

Professional Legal/Law Experience is defined as:

Having experience including judicial clerkship(s), in the area of Trademark litigation. Trademark litigation includes federal district court trademark cases, trademark appeals to the U.S. federal courts of appeal, and trial proceedings before the Trademark Trial and Appeal Board.
