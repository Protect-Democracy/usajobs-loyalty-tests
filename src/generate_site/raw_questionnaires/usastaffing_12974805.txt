What You Need to Do

You will be asked to respond to four prompts, each representing a critical competency needed for this vacancy announcement. For each prompt:
- Provide one specific example from your professional experience.
- Use the guiding questions to help develop a comprehensive response. You are not required to answer the questions in bullet or list form; however, your narrative should address each aspect to ensure a complete and well-developed example.
- Ensure your response addresses all required elements, including situation, responsibilities, actions taken, and outcomes.


Response Format

- Length: Maximum of 700 characters per response.
- Style: Use full sentences in narrative form.
- Structure: Use the Situation, Responsibility, Action, and Outcome (SRAO) framework to organize your response.


Important Notes

- Your AR responses must be written solely by you. Do not use AI (e.g., ChatGPT or similar tools) or receive assistance from others in drafting or refining your narratives.
- Do not include confidential or proprietary information in your responses.
- Responses are evaluated based on the level of responsibility, complexity, and results demonstrated, consistent with the grade level and position.
- Spell out technical acronyms or jargon to ensure clarity for raters.



Tips for Success

- Choose real, past examples; avoid hypothetical scenarios.
- Focus on what you personally did and the specific results of your actions.
- If describing a team effort, clearly explain your individual contributions.
- Emphasize outcomes and impact (e.g., what changed, improved, or was accomplished).
- Strong responses describe actions in sufficient detail and demonstrate appropriate responsibility, independent judgment, complexity, and clear impact aligned with the position.
