1.
Basic Requirements for Information Technology Management Series (2210) To meet the basic qualifications for this position, applicants must demonstrate at least one year of specialized experience equivalent to the GS-13 grade level in the federal service reflecting a wide range of knowledge of IT requirements and techniques. Experience must demonstrate proficiency in all nine of the following competencies: Attention to Detail -- performing work thoroughly and independently in complex IT environments Customer Service -- assessing needs and resolving complex IT service problems with little or no guidance. Decision Making -- making sound, well-informed decisions in uncertain or complex situations Information Management -- identifying information needs and organizing or maintaining information management systems Interpersonal Skills -- developing effective working relationships across varied and difficult situations Oral Communication -- delivering clear presentations on technical or sensitive topics to diverse audiences Problem Solving -- identifying complex problems, evaluating alternatives, and generating well-supported documentations Teamwork -- fostering cooperation and commitment across teams in difficult situations Technical Competence -- applying advanced IT knowledge to perform complex duties and advise others Which of the following best describes how you meet the basic qualification requirement for this position? Your response must be supported by your resume.

2.
Specialized Experience Requirement - GS-14 To be considered qualified for this position, you must have at least one year of full-time work experience performing duties similar in complexity and responsibility to work typically performed at the GS-13 level in the federal government. Your experience must demonstrate all four of the following areas. Experience in only some of these areas is not sufficient to meet the minimum qualification requirement for this position. Federal experience is not required - equivalent private sector, contractor, or military experience will be evaluated on the same basis. Your experience must include all of the following: 1.) Leading the development and evaluation of enterprise architecture solutions and serving as a liaison among stakeholders, developers, engineers, and business leaders to improve organizational mission outcomes. 2.) Leading the assessment of technology solutions to ensure compliance with enterprise architecture principles, security requirements, and business objectives. 3.) Experience leading solution architecture efforts by providing recommendations on system integration, data management, emerging technologies, and the evaluation of technology investments and modernization initiatives; 4.) Experience leading enterprise architecture and solution design efforts that integrate artificial intelligence (AI), automation, data, and enterprise platforms into a secure, scalable, and sustainable technology ecosystem. Important: Your resume must clearly describe the specific duties you performed, the scope of your responsibilities, and the timeframe of your experience in each of the four areas above. Responses that cannot be verified by your resume may result in a lower rating or ineligibility determination. Do you have at least one year of specialized experience as described above?
