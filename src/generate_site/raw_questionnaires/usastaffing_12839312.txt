 I have the ability to perform the most difficult and complex carpentry tasks, from planning and layout through final construction and installation. I am fully skilled in interpreting drawings, specifications, and multi-trade blueprints, and I apply national, state, and local building codes as well as historic preservation standards. I have the knowledge to construct, repair, and maintain a wide range of wooden and composite structures such as buildings, cabins, bridges, docks, floors, decks, doors, windows, scaffolds, and partitions. I am proficient in estimating materials, measuring and laying out work, and applying advanced carpentry techniques to produce precise and serviceable results. I consistently demonstrate safe work practices and knowledge of applicable regulations.
 I have the ability to independently perform a full range of carpentry duties, including constructing, installing, repairing, and modifying wooden and composite items such as doors, windows, floors, decks, shelves, and framing. I can interpret drawings and specifications, apply building codes, and use standard carpentry techniques to complete projects with proper fit and accuracy. I am able to plan, measure, and lay out materials using templates, specifications, and precut materials, and I follow established safety practices.
 I have the ability to perform common carpentry tasks under general supervision. I can use standard carpentry techniques to construct, install, and repair items such as doors, shelves, benches, or partitions where accuracy is not critical. I can follow instructions, drawings, and specifications, and I use basic measuring and math skills (add, subtract, multiply, divide, work with simple fractions) to complete assigned work. I demonstrate an understanding of safe work practices.
 I have limited carpentry knowledge and experience. I can perform simple carpentry tasks such as measuring, cutting, or assembling precut materials, but I require close supervision and detailed instructions. I may have assisted with construction, installation, or repair work but have not performed these duties independently.
 I do not have experience or training in carpentry.
