23.
Law requires removal of federal law enforcement officers convicted of felonies. The Omnibus Federal Budget Bill for Fiscal Year 2001 (Public Law 106-554) that was recently signed into law includes a provision that imposes requirements on federal agencies that employ law enforcement officers. Many DoD installations employ law enforcement officers. The provision, which is in Section 639 of PL 106-554, requires agencies to remove law enforcement officers from employment as law enforcement officers if they are convicted of a felony. Felony convictions that occurred before the law was enacted are not included in this requirement; only new convictions will trigger the requirement. The law went into effect on January 20, 2001. Are you willing to accept, as a condition of continued employment, that a future felony conviction will result in your mandatory removal from this law enforcement position?
