Are you requesting consideration under the Veterans Recruitment Appointment (VRA) hiring authority?

In order to be eligible, you must:

• Be a veteran separated within the last 3 years; OR

• Understand that VRA is an excepted-service appointing authority and eligible applicants may be appointed up to GS-11 (or equivalent).
