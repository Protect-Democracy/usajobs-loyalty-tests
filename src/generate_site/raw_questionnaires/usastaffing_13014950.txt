Specialized Experience:
You must possess the specialized experience equivalent to the next lower grade level in federal service (GS-14) or comparable experience not gained through federal service. For this position, specialized experience is defined as:
The incumbent will be the leader of a large organization supervising, leading, and mentoring IT Project Managers who oversee the day-to-day operations of the team requiring background, education, and experience as a first- or second-line federal supervisor of employees.
