 I have at least one full year of specialized experience comparable in scope and responsibility to the GL-09 grade level in the Federal service. Examples of qualifying specialized experience include but are not limited to 1. Performing law enforcement or investigative duties for the protection, conservation, use, and management of natural and/or cultural resources in park areas. 2.  COMBINATION of education and experience as described above.
