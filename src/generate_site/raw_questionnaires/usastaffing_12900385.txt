1.
Utilizes an extensive knowledge of logistics programs/operations and a clear understanding of automated systems to manage projects and programs for systems integration of Combat Service Support (CSS) automation.


2.
Knowledge to serve as a Systems Administrator for all CSS Automated Logistics Information Systems and, as a technical expert within the state/territory, is responsible for providing technical and functional guidance for specified Automated Logistics Information Systems and Programs to operators and supervisors.


3.
Knowledge to troubleshoot, diagnose, and resolve technical problems identified in trouble tickets and performs initial setup of specified Automated Logistics Information System computers to include loading software, configuring modem and network card(s), assigning Internet Protocol (IP) addresses with correct subnet, connectivity to the network, and completing technical inspections.


4.
Ability to serve as the primary point of contact for Functional Administrators and users of the serviced database(s) in the absence of a senior Logistics Management Specialist.


5.
Skill to provide direction and guidance to supported CSS Automated Logistics Information System users in the submission of automated systems problem reports and/or change proposals.


6.
Ability to establish effective professional working relationships with coworkers and customers, contributing to a cooperative working environment and successful accomplishment of the mission.
