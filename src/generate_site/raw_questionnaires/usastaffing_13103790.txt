FUL Engineering Equipment Operator WG-5716-10
1.
ABILITY TO DO THE WORK OF AN ENGINEERING EQUIPMENT OPERATOR WITHOUT MORE THAN NORMAL SUPERVISION (SCREEN-OUT)
 I am able to independently operate a variety of heavy engineering and construction equipment, including bulldozers, excavators, road graders, crawler tractors, loaders, and skid steers, to perform earthmoving, excavation, grading, compaction, and land management projects on steep slopes, rocky terrain, forests, soft ground, and other difficult conditions. I establish final grades to exact specifications, adjust attachments while equipment is in motion, transport heavy equipment using truck-and-trailer combinations, perform preventive maintenance and repairs on equipment and vehicles, maintain operational records, and perform facility maintenance and construction work using electrical, mechanical, plumbing, welding, carpentry, painting, and related trades skills. I perform this work without direct supervision.
 I am able to operate several types of heavy engineering equipment to excavate, backfill, grade, or level earth on varied terrain, including hills, forests, rocky ground, and steep slopes. I perform preventive maintenance and minor repairs and can complete most assignments independently.
 I am able to operate one or more types of heavy equipment on relatively uncomplicated terrain. I perform routine maintenance and repair work and require occasional guidance on more difficult assignments.
 I am able to operate equipment such as tractors, loaders, or similar equipment, but have limited experience operating heavy construction equipment or performing grading work.
 I have no experience or training related to engineering equipment operation.
