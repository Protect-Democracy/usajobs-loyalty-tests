Specialized experience is: Providing expert technical advice, guidance, and recommendations to senior leaders and other technical specialists on IT strategy and on the planning, development, and implementation of systems solutions, including emerging technologies. Designing, developing, or overseeing enterprise and solutions architectures for complex IT systems, including AI-enabled systems, with a focus on performance, resilience, scalability, and security. Working with machine learning (ML) methods and models, including designing, developing, evaluating, validating, monitoring, and maintaining ML algorithms and models, and addressing model drift and retraining.
