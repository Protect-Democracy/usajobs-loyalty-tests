10.
Do you have specialized experience at the GS-12 level, or equivalent, which is in or related to the work of the position to be filled, that has equipped you with the particular knowledge, skills, and abilities to successfully perform the duties of the position? 

This experience must demonstrate proficiency in the following: 

1.    COBOL Application Development and Modernization: Developing and maintaining mainframe COBOL applications in batch and/or CICS environments, including analyzing and modifying complex legacy COBOL code, designing reliable software solutions for new business functions, and implementing enhancements in accordance with the Systems Development Life Cycle (SDLC).

2.    Data Access and Mainframe File Management: Accessing and manipulating data stored in flat files, VSAM datasets, and relational databases such as DB2; writing efficient SQL in COBOL programs; and performing ad hoc queries using SPUFI or QMF.

3.    JCL, Mainframe Utilities, and Production Support: Developing, executing, and troubleshooting JCL scripts, using IBM utilities (e.g., IEBGENER, IDCAMS), file comparison tools such as COMPAREX, and sort utilities such as Syncsort. Experience providing post-implementation on-call support, resolving abends quickly and accurately, and ensuring successful completion of nightly batch processes.

4.    Mainframe Tools, Debugging, and Testing: Using industry tools such as TSO/ISPF, ENDEVOR, File-Aid, Expediter, InterTest, and Control-M to develop, debug, test, and manage COBOL programs; preparing and sanitizing test data; and identifying discrepancies between requirements and code through structured analysis and testing.

5.    Technical Communication and Cross-Team Collaboration: Communicating technical concepts clearly and effectively in both written and verbal formats; preparing documentation that explains system changes, impacts, and requirements; collaborating with analysts, testers, and other developers; and mentoring lower-level developers to support team and project goals.
