 I am able to perform and have held jobs in which I performed regular and recurring work leading a crew of workers performing Small Craft Operating tasks involving watercraft of varying sizes and have the ability to lead operation of a 32 foot, six ton dual outboard boat with up to 27 occupants. I have the capability to independently dock and secure boats to dock and canal structures. I possess the ability to navigate a boat in narrow canals under varying flow rates. I am able to communicate with other units involved in boat operations such as dispatch, supervisor(s), and deckhands. I maintain log books to record passenger counts. I can perform the majority of watercraft operation and navigation functions with minimum supervision from journeyman leads.
 I am able to perform the full range of Small Craft Operator tasks involving watercraft of varying sizes and have the ability to operate a 32 foot, six ton dual outboard boat with up to 27 occupants. I am capable of safely loading and unloading passengers and have sufficient knowledge of emergency procedures to respond appropriately to difficult situations on my own initiative under limited supervision subject to occasional inspection.
 I am able to perform most of the tasks listed in A with under direct supervision of a Small Craft Operator supervisor who observes tasks in progress and inspects them upon completion to make sure they are properly performed.
 I am able to perform simple tasks and have limited knowledge of operating watercraft, navigating boats in canals, communicating with others and operating a small craft on a canal system or in a river.
