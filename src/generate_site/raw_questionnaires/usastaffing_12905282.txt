 I have the ability to perform the most difficult and complex duties as a Preservationist. I am skilled in independently performing key functions of a minimum of two trades, including carpentry, roofing, and/or painting, to successfully preserve, stabilize, restore, and maintain historic structures. Related skills may include but are not limited to: 1) Carpentry: Constructing, repairing, and preserving a wide variety of historic structural items such as building frames, rafters, concrete forms, walls, staircases, door and window frames, interior and exterior trim, and miscellaneous items such as counter tops, bookcases, cabinets, etc., preserving the appearance and integrity of the items’ restoration period, including special finishes and colors; Using wood, metal, and various composite materials when installing, replacing, and repairing items; 2) Roofing: Determining the cause and extent of damage, then utilizing a variety of materials and accessories such as shingles, roofing felt, and asphalt in order to repair, restore, preserve, or install new roofs to historic structures in accordance with strict historic standards; 3) Painting: Identifying and retaining special historic painting colors and finishes, removing damaged or deteriorated areas using the gentlest method possible; Determining the methods, devices, and materials that are best suited to the historic surface, including brushes, rollers, spray guns, and graining devices designed to match the unique texture, decorative, thickness, and level requirements of a historic structure. I am considered an expert and work with a high degree of independence
 I have the ability to perform the full range of duties as a Preservationist. I am skilled in performing the following tasks with some guidance, 1) Performing preservation and restoration carpentry, including planning, estimating materials, and installing structural framing such as stair stringers, roof rafters, floor joists, beams, headers, posts, etc.; 2) Planning and estimating materials for installation of roof sheathing, roofing, flashing, and gutters, ensuring historic integrity is properly identified and replicated throughout the work process; 3) Preparing historic surfaces for painting, identifying the gentlest appropriate method possible, such as smoothing with pumice and rottenstone, filling, shaping, and leveling with metal and synthetic resins; Mixing and applying precoating agents; Utilizing brushes, rollers, spray guns, and graining devices to ensure the end result matches the texture, decorative, thickness, and level requirements listed in historic documentation. I work on my own initiative under general supervision subject to occasional inspection.
 I have the ability to perform simple Preservationist tasks including: 1) Reconstructing, restoring, or rehabilitating wood structures, using instruments and tools such as rulers, try squares, levels, transits, framing hammers and other traditional carpentry tools; 2) Locating roof leaks and determining the extent of their damage; Selecting the proper work method to be followed for repair, using common materials such as shingles, roofing felt, and asphalt to maintain historic standards; 3) Preparing painting surfaces using smoothing, filling, shaping, and leveling techniques; Mixing, tinting, and toning both precoating and coating agents, applying final coating materials, and ensuring end results match all historic painting elements from the restoration period. I work under normal supervision of a work leader or journey-level employee who observes tasks in progress and upon completion to make sure they are properly performed.
 I do not have on the job training or the abilities described above.
