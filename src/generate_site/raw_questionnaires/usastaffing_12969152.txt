Conditions of Employment
1.
Incumbent uses frequent and repetitive arm and leg movements to operate tractor controls and equipment controls.  The operator lifts and handles heavy equipment attachments when connecting them to tractor.  Incumbent also lifts materials, such as bags of fertilizer and seed, weighing up to 50 pounds.

Are you willing and able to meet the physical demands of the position?
 Yes
 No


2.
Work is performed outside, subject to long periods of in the sun and sometimes in inclement weather as well heat, high humidity and chilly temperatures.  Normal exposure to dirt, dust and mud as well as chemical fertilizers and there is a chance of minor cuts and bruises.
