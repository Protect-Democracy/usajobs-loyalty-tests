Assessment 1
This Leader position requires individuals to work independently to perform housekeeping duties that include, but are not limited to: leading the work or a various group of individuals; sweeping, dusting, cleaning, polishing, vacuuming, and trash removal; preparing chemicals for cleaning and sanitizing according to prescribed application; reviewing standard operating procedures to decide which procedure, pieces of equipment and/or cleaning agent is most appropriate; handling and disposition of infectious and biohazardous materials
