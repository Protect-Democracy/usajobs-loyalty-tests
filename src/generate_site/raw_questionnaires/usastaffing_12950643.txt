This assessment presents 12 scenario-based situational judgment questions designed to evaluate candidates' competency across the major duty areas for the GS-14 Assistant Special Agent in Charge position. The ASAC serves as the first-line supervisory leader over criminal investigators (GS-7 through GS-14) conducting complex, sensitive investigations of DHS employees, contractors, and senior officials - and reports directly to the Special Agent in Charge (SAC). The position requires Top Secret/SCI clearance and may serve as Acting SAC.


Instructions: Read each scenario carefully. Select the single response that best reflects effective judgment for a GS-14 ASAC. Each response is weighted on a 0-4 point scale. Select only one response per question. The best answer is not always the same letter.

What is your most appropriate course of action?

 Notify the U.S. Attorney's Office of the affidavit issue immediately to protect the prosecution, then brief the SAC and conduct the internal accountability review as a secondary priority.
 Assess the materiality of the inaccuracy before taking action — if the inaccuracy is minor and would not have independently affected the warrant's issuance, document the finding internally and continue the investigation without external notification.
 Suspend the agent from the case immediately, reassign the investigation, and initiate formal misconduct proceedings before consulting the SAC, to prevent any appearance of supervisory inaction.
