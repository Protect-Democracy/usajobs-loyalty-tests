1.
Demonstrated ability to plan, prioritize, and allocate personnel, funding, and equipment across Aircraft Generation and Component/Equipment Maintenance functions to meet daily through annual production goals, contingency demands, and Total Force integration requirements; adept at analyzing subordinate plans, forecasting workload, and rebalancing resources to recover schedule and readiness.


2.
Proven capability to lead subordinate supervisors; set and enforce standards; appraise performance; recommend promotions/reassignments; administer leave, incentive/recognition, EEO, and labor-management programs; and hear/resolve grievances while sustaining a fair, safe, and productive workplace.


3.
Expertise in implementing safety regulations; assuring production/quality standards; and preparing for inspections, readiness evaluations, mobilization, and command support exercises; experienced aligning processes and documentation to meet compliance and inspection criteria.


4.
Broad, in-depth knowledge of aircraft systems maintenance practices-installing, aligning, troubleshooting, and validating mechanical/pneudraulic/ electrical systems; applies technical judgment to plan work, adapt methods to new situations, and mentor supervisors/technicians; holds compatible AFSC and senior-level experience commensurate with WS-13 supervision.


5.
Skillful at coordinating maintenance operations with primary/secondary shifts, adjacent shops, higher headquarters, and external stakeholders; communicates effectively in writing and orally to translate leadership guidance into executable plans, operating instructions, and status updates.
