To be creditable, specialized experience must be at the next lower grade level or equivalent under other pay systems in the Federal service, military, or private sector. Read each of the statements below completely before making your selection.
 I have one year of specialized experience at the next lower grade (GS-12) or equivalent in the federal service (experience may have been gained in the private sector). Specialized Experience is defined as: - Applies knowledge of transportation concepts, principles, regulations, and methods to the management of the agency's Transportation Mission Execution and Requirements Cell (TMERC), non-tactical vehicle fleet, Smartcard, and combating weapons of mass destruction (CWMD) transportation requirements that are global in nature. - Applies knowledge of the mission, regulations, and objectives of the agency transportation requirements in support of CWMD in order to solve complex problems arising from specific geographical locations that require high level coordination due to sensitive and classified nature of materials being transported; and to conduct studies of transportation activities, transportation feasibility for mode selection, and develops courses of action to resolve issues. - Spearheads special handling and movement requirements in order to handle domestic/export freight shipment, ammunition and other weapons of mass destruction(WMD)/hazardous materials, as well as a vast knowledge of the relationship of transportation management within other Department of War (DoW) agencies and combatant commands’ theater of operation. - Oversees the General Services Administration (GSA) fleet management and reporting system and management of GSA owned and Agency leased fleet of vehicles in order to provide fleet management services and support for assigned customers and supplier accounts.
 My experience does not meet the requirements listed above.
