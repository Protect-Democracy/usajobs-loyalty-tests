 I have the ability to perform the most difficult and complex Maintenance Worker (Trails) duties. This includes trail construction of major trail bridges, staircases, retaining walls, switchbacks, turnpikes, and water bars without assistance. I have utilized advanced rigging techniques using block, tackle, and winch systems. I have used chainsaws to fall hazard trees, remove root balls, and clear fallen trees from trails. I have led the logistics, set up, and removed backcountry camps. I am considered an expert and work with a high degree of independence. I can lift and carry objects weighing more than 100 pounds.
 I have the ability to perform the full range of Maintenance Worker (Trails) duties. This includes trail construction and maintenance of minor trail bridges, steps, retaining walls, water bars, and turnpikes without assistance. I have utilized advanced rigging techniques using block, tackle, and winch systems. I have used chainsaws to fall trees for bridge stringers, remove root balls, and clear fallen trees from trails. I have led the logistics, set up, and removed backcountry camps. I work on my own initiative under general supervision subject to occasional inspection. I can lift and carry objects weighing more than 100 pounds.
 I have the ability to perform common Maintenance Worker (Trails) duties. This includes trail construction and maintenance of trail signs, turnpikes, water bars, step over drains, grade reversals, and trail tread. I have utilized rigging techniques using block, tackle, and winch systems. I have used chainsaws to fall trees and clear fallen trees from trails. I have assisted with the logistics, set up, and removed front country camps. I have completed these tasks under normal supervision. I can lift and carry objects weighing more than 100 pounds.
 I do not possess the abilities described above.
