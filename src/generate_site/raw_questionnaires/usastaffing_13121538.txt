Health Technician (Intermediate Care Technician), GS-5 (Entry Level) Experience. None beyond the basic requirements. Employees at this grade level serve in entry-level ICT positions. They are expected to receive guidance from more experienced staff members and require close daily supervision. The ICT is expected to apply comprehensive working knowledge of organizational principles and the anatomy and physiology of human systems to patient care. The ICT participates in daily team huddles and completes pre-visit planning. The ICT performs and documents patient intake, health history, chief complaints, self-reported screenings, and medication reviews. The ICT identifies emergency medical conditions or other health-related conditions, obtains and documents vital signs, and assists in behavioral or psychiatric situations in emergency, hospital, or outpatient settings. The ICT prepares patients for medical evaluation, sets up equipment, assists with procedures, collects specimens, and reviews laboratory results. The ICT performs basic life support and safe patient handling and performs minor procedures, interventions, and treatments. The ICT provides patient education on procedures and processes related to ICT tasks.

Health Technician (Intermediate Care Technician), GS-8 (Full Performance Level) Employees at this grade level serve as the full performance level ICTs. The ICT performs more complex medicine assignments within the full range of duties allotted and is responsible for assisting licensed personnel with the delievery of care and examinations to patients with complex, specialized, or potentially life-threatening situations. The ICT administers advanced life support care in accordance with established medical standards, which may include CPR, airway management, and drug treatments. ICTs at this level independently perform more highly technical skills that are typically performed in an ambulatory care setting, such as, but not limited to, skin procedures (punch biopsy, callus removal, wound closure with suture/staples, nail care), gastrointestinal procedures (nasogastric tube placement), musculoskeletal (splinting, ambulation aid sizing), cardiovascular (ultrasound guided IV placement), and otolaryngology (epistaxis control, ear irrigation). Applicants must have one year of experience comparable to the next lower grade level (GS-7) which demonstrates the knowledge, skills, and abilities related to the position to be filled. In addition to meeting the experience or educational requirements for this grade level, the candidate must fully demonstrate the following KSAs: (a) Knowledge of complex integrated health care and its interactions with other healthcare systems.
