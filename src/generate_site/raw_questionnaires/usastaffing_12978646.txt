 Education: One of the following types of education in a related field (e.g., Criminal Justice, Law): a master's degree or equivalent graduate degree OR 2 full years of progressively higher-level graduate education leading to a master's degree, OR LL.B. or J.D., if related, from an accredited college or university that provided the knowledge, skills, and abilities necessary to perform the duties of this position.  Combination of Education and Experience: A combination of specialized experience and graduate education (beyond the first full year of graduate study) that when combined equals 100 percent of the qualification requirement.
