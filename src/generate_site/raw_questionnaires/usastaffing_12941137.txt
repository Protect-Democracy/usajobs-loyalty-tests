Ability To Do The Work Of The Position Without More Than Normal Supervision - From the descriptions below, select the appropriate response based on your level of experience, education, and/or training, that you fully possess and that demonstrates your ability to perform Carpentry Worker duties without more than normal supervision.
 I have the ability to perform the most difficult and complex carpenter tasks including; modifying various wood and wood substitute structures; applying advanced woodworking techniques and shop mathematics; installing foundations, walls, floors, ceilings and roofs using materials such as wood, steel, metal, concrete, plastics, and composites of multiple materials; installing doors, window frames, stairs and railings; preparing project layout and determine dimensions and materials required; removing damaged or defective parts or sections of structures and repaired or replaced them; inspecting ceiling or floor tile, wall coverings, siding, glass or woodwork to detect broken or damaged structures; laying out and constructing forms; assisting masons with cement pour and finishing. I am skilled in utilizing the full range of floor mounted and portable carpentry/woodworking equipment and tools. I am considered an expert and work with a high degree of independence.
 I have the ability to perform the full range of carpentry tasks and have performed the majority of the following duties with minimal supervision: repairing a wide variety of interior and exterior structural components to include framing, walls, floors and roof utilizing standard woodworking techniques and basic shop mathematics; utilizing blueprints, sketches and drawings to plan work and determine materials; assisting with the installation of components such as door and window frames, interior and exterior trim; working with a variety of materials to include but not limited to wood, sheetrock, plaster; replacing door and cabinet components and hardware. I am skilled in assisting journeyman carpenters and mason with laying forms, pouring and finishing concrete. I work on my own initiative under general supervision subject to occasional inspection.
 I have the ability to perform common carpentry tasks perform common carpentry tasks utilizing blueprints, sketches and diagrams to install pre-planned framing and partitions of wood or fabricated material; using pattern, spacing and techniques for nailing, fastening, bracing, splicing and cleating, and using standard joints and grooves; using basic shop arithmetic to fit and measure boards or sheets of material; using templates and precut or preplanned materials to complete assigned carpentry projects. I can perform surface prep and painting of various materials and structures. I work under close supervision of a work leader or journey-level employee who observes tasks in progress and upon completion to make sure they are properly performed.
 I have the ability to perform simple tasks including applying basic arithmetic; utilizing simple drawings and blueprints to determine work processes; selecting, measuring and cutting materials to desired length; carrying, lifting and holding material in place during installation, and assembling pre-fabricated materials or I have received training on reading blueprints, measuring and cutting materials and assembling pre-fabricated materials.
