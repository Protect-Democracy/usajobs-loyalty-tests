These Knowledge, Skills, and Abilities (KSAs) reflect the critical competencies needed for successful performance in this position:
Skill in applying fish and wildlife laws and regulations, including import/export requirements, search and seizure authorities, and federal statutes related to smuggling, conspiracy, money laundering, and mail fraud.
Knowledge of investigative, compliance, and legal principles; advanced analytical and surveillance techniques; and evidence development practices to conduct sensitive investigations, analyze complex data, resolve inconsistencies, support prosecutorial actions, provide expert testimony, and develop methods for complex enforcement issues.
Knowledge of international investigative principles, foreign legal systems, and multinational enforcement practices to coordinate with global partners, analyze cross-border intelligence, and secure evidence for complex international wildlife crime investigations.
Skill in applying effective communication techniques to convey complex information clearly, organize messages logically, and tailor briefings and reports to diverse audiences.
Skill in oral communication to present findings in clear, concise, and accurate briefings and presentations to varied audiences.
