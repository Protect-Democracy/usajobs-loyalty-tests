I am skilled in operating gasoline or diesel-powered heavy equipment, the use of motor vehicles of more the 32,000 pounds capacity to transport material to and from the work site; operating a front-end loader, trucks of more the 32,000 pounds capacity with snow plows, and/or blowers, liquid tanks, and salt spreaders and dump trucks up to ten ton capacity. I work under supervision of a work leader or journey-level employee who reviews my work for completion.
 I have the ability to perform common Motor Vehicle Operator duties. such as operating gasoline or diesel-powered heavy equipment, the use of motor vehicles of more the 32,000 pounds capacity to transport material to and from the work site; operating a front-end loader, and trucks of more the 32,000 pounds capacity with snow plows and/or blowers, liquid tanks, and salt spreaders. I work under close supervision of a work leader or journey-level employee who observes tasks in progress and upon completion to make sure they are properly performed.
