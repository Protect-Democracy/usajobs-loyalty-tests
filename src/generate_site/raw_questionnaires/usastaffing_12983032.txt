1.
Specialized Experience
You must possess the specialized experience equivalent to the next lower grade level in federal service (GS-14) or public or private sector equivalent. For this position, specialized experience is defined as:
Mastery of management and program analysis principles, methods, and practices sufficient to direct and evaluate complex workforce management and organizational effectiveness programs.
Expert knowledge of strategic workforce planning, organizational management, and human capital principles sufficient to support division-wide workforce initiative and operational priorities.
Mastery of analytical and evaluative methods sufficient to assess workforce challenges, identify systemic issues, and develop solutions to complex organizational problems.
Comprehensive knowledge of Federal workforce policies, Executive Orders, DHS directives, Intelligence Community guidance, and workforce management regulations.
Knowledge of organizational design, talent management, succession planning, position management, and performance management sufficient to advise leadership and improve organizational effectiveness.
Mastery of communications, collaboration, and stakeholder engagement techniques sufficient to represent the organization before senior officials and interagency partners.
NOTE: Your resume must explicitly indicate how you meet this requirement, otherwise you will be found ineligible.
