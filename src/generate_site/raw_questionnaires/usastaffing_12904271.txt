 Performed the most difficult and complex laborer leader tasks independently including ability to serve as a work leader over a crew of three or more workers. I provide laboring assistance and assign immediate tasks to crew members in the maintenance of buildings, grounds, and trails; operate power equipment such as chainsaws or pruning saws, and safely use a variety of hand tools such as shovels, rakes, hammers, Pulaski, fire rakes, loppers, axes, adzes, and drawknives, rock bars, etc. I have experience performing operator maintenance on all equipment operated (e.g. monitoring fluid levels, checking tire pressure and blade replacement/sharpening). I perform these duties as an expert with a high degree of independence. My supervisor assigned work orally, through work orders, or with written specifications. My work was checked to see that it met compliance with work and safety standards and policies.
 I performed common laborer leader tasks including leading a crew of three or more. Tasks include leading others in both trail and campground maintenance and construction, assigning immediate tasks to be performed by individual crew members, planning work assignments several days in advance, anticipating needs, evaluating site conditions, and developing strategies prior to assigning work. Taught safe work practices and techniques while ensuring proper use of equipment in the construction of foot bridges, walkways (boardwalks), unpaved trail erosion control, removal of hazard trees (rigging, felling, chainsaw operation), grading, sign repair/installation, selective brush removal, building tent pads, and installation/maintenance of privies. I utilize most hand and power tools associated with these tasks. I performed these tasks as a skilled leader under general supervision and my work was checked during and after completion.
 I have performed common tasks associated with the position of Laborer Leader with close supervision. Taught safe work practices and techniques while ensuring proper use of equipment in the construction of foot bridges, walkways (boardwalks), unpaved trail erosion control, removal of hazard trees (rigging, felling, chainsaw operation), grading, sign repair/installation, selective brush removal, building tent pads, and installation/maintenance of privies.
