4.
Are you applying based upon the Veterans Opportunity to Work (VOW) to Hire Heroes Act of 2011?
 
Many service members begin their civilian job search prior to being released from Active Duty service, and may not have a DD-214 form to submit along with their resume. VOW allows certain Active Duty service members to be treated as preference eligibles in the competitive or excepted service, even though the service member has not been discharged or released from Active Duty. Under the VOW Act, tentative preference will be granted to a active duty service member who meets the following:Submit a certification (in lieu of a DD-214 form) along with their job application materials. (Agencies will verify the individual meets the definition of 'preference eligible' under 5.U.S.C. 2108 prior to appointment).
A "certification" is any written document from the Armed Forces that certifies the service member is expected to be discharged or released from Active Duty service in the Armed Forces within 120 days after the certification is submitted by the applicant.
The certification letter should be on letterhead of the appropriate military branch of service and contain (1) the military service dates including the expected discharge or release date; and (2) the Character of Service.Note:  If the certification has expired; you must provide documentation (e.g., a copy of the DD form 214) that demonstrates the service member is a preference eligible per 5 U.S.C. 2108, in your application package before veterans' preference can be awarded.

To Learn more about VOW, please go to: https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/veterans-employment-initiative/vet-guide

1.
Applicants must meet the following SCREENOUT: Ability to do the work of a Machinist without more than normal supervision. Examples of experience include analyzing interrelated dimensions or complex configuration problems and determining effective solutions; troubleshooting on a wide range of machines and determine repair processes in conjunction with appropriate engineering and technical authority; assembling, installing, maintaining and repairing a variety of printing, binding and related machinery and equipment (i.e. printing and bindery, industrial trucks, power plant and air conditioning machinery); making independent interpretations of drawings and specifications that are lacking in dimensions, tolerances and types of fits and finishes, and making repairs.

From the descriptions below select ONE response that best demonstrates your ability to perform the duties of a Machinist based on your current level of experience.
 I independently perform the most difficult duties of a journeyperson machinist without more than normal supervision. I perform bench, machine, and hand tool work in shaping, fitting, finishing and assembling machine parts from metal stock. I assemble, install, maintain and repair a variety of printing, binding and related machinery and equipment. I am able to read and understand shop drawings, blueprints and specifications. I have knowledge of welding, cutting and brazing techniques, I am able to operate lathes, milling machines, grinders and other machine shop equipment for particular projects.
 I am able to perform the full range of journeyperson machinist duties on my own initiative, with general supervision. I assemble, install, and repair machinery; complete assignments as described in oral and written instructions and/or work orders with appropriate references such as blueprints, building plans, and sketches; and perform precision hand work (e.g. scraping, lapping, and filing) to fit machined parts.
 I am able to perform the tasks of a journeyperson machinist according to instructions given by a supervisor or leader. I plan and perform specific jobs in the machinist trade. I select proper cutting tools according to the type of metal being machined; make manual cutter compensation adjustments to obtain tolerances; makes repairs according to instructions supplemented by blueprints, specifications and sketches.
 I am able to perform machinist tasks as a skilled helper and work under close supervision of a leader or journeyperson machinist. My work is reviewed in progress upon completion to ensure tasks are properly performed.
 I have very little experience and/or training as a machinist.
