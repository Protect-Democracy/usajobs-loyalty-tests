Assessment 1

QUALIFICATION REQUIRMENTS: To qualify for this position, you must meet the qualification requirements outlined below:

SPECIALIZED EXPERIENCE/EDUCATION GS-09: To be eligible for this position, you must have one (1) year of specialized experience at a level of difficulty and responsibility equivalent to the GS-07 grade level in the Federal service.
Specialized experience includes:

Experience applying fact-finding, analytical, and evaluative methods to gather, organize, and examine information concerning program operations, work processes, or administrative activities; identifying procedural issues; and documenting findings and recommended corrective actions.
Experience preparing and presenting data, interpretations, conclusions, and recommendations orally and in writing to support reviews of program operations and the achievement of established goals and objectives.
OR
EDUCATION: You may substitute education for specialized experience as follows:
A master's or equivalent graduate degree in any field of study.
OR
Two (2) full years of progressively higher-level graduate education leading to any field of study.
OR
LL.B. or J.D., if related.
OR
COMBINATION OF EDUCATION AND EXPERIENCE: A combination of graduate education and experience equivalent to that described above.

SPECIALIZED EXPERIENCE/EDUCATION GS-11: To be eligible for this position, you must have one (1) year of specialized experience at a level of difficulty and responsibility equivalent to the GS-09 grade level in the Federal service.
Specialized experience includes:
Experience gathering, assembling, and analyzing facts concerning functional programs, operations, and work processes; conducting studies or evaluations; identifying problems or procedural deficiencies; developing alternative courses of action; and recommending changes to methods, processes, or administrative procedures.
Experience planning and coordinating project activities; establishing work objectives, schedules, and milestones; monitoring project progress; and preparing and presenting plans, findings, and recommendations orally and in writing to management or other stakeholders.
OR
EDUCATION: You may substitute education for specialized experience as follows:
A Ph.D. or equivalent doctoral degree.
OR
Three (3) full years of progressively higher-level graduate education leading to a Ph.D. or equivalent doctoral degree.
OR
LL.M., if related.
OR
COMBINATION OF EDUCATION AND EXPERIENCE: A combination of qualifying graduate education and experience equivalent to that described above.

SPECIALIZED EXPERIENCE GS-12: To be eligible for this position, you must have one (1) year of specialized experience at a level of difficulty and responsibility equivalent to the GS-11 grade level in the Federal service.
Specialized experience includes:
Experience conducting operational reviews, analytical studies, or program evaluations; analyzing inventory, workload, workflow, or performance information; identifying delays, gaps, overlapping activities, or procedural deficiencies; evaluating the effects of proposed changes; and recommending revisions to operating methods, staffing, work processes, or administrative procedures.
Experience planning, scheduling, implementing, monitoring, and evaluating projects or studies; establishing project objectives, milestones, resource requirements, and evaluation criteria; coordinating project activities; and documenting project results and recommended actions.
Experience analyzing business measures against established standards; developing spreadsheets, reports, or other work products to track and communicate program data; identifying operational problems; evaluating alternative solutions through cost-benefit, risk, or comparable analyses; and recommending a course of action.
Experience preparing and delivering reports, project papers, manuals, directives, job aids, training materials, briefings, or other technical products; presenting findings and recommendations to management; and negotiating resolution of disputed recommendations or proposed changes.
