1.
I have one (1) year of GS-11 specialized experience that has equipped me with the particular knowledge, skills, and abilities (KSAs) to successfully perform the duties of the position, and that is typically in or related to the position to be filled. To be creditable, the specialized experience must have been at the GS-11 level or equivalent under other pay systems in the federal service and must have included: 1) Broad knowledge of the theories and principles of logistics management through all project phases; 2) Thorough knowledge of the mission, roles, functions, organizational structures, and operation of the DoW, Air Force, and organizations that govern, interface with, and/or influence the logistics management program processes; 3) Comprehensive knowledge of the policies, practices, regulations, and laws concerning acquisition and logistics management processes and initiatives sufficient to prepare long-range plans, analyze, and coordinate a system or portion of a major system's acquisition and transfer into operational use; 4) Expert knowledge of the application of a wide range of qualitative and quantitative assessment methods sufficient to improve logistics planning processes and enhance the quality and effectiveness of complex logistics programs; 5) Knowledge of military command structures, missions and programs for organizations involved in logistics planning; 6) Comprehensive knowledge of the Logistics support needs of assigned programs, systems, equipment and components; 7) Expert knowledge of the administrative processes and documentation requirements of logistics planning; 8) Knowledge of regulations and practices pertaining to programming and budgeting for Logistics functions; 9) Knowledge of DoW weapons systems management and sustainment program goals, sequences of key weapons systems management and sustainment events and milestones, and methods of evaluating weapons systems management and sustainment; and 10) Ability to apply knowledge to design and conduct comprehensive studies of logistics management information systems analysis issues for which boundaries are extremely broad and difficult to determine in advance.
