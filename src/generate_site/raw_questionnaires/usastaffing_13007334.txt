You are a Drug Testing Technician assigned to the specimen intake and collection area of a legitimate drug testing facility. Your duties include observing donors as they enter the collection site and ensuring that urine specimens are collected, handled, and documented according to strict federal procedures.

As a Drug Testing Technician or Specimen Collector working for an organization, agency, company, or medical facility, you are required to follow established protocols that protect specimen integrity and maintain an unbroken chain of custody.

You will be presented with three scenarios related to specimen handling, documentation, and procedural compliance.

This assessment evaluates your understanding of:

Proper laboratory and collection-site procedures
Chain-of-custody requirements
Specimen integrity and handling
Documentation accuracy
Safety and compliance practices
