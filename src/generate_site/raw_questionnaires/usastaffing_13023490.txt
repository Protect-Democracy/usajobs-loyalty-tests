 I have the ability to perform the most difficult and complex pipe fitting and fixtures; locating and marking spots where equipment will be installed; threading and pipe bending; placing and connecting water fixtures and facilities such as hydrants, water mains, water closets, lavatories, showers, sinks, ice machines, dishwashing machines, unit gas heaters, stoves, and air compression equipment. I am skilled in clearing difficult stoppages in drainpipes, Operating influent discharge valves to balance waste pond sewage levels and performing preventive maintenance inspection and repairs on valves and equipment. I am considered an expert and work with a high degree of independence.
 I have the ability to perform the full range of plumbing tasks including pipe fitting; placing and connecting water fixtures and facilities such as hydrants, water mains, water closets, lavatories, showers, sinks, ice machines and dishwashing machines; I am skilled in clearing drain stoppages; testing joints and piping for leaks; and check piping, fittings and fixtures for defective parts. I work on my own initiative under general supervision subject to occasional inspection.
 I have the ability to perform common plumbing repairs and installation. I can check piping, fitting, or fixtures for defective parts or connections or leaks. Assisted with replacement or repairs of various plumbing fixtures or pipes using hand tools such as rulers, saws and pipe wrenches. I work under normal supervision of a work leader or journey-level employee who observes tasks in progress and upon completion to make sure they are properly performed.
 I have the ability to perform simple plumbing repairs such as connecting fittings and unclogging minor drain stoppages or I have received training on plumbing repair and the use of basic hand tools.
