Assessment 1
The GS-12 Supervisory Recreational Therapist plans, develops and administers a therapeutic and diversional program that asserts the dignity and worth of self-capacity for self-realization through Recreation Services throughout the facility. The Supervisory Recreation Therapist (SRT) at the Carl Vinson VA Medical Center must have at a minimum one year of creditable experience at the next lower grade level and carry and active certification from National Council for Therapeutic Recreation Certification (NCTRC).
