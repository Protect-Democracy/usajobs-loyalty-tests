2.
Do you have the ability to do the work of the Preservation Helper, WG-4701-05, position without more than normal supervision?

 I have the ability to perform the work of a Preservation Helper, WG-4701-05 without more than normal supervision on various preservation projects and tasks. Simple and routine preservation trade techniques are performed in an array of different work environments, involving historic buildings, monuments, memorials, statues, markers, signs, tablets, or large exterior or interior objects. The work may also include elements of restoration or reconstruction of historic properties. Typical work assignments include, but are not limited to tasks where the incumbent has a basic level of skill in preserving, restoring, maintaining, or building historic structures and landscapes, and the altering or repairing structural elements.
 I have the ability to perform the work of a Preservation Helper. I have assisted or been trained in the maintenance and repair of a variety of historic structures, buildings, monuments, or objects. Some of the work I have done is on historic structures. I have done this work with minimal supervision.
 I have the ability to perform the work of a Preservation Helper. I am able to assist journey-level Preservationists in repairing, maintaining or constructing a variety of historic structures, monuments, or objects. I can repair damaged portions of historic structures. I can preserve or restore historic buildings, monuments, memorials, or objects. I am able to carry out this work through oral or written instruction with minimal supervision
 I am able to perform simple Preservation Helper tasks with consistent supervision of my work.
 I do not have on the job training or experience as a Preservation Helper.
