GS-13 SPECIALIZED EXPERIENCE: To qualify for this position you must have one (1) year of specialized experience at a level of difficulty and responsibility equivalent to the GS-12 grade level in the Federal service. Specialized experience for this position includes: Researching procedural guides encompassing references to multiple business units, legal research background, or other experience that required a broad understanding of cross functional operations and procedures, regulations, or other areas related to the position to be filled. Preparing, reviewing, or applying standard procedures such as the Internal Revenue Manual, advisory letters, memoranda, etc. related to procedures in multiple functions in addition to cross functional cooperation; analyzing and adjudicating taxpayer claims, appeals, negotiation strategies, or similar work related to Federal tax operations; and conducting and interpreting FOIA law, regulations, and procedures. Applying knowledge of Federal tax laws, IRC, IRM, and the procedures of multiple business units or divisions of the IRS to address taxpayer's needs. Interpreting laws, regulations, and other technical matters to prepare clear, concise, logically arranged, well written and easy to understand explanatory material or briefs.
