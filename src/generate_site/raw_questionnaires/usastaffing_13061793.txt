2.
Minimum Qualifications: Do you possess One year of Specialized Experience AND/OR Do you Qualify for the Education Requirement related to this position OR a combination of both Specialized Experience and Education?

Specialized experience include;  Cashier functions and workflow of transactions, cash management, and disbursement of VA funds. Monitors, maintains, and balances (on a daily basis), the credit union account, the cash on hand, and ensures adequate cash flow is always on hand to operate the daily cashier functions. Independently monitor all allotments of Medical care. Process and submit daily Fiscal and Revenue deposits Over the Counter Chanel Application (OTC net). Monitor daily debit voucher reports as a means of tracking bounced checks. Performs monthly reconciliations. Complete monthly reports within the expected timeframe to allow other parties (i.e., accounting) to complete their portion of the monthly reports on time as well. Disburses funds in accordance with authority delegated by the Treasury Department, VA, and VHA policy such as the following: (1) Treasury checks, (2) convenience checks, (3) Patient Funds which are withdrawn from the patient's Veteran's Personal Finance System (VPFS) accounts and managed by the Patient Funds Clerk, (4) Travel vouchers which are issued and managed by the Travel Clerk, and (5) Work-Compensated Therapy vouchers which are issued and managed by the accounting department.

Education Requirement: 4 years above high school. Successfully completed education above the high school level in any field. This education must have been obtained in an accredited business, secretarial or technical school, junior college, college or university. One year of full-time academic study is defined as 30 semester hours, 45 quarter hours, or the equivalent in a college or university, or at least 20 hours of classroom instruction per week for approximately 36 weeks in a business, secretarial, or technical school. (Must submit transcripts)

Combining Education and Experience: Equivalent combinations of successfully completed post-high school education and experience may be used to meet total experience requirements.
 Yes, I meet the Specialized Experience and/or Education requirement or Combination of both. Transcripts are included with my application regarding education.
 No, I do not meet the Specialized Experience and/or Education requirement or Combination of both.
