Minimum Qualification
 I have one (1) year of specialized experience equivalent to the GS-4 grade level or higher in the Federal service which involves a variety of support tasks related to avian and/or wildlife. Specialized experience is described as 1) conducting surveys of avian species or wildlife; 2) recording scientific data and preparing reports; 3) assisting in collecting and recording information on avian species; and 4) identifying, observing population dynamics, and conducting nest searches for avian species. Note: Your resume must clearly show demonstrated experience in all of these areas.
