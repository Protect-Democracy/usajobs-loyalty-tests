Are you currently employed with Peace Corps?

Please note: If you are a US Direct Hire, Expert, or Temporary Employee, you MUST submit a recent Standard Form-50 (SF-50) that verifies you meet the time-in-grade requirement for the grade at which you are applying.  See the "Qualifications" section of the job announcement for a description of time-in-grade requirements.  If you are applying for a higher grade and your SF-50 has an effective date within the past year, it may not clearly demonstrate you meet the one-year time-in-grade requirement.  If you do not submit an SF-50 with your application materials, your application will be disqualified from further consideration.

Some examples of appropriate SF-50s include:
Within grade increases at the highest grade held; or
Promotions with an effective date more than one year old; or
SF-50s at the highest grade held with an effective date more than one year old.
DO NOT submit the following SF-50s, since they will not demonstrate that you meet time-in-grade:
Cash or time-off award SF-50s; or,
General Adjustments with an effective date less than a year old.
If you are a US Citizen Personnel Service Contractor, you MUST submit documentation to verify your employment.  If you do not submit documentation of your Personnel Service Contract with your application materials, your application will be disqualified from further consideration.

Has your background or work history involved a connection to intelligence activities?

"Intelligence activity" includes any activities or specialized training involving or related to the clandestine collection of information, or the analysis or dissemination of such information, intended for use by the United States Government or any foreign government in formulating or implementing political or military policy in regard to other countries.
