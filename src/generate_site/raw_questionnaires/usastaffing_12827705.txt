1.
Inventory Management & Control: Knowledge of inventory management principles, procedures, and techniques applicable to Army supply systems. Skill in utilizing automated to accurately track, manage, and control the flow of organizational clothing and individual equipment (OCIE).


2.
Logistics & Supply Chain Management: Knowledge of logistics principles, including storage, distribution, and transportation of supplies. Understanding of the Army's supply chain from national-level support to the point of issue at the CIF.


3.
Data Analysis & Reporting: Skills in collecting, analyzing, and interpreting data related to inventory levels, issue rates, and loss/damage reports. Ability to generate accurate reports and identify trends to inform supply management decisions. Proficiency in Microsoft Excel or similar data analysis tools. Capable of preparing detailed reports, briefings, and recommendations for improving supply chain efficiency.


4.
Army Regulations & Policies: Familiarity of relevant Army regulations and policies pertaining to supply management, property accountability, and CIF operations.


5.
Problem Solving & Communication: Ability to identify and resolve complex supply issues, such as discrepancies in inventory, shortages, or damaged items. Effective communication skills (written and verbal) to interact with Soldiers, leaders, unit representatives, warehouse staff and other co-workers.
