Using GIS and land records systems to analyze spatial data, verify land status, research case histories, and support decision-making for complex lands and minerals actions. 7.) Communicating findings and recommendations clearly in writing and verbally, including preparing case summaries, correspondence, and technical documentation for internal and external stakeholders.
