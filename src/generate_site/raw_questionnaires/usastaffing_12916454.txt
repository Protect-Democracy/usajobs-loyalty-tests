Assessment 1
MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS for this GS-15 Contract Specialist (Senior Technical Advisor) position are based on an Individual Occupational Requirement (IOR) for the federal 1102 series including at least 52 weeks of Specialized Experience.


SPECIALIZED EXPERIENCE must have been gained in a federal position at or above GS-14 OR (if your position and/or experience is in another federal pay scale OR outside of federal service) by performing duties at a level of responsibility and difficulty equivalent to GS-14.  Such experience must include work that primarily consists of providing coordinative, analytical, consultative and advisory services on complex acquisition matters, including contract placement principles, contract management and oversight principles and specialized aspects of contracting.
