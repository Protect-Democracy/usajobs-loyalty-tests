Preferences
This position is focused on the technical capability of Cybersecurity Engineering. As part of the assessment process for this position, you will be required to demonstrate your expertise in Cybersecurity Engineering.

If Cybersecurity Engineering is your primary technical capability, reflecting the majority of your cybersecurity technical expertise and experience, please continue.

If not, please contact cybersecurityservice@hq.dhs.gov for assistance before you proceed. To learn more about DHS Cybersecurity Service technical capabilities visit: http://dhscs.usajobs.gov/Jobs.


1.
In the DHS Cybersecurity Service, employees in the Technical Track typically have 5+ years of cybersecurity work experience in progressively-difficult individual contributor roles. They also have fully-formed cybersecurity technical expertise, and some are even resident experts relied on by their organizations to address the most pressing technical challenges.

Select the response that best describes your cybersecurity technical work experience. Please ensure that your resume clearly reflects cybersecurity technical work experience.
