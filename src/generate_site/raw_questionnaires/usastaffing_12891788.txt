Successful completion is evidenced by documentation of successful completion by the training organization. OR I possess successful completion of a formal certificate program in any of the areas (or related areas) listed in Training 1 (above) or those closely related as evidenced by the course or programs. The courses must have been provided by an accredited college, university, organization or company and must have required assignments and/or other course work. Successful completion is evidenced by documentation of completion by the training organization.
