Read all the statements below, and then select the response that best describes your highest level of experience and/or training as it relates to your ability to perform the duties of the position.

 I have the ability to perform the most difficult and complex Boiler Plant Operator tasks. I am consulted by other workers because of my expertise in boiler plant operations that include: comprehensive knowledge of all operational phases of power boiler plant operations, (e.g. water treatment, fuel systems, steam generation, and pollution control.) Skill in reading, recording, and analyzing information from gauges, meters, analog displays, and computer generated data to determine the operational status. Knowledge of mechanical systems and devices to install, maintain, troubleshoot and repair various types of equipment. I am considered an expert in this field and work with a high degree of independence.
 I have the ability to perform the full range of Boiler Plant Operator tasks in setting up and performing various types of boiler plant operations that include: sufficient knowledge of all operational phases of power boiler plant operations, (e.g. water treatment, fuel systems, steam generation, and pollution control.) Skill in reading, recording, and analyzing information from gauges, meters, analog displays, and computer generated data to determine the operational status. I independently resolve boiler plant problems during the actual process. Knowledge of mechanical systems and devices to install, maintain, troubleshoot and repair various types of equipment. I work on my own initiative under general supervision subject to occasional inspection.
 I have the ability to perform common Boiler Plant Operator tasks to include: basic knowledge of all operational phases of power boiler plant operations, (e.g. water treatment, fuel systems, steam generation, and pollution control.) Skill in reading, recording, and analyzing information from gauges, meters, analog displays, and computer generated data to determine the operational status. Knowledge of mechanical systems and devices to install, maintain, troubleshoot and repair various types of equipment. I work under close supervision of a work leader or journey-level employee who observes tasks in progress and upon completion to make sure they are properly performed.
