Assessment 1

Minimum Qualification Requirements for the GS-05 Human Resources Assistant Position

To qualify for this position, you must meet one of the following requirements:

Option 1: Specialized Experience

You have at least one year (52 weeks) of specialized experience comparable in difficulty and responsibility to the GS-04 grade level.

Federal experience is not required. Qualifying experience may have been gained in Federal service, the military, state or local government, private industry, nonprofit organizations, educational institutions, or other organizations.

Specialized experience includes:

Assisting with processing basic personnel actions using HR systems and established procedures to support accurate data entry and maintenance of personnel records;
Maintaining personnel records and supporting documentation using established filing systems and checklists to ensure completeness and accessibility of employee files;
Assisting with onboarding and separation tasks using standard procedures and instructions to support timely completion of required forms and documentation; and
Recording personnel data in HR systems using standard input methods to maintain current and accurate information.

Part-time experience will be credited on a prorated basis. To receive credit for one full year of specialized experience, you must have completed the equivalent of 2,087 hours of work experience.

Option 2: Education

You have successfully completed 4 years of education above the high school level at an accredited college, university, junior college, business school, technical school, secretarial school, or similar institution.  You must submit transcripts with your application for an HR Specialist to verify you satisfy this requirement.

Option 3: Combination of Education and Experience

You have a combination of qualifying post-high school education and specialized experience that together meet the qualification requirements for this position.

Your education and experience will be evaluated based on the information provide in your application, including your resume and any transcripts submitted. Only education beyond the second year of post-high school study may be used toward the combination qualification requirement.

Important

Your response must be supported by the information provided in your application materials. We will verify your qualifications based on your resume and any education documents you submit.

Minimum Qualification Requirements for the GS-06 Human Resources Assistant Position
To qualify for this position, you must have one year (52 weeks) of specialized experience comparable in difficulty and responsibility to the GS-05 grade level.
Federal experience is not required. Qualifying experience may have been gained in Federal service, military service, state or local government, private industry, nonprofit organizations, educational institutions, or other organizations.
Examples of qualifying specialized experience include:
Processing personnel actions using HR systems and established procedures.
Reviewing personnel records and supporting documents to identify errors, verify completeness, and maintain accurate records.
Assisting with employee onboarding and separation activities using HR systems and standard procedures.

Part-time experience is credited on a prorated basis. Your total qualifying experience will be converted to a full-time equivalent. To receive credit for one full year of specialized experience, you must have completed the equivalent of 2,087 hours of work experience.
