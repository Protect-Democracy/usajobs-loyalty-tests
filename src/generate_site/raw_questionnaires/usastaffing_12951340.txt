2.
Do you have the ability to do the work of the Mason Helper, WG-3603-5 position without more than normal supervision?

 I have the ability to perform the work of a Mason Helper, WG-3603- 05 without more than normal supervision on various masonry projects. Simple and routine masonry trade techniques are performed in an array of different work environments, involving historic and/or contemporary buildings, features, structures, or large exterior or interior objects. The work may also include elements of new construction and/or demolition. Typical work assignments include, but are not limited to tasks where the incumbent has a basic level of skill in construction or repair of ornate or decorative building features and components of historic structures, and the altering or repairs to structural elements.
 I have the ability to perform the work of a Mason Helper. I have assisted or been trained in the maintenance and repair of a variety of structures of brick and mortar, terra cotta, and stone. Some of the work I have done is on historic structures. I have done this work with minimal supervision.
 I have the ability to perform the work of a Mason Helper. I am able to assist journey-level masons in repairing, maintaining or constructing a variety of structures and surfaces of brick, stone or mortar. I can remove damaged portions of walls, partitions. I can tear out portions of walls for the installation of windows and doors. I am able to carry out this work through oral or written instruction with minimal supervision.
 I am able to perform simple Mason Helper tasks with consistent supervision of my work.
 I do not have on the job training or experience as a Mason Helper
