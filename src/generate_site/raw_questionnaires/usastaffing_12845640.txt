 I have the ability to perform the most difficult and complex tasks which include utilizing manual and computer-controlled machining equipment while conducting tooling adjustments, coolant checks, and regular machine maintenance; utilizing micrometers, calipers, indicators, and other various handheld measuring tools to verify dimensions meet specified tolerances; and operating conventional or computer machinery to construct mechanical parts and assemblies. I am considered an expert and work with a high degree of independence.
 I have the ability to perform the full range duties which include making minor changes in machine set up such as realigning the work piece for different sizes or changes in shape; reporting major or complex changes in machine set-up to supervisor; and operating conventional or computer machinery to construct mechanical parts and assemblies. I am skilled in these duties, and I work on my own initiative under general supervision subject to occasional inspection.
 I have the ability to perform common duties which include operating conventional or computer machinery to construct mechanical parts and assemblies; and making minor offset adjustments to maintain sizes and shapes of work produced. I work under normal supervision of a work leader or journey-level employee who observes tasks in progress and upon completion to make sure they are properly performed.
 I have the ability to perform simple tooling maintenance and machine clean-up, or I have received training on this type of work.
