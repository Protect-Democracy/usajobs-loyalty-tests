Specialized Experience: You must have one year of specialized experience equivalent to at least the next lower grade GS-07 in the normal line of progression for the occupation in the organization. Examples of specialized experience would typically include, but are not limited to: Oversee administrative programs including records management, inventory, and time sheets. Education: Applicants may substitute education for the required experience. OR,
Combination: Applicants may also combine education and experience to qualify at this level. You must have the appropriate combination of GS-07 level specialized experience and only graduate-level education beyond the first year (18 semester hours or 27 quarter hours).
