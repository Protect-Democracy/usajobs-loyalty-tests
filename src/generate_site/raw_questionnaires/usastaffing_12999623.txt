For this position, there is a  Certification/Training Requirement:

In Addition to the basic education and experience requirements, you must meet the Federal Acquisition Certification in Contracting (FAC-C) (Professional) requirement through one of the options listed below:

1) Must already possess Federal Acquisition Certification in Contracting (FAC-C) (Professional) certification

OR

2) Must possess legacy program FAC-C Level II, or III Certification from the Federal Acquisition Institute (FAI) or Defense Acquisition University (DAU).

NOTE: To keep FAC-C (Professional) Certification once achieved, contracting professionals are required to earn 100 Continuous Learning Points (CLPs) every two years. All CLP classes shall be approved by the first line supervisor. For those who are already certified, the two-year period begins on issue date of the established training policy for contracting professionals in Acquisition Services.
