Preferences
1.
In the last five years (from the closing date of this announcement) have you served, or are you currently serving, as an Executive Branch Political, Schedule C, or Non-career Senior Executive Services appointee? You can find out if you have held one of these appointment types by looking at your SF-50s, in Section 5 where the legal authorities are listed.
A political appointee is an appointment made by the President without confirmation by the Senate (5 CFR 213.3102(c)) OR an assistant position to a top-level federal official if filled by a person designated by the President as a White House Fellow (5 CFR 213.3102(z)).
A Schedule C appointee occupies a position excepted from the competitive service by the President, or by the Director of OPM, because of the confidential or policy-determining nature of the position duties (5 CFR 213.3301 and 5 CFR 213.3302).
A Non-career SES appointee is approved by the White House and serves at the pleasure of the appointing official without time limitations (5 CFR 317 Subpart F).

SPECIALIZED EXPERIENCE GS-15

You must have at least one year of specialized experience equivalent to the next lower grade level in federal service (GS-14) or public or private sector equivalent. For this position, specialized experience is defined as:
Lead and manage major agency-wide or government-wide research and development terrorism and/or transnational organized crime programs with significant impact on organizational mission, congressional priorities, and public interests.
Direct complex projects requiring coordination across multiple internal and external stakeholders, including senior officials within the Department, customer organizations, and other federal agencies.
Develop and implement strategic plans, policies, and procedures to ensure effective program execution and alignment with agency goals addressing transnational organized crime and terrorism.
Establish and maintain effective working relationships with senior leadership, customer organizations, and partner agencies to facilitate collaboration and achieve program objectives.
Oversee program performance, resource allocation, and risk management to ensure delivery of high-impact outcomes supporting the agency's mission.
