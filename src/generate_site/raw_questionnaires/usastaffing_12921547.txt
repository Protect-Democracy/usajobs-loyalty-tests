 I am able to perform the most difficult and complex Powered Support Equipment Mechanic tasks. I have the ability to install, operate, inspect, maintain, and repair electrical power production equipment and associated components, electrical distribution equipment, diesel, gasoline, and/or multi-fuel engines, and aircraft arresting barriers. I have the ability to operate manual and remote switches, circuit breakers, rheostats, and other controls on a variety of automatic transfer switches, switchgears, and power distribution panels to perform necessary electric power control and distribution functions. I have the ability to check pumps, batteries, fan belts, and spark plugs for wear and tear or replacement and check engine performance, generator bearings, and oil and temperature gauges. I can analyze and interpret blueprints, wiring schematics, control panel diagrams, technical manuals, and manufacturer’s manuals. I can make adjustments, repairs, and replace major assemblies and components such as rings, pistons, rods, rod bearings, main bearings, seals, camshaft bearings, valves, valve guides, and gaskets for engines up to 3 to 8 cylinders and 500 horsepower.
 I am able to perform the full range of Powered Support Equipment Mechanic tasks. I have the ability to operate and adjust power production components to ensure efficient electric power control and distribution to meet electrical load demands. I have the ability to check equipment for fuel, lubricants, coolant, and battery water levels and check pumps, batteries, fan belts, and spark plugs for wear and tear or replacement. I can accurately interpret schematic wiring diagrams, blueprints and technical manuals to solve complex repair problems. I have the ability to promptly and accurately troubleshoot and diagnose complex repair situations involving the electrical components of electrical power plants and power distribution equipment.
 I am able to perform the common Powered Support Equipment Mechanic tasks. I have the ability to operate equipment in a safe manner, applying established safety rules and regulations to minimize minor violations and to avoid major violations due to employee error or negligence. I am capable of performing repairs on components, make adjustments, alignments, and calibrations. I can assist higher level workers with operating and adjusting power production components to ensure efficient electric power control and distribution and to meet electrical load demands.
 I am able to perform the simple Powered Support Equipment Mechanic tasks. I am familiar with tools used by higher grade mechanics used to perform testing and troubleshooting equipment and system malfunctions.
