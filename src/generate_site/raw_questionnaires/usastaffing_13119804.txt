Do you possess one year of progressively complex experience equivalent to the GS-11 grade level, and can you demonstrate all of the following knowledge, skills, and abilities? Skill to effectively communicate both orally and in writing with staff at all organizational levels, with Veterans and their family members, with community groups, and individuals with varying degrees of understanding.
