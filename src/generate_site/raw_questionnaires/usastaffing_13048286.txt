Assessment 1
GS-09 SPECIALIZED EXPERIENCE: You must have one year of specialized experience at a level of difficulty and responsibility equivalent to the GS-8 grade level in the Federal service. Specialized experience must include:
Applying the methods, precedents, and guidelines related to individual and/or business accounts to determine the appropriate amount of tax, penalties, interest, or payments.
Applying methods, precedents, and guidelines related to Accounts Management programs. (e.g. applying IRM guidelines to review case actions and ensure compliance with established procedures)
Communicating specialized information with technical procedural determinations to internal stakeholders (such as Frontline Managers and Department Managers) to clarify, defend, or resolve disputed quality review defects and provide options available for meeting tax obligations and assessing the effectiveness and accuracy of those communications.
navigating technical disagreements or conflicting interpretations of guidelines between operational teams and quality review staff to achieve compliance and accurate account determination.
Utilizing on-line systems to conduct comprehensive, multi-system account reconstruction to resolve formal tax administration or quality disputes.
Applying written communication to prepare and present reports, draft proposals, conduct presentations, coordinate work processes, provide constructive feedback, write formal reconsideration responses, and author directorate-wide Quality Improvement Initiatives.
Utilizing inventory management procedures to control casework and conduct research including working with automated systems to select and/or assign cases in a fair and consistent manner.
Identifying discrepancies or inconsistencies in casework  and evaluating, researching, and issuing final technical determinations on contested case files or first-level formal rebuttals/reconsiderations within established timeframes.
