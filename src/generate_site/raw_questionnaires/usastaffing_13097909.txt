 I possess one year of specialized experience at or equivalent to the GS-4 grade level in the Federal service performing work in the field of, or directly related to, biological science including assisting in the collection of data in the field; entering data into simple automated databases; and using basic fish capture equipment.  I have completed a four-year course of study leading to a bachelor's degree with major study or at least 24 semester hours in any combination of courses such as biology, chemistry, statistics, entomology, animal husbandry, botany, physics, agriculture, or mathematics.
