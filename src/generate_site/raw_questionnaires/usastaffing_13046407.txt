Assessment 1
Specialized experience is one year of experience at the GS-13 level or equivalent in other public or private sectors, that is directly related to the position listed in this announcement and which has equipped the candidate with the knowledge, skills, and abilities to successfully perform the duties of the position. To be credited, your experience must have been performed independently for at least one year at a level of responsibility equivalent to the GS-13 (or comparable public or private sector experience). Select only those duties you performed as a regular part of your assigned responsibilities. Specialized experience is defined as:

- Supervising production operations, including directing work, evaluating performance, resolving personnel issues, and developing staff; AND
- Allocating and scheduling personnel, equipment, and other resources to meet production schedules and organizational priorities: AND
- Applying continuous improvement principles, such as Lean Manufacturing or similar process improvement methodologies, to improve productivity, quality, and operational performance.
