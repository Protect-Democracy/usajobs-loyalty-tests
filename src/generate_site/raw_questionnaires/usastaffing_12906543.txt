 I have the Ability to Lead or Supervise the most difficult and complex Maintenance Mechanic duties, such as tests circuits and equipment by use of a variety of diagnostic tools and equipment such as ohmmeters, ammeters, voltmeters, Wiggins, etc. Measures, cuts, threads, bends, assemble, and install conduit. Inserts, splices, and connects wires to fixtures, outlets, switches, receptacles, and power source. Maintains and repairs industrial electronic controls and equipment. Maintains additional air conditioning units including those with a cooling capacity of up to 1200 tons, and up to 1200 ton centrifugal cooling compressor, powered by a two-speed electric motor. When major breakdown occurs, completely tears down unit, checking all parts, e.g., pump, coils, valves, controls and completely rebuilds, replacing any required parts. Tests, troubleshoots and repairs air conditioning controls including pneumatic; electronic; electric; modulating type; low voltage (24V); line voltage controls to maintain humidity, pressure, and temperature (cooling or heating); hydraulic operated valves and damper operators; and swimming pool temperature controls using specialized test equipment. Determines parts and equipment and estimate costs for repair and modification of systems. Modifies systems to meet energy-saving requirements and locates and modifies parts to replace obsolete parts. Inspects, adjusts, replaces or repairs thermostats; digi-stats; sensors switches; fuel cut off and feed systems; burners; flame safeguard controls; bearings; drive gears; springs; gaskets, fans and couplings; and damaged parts. Repairs and replaces malfunctioning electronic and other control and safety devices. Services systems by replacing filters, cleaning strainers, orifices, ignition electrodes, pumps and fans, adjusting dampers, and testing and adjusting tire for combustion efficiency. I am considered an expert and work with a high degree of independence.
