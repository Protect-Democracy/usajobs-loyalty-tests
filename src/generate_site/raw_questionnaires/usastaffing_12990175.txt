Assessment 1
For the GS-9, you must have one year of specialized experience at a level of difficulty and responsibility equivalent
to the GS-7 grade level in the Federal service. Specialized experience for this position includes:

- Experience in interpreting and adapting to guidelines (e.g. library and agency information policies, regulations,
operating procedures, and cataloging rules and formats, etc) ; AND
- Experience maintaining up-to-date information for the interests and needs of a geriatric community.
OR
2 full years of progressively higher level graduate education or master's in library science or equivalent graduate
degree, e.g., LL.B. or J.D., related to the position.
OR
A combination of education and experience may be used to qualify for this position as long as the computed
percentage of the requirements is at least 100%. NOTE: You must submit transcripts with your application. (Graduate
education beyond the first 18 semester hours or 27 quarter hours can be combined with GS-7 level experience)
