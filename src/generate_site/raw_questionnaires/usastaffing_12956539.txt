Assessment 1
For the GS-13, you must have one year of specialized experience at a level of difficulty and responsibility equivalent to the GS-12 grade level in the Federal service which have equipped the candidate with the particular knowledge, skills, and abilities to successfully perform the duties of the position. Specialized experience for this position includes demonstrated achievement as a senior IT systems administrator within an enterprise scale IT infrastructure, with experience utilizing a wide range of server operating systems and technical support functions .

Examples of such experience include:
- Provide recommendations on architectural modifications, system enhancements, and technology selections to ensure solutions are secure, scalable, and aligned with organizational strategy; OR
- Perform application development and enhancement work across multiple systems to support workflow optimization, process automation, data integration, and customer-facing service delivery; OR
-Serve as a technical authority in the design, acquisition, implementation, and operation of Microsoft server operating system platforms and associated Commercial Off-The-Shelf (COTS) software.
