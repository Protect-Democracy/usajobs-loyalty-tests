Specialized Experience:
Applicants must have one (1) year of specialized experience equivalent to at least the next lower grade level (GS-12) in the normal line of progression for the occupation in the organization. Specialized experience is defined as experience demonstrating all of the following:
Experience developing, recommending, and implementing basic policies to assist departmental budget staff in preparing and submitting budget estimates for these programs.
Experience developing long-range budgetary and program requirements to assure their conformance with established agency policies.
Experience developing monthly analyses of budget program actual and possible trends, with detailed explanation of significant deviations.
Experience providing expert advice and guidance to program and line managers on the interpretation of budget estimates and the formulation of budget requests in support of the financial assistance programs.
Experience developing cost data for legislative estimates in order verify accuracy or justify estimates.
Experience providing management and technical staff with advice and guidance on preparation of written documents, including administrative issues, forms and form letters, communications, and records management, and maintaining history file.
