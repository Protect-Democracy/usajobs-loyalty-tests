Basic Education Requirement
 I have a degree in biological science from an accredited college or university that included at least 9 semester hours in such wildlife subjects as mammalogy, ornithology, animal ecology, wildlife management, or research courses in the field of wildlife biology; and 12 semester hours in zoology in such subjects as general zoology, invertebrate zoology, vertebrate zoology, comparative anatomy, physiology, genetics, ecology, cellular biology, parasitology, entomology, or research courses in such subjects (Excess courses in wildlife biology may be used to meet the zoology requirements where appropriate.); and 9 semester hours in botany or the related plant sciences.
 Combination of education and experience: courses equivalent to a major in biological science (i.e., at least 30 semester hours), with at least 9 semester hours in wildlife subjects, 12 semester hours in zoology, and 9 semester hours in botany or related plant science, as shown in A above, plus appropriate experience or additional education.
 I do not meet the basic education requirements mentioned above.

Minimum Qualification GS-0486-12
 I have one year of specialized experience equivalent to the GS-11 grade level or higher that demonstrates my ability to perform successfully the duties of this position and that is typically in or related to the work of this position to be filled. Specialized experience is defined as experience: 1) Complete biological opinions on large, complex, high-profile projects with effects to multiple species. 2) Lead teams or coordinate with multiple individuals with diverse perspectives and objectives to achieve a common goal. 3) Develop science-based conservation goals for projects to help improve the status of listed species leading to recovery and demonstrate creativity to achieve conservation under section 7 or other sections of the Act. 4) Work on high-profile projects with aggressive timelines, brief higher level agency representatives, and organize administrative records.
