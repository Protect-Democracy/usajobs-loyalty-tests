 I have the ability to perform the full range of Engineering Equipment Operator (Maintenance Mechanic) duties under minimal supervision. I am able to operate and maneuver heavy equipment in confined locations. I am skilled in performing maintenance and repair of electrical, heating, plumbing, electrical work, carpentry and landscaping work. I consult with the supervisor or work leader on complex equipment operation. I work on my own initiative under general supervision subject to occasional inspections.
 I have the ability to perform common Engineering Equipment Operator (Maintenance Mechanic) duties under general supervision.
