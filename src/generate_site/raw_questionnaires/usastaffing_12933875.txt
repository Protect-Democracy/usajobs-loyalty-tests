1.
Ability To Do The Work Of The Position Without More Than Normal Supervision: To be qualified for this position, applicants must have the skills, knowledge and ability to perform the work without more than normal supervision. The ability to independently install, overhaul, repair and perform preventative maintenance on a variety of industrial equipment controls. The equipment served shall include, but not be limited to, variable frequency controllers, microprocessors, electro-pneumatic receiver/controllers, pneumatic, electric, and electronic controls, sensors, and alarms involved with large and small air conditioning systems, air handling units, chillers, pumps, compressors and other similar equipment integral to refrigeration, heating, ventilation, and energy management systems. Ability to maintain and update graphical interface, equipment graphics and control software through Windows operating systems. The incumbent uses devices and software such as workstation computers, servers, and laptop computers to perform network and communications, trouble shooting of control systems for equipment such as Siemens, Honeywell, Johnson controls, and other Direct Digital Control (DDC) systems.
