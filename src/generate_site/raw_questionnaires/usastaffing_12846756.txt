Applicant Accomplishment Record Instructions: You are invited to complete the Accomplishment Record (AR) portion of your application. This assessment is designed to gather examples of your past experience that reflect the critical competencies required for the position.

What You Need to Do: You will be asked to respond to four prompts, each representing a critical competency needed for this vacancy announcement. For each prompt:
-Provide one specific example from your professional experience.
-Use the guiding questions to develop a comprehensive response. You are not required to answer them in bullet or list form, but your narrative should address each aspect to ensure a strong and complete example.
-Your response should be a narrative that clearly communicates what you did, how you did it, and what the outcome was.

Response Format:
-Character limit: Up to 2,500 characters per response.
-Style: Use full sentences and narrative form.
-Structure: We recommend using the STAR format (Situation, Task, Action, Result) to organize your response clearly and effectively.

Important Notes:
-Your AR responses must be written solely by you. Do not use AI (e.g., ChatGPT or similar tools) or receive assistance from others in drafting or refining your narratives.
-Do not include confidential or proprietary information in your responses.
-Responses are evaluated based on the level of responsibility, complexity, and results demonstrated, aligned with the grade and position.

Tips for Success:
-Choose real, past examples-avoid hypothetical scenarios.
-Avoid vague generalizations-focus on what you did and the specific results of your actions.
-If discussing a team effort, highlight your individual role and contributions.
-Think about the results: What changed, improved, or was accomplished because of your efforts?
-The strongest responses will describe your actions in detail and demonstrate independent decision-making, the complexity of the situation, and clear impact.
-Responses that show comprehensive, high-level performance aligned with the role are more likely to receive the highest scores.
