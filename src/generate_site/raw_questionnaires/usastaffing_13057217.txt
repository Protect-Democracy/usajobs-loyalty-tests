Assessment 1
Specialized experience is one year (52 weeks) of experience at the GS-13 level, or equivalent that has equipped you with the knowledge, skills, and abilities to successfully perform the duties of the position. Specialized experience includes:

- Applying a wide range of human resources (HR) concepts, principles, laws, regulations and practices involving at least five of the following HR specialties: staffing acquisition; executive resources; classification and position management; pay and leave administration; learning and development; performance management; benefits; work-life programs; or HR Information Systems; AND
- Providing comprehensive HR services and consultation to management and employees by analyzing, evaluating, and recommending appropriate resolutions to interrelated problems and issues; AND
- Assisting with the development, interpretation, and/or advisement to management on human resources policies and administration; AND
- Analyzing proposed changes in recruitment and placement operations and assessing the impact of those changes on human capital programs;  AND
- As part of an HR team, collaborating with key stakeholders as necessary to advise or guide managers in the most effective personnel management program initiatives.
