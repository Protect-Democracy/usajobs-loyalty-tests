Audiologist (Clinical Specialist), GS-13.
Experience. At least two years of experience as a professional audiologist, with at least one year comparable to the next lower grade level.
GS-12 Audiologist experience is demonstrated by: Licensed to practice independently in the provision of audiologic services, consulting with peers and supervisors as appropriate. Serve as consultants to other health care professionals and are recognized subject matter experts (SME) on matters related to hearing, tinnitus, and balance disorders. May have been assigned as staff audiologists in any setting, such as inpatient or outpatient medicine, primary care, surgery, hearing health, neurology, rehabilitation medicine, geriatrics, compensation, and pension. Diagnose audiologic disorders, conduct audiologic, or neuro-audiologic assessments; treat auditory disorders through various modalities; and provide adjunctive interventions for the treatment of medical disorders. Audiologists consult with medical center staff on various patient care issues. May be involved in program evaluation, teaching, training, and research activities. Modalities provided may be individual, family, and group audiologic rehabilitation.
Demonstrated Knowledge, Skills, and Abilities (KSAs). In addition to the KSAs required at the full performance level, the following KSAs are required:
Advanced knowledge in a focused area of contemporary audiology practice to provide oversight of a specialty program.
Skill to evaluate the specialty program to ensure deadlines and goals are met.
Skill to coordinate initiatives and build consensus among interdisciplinary team members.
Ability to monitor and report on work progress in the specialty program to adjust workload and processes.
Ability to serve as the SME and provide education relating to the specialty program.
