QUALIFICATION REQUIRMENTS: To qualify for this position, you must meet the qualification requirements outlined below:

 SPECIALIZED EXPERIENCE FOR GS-07:
You must have 1 year of specialized experience at a level of difficulty and responsibility equivalent to the GS-06 grade level in the Federal service. Specialized experience for this position includes: experience that is related to the work of this position and has provided you with the competencies required for successful job performance. 
Utilizing experience gained in positions dealing with the public analyzing tax or accounting problems and resolving high-conflict differences through use of established guidelines, or in a position dealing with customers in person, in writing or by telephone in a variety of situations that require solicitation of information to identify concerns and offer solutions.
Interpreting account information to address a variety of issues, guidelines, past transactions and tax examination specialization of assigned workload. researching, analyzing, and interpreting taxpayer account information and procedural guidance to identify issues, determine appropriate actions, and communicate technical information effectively to employees and management officials.
Experience with organizational functions, terminology, quality assurance techniques, and reporting requirements to coordinate workflow and resolve related problems.
Applying administrative policies, procedures and functions to provide support to the supervisor or to use when acting as supervisor.
Applying established workflow through the function and ability to assess the performance capabilities of employees to assign work appropriately.
