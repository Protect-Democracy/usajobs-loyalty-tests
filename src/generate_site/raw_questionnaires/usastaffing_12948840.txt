Specialized Experience GS-09- To be creditable for this position at the GS-09 level, applicants must have at least 52 weeks of specialized experience equivalent to the next lower grade level GS-07 in the normal line of progression for the occupation in the organization. 

Specialized experience for this position includes but is not limited to: Ability to use qualitative and quantitative techniques for analyzing and measuring the effectiveness, efficiency, and productivity of administrative and technical programs.
