The duties of this position consist of:

- Operating, maintaining, troubleshooting, and repairing exterior irrigation systems, including pumps, time clocks, valves, sprinkler heads, and piping.
- Performing basic carpentry and plumbing work associated with the maintenance and repair of buildings, structures, utilities, and exterior site features.
- Performing grounds and landscape maintenance, including mowing, trimming, pruning, grading, seeding, and related upkeep.
- Operating and maintaining tools and equipment used for grounds and facility maintenance, including tractors, loaders, mowers, line trimmers, and snow-removal equipment.
