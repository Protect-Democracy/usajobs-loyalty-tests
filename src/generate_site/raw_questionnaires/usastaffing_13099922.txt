From the descriptions below, select the response that best matches your level of experience, education, and/or training. You must fully possess the selected qualification and demonstrate the ability to perform the duties of this position without more than normal supervision. The duties of this position include: Troubleshooting operating and maintenance problems involving high-pressure steam, industrial chiller, cogeneration, and associated utility systems. Determining appropriate maintenance or repair methods for utility system equipment based on operating conditions and established procedures. Analyzing instrument readings and test results to identify equipment malfunctions and determine necessary adjustments or repairs. Interpreting blueprints, operating and maintenance manuals, and technical specifications to perform utility system work. Using specialized tools and equipment to adjust, maintain, and repair high-pressure steam, industrial chiller, cogeneration, and associated utility systems.
 I have no education, training, or experience performing work related to the operation, maintenance, repair, testing, or troubleshooting of HVAC or utility systems.
 I have education, training, or experience performing basic or routine HVAC/utility system work, such as assisting with equipment inspections, preventive maintenance, minor repairs, taking system readings, or using basic tools and measuring instruments.
 I have experience performing a range of HVAC/utility system operation and repair work, such as troubleshooting equipment problems; performing maintenance and repairs; using testing and measuring instruments; and interpreting equipment manuals, diagrams, blueprints, or specifications to complete assigned work.
 I have experience performing difficult utility system operation, maintenance, and repair work, including troubleshooting equipment malfunctions; analyzing instrument readings and test results; determining appropriate maintenance or repair methods; interpreting blueprints, specifications, and technical manuals; and adjusting, maintaining, and repairing high-pressure steam, industrial chiller, cogeneration, or associated utility systems.
 I have extensive experience performing complex utility system operation, maintenance, and repair work, including independently diagnosing and resolving complex or unusual system problems; determining appropriate procedures, methods, tools, and corrective actions; interpreting and applying technical specifications and operating manuals; and assisting others in troubleshooting high-pressure steam, industrial chiller, cogeneration, or associated utility systems.
