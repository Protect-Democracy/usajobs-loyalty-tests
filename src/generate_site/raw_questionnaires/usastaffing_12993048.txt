1.
From the descriptions below, select the appropriate response based on your level of experience, education, and/or training, that you fully possess and that demonstrates your ability to perform the Forklift Operator (Materials Handler) duties without more than normal supervision.
 I have the ability to perform the most difficult and complex duties handling equipment while loading/unloading explosives and inert material into/out of railcars, trucks, and loading docks. I am skilled in operating forklifts to lift heavy loads to extreme heights and maneuver fragile or hazardous materials through tight spaces, narrow aisles, and rough terrain. I handle these complex material handling tasks with minimal supervision and occasional inspection. I am considered an expert and work with a high degree of independence.
 I have the ability to perform the full range of materials handling equipment while loading/unloading explosives and inert material into/out of railcars, trucks, and loading docks. I am skilled in operating a full range of material handling equipment which includes operating heavy-duty forklifts to lift extreme weights to high places and maneuvering safely through rough terrain, confined indoor spaces, and narrow aisles. I work on my own initiative under general supervision subject to occasional inspection.
 I have the ability to perform common materials handling equipment while loading/unloading explosives and inert material into/out of railcars, trucks, and loading docks. I can handle controls for starting, stopping, backing and driving forklift trucks through narrow aisles in buildings, up and down ramps and in and out of railroad cars and trucks. I can visually conceptualize whether a specific load is maneuverable through a particular space. I work under normal supervision of a work leader or journey-level employee who observes tasks in progress and upon completion to make sure they are properly performed.
 I have the ability to perform simple operations of material handling equipment while loading/unloading explosives and inert material into/out of railcars, trucks, and loading docks or I have received training on operating a forklift moving materials.
