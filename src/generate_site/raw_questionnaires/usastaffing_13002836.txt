Assessment 1
You will now be asked to complete the second assessment.  The following narrative questions will be reviewed and assessed by a Subject Matter Expert (SME) to determine your level of proficiency. 
For each of the elements, please provide a short-written description in your own words, without using artificial intelligence (AI) tools such as a large language model (LLM) like ChatGPT or Copilot. Suggested length for each job element is no more than 300 words.
Please allow sufficient time to complete before midnight on the close date of the announcement.  Non-detailed and answers such as N/A, See Resume, etc. will be deemed ineligible.


1.
Describe your experience planning, conducting, supervising, and reporting information technology or cybersecurity audits in accordance with Generally Accepted Government Auditing Standards (GAGAS) or equivalent non-federal auditing standards and guidelines. Include examples of how you applied FISMA, OMB Circular A-130, NIST standards and guidance, or other relevant criteria during the audit. Your response should describe the audit objectives, your role, methodologies used, challenges encountered, how issues were resolved, results achieved, and where the experience is reflected in your resume.



2.
Describe a situation in which you led an audit team through a complex or high-risk IT or cybersecurity audit. Include your role in planning and executing the engagement, assigning work, monitoring progress, resolving disagreements or obstacles, making key decisions, and ensuring compliance with applicable auditing standards. Discuss the outcome and where this experience is reflected in your resume.


3.
Describe a situation where you communicated complex IT, cybersecurity, or audit-related issues to senior leadership, program officials, or other stakeholders. Include the purpose of communication, the audience, any challenges encountered, how you tailored your message, and the outcome. Reference where this experience is reflected in your resume.


4.
Describe a situation where you were required to analyze and synthesize large amounts of technical, operational, or audit-related information to support an audit conclusion or management decision. Include the information reviewed, your analytical approach, decisions made, and the outcome. Reference where this experience is reflected in your resume.


5.
Describe a situation where you worked with stakeholders having differing priorities, perspectives, or interests during an audit. Explain how you built consensus, resolved disagreements, maintained productive relationships, and achieved the desired outcome. Please identify where this experience is reflected in your resume.
