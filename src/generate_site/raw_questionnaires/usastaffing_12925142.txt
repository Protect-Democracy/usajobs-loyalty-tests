Competitive Service: Positions with agencies that follow the U.S. Office of Personnel Management's hiring rules and pay scales. Can apply to merit promotion jobs.
Excepted Service: Positions with agencies that have their own hiring rules, pay scales and evaluation criteria. Cannot apply to merit promotion jobs unless your agency has an interchange agreement with our agency. You can apply to jobs that are open to the public or federal employees - excepted service.  
Senior Executive Service: Managerial, supervisory and policy positions classified above General Schedule (GS) grade 15 or equivalent positions in most Executive Branch agencies. Cannot apply to merit promotion jobs. You can apply to jobs that open to the public or Senior Executive Service.

This category can cover civilian employees serving on a permanent appointment, civilian employees serving on an Excepted Service Veterans Recruitment Appointment (VRA), civilian employees serving on an excepted appointment covered under an interchange agreement, civilian employees serving on a Temporary or TERM appointment with previous permanent competitive service.
