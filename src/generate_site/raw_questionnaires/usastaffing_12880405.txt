Assessment 1
1.
From the description below, select the letter that best describes your level of specialized experience.


 I have one year of specialized experience at the next lower grade (NH-02/GS-11) or equivalent in the federal service (experience may have been gained in the private sector) in 2 of 4 of the following specialized experiences. Specialized Experience is defined as: Experience managing oversight of special access program (SAP) personnel security activities, SAP information, industrial, personnel security principles and access management for an Agency or large DoD office. With advanced knowledge of all SAP policies and directives. Experience managing oversight of all access management requirements within the Joint Access Database Environment (JADE) and working in other personnel security databases (e.g. CVS, DISS, Scattered Castles). Experience in the processes and procedures of SAP security compliance inspections within the DoD SAP enterprise and the Defense Industrial Base (DIB). Experience in the development of security education, training and awareness briefings for diverse audiences, using a variety of methods (e.g. In-person, CLMS, CDSE).
 My experience does not meet the requirements listed above.
