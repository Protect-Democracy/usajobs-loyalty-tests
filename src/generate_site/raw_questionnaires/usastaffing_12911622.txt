 I have the ability to perform the most difficult and complex Boiler Plant operations, maintenance and repairs. I am considered an expert and work with a high degree of independence.
 I have the ability to perform the full range of Boiler Plant operations, maintenance and repairs. I work on my own initiative under general supervision subject to occasional inspection.
 I have the ability to perform some tasks including operating and monitoring of high pressure Boiler Plants and auxiliary equipment. I can recognize and detect abnormal high pressure boiler plant and steam system operating conditions and take immediate actions to eliminate emergency situations and restore normal operating conditions; collect and test water samples and determine the amount and type of chemicals needed to control or meet various boiler chemistry parameters and achieve desired results.
