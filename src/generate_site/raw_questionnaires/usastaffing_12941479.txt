1.
From the descriptions below, select the appropriate response based on your level of experience, education, and/or training, that you fully possess and that demonstrates your ability to perform High Voltage Electrician duties. 

 I have the ability to perform the most difficult and complex High Voltage Electrician (Motor Vehicle Operator) activities including working with voltages up to 115,000 volts; planning, laying out, and installing substation equipment and controls; adjusting and repairing remotely controlled high voltage switchgear, regulators and reclosures; installing, repairing, and maintaining all commonly used electric power generation and distribution equipment without the need for special advice; determining types and sizes of wire, conduit, transformers, and safe operation of distribution systems; reading and understanding circuit diagrams of interconnections in order to diagnose problems in the electrical distribution system; diagnosing problems and determining corrective actions for electric power equipment and distribution feeders. I am considered an expert and work with a high degree of independence.
 I have the ability to perform the full range of High Voltage Electrician (Motor Vehicle Operator) tasks including testing, repairing, and calibrating defective indicating and recording instruments such as watt-hour meters, power-factors meters and synchroscopes; working with voltage sup to 115,000 volts; repairing most electrical equipment used for power generation or distribution; using knowledge of elementary electronics theory and measuring equipment in order to test repair and calibrate control devices, voltage regulators, reclosures, simple amplifier circuits; reading and understanding electrical blueprints, schematics and diagrams; working efficiently and safely on high voltage equipment under extremely hazardous conditions. I work on my own initiative under general supervision subject to occasional inspection.
 I have the ability to perform common High Voltage Electrician (Motor Vehicle Operator) tasks including splicing power cable, replacing a pot head, or pulling a conductor into a conduit or onto a pole cross arms; using a basic knowledge of electrical theory such as Ohm’s law and series and parallel circuits in order to understand and interpret instructions and assignments; understanding commonly used drawings and diagrams of distribution networks and equipment interconnections; working safely in hazardous situations under close guidance of an onsite supervisor; diagnosing commonly encountered problems such as defective switches or blown fuses. I work under supervision of a journey-level employee who reviews my work for completion.
 I have the ability to perform simple High Voltage Electrician (Motor Vehicle Operator) tasks such as tasks such as assisting a journeyman high voltage electrician and am familiar with basic electrical installation practices; using limited knowledge in applications of electrical theory such as name and purpose of various items of electrical equipment; passing tools or giving assistance when required in a work situation free from special hazards., or I have received training in this area.
 I do not possess the abilities described above.
