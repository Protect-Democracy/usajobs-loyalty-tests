Ability to do the work of a Gardener, WG-5003-08 without more than normal supervision.


1.

 I have the ability to perform the full range of tasks to provide proper care and maintenance for lawns, flowers, shrubs and trees. Such duties include pruning and trimming trees and shrubs to obtain the shape, size and desired growth characteristics, and to remove diseased limbs or excess branches, transplant trees, shrubs, flowers, and other plants by digging proper sized holes and preparing the soil to receive the plants, computing quantities of plants, seeds, and other materials needed for specific areas, selecting chemicals for the control of plant disease, plant insects and weeds, and fertilizing watering, and seeding turf areas, removing rocks, leaves, trash, and debris from landscaped areas.
