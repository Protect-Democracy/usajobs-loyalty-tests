1.
SPECIALIZED EXPERIENCE:
To qualify for the GS-15 position, you must possess the specialized experience equivalent to the GS-14 grade level in the Federal government, or equivalent in the private section experience. For this position, specialized experience is defined as:
Mastery of administrative and program management principles sufficient to direct and integrate facilities, logistics, security, continuity, emergency preparedness, and exercise programs. 
Expert knowledge of organizational planning, resource management, policy implementation, and performance management sufficient to establish priorities, allocate resources, and evaluate program effectiveness.
Knowledge of DHS, Intelligence Community, and Federal policies, regulations, and directives governing mission assurance activities and related support operations. 
Knowledge of management methods and techniques sufficient to assess organizational requirements, develop operational improvements, and resolve complex administrative issues affecting mission readiness. 
Skill in advising senior leadership on mission assurance requirements, organizational risks, operational priorities, and resource considerations.
Ability to coordinate and integrate the work of subordinate supervisors, branch chief, and professional staff responsible for diverse mission support functions.
Ability to develop strategic objectives, establish performance expectations, and ensure effective execution of programs supporting headquarters and field operations.
