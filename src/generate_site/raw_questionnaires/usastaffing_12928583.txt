1.
From the descriptions below, select the response that best describes your level of experience and ability to perform the duties of an Electronic Industrial Controls Mechanic (WG-2606-11).
 I have the ability to perform the most difficult and complex functions of an Electronic Industrial Controls Mechanic. I independently install, modify, troubleshoot, and repair complex electronic industrial control systems and electrical distribution systems, including emergency generators, UPS systems, transfer switches, transformers, and integrated facility equipment. I analyze system interactions, interpret electrical schematics and control diagrams, and isolate malfunctions to the component level under operating conditions. I program or adjust automated control systems (e.g., PLC/DDC), develop non-standard troubleshooting methods, and make independent decisions on system safety, removal from service, and corrective actions. I work with a high degree of independence and am considered an expert.
 I have the ability to perform the full range of duties of an Electronic Industrial Controls Mechanic. I install, maintain, troubleshoot, and repair electronic control systems and electrical distribution equipment, including generators, transformers, and control systems. I use test equipment to diagnose problems, interpret schematics, and make repairs to restore system operation. I perform preventive maintenance, assist with control system adjustments, and complete work with minimal supervision, subject to occasional inspection.
 I have the ability to perform common duties of an Electronic Industrial Controls Mechanic. I assist in installing and maintaining electrical and electronic systems, use tools and test equipment to perform routine troubleshooting and repairs, and follow established procedures. I work under normal supervision of a higher-level mechanic who reviews my work.
 I have the ability to perform simple tasks related to electronic and electrical systems, such as assisting with installations, using basic tools, and performing routine maintenance. I require close supervision and guidance to complete assignments.
 I do not possess the experience or ability described above.
