Specialized experience includes:

Assist in evaluating training programs by collecting feedback and reviewing results to improve course effectiveness and participant experience.
Review external training requests (like the SF-182) to ensure forms are complete, accurate, and compliant with policy requirements.
Coordinate training activities by scheduling sessions, maintaining course information in the learning management system, and supporting training logistics to ensure smooth delivery.
