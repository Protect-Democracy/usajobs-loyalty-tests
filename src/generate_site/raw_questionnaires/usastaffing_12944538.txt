QUALIFICATION REQUIREMENTS:

GS-14 SPECIALIZED EXPERIENCE: You must have one year of specialized experience at a level of difficulty and responsibility equivalent to the GS-13 grade level in the Federal Service. Specialized experience must include:
Applying a wide range of analytical and evaluative methods and techniques, both qualitative and quantitative, relating to call center and campus operations; providing Contact Recording and technical support.
Applying management and organizational principles to programs, projects and studies concerning the efficiency and effectiveness of operations improvement and project management to evaluate complex projects and studies and identify problem areas, trends, deficiencies and other similar factors affecting operations.
Utilizing communication techniques to present findings, deliver briefings, and negotiate solutions to disputed recommendations.
Identifying, troubleshooting, and elevating Information Technology issues and using Information Technology systems integration, automation, and application lifecycle management.
Utilizing Microsoft Power Platform tools, specifically PowerApps and Power Automate to modernize processes and improve operational efficiency. Using data analytics tools and environments (including Microsoft SQL Server) to extract, analyze, and interpret complex data sets to support program objectives.
Identifying and resolving or elevating identity theft issues, trends, and mitigation strategies within a tax administration or financial environment.
