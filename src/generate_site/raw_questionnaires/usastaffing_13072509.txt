 I have one year of specialized experience equivalent to at least GS-07. Specialized experience is defined as experience that is in or directly related to the line of work of this position and has equipped you with the knowledge and competencies to successfully perform the duties of this position. More specifically, this requirement involves experience in: conducting quality reviews of contract closeout packages to confirm required documents are present and consistent with policy, procedures, and closeout readiness checklists; verifying data integrity across acquisition/financial systems by cross-checking key fields and flagging discrepancies for resolution; assisting with statistical sampling or other qualitative/quantitative techniques to assess completeness of closeout records; preparing worksheets or summaries of findings for senior staff review; drafting deficiency notices or suggested corrective actions for issues and routing to Contract Specialists/Contracting Officers for action; supporting updates to internal guidance (SOPs, job aids, checklists, forms) by documenting observed process gaps, suggesting clarifications, or participating in user testing of program tools; compiling status reports to track closeout lifecycle milestones, throughput, and backlog; presenting summarized data to the team or supervisor; participating in special projects (under direction) to identify procedural bottlenecks, gather data, and contribute to process improvement recommendations focused on compliance and efficiency; and providing routine liaison support by coordinating information requests, clarifying checklist expectations, and elevating non-standard issues to leadership.
