Assessment 1
Please select the response below that best describes the specialized experience you possess that demonstrates your ability to perform the work of a GS-14 Supervisory Management and Program Analyst.  Specialized experience is described as: 

1. Providing senior-level leadership and oversight of multiple administrative programs, evaluating performance data, analyzing complex information, and reconciling discrepancies.  
2. Serving as key advisor to senior leadership on administrative policies, organizational effectiveness, workforce planning, and strategic resource management.
3. Developing and presenting executive briefings, reports, and recommendations that influence organizational policy and programs.
4. Supervising a cross-functional team directing, planning, organizing, and providing day-to-day operational directions to subordinate staff.
5. Exercising supervisory authority over professional, administrative, and support staff, including workforce planning, recruitment, performance management, employee development, and fostering an inclusive, high-performing work environment.
