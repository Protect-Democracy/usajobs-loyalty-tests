 I have the ability to perform the most difficult and complex Wastewater Treatment Plant Operator duties. I am skilled in operating and maintaining the facilities for pumping and treating waterborne sewage, operating the sewer treatment plants; operating, cleaning, and maintaining pumping and treatment facilities in an Industrial Wastewater and Domestic Wastewater Treatment Plant; performing scheduled preventive maintenance on plant equipment and operating industrial and domestic wastewater collection pumping stations; performing a wide range of analytical analysis, i.e., pH, alkalinity, solids, ammonia, nitrate, dissolved oxygen (DO), etc. I am considered an expert and work with a high degree of independence.
 I have the ability to perform the full range of Wastewater Treatment Plant Operator duties. I am skilled in ensuring instructions on work sequence, procedures, methods, and deadlines and work assignments are carried out by other members of the group; work along with other employees engaged in industrial wastewater treatment and domestic sewage treatment plant operations/repairs; ensuring that needed plans, blueprints, materials, and tools are available, and that needed stock is obtained from supply locations. I work on my own initiative under general supervision subject to occasional inspection.
 I have the ability to perform common Wastewater Treatment Plant Operator duties. I can perform scheduled preventive maintenance on plant equipment and operated industrial and domestic wastewater collection pumping stations. I work under normal supervision of a work leader or journey-level employee who observes tasks in progress and upon completion to make sure they are properly performed.
