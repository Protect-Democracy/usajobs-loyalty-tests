1.
Training Instructor, GS-1712 Individual Occupational Requirement (IOR)

To qualify for this position, you must meet one of the following requirements:

Education: Successfully completed a major field of study at the undergraduate or graduate level in education or a subject area related to the position being filled.  Examples of qualifying fields of study include Finance, Economics, Human Resources Management, Business Administration, Actuarial Science, Education, Instructional Design, Communications, or other closely related fields.

OR

Specialized Experience: Experience demonstrating practical knowledge of the subject matter and methods and techniques of instruction, such as:
Teaching or instructing adults in an educational, military, or industrial setting;
Supervising or administering a training program;
Developing, reviewing, or evaluating training materials, courses, or training results; or
Performing work in the subject-matter field while regularly training or instructing others.

Your resume must clearly demonstrate that you meet the specialized experience requirements described in this announcement. Human Resources will only credit specialized experience that is supported by your resume.


Part-time experience will be credited on a prorated basis (e.g., 20 hours per week for two years equals one year of full-time experience).



2.
GS-09 Qualification Requirements

To qualify for the GS-09 grade level, you must meet one of the following requirements:

Specialized Experience: One year of specialized experience equivalent to the GS-07 level that demonstrates knowledge of instructional methods, training principles, and adult learning concepts. Examples of qualifying specialized experience include:
Delivering instructor-led training, presentations, briefings, or other instructional sessions to groups of participants to communicate information and improve understanding of policies, procedures, programs, products, or services;
Adapting or modifying existing training materials, presentations, visual aids, job aids, or instructional content to meet audience needs and support learning objectives;
Evaluating participant feedback, survey results, assessments, or other training data to identify training needs and recommend improvements to training materials or instructional delivery; and
Using virtual training platforms, presentation technologies, or classroom tools and maintaining training records, attendance data, or other documentation to support training operations and evaluate results.

OR

Education: A master's or equivalent graduate degree, two full years of progressively higher-level graduate education leading to such a degree, or an LL.B. or J.D., if related to the position.

OR

Combination of Education and Experience: A combination of specialized experience and graduate-level education that together qualify you for the GS-09 grade level.

Which of the following statements best describes your qualifications?
 Yes, I have at least one year of specialized experience equivalent to the GS-07 level that demonstrates all of the specialized experience requirements described above.
 Yes, I possess a master's or equivalent graduate degree, two full years of progressively higher-level graduate education leading to such a degree, or an LL.B. or J.D. that is related to this position.
 Yes, I possess a combination of specialized experience and graduate-level education that meets the GS-09 qualification requirements described above.
 No, I do not possess the specialized experience, education, or combination of specialized experience and education required to qualify for this position at the GS-09 level.
