QUALIIFICATION REQUIREMENTS:

GS-14 SPECIALIZED EXPERIENCE: You must have one year of specialized experience at a level of difficulty and responsibility equivalent to the GS-13 grade level in the Federal service. Specialized experience for this position must include:
Conducting meetings and briefings, preparing reports, promoting work efforts, products and initiatives, and taking other actions needed to inform, communicate, and market programs to executives, leadership, and other stakeholders;
Coordinating and maintaining collaborative information management sites or platforms to support program operations and information sharing.
Gathering, consolidating and ensuring accuracy of data and information for senior level operations reviews
Experience with reviewing and verifying data for performance measures and data reporting
Working cross-functionally with stakeholders to deliver timely and accurate products and deliverables.
Developing written program guidance such as policies, procedures, Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), guidelines or other operational documentation.
