Assessment 1
1.
Basic Requirements for Information Technology Management Series (2210)
To meet the basic qualifications for this position, applicants must demonstrate at least one year of specialized experience equivalent to the GS-13 grade level in the federal service reflecting a wide range of knowledge of IT requirements and techniques.  Experience must demonstrate proficiency in all nine of the following competencies:
 
Attention to Detail -- performing work thoroughly and independently in complex IT environments
Customer Service -- assessing needs and resolving complex IT service problems with little or no guidance.
Decision Making -- making sound, well-informed decisions in uncertain or complex situations
Information Management -- identifying information needs and organizing or maintaining information management systems
Interpersonal Skills -- developing effective working relationships across varied and difficult situations
Oral Communication -- delivering clear presentations on technical or sensitive topics to diverse audiences
Problem Solving -- identifying complex problems, evaluating alternatives, and generating well-supported documentations
Teamwork -- fostering cooperation and commitment across teams in difficult situations
Technical Competence -- applying advanced IT knowledge to perform complex duties and advise others

Which of the following best describes how you meet the basic qualification requirement for this position?  Your response must be supported by your resume.
 I have at least one year of specialized experience equivalent to the GS-13 grade level that demonstrates all nine competencies described above at an Advanced (Level 4) proficiency level, meaning I apply each competency in considerably difficult situations and generally require little or no guidance.
 I have at least one year of specialized experience equivalent to the GS-13 grade level that demonstrates the majority of the nine competencies described above at an Advanced (Level 4) proficiency level, with demonstrated strengths in other competency areas that offset any deficit, as supported by my resume.
 I have IT-related experience or intensive IT training that demonstrates specialized experience equivalent to the GS-13 grade level, but my experience does not yet fully demonstrate all nine competencies described above at the Advanced (Level 4) proficiency level.

2.

Specialized Experience Requirement - GS-14

To be considered qualified for this position, you must have at least one year of full-time work experience performing duties similar in complexity and responsibility to work typically performed at the GS-13 level in the federal government. Your experience must demonstrate all four of the following areas. Experience in only some of these areas is not sufficient to meet the minimum qualification requirement for this position. Federal experience is not required - equivalent private sector, contractor, or military experience will be evaluated on the same basis.
 

Your experience must include all of the following:

Approving the security design and implementation of enterprise technology solutions to ensure systems meet security requirements and support organizational objectives.
Developing security architectures for enterprise systems and networks to support secure operations across complex technology environments.
Evaluating cybersecurity risks, conducting security reviews, identifying gaps in security controls, and recommending corrective actions to improve organizational security posture.
Overseeing cybersecurity projects, contractor performance, or technology service delivery efforts to ensure successful implementation of security requirements and achievement of organizational goals.


Important: Your resume must clearly describe the specific duties you performed, the scope of your responsibilities, and the timeframe of your experience in each of the four areas above. Responses that cannot be verified by your resume may result in a lower rating or ineligibility determination.
