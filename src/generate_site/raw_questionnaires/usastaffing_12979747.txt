I have performed these duties without technical supervision or instruction and sought the advice of a higher-level manager when complex issues arise.
 I am able to perform common to motor vehicle operation duties, including operating vehicles to haul cargo and transport passengers; determining the best route to take depending upon the size and weight of the vehicle; reading and interpreting road maps and or navigation; and operating vehicles through a variety of road, traffic and weather conditions.
