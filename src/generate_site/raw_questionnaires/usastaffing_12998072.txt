1.
Select the one statement which best describes the highest level of experience you possess and which demonstrates your ability to perform the duties of a Blocker and Bracer (Wood Worker Packer), WG-4602-08, with the Ability to Do the Work without More Than Normal Supervision. Note: Your resume must support the response you select.
 I am able to perform Blocker and Bracer (Wood Worker Packer) tasks including operating perform work in the shipping/receiving warehouse or at the site of the items shipped, plan packing, crating, and loading large complex items. Properly label containers with shipping information document and applies warnings, lead seals. Stacking, moving, and arranging items on crates considering height, weight, and special handling requirements. Using automated equipment, such as optical readers, scanners, and computer keyboards to enter and extract data. Completing required forms and documents indicating stock description, quantity, unit of issue, and labeling requirements for incoming and outgoing stock and the ability to screen documents and find missing data. Loading and arranging cargo properly onto vehicles, using appropriate tie-downs, such as chains, binders, or straps for safe transit on military and commercial aircraft and public roads at highway speeds or under similar driving circumstances. Conducting check for proper blocking and bracing, cargo arrangement and possible overloading. Ensuring the proper types of gates, banding and bracing are used in view of the condition, type, size and weight of cargo.
 I am able to perform a full range of Blocker and Bracer (Wood Worker Packer) tasks including operating one or more fork lifts, loaders, and tractor trailers; packing the freight using special preservation packaging materials and techniques, safely loading and unloading the freight. Judging size of doorways and aisles, size of load (i.e., height, length, width, and weight), capability of fork lift truck, and proper load balance in order to safely load and unload items. Properly labels containers with shipping information. Packages items and components in reusable metal, wood, aluminum, plastic and fiberboard shipping containers and ensures material properly placed, items completely stabilized in containers. Using appropriate tie-downs, such as chains, binders, or straps for safe transit on military and commercial aircraft and public roads at highway speeds or under similar driving circumstances on my own initiative under limited supervision subject to occasional inspection.
 I am able to perform common Blocker and Bracer (Wood Worker Packer) tasks includes determines need for load permits and assures all correct paperwork accompanies shipment. Oversees or assists with loading operations in the carrier, inspects motor vehicles to be used to transport hazardous or explosive materials over public highways for compliance with established safety regulations. Oversees or assists with offloading operations from carriers at proper site. Assures receiving documents are correct, visually inspects property for proper item, quantity, and for damages. Makes written report of any discrepancies, photographs damaged goods and notifies appropriate parties. I work under the close supervision of a journeyman Blocker and Bracer who observes tasks in progress and upon completion to make sure they are properly performed.
 I am able to perform simple Blocker and Bracer (Wood Worker Packer) tasks including performing a wide range of material handling and heavy capacity crane/fork lift operating duties. Receiving, inspecting, storing, selecting, shipping, pickup, and delivery of general, oversized, or specialized bulk materials and equipment.
 I have no experience or training as a Blocker and Bracer (Wood Worker Packer).
