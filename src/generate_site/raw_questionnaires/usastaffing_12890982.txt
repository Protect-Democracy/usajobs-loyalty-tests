Senior leadership is pressuring for immediate action, but you must ensure compliance with acquisition regulations while meeting the mission-critical timeline.

 Break the requirement into multiple smaller competitive contracts under simplified acquisition thresholds to expedite the process while maintaining some level of competition.
 Conduct a competitive procurement with justified expedited procedures, documenting the urgency while ensuring adequate competition and compliance with source selection requirements for this contract value.
 Award a sole source contract immediately based on senior leadership direction, then prepare the required justification documentation after contract award to avoid further delays.
 Issue a sole source contract with detailed justification citing urgent and compelling circumstances, while simultaneously initiating a competitive follow-on procurement for future similar requirements.
 Proceed with the fastest procurement method available regardless of value thresholds, focusing solely on meeting the 30-day deadline without considering regulatory compliance requirements.

 Instruct the specialist to continue with contract performance as planned while preparing a basic response to the protest, since most protests are unsuccessful and cause unnecessary delays.
 Have the specialist compile all relevant procurement documents for legal review while you assess whether the protest has merit and determine if performance suspension is necessary.

 Halt the evaluation, conduct training with the inconsistent evaluator on proper criteria application, and establish a process to re-evaluate affected proposals while excluding unrequested information from consideration.
 Replace the inconsistent evaluator with a qualified substitute, restart the technical evaluation for affected proposals, and seek legal guidance on handling the unrequested information.
 Document the evaluation inconsistencies, provide corrective guidance to the evaluator, and continue the evaluation while ensuring all unrequested information is disregarded in the final scoring.
 Accept the current evaluation results as substantially complete and focus on expediting the source selection decision to avoid further delays in the procurement timeline.

They argue this addition is essential for mission success and want to proceed immediately through a bilateral modification. The original contract was competitively awarded six months ago, and the new requirement wasn't anticipated in the original acquisition planning. They're frustrated with procurement "red tape" and need your guidance.

 Review the original contract scope to determine if the new requirement could be considered within scope, explain the regulatory limits on contract modifications, and provide options for both in-scope and out-of-scope scenarios.
 Explain that the significant scope increase likely requires new competition, discuss the rationale behind competition requirements, and help develop an acquisition plan for the additional requirement while exploring interim solutions.
 Clarify the difference between in-scope and out-of-scope modifications, explain the competition requirements for new work, and work with the program manager to develop a compliant acquisition strategy.
 Process the modification as requested since the existing contractor is already familiar with the work and can provide the fastest solution, documenting the operational necessity for the scope increase.

The project is essential for flood protection, and delays could impact the upcoming storm season.

 Reject the contractor's request since adequate site investigation was conducted and the risk of unforeseen conditions should have been included in their original pricing and schedule.
 Review relevant FAR clauses regarding changed conditions and contractor entitlements, examine the adequacy of original site investigations, and consult with technical experts to validate the contractor's claims before making a determination.
 Research FAR provisions on differing site conditions and equitable adjustments, analyze the original contract terms and site investigation requirements, then develop a comprehensive position based on regulatory guidance and contract interpretation.
 Investigate the regulatory requirements for equitable adjustments and differing site conditions, coordinate with legal counsel and technical staff to assess the validity of the contractor's request and determine appropriate government response.
 Focus on the mission-critical nature of the project and approve the contractor's request to avoid delays, while documenting the decision based on operational necessity and the unforeseen nature of the site conditions.
