Do you have one (1) year of specialized experience equivalent to the next lower grade level (GS-13) that is directly related to the line of work of this position. Specialized experience is experience that equipped the applicant with the knowledge, skills, and abilities to perform successfully the duties of the position, and that is typically in or related to the work of the position to be filled. To be creditable, specialized experience must have been equivalent to at least the next lower grade level in the normal line of progression for the occupation in the organization. 

Specialized experience for this position must include:
Advising senior and executive level management on issues involving budget, resource allocation, and procurement.
Demonstrating expertise with writing and presentation skills in preparing annual and other reporting submissions, responding to queries, and making presentations for senior managers and executives, related to contracting, procurement, budget, financial, security, human resource management, and equivalent administrative issues.
