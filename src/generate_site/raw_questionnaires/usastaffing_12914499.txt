1.
Do you have the ability to perform the duties of an Electrician WG-10, without more than normal supervision?


Related experience includes: Possessing knowledge of the make-up, operation and installation of a variety of electrical and electronic systems, circuits, equipment and controls with the ability to read blueprints, wiring diagrams and other such drawings, as well as perform work from verbal instructions using formulas specific to the trade to figure such things as current, voltage and resistance.

Ability to work with all trades and has full skills required to bend conduit with power or manual type benders. Utilization of skill in the use of hand strippers, pliers, drills, soldering irons, screwdrivers, cable and wire cutters, test meters, voltmeters, amp meters, watt meters, ohm meters and meggers to install and maintain complete Industrial/Medical electrical systems and associated equipment. Utilization of knowledge in various gauges, sizes and types of wire, conduit, fittings, junction boxes, relays, contactors, circuit breakers, transformers, lighting ballasts and other electrical and electronic devices to arrange and install circuit breakers and other electrical devices in ways that ensure proper and safe operation of systems, circuits and equipment in accordance with the NEC/OSHA, VA standards and manufacturers requirements. Utilization of knowledge of the construction, installation, operation and troubleshooting of sophisticated circuitry and controls associated with unique projects to lay out electrical circuits, perform electrical load management test to ensure that specified electrical test conditions are attained and troubleshoot the electrical portions of the test equipment during test programs to determine cause of malfunctions.
