Assessment 1
Next, complete the test below. This test assesses your ability to perform the duties associated with this position. Completing this test is a mandatory step in the application process.


Structure of the Test:
You will read 10 scenarios.
Each scenario has two questions.
Each question has four responses.
You will rate the effectiveness of each response to show your judgment and decision-making skills. Rate each response based only on the information given in the scenario. Consider the scenario and questions as if you were the supervisor or leader of the team. Evaluate each response option individually and not in comparison to one another, or as a series of sequential steps.


Evaluate each response using the following scale:
Very Effective: Produces positive outcomes and fully achieves objectives.
Effective: Produces positive outcomes with significant improvements and mostly achieves objectives.
Somewhat Effective: Produces moderate outcomes with some improvements, but not consistently.
Ineffective: Produces limited outcomes with no improvements.
Very Ineffective: Produces poor outcomes with negative impact.
