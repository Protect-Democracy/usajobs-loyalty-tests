2.
Describe your specialized experience at the GS-11 level or equivalent, where you had primary responsibility for managing a wing-level or equivalent deployment program. In your narrative, please provide specific examples that address the following four key performance areas as detailed in the position description:

- Program Management and Policy Implementation: Describe your experience managing, directing, and implementing installation deployment guidance based on higher headquarters directives (e.g., USAF, NGB, MAJCOM). How did you establish local deployment policies, analyze time-phased force deployment requirements, and control the movement of personnel and equipment for exercises or real-world contingency operations?

- Logistics Systems Integration: Detail your experience managing and directing the implementation of automated deployment systems. Specifically, explain your role in using the Integrated Deployment System (IDS) and its components (e.g., LOGMOD, MANPER, CALM, CMOS) to manage deployment data, establish system responsibilities, and ensure accurate reporting for all assigned and geographically separated units.

- Readiness Reporting and Resource Analysis: Provide an example of how you analyzed and validated logistical inputs for readiness reporting systems like the Defense Readiness Reporting System (DRRS). How did you monitor unit logistical readiness, identify support deficiencies, and recommend corrective actions to mitigate their effects on the total logistical posture?

- Supervision and Training Oversight: Explain your experience providing limited supervision or quasi-supervisory oversight. How did you appraise worker performance, identify developmental and training needs, and ensure subordinates were trained to execute deployment functions effectively?
