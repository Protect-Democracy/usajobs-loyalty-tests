Specialized Experience Requirement - In addition to the IOR, candidates must have 52 weeks of specialized experience equivalent to a the least the next lower grade level in the Federal service. Specialized experience is experience that equipped the applicant with the particular knowledge, skills, and abilities (KSA's) to successfully perform the duties of the position, and that are typically in or related to the position to be filled.


2.
Specialized Experience - Health Technician (GS-6). You must have one year of specialized experience equivalent to at least the next lower grade GS-5 in the normal line of progression for the occupation in the organization. Specialized experience includes, but not limited to:
Knowledge of and ability to communicate and educate on a very wide variety of medical problems as well as preventive health measures in order to effectively convey discharge instructions to patients and families and to be able to answer questions as needed.
Knowledge of the Business Office in the following areas: Outpatient processing, eligibility, DHCP applications, clinical, and ancillary services.
Knowledge of medical terminology and usage covering the full range of general medical, surgical, pharmaceutical, hospital-specific terms, medical abstracts, and abbreviations.
Knowledge of anatomy and physiology to properly understand the major anatomical systems and related disease processes. Knowledge of and ability to use the VA medical record systems CPRS and VISTA.
Ability to analyze and correlate laboratory test results, procedures and treatments with diagnoses.
Basic knowledge of coding classification systems and appropriate references. Knowledge of computer systems; i.e. VISTA, CPRS, word processing, spreadsheets, etc.

OR,

Education - Graduate education or an internship meets the specialized experience required above GS-5 only in those instances where it is directly related to the work of the position. One year of full-time graduate education is considered to be the number of credit hours that the school attended has determined to represent one year of full-time study. If that information cannot be obtained from the school, 18 semester hours should be considered as satisfying the one year of full-time study requirement. Part-time graduate education is creditable in accordance with its relationship to a year of full-time study at the school attended. (Copy of Transcript Required)

OR,

Combination of Education and Experience - Applicants may also combine education and experience to qualify at this level. You must have an equal combination of specialized experience and graduate education.

PLEASE NOTE: A full year of work is considered to be 35-40 hours of work per week. Part-time experience will be credited on the basis of time actually spent in appropriate activities. Applicants wishing to receive credit for such experience must indicate clearly the nature of their duties and responsibilities in each position and the number of hours a week spent in such employment.
