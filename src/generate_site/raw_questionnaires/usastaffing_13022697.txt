MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS for this Supervisory Security Specialist position are based on Specialized Experience only. See the QUALIFICATIONS section of the announcement for more information.


If your qualifying experience was gained in a prior position, also submit an SF-50 that verifies your grade at the time your experience was gained. All SF-50s you submit should coincide with the information provided on your resume.


SPECIALIZED EXPERIENCE must have been gained in a federal position at or above GS-14 OR (if your position is on another federal pay scale OR outside of federal service) by performing duties at a level of responsibility and difficulty equivalent to GS-14. To qualify for this GS-15 Supervisory Security Specialist position, you must demonstrate at least 52 weeks of Specialized Experience.


SPECIALIZED EXPERIENCE is work that primarily consists of developing, implementing or managing an organization/agency-wide insider threat program, plans, and policies. Experience must also include leading or managing a team.
