customers, despite passing all in-house final inspections. As a Quality Assurance Specialist, which
of the following is the MOST effective approach to address this recurring issue preventatively? (Select all that apply)
 A. Increase the frequency of final product inspections to catch more defects before shipment.
 B. Implement a new statistical process control system to monitor critical manufacturing parameters in real-time and identify deviations before they lead to defects.
 C. Revise the product warranty policy to cover intermittent failures, ensuring customer satisfaction.
 D. Conduct a comprehensive review of supplier quality certificates for raw materials used in the affected components.
