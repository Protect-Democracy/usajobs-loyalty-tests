 Supervise the purchasing process by reviewing all requisitions from the HVAC and plumbing shops for the next quarter to control spending, identify the electrical shop's training needs to maximize efficiency with current staff levels, and reconcile each trade shop's cost center purchases on a monthly basis to track spending patterns.
 Monitor the HVAC shop's remaining budget closely and independently prepare revised budget estimates for the plumbing shop's equipment needs, purchase the necessary materials and supplies for the electrical shop to maximize productivity, and ensure the carpentry shop's equipment is serviced and maintained according to requirements.
