Assessment 1
QUALIFICATION REQUIREMENTS:
SPECIALIZED EXPERIENCE FOR GS-12:
You must have one (1) year of specialized experience at a level of difficulty and responsibility equivalent to the GS-11 grade level in the Federal service. Specialized experience for this position must include:
Applying Equal Employment Opportunity (EEO) laws, regulations, and procedures to develop and execute assignments and program activities.
Providing technical guidance to management and employees to support compliance within assigned programs (e.g., Alternative Dispute Resolution (ADR), Civil Rights, Anti-Harassment).
Applying oral and written communication to resolve sensitive EEO matters as requested by management, junior specialists, and support staff.
Analyzing EEO data to identify trends or issues and provide recommendations for corrective action.
Collaborating with the First-level Manager or Area Director in developing deadlines and approaches to novel and sensitive problems within EEO programs.
Reviewing multi-factorial assignments, including updated organizational practices, conflicting evidence provided by complainant and varying EEO law interpretation, to provide actionable recommendations.
