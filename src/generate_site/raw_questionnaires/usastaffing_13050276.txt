SPECIALIZED EXPERIENCE:
GS-13 Level: You must have one year of specialized experience at a level of difficulty and responsibility equivalent to the GS-12 grade level in the Federal service. Specialized experience includes:
Experience in Integrated Data Retrieval System (IDRS), Accounts Management System (AMS), Correspondence Imaging Inventory (CII), Business Masterfile Database (BMFIC), Enterprise File Services (EFS) or Electronic Fraud Detection System (EFDS) to analyze taxpayer data. Adhering to disclosure, adjustment and statute procedures. Recognizing tax issues that impact assigned programs to make recommendations.  Working refund fraud and identity theft.
Utilizing IDRS and program database report functions to collect data from completed reviews and forecast inventory performance metrics based on historical data.
