A Schedule C appointee occupies a position excepted from the competitive service by the President, or by the Director of OPM, because of the confidential or policy-determining nature of the position duties (5 CFR 213.3301 and 5 CFR 213.3302).
A Non-career SES appointee is approved by the White House and serves at the pleasure of the appointing official without time limitations (5 CFR 317 Subpart F).

From the descriptions below, please choose the response that best indicates how you meet the minimum qualification for the Language Specialist, GS-1040-12.
 I have at least one full year of specialized experience equivalent to the GS-11 grade level in the Federal service (obtained either in the private or public sectors). I understand that specialized experience means I have performed professional work that demonstrates expert proficiency in translating and interpreting between Spanish and English, both orally and in writing, across a full range of difficulty levels, subject matters, and linguistic variations. To meet the minimum qualifications, I must be able to show that I have performed at least five (5) of the following six (6) tasks: (1) interpreting at legal conferences involving attorneys, defendants, and/or witnesses where agreements are negotiated; (2) preparing Spanish-speaking witnesses for trial; (3) producing certified transcriptions/translations and translations of court documents, law enforcement reports, and related materials (4) testifying in federal court to defend certified transcriptions or translations presented as evidence; (5) providing sight translations for attorneys; and/or (6) delivering Spanish to English and English to Spanish interpretations required in criminal and civil proceedings.
