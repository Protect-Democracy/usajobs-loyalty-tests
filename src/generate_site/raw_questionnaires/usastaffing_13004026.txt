 I have the ability to perform the most difficult and complex Maintenance Worker Supervisor duties and provide first level supervision and technical advice and be responsible for daily operational maintenance, rehabilitation, and preventive maintenance in campgrounds and picnic areas, I have also supervised daily operations of custodial duties in visitor use buildings and administrative buildings. I plan and program schedules, assign work, track progress of and completion of various projects and maintenance duties, and the overall quality of completed projects, and maintenance duties of subordinate employees through performance appraisal process.
