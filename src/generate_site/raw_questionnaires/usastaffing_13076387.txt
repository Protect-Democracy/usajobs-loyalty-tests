QUALIFICATION REQUIREMENTS: SPECIALIZED EXPERIENCE : To be minimally qualified for this position you must have at least one year of specialized experience equivalent to the next lower-level payband or GS grade in the normal line of progression. Specialized experience would include one year of substantive supervisory or program management experience at the next lower-level or equivalent level manager or comparable management or leadership experiences outside of Federal service. Specialized experience for this position must include: Leading business operating division-level programs, projects, or initiatives involving business process improvement, organizational transformation, strategic planning, or business process reengineering. Identifying business needs and translating them into functional requirements for technology-enabled solutions, including case management systems, workflow automation, or other business applications. Leading and supporting the implementation of business process improvements through the effective use of technology, automation, artificial intelligence (AI), or other emerging capabilities to improve efficiency, service delivery, or organizational performance. Evaluating existing business processes, conducting business analysis, developing improvement strategies, and oversee implementation to achieve measurable business outcomes. Collaborating with executives, program managers, information technology organizations, and cross-functional stakeholders to prioritize technology investments, manage competing business needs, and deliver integrated business solutions. Ensuring compliance with applicable information technology policies, cybersecurity requirements, and the Federal Information Security Modernization Act (FISMA).
