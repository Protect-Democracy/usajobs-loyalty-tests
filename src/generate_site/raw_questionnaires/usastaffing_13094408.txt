 I have the ability to perform the most difficult and complex overhaul, mechanical rebuild, and diagnostic testing tasks on gas- and oil-fired high- and low-pressure boiler systems and auxiliary plant equipment. I am skilled in conducting non-destructive tube evaluations, performing compound tube bends, rolling precision steam-tight expansions in drums and headers, laying custom refractory firebrick and burner arches, and executing hydrostatic and chemical scale-removal procedures on critical pressure vessels. I am considered an expert and work with a high degree of independence.
 I have the ability to perform the full range of boiler maintenance, installation, repair, and operational tending duties on high- and low-pressure steam generation systems. I am skilled in extracting and replacing boiler tubes, dressing gasket seating surfaces, rebuilding refractory walls, troubleshooting automatic regulating controls and safety cutoffs, overhauling draft motors and feedwater systems, and performing hazardous material handling in compliance with safety standards. I work on my own initiative under general supervision subject to occasional inspection.
 I have the ability to perform common boiler maintenance routines, equipment servicing, and basic operational tending tasks under guidance. I can assist in pulling and cleaning boiler tubes, chipping and laying standard firebrick, resurfacing handhole and manway gaskets, replacing basic valves and gauges, and operating boilers under established procedures. I work under normal supervision of a work leader or journey-level employee who observes tasks in progress and upon completion to make sure they are properly performed.
 I have the ability to perform simple preventative maintenance tasks, shop clean-up, and trade assistance under direct guidance, or I have received training on the use of basic hand and power tools, mechanical measuring instruments, boiler room safety protocols, and the fundamental handling requirements for hazardous materials and asbestos abatement as outlined in 29 CFR 1910.
