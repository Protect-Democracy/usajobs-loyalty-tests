Prior Primary Wildland Firefighting Experience - Applicants for this position must possess knowledge of the principles, methods, and techniques of wildland firefighting as demonstrated by direct primary wildland firefighting experience. To receive credit, you must provide a written description of your experience of wildland firefighting in your resume. The substitution of education for primary wildland fire experience will not satisfy or meet this requirement.
Please select one response below to indicate whether you meet this mandatory requirement:
 I have at least one fire season of experience in a primary wildland firefighting role.
