Assessment 1
This position includes a screen-out element and a selective placement factor that are critical to successful performance. The screen-out element measures your ability to do the work of a High Voltage Electrician without more than normal supervision (Element 1166) at the WG-08 and WG-10 levels. 

Your responses must be supported by your resume and application materials. If your responses are not supported, you may be found ineligible, or your score may be adjusted. For each question, select the one response that best describes your background and experience.


1.
Screen-Out Element - WG-08 Level (Element 1166: Ability to do the work of a High Voltage Electrician without more than normal supervision).

Choose the one statement that best describes your experience performing High Voltage Electrician work at the WG-08 level or equivalent. Your answer must be supported by your resume.
 I do not have training or experience performing high voltage electrician work as described in the job opportunity announcement.
 I have only performed duties as a helper, laborer, or trainee assisting a High Voltage Electrician and worked under close, constant supervision. I have not performed the full range of high voltage electrician duties.
 I have performed some high voltage electrician tasks (such as assisting with installation, testing, repair, or maintenance of high voltage equipment), but I have not completed lineman/lineworker training or at least 500 hours of on-the-job training, and I have not performed the full range of duties independently.
 I have completed formal lineman/lineworker training or I have at least 500 hours of on-the-job training in high voltage electrical work, and I have experience performing duties such as installing, testing, repairing, and maintaining high voltage equipment (e.g., generators, transformers, regulators, air switches, circuit breakers, control equipment, and substation equipment), and working in substations or on underground and/or overhead high voltage primary distribution systems. I performed these duties with normal supervision and did not require constant guidance.
 I meet all of the experience described in response D and I have frequently been relied upon to carry out these assignments independently, determining work methods and sequences with minimal supervision, at a level equivalent to WG-08.


2.
Screen-Out Element - WG-10 Level (Element 1166: Ability to do the work of a High Voltage Electrician without more than normal supervision).

Choose the one statement that best describes your experience performing High Voltage Electrician work at the WG-10 (journey) level or equivalent. Your answer must be supported by your resume.
 I do not have experience performing High Voltage Electrician work at the journey (WG-10 or equivalent) level.
 I have performed some high voltage electrician duties but primarily as a helper, trainee, or under close supervision. I have not independently performed the full range of duties of a journey-level High Voltage Electrician.
 I have performed some of the duties of a High Voltage Electrician, but I have not regularly worked independently, and I have not performed the full range of journey-level duties as described below.
 I have experience performing most of the duties of a journey-level High Voltage Electrician, including installing, testing, repairing, and maintaining high voltage equipment (such as generators, photovoltaic systems, transformers, regulators, air switches, oil and vacuum circuit breakers, control equipment, and substation equipment), and working in substations and on underground or overhead high voltage primary distribution systems. I performed this work with normal supervision and carried out many assignments independently.
 I have journey-level experience at WG-10 or equivalent independently performing the full range of High Voltage Electrician duties described in response D, including determining work methods and sequences without more than normal supervision, and being relied upon as a fully proficient journey-level High Voltage Electrician.
