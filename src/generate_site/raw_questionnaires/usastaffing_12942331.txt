Eligibilities
If you are claiming 5-Points Preference, the following must be submitted:

-DD214, Certificate of Release or Discharge from Active Duty (must identify your Character of Discharge, provide Service 2 Copy or Member 4 Copy only)

If you are claiming 10-Points Preference, the following must be submitted:

-DD214, Certificate of Release or Discharge from Active Duty (must identify your Character of Discharge, provide Service 2 Copy or Member 4 Copy only)
-SF-15, Application for 10-Point Veterans' Preference (must be complete)
-Letter from the VA Regional Office stating what your percentage of disability is. It is not necessary to send documentation of what your specific disabilities are.

-SF-15, Application for 10-Point Veterans' Preference (must be complete)
-Letter from the VA Regional Office showing that your spouse is unemployable/100-percent service-connected. If deceased, a copy of the death certificate.
