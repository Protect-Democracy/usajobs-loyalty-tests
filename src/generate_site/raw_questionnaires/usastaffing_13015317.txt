(c) Non-certified individuals may only be appointed at the entry level and may not be promoted until certification is obtained. Grade Determination - GS-9 Health and Wellness Coach.

Do you meet

1.) Experience.  One year of creditable experience equivalent to the next lower grade (GS-7*).

*Examples of experience at the GS-7 include, but are not limited to: Conduct interventions using one-on-one, couple or group modalities in outpatient and inpatient settings using multiple formats (face to-face, telephone, virtual, etc.) in order to reduce or eliminate high-risk behaviors while increasing/promoting a positive lifestyle (habits and behaviors) resulting in increased well-being; facilitate development of personalized health plans focused on behavioral interventions based on current scientific knowledge and research; equip and empower individuals to live their lives to the fullest by improving quality of life across the lifespan that reduces risk for chronic disease, reverses or slows progression of chronic disease and increases the sense of well-being at the end of life. These plans include person-centered customized goals, action steps and interventions developed by the individual and include input from other members of the health care team; Knowledge of health and wellness coaching processes and techniques; Ability to promote positive lifestyle choices; Ability to effectively communicate in writing through a variety of modalities; Ability to effectively communicate verbally/non-verbally.

And

2.) Demonstrated Knowledge, Skills, and Abilities (KSAs). In addition to the basic requirements, the candidate must demonstrate all of the following KSAs:

Knowledge of current research literature related to health and wellness, including behaviors that promote a healthy lifestyle and well-being.
Skill in using health and wellness coaching techniques to promote positive behavior change using one-on-one, couple or group modalities.
Skill in exercising effective interpersonal communication techniques to develop and maintain strong interpersonal relationships.
Ability to assist the client in developing a comprehensive personalized health plan based on a patient's action-oriented goals.
Ability to identify resources internal and external to the organization that are necessary for achieving patient overall health and well-being goals.
Ability to use information technology (IT) equipment and various/emerging technologies to carry out work responsibilities.
Ability to document patient information using appropriate systems.
