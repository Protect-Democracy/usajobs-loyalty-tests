1.
Ability to troubleshoot, install, remove, repair, modify, adjust, inspect, and test such components as regulators, flow indicators, actuator motors, thruster and positioning mechanisms, seat actuators, catapult guide rails, ejection seats, rocket packs, and canopies, etc.


2.
Ability to Inspect for safety, serviceability, and maintainability of systems prior to installation, removal, repair, or adjustments of components and insures proper installation and positioning of safety pins. initiators, simulators, and clearing mechanisms.

Ability to perform installations, modifications, and repairs of aircraft egress systems.
