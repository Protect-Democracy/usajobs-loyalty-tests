QUALIFICATION REQUIREMENTS: To qualify for this position, you must meet the qualification requirements outlined below. GS-11 QUALIFICATIONS: You must have one (1) year of specialized experience at a level of difficulty and responsibility equivalent to the next lower level (GS-9) in the normal line of progression in the Federal service. Specialized experience for this position includes: Experience applying HR laws, principles, systems, policies, established procedures, and program guidance to support the new hire orientation program; providing factual information, tracking required actions, responding to routine questions from new employees, gathering missing information, and reviewing records. Experience independently planning, developing, scheduling, and coordinating assigned orientation events using approved systems and technologies to support timely and consistent session delivery; coordinates with BOD's, HCO Talent Acquisition (TA), and FMSS to identify space needs and document logistical requirements. Experience researching and applying guidance, analyzing facts, and resolving recurring or moderately complex issues. Experience with special projects and programs, utilizing various automated information systems to prepare documents, input data, compile and generate reports, and monitor administrative controls. Experience with communication techniques to facilitate presentations for new hire orientations including training materials, session resources, and established curriculum used by team members to support new employee orientation sessions. OR you may qualify based on education as follows: A Ph.D., or equivalent doctoral degree in a field of study that demonstrates the knowledge, skills, and abilities necessary to do the work of this position such as human resources, public administration, organizational psychology, or other administrative and management fields. OR three (3) full years of progressively higher-level graduate education leading to a Ph.D. in a field of study that demonstrates the knowledge, skills, and abilities necessary to do the work of this position such as human resources, public administration, organizational psychology, or other administrative and management fields. OR LL.M. in a field if study that demonstrates the knowledge, skills, and abilities necessary to do the work of this position such as human resources, public administration, organizational psychology, or other administrative and management fields. OR you may qualify with at least one (1) year of combined education and experience as defined above. GS-12 QUALIFICATIONS: You must have one (1) year of specialized experience at a level of difficulty and responsibility equivalent to the next lower level (GS-11) in the normal line of progression in the Federal service. Specialized experience for this position includes: Experience researching, interpreting, and applying established HR policies, regulations, procedures, and program guidance to resolve orientation and onboarding issues and provide technical information to employees, managers, and stakeholders. Experience conducting or facilitating new hire orientation sessions, training, or other presentations; explaining established human resources and benefits information; and using structured materials and resources appropriate for the intended audience. Experience responding to routine and recurring questions from new employees, researching missing information, and resolving issues involving onboarding documentation and operational processes. Experience communicating factual and procedural information clearly, orally, and in writing including assisting with development and delivery of training materials, session resources, and established curriculum to support new employee orientation sessions.
