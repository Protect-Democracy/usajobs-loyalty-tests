Are you the spouse of a member of the Armed Forces who: 

• Is on Active Duty for more than 180 days other than for training?

-OR-

Are you the spouse of a member of the Armed Forces who has a disability rating of 100 percent due to injury while on active duty?

Are you the un-remarried widow or widower of a member of the Armed Forces killed while in active duty status?

To verify your eligibility: You are required to submit acceptable documentation of your eligibility which may include:
documentation to support active duty service for more than 180 consecutive days (current military orders/statement of service)
verification of the marriage to the service member (i.e., a marriage license or other legal documentation verifying marriage);
verification of the member's 100 percent disability; (VA Letter AND the service members DD-214) and/or;
verification of the member's death while on active duty. (DD-1300)


Additionally, you may need to submit a Standard Form-15 (SF-15), an Application for 10-Point Veterans' Preference, and applicable supporting documents as noted on the form. To obtain a copy of SF-15, go to http://www.opm.gov/forms/pdf_fill/SF15.pdf.
