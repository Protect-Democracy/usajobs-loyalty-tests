I use the Logistic Modernization Program (LMP) for time and attendance reporting, production schedules, expenditures reports, parts availability, reporting non-conforming or damaged parts and reporting shop equipment failures. I work under normal supervision of a work leader or journey-level employee who observes in progress and upon completion to make sure they are properly performed.
