1.
From the descriptions below, select the appropriate response based on your level of experience, education, and/or training, that you fully possess and that demonstrates your ability to perform Heavy Mobile Equipment Repairer duties without more than normal supervision.

 I have the ability to perform the most difficult and complex maintenance, troubleshooting, modification, and repairs to heavy mobile equipment. I am skilled in major systems, components and parts repairs; interpreting manuals and diagrams to determine the best repair method, and conducting various operational tests to perform final inspection. I am considered an expert and work with a high degree of independence.
 I have the ability to perform the full range of maintenance, troubleshooting, modification, and repairs to heavy mobile equipment. I am skilled in removing, adjusting or replacing defective or worn parts and components; a basic understanding of mechanical, electrical, and hydraulic repairs applying to heavy mobile equipment; and conducting various operational tests to perform final inspection. I work on my own initiative under general supervision subject to occasional inspection.
 I have the ability to perform common maintenance, troubleshooting, and repairs to heavy mobile equipment. I can perform preventive maintenance checks and services; repair, replace, install, or make adjustments to mobile equipment components and parts; and conducting various operational tests to perform final inspection. I work on my own initiative under general supervision subject to occasional inspection. I work under close supervision of a work leader or journey-level employee who observes tasks in progress and upon completion to make sure they are properly performed.
 I have the ability to perform simple maintenance and repairs to heavy mobile equipment. I have received training on interpreting parts lists for repairs and the makeup and operation of the various individual systems regarding heavy mobile equipment.
