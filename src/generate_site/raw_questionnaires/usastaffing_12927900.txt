 I have one year of experience in the same or similar work equivalent to at least the next lower grade level (GS-12) requiring application of the knowledge, skills, and abilities of the position being filled. Candidates for this position must clearly demonstrate in their resume that they possess specialized experience in: Serving as a technical subject matter expert for network system security within TSD, specifically in the area of on-prem and cloud-based network infrastructure and software support with specific focus on system vulnerability management; develops system procedures to ensure systems reliability and to defend against unauthorized access; provides service and support for system security by developing baselines and performing security hardening; keeps up to date with the latest Security Technical Implementation Guides (STIG) and develops efficient and effective methods for maintaining STIG compliance; manages, tracks and reports the status of the installation of security patches; performs system scans and reviews to ensure compliance with DoD requirements. Provides technical guidance, analysis, advice, and troubleshooting/problem resolution on a wide range of extremely complex operational problems (Level Ill) that impact DSCA enterprise operations; isolate, identify, analyze, evaluate, and resolve complex hardware, software and/or communications problems.
