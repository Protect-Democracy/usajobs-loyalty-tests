Specialized Experience
To qualify for the GS-15 you must possess the specialized experience equivalent to the GS-14 grade level, OR equivalent public or private sector experience. For this position, specialized experience is defined as:
Mastery of, and skill in applying, the concepts, principles, laws, and regulations of budgeting and finance.
Expert knowledge of the agency/department and the Federal budget process, and all associated policies, directives, procedures, and regulations.
High degree of skill in analyzing, interpreting, and applying complex, legislative, policy and regulatory decisions to develop plans, policies, and guidance necessary to adjust the budget during the budget process and the year of execution.
Ability to communicate information orally and in writing. Advises Senior officials regarding budgetary tradeoffs and prisonizations both during budget formulation and budget execution.

NOTE: Your resume must explicitly indicate how you meet these requirements, otherwise you will be found ineligible.
