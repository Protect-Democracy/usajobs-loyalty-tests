Assessment 1

QUALIFICATION REQUIREMENTS: To qualify for this position, you must meet the qualification requirements outlined below:

SPECIALIZED EXPERIENCE GS-14: To be eligible for this position, you must have one (1) year of specialized experience at a level of difficulty and responsibility equivalent to the GS-13 grade level in the Federal service.
Specialized experience includes:

Leading projects through initiation, planning, execution, monitoring, and closeout by developing project scopes, schedules, resource plans, budgets, performance measures, and risk mitigation strategies.
Evaluating programs, operations, or business processes by collecting and analyzing performance information, identifying improvement opportunities, and recommending actions to improve organizational performance.
Implementing process improvement initiatives by developing or applying project management methodologies, performance measures, or process improvement techniques to improve operational efficiency and service delivery.
Coordinating projects involving multiple organizations or stakeholders by facilitating meetings, resolving project issues, monitoring project milestones, and communicating project status and recommendations.
Preparing analytical reports, executive briefings, recommendations, or other written products used to support organizational planning, resource allocation, or management decisions.
Monitoring project performance by tracking schedules, costs, risks, performance indicators, and project outcomes and recommending adjustments to support successful project completion.
