 I am able to operate several types of engineering equipment such as road graders, front-end loaders, bulldozers, and excavators to fine grade slopes, inclines, ramps, curves, and excavations by scraping, scooping, rolling, or leveling on rough and rocky terrain, winding roads, steep grades, or in close proximity to buildings & equipment. I am able to work to fine specifications from grade stakes set by engineers. I am able to steer and manipulate the control levers and attachments to reach the exact level of the surface under the most difficult operating conditions.
 I am able to operate several types of heavy equipment to excavate backfill, grade, or level earth to rough specifications on projects such as breaking new ground for trails, roads, or construction sites. I am able to move earth on mountains and slopes, graded curves and shoulders, rocky or soft ground, hilly forests, and other surfaces with similar rough features. I am able to grade surfaces to rough or fine specifications by adjusting attachments while the vehicle is in motion and on all types of terrain. I am able to operate close to buildings, trees, drop-offs, rocks or other obstructions.
 I am able to operate several types of heavy equipment to push, pull, pile or load materials such as sand, gravel, earth, coal, or refuse. I am able to adjust the attachments for proper level, angle, or depth, according to the work to be done and the type of equipment used. I have a basic knowledge of the nature of the soil and features of terrain to determine the proper approach according to the condition of the surface and subsurface. I am able to move the equipment about in confined areas.
 I am able to operate equipment such as farm tractors, cranes, dragline buckets, orange peel buckets, and or snow removal equipment, but have little or no experience in operating heavy equipment.
 I have no experience or training in heavy or construction equipment operation.
