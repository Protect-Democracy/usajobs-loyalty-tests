Assessment 1
Thank you for applying to the position of Air Conditioning Equipment Mechanic, with the Department of Defense, Washington Headquarters Services, Raven Rock Mountain Complex.


1.
From the description below, choose the one letter that best describes the highest level of experience you possess and that demonstrates your ability to perform the duties of a Air Conditioning Equipment Mechanic, WG-5306-11 without more than normal supervision. Select only one option.
 I have the ability to perform the most difficult and complex tasks including: 1) Repairing commercial and domestic refrigeration equipment such as walk-in refrigerators and freezer and repairing an assortment of commercial and industrial chilled water, direct expansion air conditioning units as well as heat pumps; 2) Applying skilled mechanical, electrical and electronic knowledge on one of a kind blast valves. Perform blast valve installation, maintenance and repair; 3) Analyzing malfunctions in equipment by means of pressure gauges, temperature probes, lead detectors, multi-meters, amp-meters, ohm-meters and other instruments for locating defects in complex mechanical, electronic and electrical systems and control; 4) Performing operation, maintenance, testing and balancing of the facility overpressure management system; and 5) Reading and interpreting detailed equipment blueprints. I am considered an expert and work with a high degree of independence.
 I have the ability to perform the full range of tasks including: 1) working from blueprints, oral instructions, and work orders to plan and lay out work; 2) installing and repairing a variety of HVAC systems; and 3) Retro-fitting systems to use refrigerants. I work on my own initiative under general supervision subject to occasional inspection.
 I have the ability to perform common tasks including: 1) interpreting instructions, specifications and blueprints to repair air conditioning and refrigeration systems; 2) Troubleshooting air conditioning and refrigeration systems operations to detect component malfunctions; and 3) installing and repairing a variety of large commercial and industrial refrigeration and air conditioning equipment. I work under close supervision of a work leader or journey-level employee who observes tasks in progress and upon completion to make sure they are properly performed.
 I have the ability to perform simple tasks, or I have received training on this type of work.
 I do not have the on-the-job training or the abilities described above.
