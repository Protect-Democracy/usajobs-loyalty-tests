NOTE: Current SES Career Appointees, SES Reinstatement Eligibles, and OPM Candidate Development Program graduates are not required to complete the Executive Core Qualifications listed below; however, you must address the mandatory technical competencies.


The Core Focus of the Executive Core Qualifications are cross-cutting and should be addressed within your two-page resume. The following are the foundation for success in each of the ECQs below: Constitutional fidelity and service to the American people; Efficient resource use, cost-saving, process modernization; Technical excellence and high-quality performance; Team leadership, accountability, performance culture; and Meeting and exceeding mission-aligned goals.


ECQ 1 - This core qualification encompasses the ability to uphold the Constitution and American governing ideals - a foundation for federal leadership.


ECQ 2 - This core qualification involves the ability to demonstrate effective resource management, budget discipline, waste reduction, and leveraging technology or process improvements.


ECQ 3 - This core qualification stresses the delivering of exceptional quality work through technical skill, knowledge, and reliable performance.


ECQ 4 - This ECQ involves the ability to inspire and guide teams toward mission objectives while fostering accountability, performance, and adaptability.


ECQ 5 - This core qualification involves consistently delivering outcomes aligned with organizational goals and the broader mission set by leadership.


MANDATORY TECHNICAL QUALIFICATIONS:  All candidates must address the following Mandatory Technical Qualifications within their two-page resume.


Demonstrated experience leading a team of OIG lawyers providing general counsel to all component activities of an OIG including direct support to the core audit, investigations, and inspection functions of an OIG. 




Demonstrated experience in reviewing--and leading others in the review of--current and proposed legislation, regulations, trends and initiatives to inform senior executives on the contours of each and compliance with such laws and guidance.
