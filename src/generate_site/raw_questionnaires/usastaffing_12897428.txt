2.
Please indicate the functional area(s) in which you are most interested. The summaries below describe representative responsibilities and are not intended to limit the full scope of the position. All functional areas contribute to surge and priority hiring initiatives in support of mission-critical staffing needs.
 A. Applicant Coordination Center (ACC) - Orchestrates surge hiring operations through applicant coordination and communication, to ensure efficient processing and candidate movement (minimal travel required-typically less than 10%).
 B. Recruitment Division (REC) - Advances surge and priority hiring efforts through outreach, hiring process coordination, applicant engagement, and employer branding initiatives (frequent travel required – up to 50%).
 C. Expedited Hiring Division (XPD) - Leads accelerated surge hiring initiatives to meet urgent, mission-critical staffing demands and operational priorities (frequent travel required – up to 50%).
 D. Classification and Position Management Division (CPM) - Establishes the structural foundation for surge workforce initiatives through position management, organizational design, and classification expertise (minimal travel required – typically less than 25%).
 E. No Preference - Open to consideration for placement in any functional area based on organizational needs.
