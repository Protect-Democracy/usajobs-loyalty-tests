Conditions of Employment
1.
This position is part of the DoD Expeditionary Civilian Program. Applicants must understand that if selected, they MAY have the following austere conditions:
Stressful work environments, i.e., long work hours
Austere living conditions with little privacy
Living/working on a military compound/forward operating base (FOB) that may receive indirect fire attack/IED strikes
Physically challenging in terms of wearing body armor in extreme heat, carrying heavy duffel bags, hopping on/off helicopters and possibly running to a bomb shelter during a mortar attack
Lodging and meals (typical military style) are provided, so per diem will be limited to $3.50 per day (field conditions)
Lodging is Containerized Housing Units (CHU) with normally four people to a CHU (bunk beds)
Limited access to non-emergency medical/dental care
The work day is usually 10 or more hours a day for 6-1/2 to 7 days a week.
