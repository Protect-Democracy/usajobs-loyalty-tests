Assessment 1
QUALIFICATION REQUIREMENTS: To qualify for this position, you must meet the qualification requirements outlined below:

GS-09 Specialized Experience:
To be eligible for this position at this grade level, you must have (1) year of specialized experience at a level of difficulty and responsibility equivalent to the GS-07 grade level in the Federal service. Examples of qualifying experience for this position include:
Tracking expenditures by proper accounts and type in financial systems using charts of accounts;
Providing information on budget calculations and funding levels for incorporation into budget planning;
Analyzing and evaluating changes in program plans and funding and their effect on the budget;
Experience interpreting and applying basic fund accounting, budget instructions, administrative memoranda, internal controls, and regulatory guidelines from a procedural, technical standpoint.
OR EDUCATION:
A master's or equivalent graduate degree in a related field of study such as accounting, auditing, finance, economics, business administration or other related fields from an accredited college or university.
OR Two (2) full academic years of progressively higher-level graduate education in a related field of study such as accounting, auditing, finance, economics, business administration or other related fields.
OR An LL.B. or J.D., if related.
OR COMBINATION OF EDUCATION AND EXPERIENCE: A combination of experience and education equivalent to that described above.

GS-11 Specialized Experience:
To be eligible for this position at this grade level, you must have (1) year of specialized experience at a level of difficulty and responsibility equivalent to the GS-09 grade level in the Federal service. Examples of qualifying experience for this position include:
Experience researching budgetary and financial issues, developing and analyzing resource proposals, identifying issues related to projected budgetary and staffing requirements, and analyzing estimates;
Assisting with the planning and execution of budget resource requirements; Experience with fund accounting methodologies; Preparing reports and charts to monitor resources (e.g., equipment, supplies, facilities, and training);
Participating in budget-related policy development;
Reviewing directives, instructions, budget materials, funding documents and developing narrative justifications and strategies to ensure program delivery within the bounds of financial constraints.
OR EDUCATION:
A Ph.D. or equivalent doctoral degree in a related field of study such as accounting, auditing, finance, economics, business administration or other related fields.
OR Three (3) full academic years of progressively higher-level graduate education leading to a doctoral degree in a related field of study such as accounting, auditing, finance, economics, business administration or other related fields.
OR An LL.M., if related.
OR COMBINATION OF EDUCATION AND EXPERIENCE: A combination of experience and education equivalent to that described above.
