Assessment 2
You will now complete a Situational Judgement Test (SJT). This assessment presents realistic job-related scenarios. For each one, select the response that best reflects how you would handle the situation. Each question has four response options. these range from the most appropriate to the least appropriate action, but the options are presented in random order. Your task is to choose the option you believe is the best course of action in a federal workplace setting. 
-There are no right or wrong answers.
Your responses should reflect sound judgement and professional decision-making.
This test evaluates how you approach workplace situations, not your prior experience or background. 
Please answer honestly and thoughtfully. your responses will help determine your qualifications for this position.



1.
AS THE SAFETY AND OCCUPATIONAL HEALTH MANAGER, YOU NEED TO UPDATE THE DISTRICT'S SAFETY PUBLICATIONS TO ADDRESS NEW OSHA REGULATIONS OR EM 385-1-1 REQUIREMENTS THAT AFFECT MULTIPLE CONSTRUCTION SITES. SEVERAL PROJECT MANAGERS ARE REQUESTING IMMEDIATE GUIDANCE, BUT YOU ALSO NEED TO ENSURE THE PUBLICATIONS ARE THOROUGHLY REVIEWED AND LEGALLY COMPLIANT. WHAT IS YOUR BEST APPROACH?
 ISSUE TEMPORARY GUIDANCE MEMOS TO PROJECT MANAGERS WHILE POSTPONING COMPREHENSIVE PUBLICATION UPDATES UNTIL THE NEXT SCHEDULED REVIEW CYCLE.
 DEVELOP A COMPREHENSIVE IMPLEMENTATION TIMELINE THAT INCLUDES STAKEHOLDER INPUT SESSIONS, LEGAL REVIEW, AND PHASED ROLLOUT WITH INTERIM GUIDANCE DOCUMENTS FOR IMMEDIATE COMPLIANCE NEEDS.
 DELEGATE THE PUBLICATION DEVELOPMENT TO SUBORDINATE STAFF MEMBERS AND APPROVE THEIR DRAFTS WITHOUT DETAILED REVIEW TO MEET URGENT TIMELINES.
 FOCUS ON UPDATING THE MOST CRITICAL PUBLICATIONS FIRST, THEN ADDRESS REMAINING DOCUMENTS BASED ON PROJECT MANAGER FEEDBACK AND REGULATORY DEADLINES.
 CREATE DRAFT GUIDANCE DOCUMENTS IMMEDIATELY FOR URGENT SITUATIONS WHILE SIMULTANEOUSLY DEVELOPING COMPREHENSIVE PUBLICATIONS THROUGH PROPER REVIEW CHANNELS.


2.
YOU ARE MANAGING A TEAM OF SAFETY SPECIALISTS AND CONTRACTOR PERSONNEL WHO MUST COMPLETE SAFETY INSPECTIONS ACROSS 15 FACILITIES BEFORE THE END OF THE QUARTER. TWO TEAM MEMBERS ARE ON EXTENDED LEAVE, AND THREE HIGH-PRIORITY CONSTRUCTION SITES HAVE BEEN ADDED TO YOUR INSPECTION SCHEDULE. HOW DO YOU BEST PLAN THE WORK ALLOCATION?
 REASSIGN HIGH-PRIORITY SITES TO YOUR MOST EXPERIENCED PERSONNEL AND ADJUST THE INSPECTION SCHEDULE TO FOCUS ON CRITICAL SAFETY AREAS FIRST.
 EXTEND THE INSPECTION TIMELINE AND INFORM MANAGEMENT THAT SOME FACILITIES MAY NEED TO BE INSPECTED IN THE FOLLOWING QUARTER DUE TO RESOURCE CONSTRAINTS.
 CONDUCT A COMPREHENSIVE WORKLOAD ANALYSIS, REDISTRIBUTE ASSIGNMENTS BASED ON EXPERTISE AND CAPACITY, AND ESTABLISH CLEAR PRIORITIES WITH CONTINGENCY PLANS FOR CRITICAL INSPECTIONS.
 ASSIGN ALL NEW HIGH-PRIORITY SITES TO CONTRACTOR PERSONNEL WITHOUT ADDITIONAL OVERSIGHT SINCE THEY HAVE MORE FLEXIBLE SCHEDULES THAN GOVERNMENT EMPLOYEES.
 REQUEST TEMPORARY ADDITIONAL STAFF SUPPORT WHILE REDISTRIBUTING CURRENT ASSIGNMENTS BASED ON GEOGRAPHIC PROXIMITY AND INSPECTOR AVAILABILITY.


3.
YOU ARE TASKED WITH ESTABLISHING A COMPREHENSIVE SAFETY PROGRAM FOR PERSONNEL AT A FORWARD OPERATING BASE IN A COMBAT ZONE. THE PROGRAM MUST ADDRESS WORKPLACE SAFETY, WHILE CONSIDERING SECURITY CONSTRAINTS AND LIMITED RESOURCES. WHAT IS YOUR PRIMARY APPROACH?
 FOCUS ON HIGH-RISK WORKPLACE AREAS FIRST, THEN GRADUALLY EXPAND SAFETY COVERAGE TO SECURITY CONDITIONS PERMIT.
 IMPLEMENT BASIC SAFETY MEASURES ONLY IN WORK AREAS SINCE SECURITY FORCES ARE RESPONSIBLE FOR ALL OTHER SAFETY CONCERNS IN COMBAT ENVIRONMENTS.
 CREATE SEPARATE SAFETY PROTOCOLS FOR WORK AREAS, WHILE ENSURING COORDINATION BETWEEN SECURITY FORCES AND SAFETY PERSONNEL FOR IMPLEMENTATION.
 ADAPT EXISTING STATESIDE SAFETY PROGRAMS WITH MINIMAL MODIFICATIONS AND RELY PRIMARILY ON SECURITY PERSONNEL TO ADDRESS COMBAT-RELATED SAFETY CONCERNS.
 DEVELOP A MULTI-LAYERED SAFETY FRAMEWORK THAT INTEGRATES SECURITY PROTOCOLS, CONDUCTS RISK ASSESSMENTS FOR ALL AREAS, AND ESTABLISHES EMERGENCY RESPONSE PROCEDURES WITH REGULAR TRAINING PROGRAMS.


4.
YOU RECEIVE AN ACCIDENT REPORT FROM A CONSTRUCTION SITE INVOLVING A SERIOUS INJURY, BUT THE REPORT LACKS CRITICAL DETAILS ABOUT EQUIPMENT MAINTENANCE RECORDS, WITNESS STATEMENTS, AND ENVIRONMENTAL CONDITIONS. THE PROJECT MANAGER IS PRESSURING YOU TO APPROVE THE REPORT QUICKLY TO AVOID PROJECT DELAYS. HOW DO YOU PROCEED?
 APPROVE THE REPORT IMMEDIATELY TO AVOID PROJECT DELAYS SINCE THE BASIC INCIDENT FACTS ARE DOCUMENTED AND ADDITIONAL DETAILS CAN BE GATHERED LATER IF NEEDED.
 APPROVE THE REPORT WITH CONDITIONAL ACCEPTANCE WHILE REQUIRING SUPPLEMENTAL DOCUMENTATION TO BE SUBMITTED WITHIN A SPECIFIED TIMEFRAME.
 ACCEPT THE REPORT AS SUBMITTED BUT DOCUMENT YOUR CONCERNS IN A SEPARATE MEMO TO MAINTAIN A RECORD OF THE INCOMPLETE INFORMATION.
 RETURN THE REPORT WITH SPECIFIC REQUIREMENTS FOR MISSING INFORMATION, PROVIDE GUIDANCE ON PROPER DOCUMENTATION STANDARDS, AND ESTABLISH A REASONABLE TIMELINE FOR RESUBMISSION.
 CONTACT THE PROJECT MANAGER DIRECTLY TO DISCUSS THE DEFICIENCIES AND WORK COLLABORATIVELY TO GATHER THE MISSING INFORMATION BEFORE FINAL APPROVAL.


5.
YOUR DISTRICT IS FACING A COMPREHENSIVE SAFETY AUDIT FROM HEADQUARTERS NEXT MONTH, AND YOU MUST COORDINATE MULTIPLE ONGOING ACCIDENT INVESTIGATIONS WHILE MANAGING ROUTINE FACILITY EVALUATIONS. ONE INVESTIGATION INVOLVES A FATALITY THAT REQUIRES IMMEDIATE ATTENTION, BUT THE AUDIT PREPARATION IS ALSO CRITICAL FOR DISTRICT OPERATIONS. HOW DO YOU MANAGE THESE COMPETING PRIORITIES?
 ESTABLISH A PRIORITY MATRIX THAT ENSURES THE FATALITY INVESTIGATION RECEIVES IMMEDIATE RESOURCES WHILE DELEGATING AUDIT PREPARATION TASKS AND MAINTAINING OVERSIGHT OF ROUTINE EVALUATIONS.
 DELEGATE THE FATALITY INVESTIGATION TO CONTRACTOR PERSONNEL SO YOU CAN PERSONALLY MANAGE THE AUDIT PREPARATION AND MAINTAIN NORMAL EVALUATION SCHEDULES.
 ASSIGN YOUR MOST EXPERIENCED INVESTIGATOR TO THE FATALITY CASE WHILE PERSONALLY MANAGING AUDIT PREPARATION AND TEMPORARILY POSTPONING NON-CRITICAL FACILITY EVALUATIONS.
 FOCUS PRIMARILY ON AUDIT PREPARATION SINCE IT AFFECTS THE ENTIRE DISTRICT WHILE ENSURING THE FATALITY INVESTIGATION MEETS MINIMUM REGULATORY REQUIREMENTS.
 REQUEST ADDITIONAL TEMPORARY STAFF SUPPORT TO HANDLE ROUTINE EVALUATIONS WHILE YOU FOCUS ON THE FATALITY INVESTIGATION AND AUDIT COORDINATION SIMULTANEOUSLY.


6.
THE ANNUAL SMAP UPDATE IS DUE IN SIX WEEKS, BUT RECENT ORGANIZATIONAL CHANGES, NEW MISSION REQUIREMENTS, AND UPDATED REGULATIONS REQUIRE SIGNIFICANT REVISIONS. SEVERAL DEPARTMENT HEADS HAVE CONFLICTING PRIORITIES AND LIMITED AVAILABILITY FOR INPUT SESSIONS. WHAT IS YOUR BEST STRATEGY?
 CREATE A DRAFT PLAN BASED ON REGULATORY REQUIREMENTS AND ORGANIZATIONAL CHANGES, THEN CIRCULATE IT FOR COMMENTS WITH A DEFINED RESPONSE TIMELINE.
 DEVELOP A STRUCTURED STAKEHOLDER ENGAGEMENT PLAN WITH CLEAR TIMELINES, CONDUCT FOCUSED WORKING SESSIONS BY FUNCTIONAL AREA, AND CREATE A COMPREHENSIVE REVIEW PROCESS WITH MULTIPLE FEEDBACK OPPORTUNITIES.
 FOCUS ON MEETING THE DEADLINE BY MAKING MINIMAL CHANGES TO THE EXISTING PLAN AND ADDRESSING MAJOR REVISIONS IN THE FOLLOWING YEAR'S UPDATE.
 SCHEDULE INDIVIDUAL MEETINGS WITH KEY DEPARTMENT HEADS TO GATHER INPUT EFFICIENTLY, THEN DEVELOP THE UPDATED PLAN WITH A STREAMLINED REVIEW PROCESS.
 REQUEST A DEADLINE EXTENSION FROM THE COMMANDER WHILE CONTINUING WITH THE CURRENT PLAN UNTIL ALL STAKEHOLDERS CAN PROVIDE COMPREHENSIVE INPUT.


7.
DURING A ROUTINE INSPECTION OF A WAREHOUSE FACILITY, YOU DISCOVER MULTIPLE SAFETY VIOLATIONS INCLUDING BLOCKED EMERGENCY EXITS, IMPROPER CHEMICAL STORAGE, AND MISSING SAFETY EQUIPMENT. THE FACILITY MANAGER CLAIMS THEY ARE UNDERSTAFFED AND CANNOT ADDRESS ALL ISSUES IMMEDIATELY. HOW DO YOU HANDLE THIS SITUATION?
 DOCUMENT ALL VIOLATIONS BUT ALLOW EXTENDED TIMELINES FOR CORRECTION DUE TO STAFFING ISSUES, FOCUSING ON THE MOST VISIBLE SAFETY CONCERNS FIRST.
 ISSUE A FORMAL INSPECTION REPORT WITH SPECIFIC CORRECTIVE ACTIONS REQUIRED, ESTABLISH CLEAR DEADLINES, AND SCHEDULE A FOLLOW-UP INSPECTION TO VERIFY COMPLIANCE.
 CATEGORIZE VIOLATIONS BY RISK LEVEL, REQUIRE IMMEDIATE CORRECTION OF CRITICAL SAFETY HAZARDS, ESTABLISH A TIMELINE FOR REMAINING ISSUES, AND PROVIDE RESOURCES FOR COMPLIANCE ASSISTANCE.
 WORK WITH THE FACILITY MANAGER TO DEVELOP A PRIORITIZED CORRECTION PLAN THAT ADDRESSES THE MOST SERIOUS VIOLATIONS FIRST WHILE CONSIDERING STAFFING CONSTRAINTS.
 CLOSE THE FACILITY IMMEDIATELY UNTIL ALL VIOLATIONS ARE CORRECTED SINCE THE COMBINATION OF MULTIPLE SAFETY ISSUES CREATES AN UNACCEPTABLE RISK ENVIRONMENT.


8.
A PROJECT TEAM IS WORKING WITH SPECIALIZED EQUIPMENT MANUFACTURED OVERSEAS THAT DOESN'T FULLY COMPLY WITH STANDARD U.S. SAFETY REQUIREMENTS. THEY NEED GUIDANCE ON ADAPTING SAFETY DEVICES AND PROCEDURES TO MEET REGULATORY STANDARDS WHILE MAINTAINING OPERATIONAL EFFECTIVENESS. HOW DO YOU PROVIDE THE MOST APPROPRIATE ASSISTANCE?
 PROVIDE RELEVANT SAFETY CODES AND STANDARDS WHILE WORKING DIRECTLY WITH THE PROJECT TEAM TO IDENTIFY APPROPRIATE MODIFICATIONS AND SAFETY DEVICE ADAPTATIONS.
 PROVIDE GENERAL SAFETY GUIDELINES AND SUGGEST THE PROJECT TEAM WORK WITH CONTRACTORS TO DETERMINE APPROPRIATE SAFETY MODIFICATIONS.
 RECOMMEND REPLACING THE OVERSEAS EQUIPMENT WITH U.S.-MANUFACTURED ALTERNATIVES SINCE ADAPTING FOREIGN EQUIPMENT CREATES UNNECESSARY COMPLIANCE COMPLICATIONS.
 RESEARCH APPLICABLE STANDARDS AND REGULATIONS, CONSULT WITH ENGINEERING SPECIALISTS, AND DEVELOP COMPREHENSIVE GUIDANCE THAT INCLUDES SPECIFIC ADAPTATION PROCEDURES AND COMPLIANCE VERIFICATION METHODS.
 SUPPLY STANDARD SAFETY MANUALS AND RECOMMEND CONSULTATION WITH EQUIPMENT MANUFACTURERS TO DEVELOP COMPLIANT SAFETY MODIFICATIONS.


9.
A MAJOR CONSTRUCTION CONTRACTOR HAS SUBMITTED A SAFETY PLAN THAT MEETS BASIC CONTRACT REQUIREMENTS BUT LACKS DETAIL IN SEVERAL CRITICAL AREAS INCLUDING FALL PROTECTION PROCEDURES AND HAZARDOUS MATERIAL HANDLING. THE CONTRACTOR ARGUES THEIR PLAN IS ADEQUATE AND REQUESTS IMMEDIATE APPROVAL TO AVOID PROJECT DELAYS. WHAT IS YOUR RESPONSE?

 APPROVE THE PLAN CONDITIONALLY WITH REQUIREMENTS FOR SUPPLEMENTAL PROCEDURES TO BE SUBMITTED AND APPROVED BEFORE WORK BEGINS IN DEFICIENT AREAS.
 CONDUCT A DETAILED TECHNICAL REVIEW, IDENTIFY SPECIFIC DEFICIENCIES WITH REGULATORY CITATIONS, AND REQUIRE PLAN REVISIONS WITH CLEAR GUIDANCE ON COMPLIANCE REQUIREMENTS BEFORE APPROVAL.
 APPROVE THE PLAN IMMEDIATELY TO AVOID PROJECT DELAYS SINCE CONTRACTOR SAFETY PLANS ARE PRIMARILY THE CONTRACTOR'S RESPONSIBILITY AND LIABILITY.
 ACCEPT THE PLAN SINCE IT MEETS BASIC CONTRACT REQUIREMENTS BUT DOCUMENT CONCERNS AND INCREASE INSPECTION FREQUENCY DURING CONSTRUCTION ACTIVITIES.
 SCHEDULE A MEETING WITH THE CONTRACTOR TO DISCUSS DEFICIENCIES AND WORK COLLABORATIVELY TO ENHANCE THE PLAN WHILE MAINTAINING PROJECT TIMELINES.


10.
MULTIPLE DEPARTMENTS ARE REPORTING DIFFERENT SAFETY CONCERNS: THE CONSTRUCTION DIVISION NEEDS GUIDANCE ON NEW FALL PROTECTION REQUIREMENTS, THE FACILITIES TEAM HAS QUESTIONS ABOUT CHEMICAL STORAGE REGULATIONS, AND SUPERVISORS ARE REQUESTING TRAINING ON INCIDENT REPORTING PROCEDURES. HOW DO YOU MOST EFFECTIVELY ADDRESS THESE DIVERSE NEEDS?
 DIRECT EACH DEPARTMENT TO REVIEW EXISTING SAFETY MANUALS AND REGULATIONS INDEPENDENTLY, PROVIDING CONSULTATION ONLY FOR ISSUES THEY CANNOT RESOLVE INTERNALLY.
 CREATE GENERAL SAFETY BULLETINS ADDRESSING COMMON CONCERNS WHILE OFFERING INDIVIDUAL CONSULTATIONS FOR DEPARTMENT-SPECIFIC TECHNICAL QUESTIONS.
 DEVELOP A COMPREHENSIVE COMMUNICATION STRATEGY THAT INCLUDES TARGETED GUIDANCE DOCUMENTS, GROUP TRAINING SESSIONS BY TOPIC AREA, AND REGULAR CONSULTATION AVAILABILITY FOR ONGOING QUESTIONS.
 FOCUS ON THE CONSTRUCTION DIVISION'S IMMEDIATE NEEDS SINCE FALL PROTECTION HAS THE HIGHEST INJURY POTENTIAL, THEN ADDRESS OTHER CONCERNS AS TIME PERMITS.
 PRIORITIZE THE MOST CRITICAL SAFETY ISSUES FIRST, THEN SCHEDULE DEPARTMENT-SPECIFIC BRIEFINGS AND PROVIDE WRITTEN GUIDANCE MATERIALS FOR EACH AREA OF CONCERN.


11.
A CONSTRUCTION PROJECT REQUIRES MODIFICATIONS TO MEET NEW SAFETY STANDARDS, BUT THE ENGINEERING TEAM BELIEVES THE CHANGES WILL SIGNIFICANTLY IMPACT THE PROJECT TIMELINE AND BUDGET. THE CONSTRUCTION MANAGER IS RESISTANT TO DELAYS, WHILE SAFETY REGULATIONS ARE NON-NEGOTIABLE. HOW DO YOU FACILITATE A RESOLUTION?
 WORK DIRECTLY WITH ENGINEERING TO IDENTIFY THE MOST COST-EFFECTIVE COMPLIANCE APPROACH, THEN PRESENT OPTIONS TO CONSTRUCTION MANAGEMENT WITH CLEAR SAFETY JUSTIFICATIONS.
 EMPHASIZE THE REGULATORY REQUIREMENTS TO CONSTRUCTION MANAGEMENT WHILE ASKING ENGINEERING TO MINIMIZE DESIGN CHANGES WHEREVER POSSIBLE.
 RESEARCH ALTERNATIVE SAFETY SOLUTIONS AND PRESENT MULTIPLE COMPLIANCE OPTIONS TO THE PROJECT TEAM WITH RECOMMENDATIONS FOR THE MOST PRACTICAL APPROACH.
 ORGANIZE A COLLABORATIVE PLANNING SESSION WITH ALL STAKEHOLDERS TO EXPLORE ALTERNATIVE COMPLIANCE METHODS, EVALUATE COST-BENEFIT OPTIONS, AND DEVELOP AN INTEGRATED SOLUTION THAT MEETS SAFETY REQUIREMENTS EFFICIENTLY.
 INSIST ON FULL COMPLIANCE WITH NEW STANDARDS REGARDLESS OF COST OR TIMELINE IMPACTS SINCE SAFETY REQUIREMENTS CANNOT BE COMPROMISED UNDER ANY CIRCUMSTANCES.


12.
ROUTINE AIR QUALITY MONITORING IN AN OFFICE BUILDING HAS DETECTED ELEVATED LEVELS OF VOLATILE ORGANIC COMPOUNDS, BUT THE SOURCE IS UNCLEAR. EMPLOYEES ARE REPORTING HEADACHES AND RESPIRATORY IRRITATION, BUT BUILDING OPERATIONS CANNOT BE COMPLETELY SHUT DOWN DUE TO MISSION REQUIREMENTS. WHAT IS YOUR INVESTIGATIVE APPROACH?

 FOCUS TESTING ON THE MOST LIKELY CONTAMINATION SOURCES FIRST WHILE ADVISING EMPLOYEES TO REPORT ANY WORSENING SYMPTOMS DURING THE INVESTIGATION PERIOD.
 IMPLEMENT IMMEDIATE PROTECTIVE MEASURES FOR AFFECTED AREAS, CONDUCT SYSTEMATIC SOURCE IDENTIFICATION TESTING, COORDINATE WITH INDUSTRIAL HYGIENISTS, AND DEVELOP A PHASED INVESTIGATION PLAN THAT MAINTAINS ESSENTIAL OPERATIONS.
 INCREASE VENTILATION IN AFFECTED AREAS AND CONDUCT DETAILED TESTING DURING OFF-HOURS TO IDENTIFY SOURCES WHILE MONITORING EMPLOYEE HEALTH SYMPTOMS.
 ESTABLISH TEMPORARY WORK RESTRICTIONS IN AFFECTED AREAS WHILE CONDUCTING COMPREHENSIVE ENVIRONMENTAL TESTING TO IDENTIFY CONTAMINATION SOURCES AND EXPOSURE LEVELS.
 CONTINUE NORMAL OPERATIONS WHILE CONDUCTING TESTING SINCE THE CONTAMINATION LEVELS ARE NOT IMMEDIATELY LIFE-THREATENING AND BUILDING CLOSURE WOULD DISRUPT CRITICAL MISSIONS.


13.
A SERIOUS ACCIDENT INVOLVING HEAVY EQUIPMENT HAS OCCURRED AT A CONSTRUCTION SITE, RESULTING IN SIGNIFICANT INJURIES. THIS IS THE THIRD EQUIPMENT-RELATED INCIDENT IN SIX MONTHS ACROSS DIFFERENT SITES. YOU NEED TO CONDUCT A THOROUGH INVESTIGATION WHILE IDENTIFYING POTENTIAL SYSTEMIC ISSUES. WHAT IS YOUR COMPREHENSIVE APPROACH?
 CONDUCT A THOROUGH ACCIDENT INVESTIGATION WHILE SIMULTANEOUSLY REVIEWING EQUIPMENT MAINTENANCE RECORDS AND OPERATOR TRAINING ACROSS ALL SITES FOR POTENTIAL CONNECTIONS.
 COMPLETE THE REQUIRED ACCIDENT INVESTIGATION AND REPORTING WHILE NOTING THE PATTERN OF INCIDENTS FOR FUTURE CONSIDERATION DURING THE NEXT ANNUAL SAFETY PROGRAM REVIEW.
 SECURE THE ACCIDENT SCENE, CONDUCT DETAILED ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS, REVIEW PREVIOUS INCIDENTS FOR PATTERNS, ANALYZE TRAINING RECORDS AND PROCEDURES, AND DEVELOP SYSTEMIC CORRECTIVE MEASURES ADDRESSING IDENTIFIED TRENDS.
 LEAD A COMPREHENSIVE INVESTIGATION TEAM INCLUDING TECHNICAL EXPERTS, ANALYZE THE IMMEDIATE ACCIDENT CAUSES, AND COMPARE FINDINGS WITH PREVIOUS INCIDENTS TO IDENTIFY COMMON FACTORS.
 FOCUS ON THE IMMEDIATE ACCIDENT INVESTIGATION FIRST, THEN CONDUCT A SEPARATE ANALYSIS OF PREVIOUS INCIDENTS TO DETERMINE IF THERE ARE RELATED SYSTEMIC ISSUES.
