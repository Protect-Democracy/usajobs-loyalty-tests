1.
From the descriptions below select the one response that best describes how your experience meets the ability to do the work of the position without more than normal supervision for the Animal Packer, WG-5001-05 position.
Animal Packers at this level perform the following duties: Assist in caring for and leading pack animals in transporting various types of supplies and equipment to remote backcountry locations utilizing strings of horses, mules, and llamas. Balance and hitch loads to pack animals to ensure pack saddles and/or side pack loads are not causing injury to the animal or damage to the cargo. Work with and safely ride livestock around work groups and the public in backcountry trail settings. Provide full variety of needs for animals including saddling, grooming, feeding, and watering.
