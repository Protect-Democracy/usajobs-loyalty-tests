1.
Select the one statement that best describes the EDUCATION AND CERTIFICATION REQUIREMENTS that you possess.
 I HAVE an Accounting Degree or a degree in a related field such as business administration, finance, or public administration that included - or - was supplemented by 24 semester hours in accounting. (24 hours may include up to six hours of credit in business law)
 I HAVE at least 4 years of experience in accounting, or an equivalent combination of accounting experience, college-level education, and training that provided professional accounting knowledge AND either 1) 24 semester hours in accounting or auditing courses of appropriate type and quality OR 2) a certificate as Certified Public Accountant or a Certified Internal Auditor OR 3) have completed the requirements for a degree that included substantial course work in accounting or auditing.
 I DO NOT HAVE either 1) the degree or 2) combination of Education and Experience listed above.


2.
Select the one statement that best describes the Specialized Experience and/or education that you possess that demonstrates your ability to perform the duties of a GS-0510-11 Cost Accountant.
 I HAVE at least 1 year equivalent to at least next lower grade level (GS-09) of accounting experiences and competencies that provided me 1) knowledge of principles and procedures, 2) excellent understanding of the basic principles and concepts of the National Guard accounting business program requirements, 3) the ability to conduct accounting business, and 4) experience using statistical and/or judgmental sampling of financial records and management controls.
 I DO NOT have at least 1 year equivalent to the next lower grade level (GS-09)of experience, education or training listed above.
