Assessment 1
1.
ABILITY TO DO THE WORK OF AN ENGINEERING EQUIPMENT OPERATOR WITHOUT MORE THAN NORMAL SUPERVISION (SCREEN-OUT).
 1) I am able to independently operate one or more types of engineering equipment to fine grade slopes, inclines, ramps, curves, and excavations by scraping, scooping, rolling, or leveling on rough, rocky, and steep terrain or in dense forest areas. 2) I am able to work to fine specifications from grade stakes set by engineers. 3) I am able to steer and manipulate the control levers and attachments to reach the exact level of the surface under the most difficult operating conditions, 4) operate various heavy motor vehicles, including tractor trailers, passenger buses, and dump trucks with a gross weight over 32,000 pounds, 5) I am skilled in transporting heavy equipment, oversized loads, and handling route planning and oversized permits, 6) Operator maintains equipment by checking fluids, lubricating parts, conducting safety checks, 7) they also perform scheduled services, change filters, and replace or rebuild major components and 8) Ability to conduct basic building maintenance and repair including but not limited to replacing plumbing, outlets, sockets, lighting and power fixtures, wiring, systems, Installs outlets, switches, light fixtures, regulators, and circuit breakers. Utilize appropriate diagnostic tools to determine deficiencies in water and electrical systems.
 1) I am able to operate one or more types of heavy equipment to excavate backfill, grade, or level earth to rough specifications on projects such as breaking new ground for trails, roads, canals, tunnels, or construction sites. 2) I am able to move earth on mountains and steep slopes, graded curves and shoulders, rocky or soft ground, hilly forests, and other surfaces with similar rough features. 3) I am able to grade surfaces to rough specifications by adjusting attachments while the vehicle is in motion and on all types of terrain. 4) I am able to operate close to buildings, trees, drop-offs, rocks or other obstructions, 5) Operator maintains equipment by checking fluids, lubricating parts, conducting safety checks, and 6) operate various heavy motor vehicles, including tractor trailers, passenger buses, and dump trucks with a gross weight over 32,000 pounds.
 1) I am able to operate one or more types of heavy equipment to push, pull, pile or load materials such as sand, gravel, earth, coal, or refuse. 2) I am able to adjust the attachments for proper level, angle, or depth, according to the work to be done and the type of equipment used. 3) I have a basic knowledge of the nature of the soil and features of terrain to determine the proper approach according to the condition of the surface and subsurface. 4) I am able to move the equipment about in confined areas and 5) operate various heavy motor vehicles, including tractor trailers, passenger buses, and dump trucks with a gross weight over 32,000 pounds. I am able to perform this work without more than normal supervision.
 I am able to operate equipment such as farm tractors, and cranes, but have little or no experience in operating heavy equipment.
 I have no experience or training in heavy or construction equipment operations.
