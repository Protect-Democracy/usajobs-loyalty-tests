Current Department of Army Civilian Employees Applying on Time-Limited Assignments
    
This job opportunity announcement is open to current, permanent Army employees that would occupy the position under a time-limited promotion, reassignment or change to lower grade not to exceed (NTE). You are eligible in this category if you fall into one of the following:

•  ARMY CIVILIAN EMPLOYEES SERVING ON A PERMANENT APPOINTMENT. You must submit a copy of your most recent SF-50, Notification of Personnel Action. Block 24 of your SF-50 must be a "1" or "2" AND block 34 must be a"1." Army employees can access their SF-50 at: MyBiz.
    
•  ARMY CIVILIAN EMPLOYEES SERVING ON AN EXCEPTED SERVICE VETERANS RECRUITMENT APPOINTMENT (VRA).  If you are eligible in this category, you are required to submit an SF-50 showing current Army Excepted Service VRA appointment (block 24 must be a "1" or "2" AND block 34 must be a"2.").
    
Supporting documentation: You are required to submit the documents which prove you are a current Department of the Army employee in one of the categories described above. To obtain a copy of SF-50s from employment with the Department of Defense, go to My Biz at https://compo.dcpds.cpms.osd.mil/.

Assessment 1
1.
From the descriptions below, select the appropriate response based on your level of experience, education, and/or training, that you fully possess and that demonstrates your ability to perform Painter (Polyurea Operator/Inspector) duties without more than normal supervision.
 I have the ability to perform the most difficult and complex Painter (Polyurea Operator/Inspector) duties including performing in process and final acceptance inspection of painting operations to include adherence of paint to specifications, mixtures, and application, conduct visual inspection of assemblies, parts, and components to determine if ready for paint, inspect damage to vehicle bodies. I'm skilled in making recommendations to both lower graded workers, and first level supervisors, and conducting off site visits to contractors to ensure final product meet inspection standards. I am considered an expert and work with a high degree of independence.
 I have the ability to perform the full range of Painter (Polyurea Operator/Inspector) duties including approving all quality Inspection points for various combat, tactical vehicles and secondary items, comprehending procedures that relate to mixture, use, and application of coating materials. I'm skilled in inspecting tools along with equipment such as mill gauge, spray guns, mixing pots, brushes, rollers, gaining devices, telescopic inspection mirrors, prevail bottles, and stencils. I work on my own initiative under general supervision subject to occasional inspection.
 I have the ability to perform common Painter (Polyurea Operator/Inspector) duties including applying coating materials to various vehicle assemblies or equipment. I can demonstrate mixing paint and how to inspect paint equipment and conduct final service preparation. I work under supervision of a work leader or journey-level employee who reviews my work for completion.
 I have the ability to perform simple Painter (Polyurea Operator/Inspector) duties including assisting with mixing paint to required blend of color specified or I have received training on basic painting tasks. I work under close supervision of a work leader or journey-level employee who observes tasks in progress and upon completion to make sure they are properly performed.
