- Designing, executing, and overseeing data analysis projects and studies that are necessary for mission-critical work; AND
- Researching and evaluating new procedures, laws, regulations, and analytic tools; AND.
- Communicating the status and results of data analysis activities and resource use through effective written and oral communication
