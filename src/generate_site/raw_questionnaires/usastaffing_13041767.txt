1.
From the description below select the one response that best describes how your experience/training meets the ability to do the work of a Heavy Mobile Equipment Mechanic, WG-5803-10 without more than normal supervision.

 I have overhauled and repaired major components of heavy equipment (graders, loaders, bulldozers, buses, etc.) and vehicles (passenger cars, pick-up trucks, transit vehicles, etc.). I have performed most of the following: repair/rebuild standard or automatic transmissions; repair differentials; overhaul or rebuild engines; install new crankshafts, timing chains/belts, and piston rings; remove power train assemblies and replace seals, snap ring bearings, and gears; troubleshoot and repair/replace hydraulic systems, units, and hoses; reassembling units; troubleshooting and replacing brake pads, rotors, drums (and turning rotors/drums); and replacing clutch assemblies. I have identified the type and extent of repair needed and performed tests requiring knowledge of relationships between mechanical and electrical units and systems. I have worked independently and on my own initiative.
 I have substantial experience performing repairs on heavy trucks, equipment, and automotive vehicles in a full service auto shop. I have replaced spark plugs, ignition points, condenser and set ignition timing; replaced water hoses and installed water pumps; replaced thermostats and heat sending units; and replaced brake pads, rotors, and drums. I have worked independently and on my own initiative.
 I have experience performing common tasks under general direction in a full automotive service shop on heavy trucks, equipment, and automotive vehicles. I have performed most of the following: replaced spark plugs, replaced water hoses, and installed water pumps; replaced brake pads, replaced accessory belts, and changed the oil on gas and diesel engines.
 I have some experience performing simple repairs on vehicles under direct supervision. I do not have the experience or on-the-job training to perform these tasks without more than normal supervision.
