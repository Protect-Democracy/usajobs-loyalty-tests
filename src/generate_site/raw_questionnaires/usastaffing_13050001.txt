You have identified three contributing factors: staffing shortages due to extended leave, an increase in inmates with learning disabilities requiring specialized instruction, and outdated assessment materials. The Union has also raised concerns about teacher workload. You need to present this information clearly and provide recommendations for improvement.

Which action represents the most effective and appropriate oral communication approach in this situation?

Your institution has limited funding, so you'll need to assess resource needs and prepare external grant proposals to support new initiatives. The Supervisor of Education has tasked you with developing a comprehensive approach that addresses mandatory literacy and GED requirements, expands vocational offerings, revitalizes evening/weekend programming based on population interests, and meets the specialized needs of both the geriatric and ESL populations-all while working within budget constraints.

Which action represents the most effective and appropriate planning and organizing approach in this situation?
 Conduct a comprehensive needs assessment of the entire inmate population including cultural, age-related, and educational requirements; design and implement an integrated program that includes mandatory literacy/GED services, new technology-based vocational training, culturally responsive evening/weekend activities, and specialized programming for geriatric and ESL students with complicated needs; assess all resource requirements and prepare detailed external funding requests/grant proposals to support the new initiatives; and modify the evening and weekend program schedule based on the population interest survey results.
 Develop a schedule that ensures all required education programs are offered including literacy, ESL, vocational training, occupational training, apprenticeship programs, job skill readiness assessment, and post-secondary education courses; work with your supervisor to plan and update the existing programs to incorporate some new vocational options; and establish the newly approved technology training program in collaboration with the recreation department.
 Provide oversight of the ongoing literacy and GED programs to ensure they continue operating effectively, establish education program objectives and curriculum for the existing literacy, ESL, vocational, and occupational programs; assist in surveying the population to gather additional information about improving course offerings; and work with teachers to develop new instructional techniques to improve student learning outcomes and test performance.
