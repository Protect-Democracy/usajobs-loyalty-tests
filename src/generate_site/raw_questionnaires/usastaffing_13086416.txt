(Screen-Out) The Maintenance Mechanic at the WG-09 level performs a variety of tasks often combining trade practices such as carpentry, masonry, painting, and roofing. Skilled preservation trade techniques are performed in an array of different work environments, involving historic and/or contemporary buildings, features, structures, or large exterior or interior objects. The work may also include elements of new construction and/or demolition. Typical work assignments include, but are not limited to, carpentry, masonry, painting, or roofing tasks where the incumbent is skilled in construction or repair of ornate or decorative building features and components of historic structures, and the altering or repairs to structural elements.
