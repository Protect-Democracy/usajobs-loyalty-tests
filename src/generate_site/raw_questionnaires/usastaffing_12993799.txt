I identify and prioritize work according to seasonal requirements and instruct lower graded workers on the appropriate use of tools and materials for projects and/or work assignments, and I have demonstrated experience in training lower grade workers and/or groups of volunteers in the following trade practices: landscape maintenance, maintenance and preservation of lawns, shrubs and trees. I have identified and diagnosed insect disease and cultural pressures. I have provided recommendations and/or applied proper chemical compounds for control measures, including biological controls. I operate gasoline and diesel-powered tractors up to 70 H.P, with towed or attached equipment such as rotary and flail mowers, augers, tillers, backhoe etc. I operate ½ ton and ¾ ton pickups, stake trucks, van trucks, platform trucks and dump trucks of an approximate GVW of not more than 32,000 pounds and perform operator maintenance on all vehicles operated. I am able to work independently based on my experience and can make sound decisions according to project plans, master landscape, and long-range plans following verbal and written instruction.
