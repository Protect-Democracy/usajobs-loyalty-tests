Grade Requirement GS-06 Health Technician: Experience : Candidates must possess at least 1 year of experience comparable to the next lower grade level which demonstrates the knowledge, skills, abilities, and other characteristics related to the duties of the position to be filled. This would be experience in operating equipment related to the particular specialization for which application is made or in related functional areas. Experience gained in the operation of equipment for animal diagnosis or treatment may be credited through this grade level. Qualifying experience includes but is not limited to: performing routine to moderately complex health technician and patient care duties in an outpatient or clinical care setting under standard supervision. I.E. obtaining patient health histories, vital signs, and diagnostic indicators (e.g., 12-lead EKG, point-of-care testing); collecting and preparing laboratory specimens; assisting licensed healthcare providers with examinations, routine clinical procedures, and minor surgical setups; maintaining equipment and infection control precautions; and providing basic mental health observation or crisis intervention support. Additionally, experience involves performing medical administrative tasks such as outpatient appointment scheduling, entering clinical reminders and notes in electronic health record (EHR) systems (e.g., CPRS, VISTA), and communicating effectively with patients and multidisciplinary clinical staff. OR; Education : Applicants may substitute education for the experience required for the GS-06 level. To qualify based on education for this grade level you must have at least 9 semester hours of graduate education, but only where it is directly related to the work of the position. OR; Combination : Applicants may also combine education and experience to qualify at this level. You must have a combination of specialized experience and education beyond the GS-05 requirement. Grade Requirement GS-07 Health Technician: Experience : Candidates must possess at least 1 year of experience comparable to the next lower grade level which demonstrates the knowledge, skills, abilities, and other characteristics related to the duties of the position to be filled. This would be experience in operating equipment related to the particular specialization for which application is made or in related functional areas. Experience gained in the operation of equipment for animal diagnosis or treatment may be credited through this grade level. Qualifying experience includes but is not limited to: performing advanced health technician duties with a high degree of independence in outpatient, specialty, or surgical care settings. Assisting licensed providers with complex procedures and specialized care; operating and troubleshooting specialized clinical equipment (e.g., telehealth peripherals, digital stethoscopes, dermato-cameras); collecting and processing complex biological specimens; performing advanced wound care, catheter insertion, and suture removal per orders; responding to emergent situations using BLS/AED protocols; and executing behavioral observation and prevention techniques (PMDB) in psychiatric or crisis situations. Additionally, experience includes performing comprehensive administrative and clinical operations, such as managing complex scheduling, verifying treatment eligibility, documenting detailed procedural and progress notes in EHR systems, and assisting with training or precepting clinical staff.
