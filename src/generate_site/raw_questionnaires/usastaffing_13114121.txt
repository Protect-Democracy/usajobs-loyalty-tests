 I have experience independently performing the complex tasks of a Woodworker (Blocker and Bracer), WG-4604-08. I am often consulted by other workers because of my expertise and skill in the construction of both standard and uniquely-shaped containers and my experience in performing packing and blocking for standard as well as hazardous and/or fragile cargo. I plan and lay out construction details and materials; modify or change existing plans or patterns to construct irregular shaped containers; make cuts for accurate dimension for close, weather resistant fits and to prevent movement. I construct wooden parts or sections such as gates, bins, bracing and dividers; install shock mounts and cradle or cushion fragile items. I secure hazardous and/or non-hazardous cargo inside or upon conveyances that have regular and rectangular shapes, and unobstructed cargo areas. Perform standard and specialized preservation and packing work using various protective/cushioning and bracing techniques. I use and maintain various types of hand and/or power tools, such as nail and staple guns; hand, radial, and circular saws; hammers, joiners, and squares, etc. My work is accomplished in a safe manner and I had no accidents on the job. I perform all duties in a safe manner, utilizing safety practices and procedures, and complying with established safety rules and regulations. My work is evaluated in terms of overall results achieved.
 I have experience performing the full range of tasks of a Woodworker (Blocker and Bracer) WG-4604-08. I plan, construct, and repair a full range of standardized shipping and storage containers; non-standard and uniquely-shaped shipping and storage containers; measure, develop and lay out changes to existing container plans; make standard joints and grooves; determine materials and methods required for container construction; determine wood and wood composite material strengths and nail holding and splitting tendencies; follow work orders, item specifications, illustrations, and blueprints. I perform preservation and packing work. I secure hazardous and/or non-hazardous cargo, using a variety of items and devices to accomplish secure blocking and bracing. I determine the weight supports of large, heavy, unbalanced, and irregularly shaped items to make and place supports to prevent such items from shifting during transit or storage. I use a variety of hand and power tools such as those listed in A above. I observe all safety regulations and guidelines.
 I have experience performing the common tasks of a Woodworker (Blocker and Bracer), WG-4604-08. I can accurately read a tape measure, follow basic blueprints to construct and assemble various types of shipping and storage containers, boxes and crates, and protective bracing and framing supports; select proper fasteners for routine work requirements such as the correct size and type of nails, bolts, and metal braces; perform repetitive and routine work
 I am able to perform simple wood working duties such as the following: building fences, framing, etc. I can tear apart items and repair by such as tightening and replacing nails, screws, broken boards, hardware, and other easy to replace parts. Load and unload materials, stack lumber, and other items.
