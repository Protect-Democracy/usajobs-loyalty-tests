Resource Utilization Duties: Reviews and analyzes computer-generated cost and budget reports on a monthly/ quarterly basis for accuracy; Verifies unit cost and other outcome variables; Analyzes the budget, manpower, space, and equipment resources projected to be available; Prepares monthly and quarterly cost summary reports; Performs cost feasibility studies and special projects; Validates externally prepared justifications; Trains facility staff in workload capture, cost accounting, and return on investment methodologies.
