Examples of this experience could include:
-Working with customers to provide guidance and resolve problems; OR
-Aiding the development of communication materials such as letters, presentations, training aids, and fact sheets; OR
-Gathering data to produce reports and queries from various systems or databases; OR
-Assisting with the preparation of data analysis (such as statistical summaries, comparisons, identification of trends and patterns, documenting process flows) and presentations for meetings and briefings that effectively and accurately display program information to individuals or groups.

You may qualify on a combination of education and experience.
