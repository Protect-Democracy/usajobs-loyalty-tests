1.
Do you have the ability to do the work of a Carpentry Worker without more than normal supervision in performing the full range of carpentry duties? 

 I am able to perform the most difficult and complex tasks of carpentry work that may include: performing mill work, constructing framing, and making items such as benches, partitions, frames and tables. I am consulted by other workers because of my expertise in carpentry operations that include the repairing of office-type furniture such as regluing loose joints and replacing shelving and slide mechanisms requiring close tolerance fit, structural soundness, and a finished appearance.  I am considered an expert in this field and work with a high degree of independence.
 I am able to perform the full range of tasks of carpentry work that may include: performing mill work, constructing framing, and making items such as benches, partitions, frames and tables. I am able to assist other workers with carpentry operations that include the repairing of office-type furniture such as regluing loose joints and replacing shelving and slide mechanisms requiring close tolerance fit, structural soundness, and a finished appearance. I independently resolve carpentry problems during the actual process. I work on my own initiative under general supervision subject to occasional inspection.
 I am able to perform common tasks of carpentry work which may include: performing mill work, constructing framing, and making items such as benches, partitions, frames and tables. I help other workers with carpentry tasks that include the repairing of office-type furniture such as regluing loose joints and replacing shelving and slide mechanisms requiring close tolerance fit, structural soundness, and a finished appearance. I work under close supervision of a work leader or journey-level employee who observes tasks in progress and upon completion to make sure they are properly performed.
 I am able to perform simple carpentry work tasks, or I have received training on this type of work.
 I do not have on-the-job training or experience in the area as described above.
