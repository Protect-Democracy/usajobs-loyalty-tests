Assessment 1
You are invited to complete the Accomplishment Record (AR) portion of your application. This assessment is designed to gather examples of your past experience that reflect the critical competencies required for the position.


What You Need to Do: Respond to three prompts. For each prompt: (1) Provide one specific example from your professional experience. (2) Address the situation and your responsibility, the actions you personally took, and the result. (3) Provide verification information, including the name, title, and email address of a reference who can verify this example.


Response Format: (1) Maximum length of 700 characters per response. (2) Write in full sentences in narrative form. (3) Organize your response in this order: Situation and your responsibility, actions you personally took, and the result.



Other Requirements: (1) Your AR responses must be written solely by you. Do not use AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT) or receive assistance from others in drafting or refining your narratives. (2) Do not include confidential, sensitive, or proprietary information. (3) Spell out technical acronyms or jargon on first use to ensure clarity for raters.



Tips for Success: (1) Choose a real, past example, not a hypothetical. (2) If describing a team effort, clearly explain your individual contributions. (3) Be specific about the actions you took (what you did and how) and the results you achieved.
