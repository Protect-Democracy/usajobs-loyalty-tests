Utility Systems Repairer-Operator performs work at the journeyman level completing the most complex and unusual tasks of a Utility Systems Repairer Operator. Independently oversee the operation, maintenance, and rehabilitation of water and/or wastewater treatment facilities, water distribution systems, and/or wastewater treatment collection systems. Oversees the operation, maintenance, and rehabilitation of potable water pumping, storage, transmission, treatment and local distribution systems, wastewater collection and pumping systems; reclaim water pumping, storage, transmission systems; chlorination systems. Oversees the operation, maintenance, and rehabilitation of water and wastewater treatment facilities. Independently diagnose and repair electrical parts for continuity, voltage drop, ground shorts, opens, and overheating.

 I am able to independently perform the most difficult and complex Utility Systems Repairer- Operator tasks listed above as well as lead and instruct others.
 I am able to perform the full range of Utility Systems Repairer- Operator tasks listed above with little or no supervision.
 I am able to perform the full range of Utility Systems Repairer- Operator tasks listed above under normal supervision.
 I am able to perform simple plumbing tasks. I know little about wastewater treatment and/or collection or water production/treatment/distribution.
 I do not have the experience or on-the-job training to perform these tasks.
