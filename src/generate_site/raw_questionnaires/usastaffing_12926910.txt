1.
Do you possess the Ability To Do The Work Of The Position Without More Than Normal Supervision?

(1) The incumbent must have a sound working knowledge of prosthetic work orders, physician prescriptions, utility work orders and warehousing operations, as well as knowledge of basic medical terminology, anatomy, physiology and materials, particularly those required in the care and treatment of patients related to durable medical equipment, wheelchairs and scooters.
(2) The incumbent will have a broad knowledge of the different types of manual and electrically powered wheelchairs, accessories, parts, complex rehabilitation seating and positioning equipment, and rehabilitative medical equipment and supplies available from contract wheelchair supply vendors and other durable medical equipment manufacturers and distributors.
(3) The incumbent will be experienced and skillful with the assembly, fitting, modification, and repair of rehabilitative equipment for Veterans with spinal cord injury and associated neurologic disorders.
(4) The incumbent will not only be knowledgeable of the Durable Medical Equipment products, but also astute in the understanding of how the appliances work; able to diagnose and provide solutions to common repairs and experienced with skillful repairs and modifications in unusual cases.
(5) The incumbent has a thorough understanding of the technical aspects in determining measuring selection, fitting, repair and modification of standard manual wheelchair and power wheelchairs.
(6) The incumbent is expected to demonstrate skillful assembly, fitting, modification, and repair of manual wheelchairs, power seat functions, 3 or 4-wheel scooters, electrical environmental control systems, augmentative communication devices to power and operate wheelchairs and posture fitting modifications.
(7) Knowledge of procedures for maintaining warehousing and storage of charging batteries, high.-cost materials, bulk supplies and hazardous and flammable materials. A thorough understanding of warehousing principles and theory will enhance the ability to design and maintain the numerous items stocked by the Service, including the methodology of electronic warehousing and manual records.
(8) Skills to make modifications to electrical, electronic and mechanical components of both power drive and manual wheelchairs, as required, to fit the special needs of individuals patients. Skill in the use of tools and equipment such as soldering irons, plies, screwdrivers, wire-cutters, test lamps, ammeters, ohmmeters another special tools alignment and test equipment found in the electrical mechanical wheelchair repair shop.
(9) Skill and ability to trace and repair the wiring used throughout a power wheelchair when no diagrams or schematics are available or if modifications to the system have been made, or the system altered in any fashion. Must be able to fabricate upholstery and special seating configurations when necessary to accommodate the patient's activity level.
