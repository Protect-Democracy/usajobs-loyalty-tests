Eligibilities
1.
Current Department of Army Civilian Employees Applying on Time-Limited Assignments
    
This job opportunity announcement is open to current, permanent Army employees that would occupy the position under a time-limited promotion, reassignment or change to lower grade not to exceed (NTE). You are eligible in this category if you fall into one of the following:

•  ARMY CIVILIAN EMPLOYEES SERVING ON A PERMANENT APPOINTMENT. You must submit a copy of your most recent SF-50, Notification of Personnel Action. Block 24 of your SF-50 must be a "1" or "2" AND block 34 must be a"1." Army employees can access their SF-50 at: MyBiz.
    
•  ARMY CIVILIAN EMPLOYEES SERVING ON AN EXCEPTED SERVICE VETERANS RECRUITMENT APPOINTMENT (VRA).  If you are eligible in this category, you are required to submit an SF-50 showing current Army Excepted Service VRA appointment (block 24 must be a "1" or "2" AND block 34 must be a"2.").
    
Supporting documentation: You are required to submit the documents which prove you are a current Department of the Army employee in one of the categories described above. To obtain a copy of SF-50s from employment with the Department of Defense, go to My Biz at https://compo.dcpds.cpms.osd.mil/.
    
Note: The "current Army employee" category does not include permanent Army National Guard employees in the competitive service.

I am highly skilled in conducting non-film radiographic testing on complex explosive munitions, projectiles, bombs, fuses, and motor tubes using high-energy Linear Accelerator X-ray machines (up to 9 MeV rating) and 120 to 600 kV X-ray tubes. As a certified NAS-410 Level 2 (or 3) radiographer, I have extensive experience performing final accept/reject determinations. I am skilled in calculating critical equipment settings, focal distances, and penetrameter tolerances, as well as utilizing Digital Radiography (DR) non-film systems and Computed Tomography (CT). I possess extensive written and oral communication skills, regularly briefing team members, upper management, and command on project status, causes of delays, and training requirements, while effectively resolving conflicts and complex technical problems. I am considered an expert and work with a high degree of independence.
 I have the ability to perform the full range of Non-Destructive Tester Leader duties. I am skilled in distributing daily work assignments, tracking team progress, guiding co-workers on work procedures and safety precautions, and communicating project status and delays to supervisors. I am skilled in setting up and operating standard 120 to 600 kV X-ray tubes and radiographic equipment to inspect ferrous and non-ferrous parts, components, and assemblies. As a certified NAS-410 Level 2 radiographer, I regularly perform accept/reject determinations. I am skilled in preparing, cleaning, and positioning test objects in fixtures; calculating standard equipment settings to obtain required resolution and image density; utilizing Digital Radiography (DR) non-film systems; and assisting with Computed Tomography (CT) scans. I possess strong communication skills to handle day-to-day team coordination, resolve minor work conflicts, and interact with management. I am considered an expert and work with a high degree of independence.

As a certified NAS-410 Level 2 radiographer, I am skilled in performing accept/reject determinations in accordance with technical manuals, blueprints, and established criteria.
