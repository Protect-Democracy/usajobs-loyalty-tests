Select the one statement that best describes your highest level of experience as it relates to your ability to perform the duties of the Heavy Mobile Equipment Mechanic, WG-5803-12.

 I have the ability to perform the most difficult and complex duties such as troubleshooting, repairing, overhauling and/or rebuilding major systems. Operating equipment such as diesel and gasoline engines ranging up to 1000 horsepower, hydramatic and cross-drive transmissions, and assembled power packs. I am skilled in performing repair or replacement as necessary ranging from minor adjustment to complete rebuild. I am considered an expert and work with a high degree of independence.
 I have the ability to perform the full range of operating equipment that has hydraulic, mechanical, electrical, and non- mechanical systems. Diagnosing causes of deficiencies and/or malfunction noticed during operational test and subsequent formal inspection. I am skilled in completing visually checks over vehicles to identify missing parts of deficiencies prior to release for inspection. I work on my own initiative under general supervision subject to occasional inspection.
 I have the ability to perform common duties such as performing pre-shop analysis of heavy mobile and engineering equipment to determine the type of maintenance needed. Test driving for correct operations and ensure proper preventive and safety maintenance is maintained. I work under normal supervision of a work leader or journey-level employee who observes tasks in progress and upon completion to make sure they are properly performed.
 I have the ability to perform simple duties such as installing decals, applicable kit items, and straps. I have received training on how to use tools such as micrometers, dial indicators, and dial bore gages.
