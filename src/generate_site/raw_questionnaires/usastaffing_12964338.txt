I am considered an expert and work with a high degree of independence.
 I have the ability to perform the full range of duties of a Utility Worker at a hydroelectric power plant, performing general support duties to include: (1) operating light motor vehicles and fork lifts; (2) using hand and power tools; (3) assisting with equipment repairs and maintenance; and (4) performing general laborer duties. I work on my own initiative under general supervision subject to occasional inspection.
 I have the ability to perform the common tasks of a Utility Worker in an industrial setting.
