The Core Focus of the ECQs are cross-cutting and should be addressed within your two-page resume.&nbsp; The following are the foundation for success in each of the ECQs below: Constitutional fidelity and service to the American people; Efficient resource use, cost-saving, process modernization; Technical excellence and high-quality performance; Team leadership, accountability, performance culture; and Meeting and exceeding mission-aligned goals. ECQ 1 - This core qualification encompasses the ability to uphold the Constitution and American governing ideals - a foundation for federal leadership. ECQ 2 - This core qualification involves the ability to demonstrate effective resource management, budget discipline, waste reduction, and leveraging technology or process improvements. ECQ 3 - This core qualification stresses the delivering of exceptional quality work through technical skill, knowledge, and reliable performance. ECQ 4 - This ECQ involves the ability to inspire and guide teams toward mission objectives while fostering accountability, performance, and adaptability. ECQ 5 - This core qualification involves consistently delivering outcomes aligned with organizational goals and the broader mission set by leadership. MANDATORY TECHNICAL QUALIFICATIONS:&nbsp; All candidates must address the following Mandatory Technical Qualifications within their two-page resume. Demonstrated expert knowledge of federal communications and media law, including the Communications Act of 1934, as amended, and the regulatory frameworks governing broadcast, cable, satellite, and emerging media services; and demonstrated ability to interpret and apply complex statutes, Commission rules, and judicial precedent to develop and implement legally sound, policy-driven regulatory actions. Demonstrated executive-level experience leading complex regulatory programs within a federal agency or comparable organization, including oversight of legal, policy, and operational functions related to media or telecommunications. Demonstrated experience in directing multidisciplinary teams, managing significant resources; establishing strategic priorities, advising senior leadership; and coordinating with internal and external stakeholder (e.g., Congress, industry, public interest groups and other government entities) to implement media-related policies and ensure effective development of programs and services.
