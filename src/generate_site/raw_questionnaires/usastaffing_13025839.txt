 I have the ability to oversee a team of maintenance supervisors (electrical, HVAC, and plumbing). I can delegate daily work orders to supervisors, prioritize facility upgrades to ensure code compliance, and manage budget allocations. I conduct site walks with supervisors to inspect repair quality, safety standards, and provide feedback.
 I have the ability to direct the activities of trade supervisors who lead electrical, plumbing, and HVAC workers. I provide oversight by reviewing work order completion reports and ensuring that preventative maintenance on utility systems is scheduled. I meet with the supervisors weekly to identify resource bottlenecks and provide necessary tools/materials.
 I have the ability to manage a facilities repair office/organization/department, providing direct oversight to HVAC and electrical supervisors. I can review project plans and inspect major work completions. I conduct toolbox talks with trade crews. I facilitate inter-trade coordination between plumbing and electrical teams to expedite utility system repairs.
 I have the ability to perform simple tasks related to various trades (electrical, plumbing, HVAC) regarding system maintenance, such as replacing filters, cleaning condensing and evaporative coils, lubing electrical and pump motors, checking refrigerant pressures, and cleaning condensation pans.
