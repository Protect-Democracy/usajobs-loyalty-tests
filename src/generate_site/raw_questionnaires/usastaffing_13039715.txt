Applicants must have at least one full year (52 weeks) of specialized experience which is in or directly related to the line of work of this position. Specialized experience is demonstrated experience in ALL of the following:
Conducting advanced static and dynamic malware analysis to identify malicious functionality, persistence mechanisms, execution flow, command-and-control behavior, and attacker tradecraft across enterprise systems.
Reverse engineering and analyzing malicious code, scripts, memory artifacts, and suspicious binaries to support incident response, forensic investigations, detection development, and threat attribution activities.
Analyzing structured and unstructured data sources, including logs from enterprise and custom applications, to detect, investigate, and support response to suspicious or malicious activity.
