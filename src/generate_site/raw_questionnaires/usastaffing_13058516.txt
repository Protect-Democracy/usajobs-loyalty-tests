The Career Transition Assistance Plan (CTAP) is an intra-agency program that helps surplus or displaced federal employees improve their chances of finding a new job in their agency, by giving them selection priority over other applicants, as long as they're qualified for the job.

To be eligible under CTAP you must meet all of the following:

4.
Are you an employee that was involuntarily separated from another agency due to a reduction-in-force (RIF)?

The Interagency Career Transition Assistance Plan (ICTAP) is an interagency program that helps "surplus" or "displaced" federal employees improve their chances of finding a new job at another agency (not their current or former agency), by giving these employees selection priority over other applicants from outside the agency.

You may be eligible for ICTAP if:
You are a current federal employee who meets the definition of a "surplus" or "displaced employee—you've received official notice that your job is no longer needed or that you will lose your job by Reduction-in-Force (RIF); AND
The agency you're applying to is accepting applications from outside of their workforce; AND
The job you're applying to is in the local commuting area; AND
You are applying to a position that does not have greater promotion potential than the position from which you were separated from; AND
You meet the qualifications and other requirements of the job you're applying for.
For example, if you work for the Department of Defense and receive a Reduction in Force (RIF) separation notice, you can apply for a competitive service job in the local commuting area at the Department of Justice.

To verify your eligibility: You must submit appropriate supporting documentation such as a Reduction in Force (RIF) separation notice to include your latest performance rating (if the notice does not have this information included, you must submit a copy of your most recent performance appraisal) and a copy of your latest Notification of Personnel Action (SF-50). For more information, review the USAJOBS Help Center.

This assessment measures the technical competencies required for the Factory Operational Accountant position. For each question, select the answer that best represents the most effective and appropriate approach. Only one answer per question will be scored.


1.
Your UNICOR remote factory location manufactures office furniture and has experienced significant job cost variances over the past three months. The Prime Business Manager has asked you to investigate why actual manufacturing costs are consistently exceeding standard costs by 15-20%. Your investigation reveals that raw material costs have been incorrectly allocated to overhead accounts rather than direct material accounts, prior period labor costs were never properly adjusted when pay rates changed, and some Buildings & Facilities (B&F) project costs that should have been transferred to the Salaries & Expenses (S&E) appropriation remain open in B&F. Additionally, management needs recommendations on pricing structures for upcoming contract bids and projected operating levels for the next quarter. You must research the issues, apply accounting principles and cost accounting standards, and provide solutions that ensure accuracy in manufacturing and job order costing.

Which action represents the most effective and appropriate application of accounting concepts to derive solutions in this situation?
 Review the job cost obligations for accuracy in accordance with written policies to ensure they were properly recorded, reconcile the current job cost variances from the standard costs by comparing actual to budgeted amounts, reconcile the general ledger account balances to the open item reports to identify discrepancies, and prepare the monthly balance and internal control check according to established procedures.
 Research the hierarchy of Generally Accepted Accounting Principles to determine the correct classification of the material and labor costs in the general ledger, process the job costing corrections in accordance with written guidelines to reallocate costs to proper accounts, research the prompt payment guidelines to ensure vendor payments are processed correctly, and provide staff assistance to the warehouse office on basic cost allocation procedures.
 Research the accounting issues causing the cost variances and present findings and solutions to management, research the prior period adjustments needed for the labor rate changes and properly identify the corrective action required to restate the affected job costs, transfer the B&F project costs to the S&E appropriation and close the B&F projects in accordance with written policies, and apply cost accounting standards to develop accurate pricing structure recommendations for management's contract bidding decisions.
 Calculate the percentage variance between actual and standard costs using the existing cost reports, notify the Prime Business Manager of the variance amounts, request guidance from Central Office on how to handle the discrepancies, and document the issues in the monthly report for review by senior management.
