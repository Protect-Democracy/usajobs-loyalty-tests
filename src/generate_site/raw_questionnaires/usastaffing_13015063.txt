1.
Specialized Experience:
To qualify for the GS-13 level you must possess the specialized experience equivalent to the GS-12 grade level in Federal service or public/private sector equivalent experience. For this position, specialized experience is defined as subject matter expert in homeland security intelligence and delivering intelligence training in support of DHS missions. Such experience may include:
Design, development and delivery of classroom, distance learning, and mobile training programs.
Develop and implement best practices in a training and curriculum development and management of educational programs and comply with accreditation requirements.
Applying quantitative and qualitative methods for the assessment and improvement of classroom and learning training programs and curriculum resources (e.g., needs analysis design/development, delivery, evaluation). 
Plan, organize, and implement programs, plans, and proposals involving substantial agency resources or that require extensive changes in established procedures.
Additional desired experience would be:
Serve as an expert-level instructor delivering all-source intelligence analysis training programs.
Serves as a subject matter expert (SME) on homeland-security all-source intelligence analysis.
Manages and administers training delivery for assigned programs including engagement with training customers, enrollment, compliance with Academy accreditation standards, and logistic and administrative support.
Conducts reviews and evaluations of training programs, concepts and materials to continuously improve training development and delivery.
Supports training initiatives in support of Department requirements. Executes complex and difficult assignments often functioning as part of a team in interagency efforts to maximize the utility of intelligence training across enterprise.
