Specialized experience is defined as demonstrated experience: 1) Serving as a "hands-on" technical lead supporting production IT systems by planning, installing, troubleshooting, and enhancing enterprise Local Area Network (LAN), Wide Area Network (WAN), Wireless Local Area Network (WLAN), and data and telecommunications networks to maintain reliable and efficient operations. 2) Planning, configuring, and troubleshooting Cisco-based network solutions by supporting core and distribution switches, routers, and firewalls/Firepower and assisting with Internet Protocol Security (IPSec) for Virtual Private Network (VPN), Multiprotocol Label Switching (MPLS), Virtual LANs (VLANs), Software-Defined Wide Area Network (SD-WAN), and routing protocols such as Border Gateway Protocol (BGP) and Enhanced Interior Gateway Routing Protocol (EIGRP) to ensure secure, stable, and efficient enterprise connectivity. 3) Resolving issues across Unified Communications, Voice over Internet Protocol (VoIP) technologies, and wireless systems by troubleshooting performance problems, supporting diagnostics, and helping sustain secure and stable communication services. 4) Supporting daily network and telecommunications operations by diagnosing and resolving service disruptions involving firewalls, VPNs, switches, routers, MPLS, VLANs, SD-WAN, VoIP, Unified Communications, and wireless infrastructure to maintain consistent availability of enterprise network and telecommunications services. 5) Resolving hardware and software failures or network outages, analyzing network disruptions using monitoring and packet-level diagnostic tools, and restoring services within Service Level Agreement response and resolution requirements. 6) Providing technical guidance on projects by supporting connectivity planning, documenting network requirements, coordinating with vendors, and ensuring network enhancements incorporate required security and privacy measures.
