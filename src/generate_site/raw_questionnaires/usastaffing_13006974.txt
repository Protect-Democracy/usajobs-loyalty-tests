1.
From the descriptions below, select the appropriate response based on your level of experience, education, and/or training, that you fully possess and that demonstrates your ability to perform Air Conditioning Equipment Mechanic duties without more than normal supervision.

 I have the ability to perform the most difficult and complex Air Conditioning Equipment Mechanic duties including working from blueprints, oral instructions and work orders to plan and lay out work; trace and locate defects to determine the type and extent of repair needed. Replace major portions of various cooling units, such as compressors, condensers, expansion valves, and thermostats, etc. Install, maintain, and repair a wide variety of air conditioning and refrigeration systems including circulating air and chilled water air conditioning systems, high, medium and low temperature refrigeration systems, and total environmental control systems. Troubleshoot a variety of large commercial and industrial heating, ventilation, air conditioning and refrigeration (HVAC&R) systems and supporting equipment. I am considered an expert and work with a high degree of independence.
 I have the ability to perform the full range of Air Conditioning Equipment Mechanic duties including assisting with the installation of commercial and industrial air conditioning and refrigeration equipment. Detecting leaks on equipment and making electrical connections. Trace and locate defects to determine the type and extent of repair needed. Plan work, select the proper tools and testing devices; perform visual, audible and mechanical checks for the proper temperature of conditioned spaces. I work on my own initiative under general supervision subject to occasional inspection.
 I have the ability to perform common Air Conditioning Equipment Mechanic duties including assisting with the installation of commercial and industrial air conditioning and refrigeration equipment. Perform routine tasks associated with the maintenance and limited repairs of industrial and commercial refrigeration and air conditioning units and equipment. Detect leaks on equipment and making electrical connections; working the refrigerant in accordance with EPA standards. I work under normal supervision of a work leader or journey-level employee who observes tasks in progress and upon completion to make sure they are properly performed.
 I have the ability to perform basic or simple tasks associated with the work of an Air Conditioning Equipment Mechanic recognizing and obtaining the appropriate tools for the trade to assist a journey worker. Holding parts to assist in assembly and cleaning up the work area as instructed, but do not possess the experience listed in A, B, or C above.
