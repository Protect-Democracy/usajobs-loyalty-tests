 I have the ability to perform the most difficult and complex assistance in operating a variety of drill rigs, such as hydrostatically driven geotechnical drills (Gus Pech), employing complex drilling techniques. I have experience in the following: operating power auger drill rigs designed for shallow-depth drilling to explore various geologic areas; preparing for drilling jobs by aligning the rig and installing proper bits and devices; operating control levers of a drill rig under normal drilling conditions; drilling in various types of geologic formations, such as clay, sand or silt; performing field maintenance of drill rig equipment such as the drive shaft, cylindrical buckets, related bits, pumps, and motors. I am considered an expert and work with a high degree of independence.
 I have the ability to assist with the full range of drilling techniques on mechanically driven geotechnical drill rigs, such as CME, Diedrich, or Mobile. I have experience in at least 3 of the following: assisting in core drilling operations; assists in the setting up and operation of auger, cable tool and rotary drilling equipment to obtain samples of soil, rock, etc., for field classification and/or laboratory analysis; assisting in setting up equipment such as leveling machine and setting up mast on drill rig; assembling and disassembling drill rods and core barrels using wrenches and other tools; assisting in removing samples from sample barrels; assisting in maintenance of drilling equipment by cleaning tools and equipment and oiling and greasing parts. I work on my own initiative under general supervision subject to occasional inspection.
 I have the ability to perform some tasks including assisting with the common drilling techniques on mechanically driven geotechnical drill rigs. Under supervision, I have assisted a journeyman worker in using drilling equipment, drilling techniques and procedures to ensure proper site modification prior to drilling, proper equipment and materials are available for the job. I have the ability to repair, operate, maintain, and perform routine repairs of drill rig equipment. I work under supervision of a work leader or journey-level employee who reviews my work for completion.
 I have the ability to perform common assistance for drilling techniques. Under supervision, I have assisted as a helper worker in using drilling equipment.
