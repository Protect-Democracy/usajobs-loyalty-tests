QUALIFICATION REQUIRMENTS : To qualify for this position, you must meet the qualification requirements outlined below: GS-08 SPECIALIZED EXPERIENCE: You must have one year of specialized experience at a level of difficulty and responsibility equivalent to the GS-7 grade level in the Federal service. Specialized experience for this position must include: Providing technical guidance, answering employee questions, and conducting classroom, structured, and on-the-job training on work procedures, program requirements, system usage, and consistent application of policies using resources such as Internal Revenue Manual (IRM), Servicewide Electronic Research Program (SERP), and local procedures. Reviewing completed and in-process case-related work for accuracy, completeness, and compliance with policies, procedures, quality standards, and program requirements. Amending or rejecting work that does not meet standards, providing corrective guidance, and referring recurring or unresolved performance issues to the supervisor. Balancing, monitoring, and reporting on workload to ensure assignments are completed timely, equitably, and in accordance with established priorities, workflow, production requirements, and deadlines. Using workload tools such as the Automated Age Listing (AAL), or comparable systems, to track aging inventory, monitor assignments, identify backlogs, and adjust work as needed. Using IRS systems, research tools, and guidance resources to provide technical instruction, resolve employee questions, support case processing, and ensure consistent application of procedures. Systems include Integrated Data Retrieval System (IDRS), Account Management Services (AMS), E-file User Portal (EUP), Internal Revenue Manual (IRM), Servicewide Electronic Research Program (SERP), Report Generation Software (RGS), Finesse, Verint, Correspondence Examination Automation Support (CEAS) Assisting internal and external customers, employees, and management by responding to inquiries, clarifying issues, conducting inbound or outbound communications, providing information needed to resolve cases or transactions, and identifying recurring issues or process improvements.
