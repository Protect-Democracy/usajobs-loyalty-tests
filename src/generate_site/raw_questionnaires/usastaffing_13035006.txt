Select the one statement which best describes the highest level of experience you possess and which demonstrates your ability to perform the duties of a Maintenance Mechanic, WG-4749-10 without more than normal supervision. Note: Your resume must support the response you select.
 I am able to perform complex Maintenance Mechanic tasks to include performing sheet metal mechanic, carpentry, welding, and/or masonry work in constructing, installing, maintaining, repairing, and/or modifying structures, equipment, and systems. Visiting worksite to analyze and determine scope of the work to be done when planning repair and modification projects and make changes to job layout based on the on-site inspection. Reviewing work order requirements reflecting structures to be constructed, installed or repaired and plan and lay out work from blueprints, sketches, drawings, specifications, and/or work orders with a high degree of independence. Provide full performance level sheet metal mechanic, carpentry, and masonry work in constructing, installing, maintaining, repairing, and modifying structures and items requiring close tolerance fit, structural soundness, fine finishes, and decorative effects.
 I am able to perform full range Maintenance Mechanic tasks to include performing sheet metal mechanic, carpentry, welding, and/or masonry work in constructing, installing, maintaining, repairing, and/or modifying structures, equipment, and systems. Visiting worksite to analyze and determine scope of the work to be done when planning repair and modification projects and make changes to job layout based on the on-site inspection. Reviewing work order requirements reflecting structures to be constructed, installed or repaired and plan and lay out work from blueprints, sketches, drawings, specifications, and/or work orders on my own initiative under limited supervision subject to occasional inspection.
 I am able to perform common Maintenance Mechanic tasks. I have to ability to perform sheet metal and carpentry/masonry work in constructing, installing, maintaining, repairing, and/or modifying equipment, systems, and structures. I work under the close supervision of a journeyman Mechanic who observes tasks in progress and upon completion to make sure they are properly performed.
 I am able to perform simple Maintenance Mechanic tasks. I have basic knowledge of performing carpentry and roofing work in constructing, installing, maintaining, repairing, and/or modifying equipment, systems, and structures.
