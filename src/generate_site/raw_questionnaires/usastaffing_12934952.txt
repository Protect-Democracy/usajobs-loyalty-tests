Incident management and root cause analysis processes within enterprise IT environments are primarily intended to:
 Assign fault to specific employees involved in system outages.
 Reduce system downtime by identifying underlying causes of failures and preventing recurrence.
 Provide documentation required for vendor contracts.
 Replace enterprise security monitoring systems.
