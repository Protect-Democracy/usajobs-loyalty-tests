SPECIALIZED EXPERIENCE GS-14: To be eligible for this position at this grade level, you must have one (1) year of specialized experience at a level of difficulty and responsibility equivalent to the GS-14 grade level in the Federal service.
Specialized experience for this position includes:

Experience applying analytical and evaluative methods to assess workforce development, training, succession planning, or human resources programs and develop recommendations to improve program performance and organizational effectiveness.
Experience applying project and program management principles to plan, coordinate, implement, monitor, and evaluate workforce development, training, or human resources initiatives, including the development of project plans, milestones, implementation schedules, and performance measures.

Experience preparing and presenting briefings, reports, analyses, and recommendations to management officials regarding program activities, organizational initiatives, workforce development strategies, or human resources program operations.
Experience conducting program evaluations, staffing analyses, workforce analyses, or organizational assessments to identify trends, assess program effectiveness, and recommend corrective actions or process improvements.
Experience coordinating with management officials, program stakeholders, and cross-functional organizations to implement workforce development, training, succession planning, or human resources initiatives and resolve program issues.
Experience preparing written analyses, program documentation, operational procedures, implementation plans, and technical recommendations to support program execution, policy implementation, and management decision-making.
