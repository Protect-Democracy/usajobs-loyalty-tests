Assessment 1
1.
Basic Requirement
To qualify for the Contract Specialist, GS-1102-13 position, choose the option below that best describes your background:
 I have at least 4 years of experience in contracting or similar roles, including at least 1 year of specialized experience at the next lower grade. I also have a bachelor's degree with at least 24 semester hours in subjects like accounting, business, finance, law, contracts, purchasing, economics, management, marketing, quantitative methods, or related fields.
 I am exempt from this policy because I was a federal employee in a 1102 position as of January 1, 2000. This exception also applies if I held the same grade in the same agency, or in two other agencies, and met the specialized experience requirement. However, to be promoted to a higher grade, I must still meet the basic and specialized experience requirements unless I receive a waiver.
 I do not have the required training, education, or experience listed in the options above.


2.
GS-13: Select the option that best describes your professional experience. To qualify, your resume must demonstrate at least one year of full-time work experience at or above the GS-12 grade level. Your experience should reflect work that is comparable in complexity, scope, and level of independence to the examples provided below. Prior federal experience is not necessary to meet the specialized experience requirements.

Specialized experience includes:  
Developing acquisition strategies and solicitation documents to support the planning, competition, and award of contracts for services, or professional support requirements.
 Evaluating technical and price proposals to determine responsiveness, assess cost and price reasonableness, and support sound award recommendations.
Negotiating contract terms, conditions, and pricing to reach agreements that reflect program requirements and ensure best-value outcomes for the organization.
Administering awarded contracts-including monitoring performance, identifying issues, recommending modifications, and closing out contracts upon successful completion of the contract and payment of all outstanding obligations-to ensure contractor compliance with requirements, schedules, and performance standards.
 I have at least one year of full-time experience doing similar work as described above, at a level as complex and independent as a GS-12 federal position or higher. I know that I need to clearly show this experience in my resume, and I must include my work hours per week or specify if the job was full-time, part-time, or intermittent.
 I do not have at least one year of specialized experience equivalent to the GS-12 federal level or higher, as described above.
