3.
How is the FMS Admin surcharge collected?

 The FMS Admin surcharge is always collected in full upon case closure.
 For all LOAs, 50% is collected in the initial deposit, and the remainder is collected upon final delivery.
 For LOAs generating $30,000 or less, 100% is collected in the initial deposit. For LOAs generating over $30,000, 35% is collected in the initial deposit, with the rest collected as articles and services are delivered.
 The surcharge is paid incrementally in equal installments over the life of the case, regardless of the amount.
