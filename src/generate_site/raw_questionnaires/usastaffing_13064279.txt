INSTRUCTIONS: Thank you for your interest in a Air Conditioning Equipment Mechanic (Motor Vehicle Operation) position with the Department of the Army. We will evaluate your resume and your responses to this assessment questionnaire to determine if you are among the best qualified for this position. 1.
From the descriptions below, select the appropriate response based on your level of experience, education, and/or training, that you fully possess and that demonstrates your ability to perform Air Conditioning Equipment Mechanic (Motor Vehicle Operation) duties without more than normal supervision.
 I have the ability to perform the most difficult and complex installation, troubleshooting, overhaul, and repair tasks on large-scale industrial heating, ventilation, air conditioning, and refrigeration (HVAC/R) systems. I am skilled in diagnosing elusive mechanical, electrical, and pneumatic malfunctions in centrifugal, absorption, vapor-compression, and multi-zone chilled water systems; balancing intricate automatic control devices, thermostats, pressurestats, and dual-remote thermostatic controls; dismantling, machining, and rebuilding open and hermetic compressors, pumps, impellers, and chillers; fitting precision crankshafts, connecting rods, bearings, and bushings; and performing emergency recovery and charging of diverse refrigerant types. I am considered an expert and work with a high degree of independence.
 I have the ability to perform the full range of routine installation, troubleshooting, maintenance, and repair on standard commercial and industrial air conditioning, heating, and refrigeration systems. I am skilled in locating common mechanical and electrical malfunctions in standard automatic controls, thermostats, and switches; disassembling, repairing, and reassembling system components such as pumps, evaporators, receivers, and compressors; replacing worn belts, pulleys, and filters; testing systems for leaks and operating pressures; charging and recovering refrigerants in compliance with EPA mandates; and operating forklifts and medium-duty utility vehicles to transport parts and equipment. I work on my own initiative under general supervision subject to occasional inspection
 I have the ability to perform common tasks including routine preventive maintenance, basic troubleshooting, and minor component replacement on standard air conditioning, heating, and refrigeration equipment. I can assist with the disassembly and cleaning of pumps, evaporators, and compressors; replace worn drive belts, pulleys, and air filters; connect manifold gauges to check system operating pressures; perform basic leak testing using electronic detectors or soap solutions; assist with the recovery and charging of refrigerants under direct instruction; and operate light utility vehicles to move tools and parts. I work under normal supervision of a work leader or journey-level employee who observes tasks in progress and upon completion to make sure they are properly performed.
 I have the ability to perform simple manual labor tasks, tool handling, and basic shop housekeeping under direct, step-by-step instructions, or I have received training on basic refrigeration theory, the proper selection and use of basic hand tools, safety regulations for handling refrigerants, and elementary electrical safety.
