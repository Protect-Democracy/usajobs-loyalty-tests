ABILITY TO DO THE WORK OF A MAINTENANCE WORKER (TRAILS) WITHOUT MORE THAN NORMAL SUPERVISION (SCREEN OUT) from the descriptions below, please choose the best response to describe your ability to perform maintenance worker (trails) work. You must document your experience in your resume.
 I have the ability to perform the most difficult and complex maintenance worker (trails) duties. This includes construction of multi-use bridges, stone staircases, multi-tier retaining walls, causeways, and reroutes. I have utilized advanced rigging techniques using block, tackle and winch systems. I have used chainsaws to fall hazard trees, remove root balls, and clear fallen trees from trails. I have led others in the performance of such tasks as a regular part of my duties. I have extensive experience in trail maintenance and can operate and repair a variety of tools and equipment related to the tasks above. I take initiative to solve complex problems and can accomplish the most complex work assignments.
 I have the ability to perform the full range of maintenance worker (trails) duties. This includes construction and maintenance of foot bridges, stone steps, multi-tier walls, water bars, and causeways. I have utilized advanced rigging techniques using block, tackle and winch systems. I have used chainsaws to fall trees and clear fallen trees from trails. I have led others in the performance of such tasks as a regular part of my duties. I work on my own initiative under general supervision subject to occasional inspection.
 I have the ability to perform intermediate maintenance worker (trails) duties. This includes construction and maintenance of checks, single-tier walls, water bars, and causeways. I have utilized basic rigging techniques using block, tackle and winch systems. I have used chainsaws to fall trees and clear fallen trees from trails. I have led others in the performance of such tasks occasionally as part of my duties. I have completed these tasks under normal supervision.
 I do not have the abilities described above. I have performed basic laborer tasks in support of trails or other maintenance work.
