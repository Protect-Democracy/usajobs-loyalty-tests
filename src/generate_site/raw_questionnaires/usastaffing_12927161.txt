Assessment 1
For the GS-15, you must have one year of specialized experience at a level of difficulty and responsibility equivalent to the GS-14 grade level in the Federal service. Specialized Experience for this position includes:
- Applying results-driven methodologies to elevate an organization's data maturity, including establishing a unified data lexicon and reference data standards, and developing and implementing enterprise data quality and metadata management policies; AND
- Demonstrating technical expertise across data analytics, data architecture, data integration, including experience with Structured Query Language (SQL), ETL/ELT pipelines, and business intelligence software; AND
- Providing leadership to cultivate and sustain a data driven culture through development and implementation of enterprise data strategies, data governance frameworks and/or AI governance models.
