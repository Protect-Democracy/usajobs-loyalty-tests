Applicant Accomplishment Record Instructions - As the next step in the application process, you are invited to complete the Accomplishment Record (AR) portion of your application. This assessment is designed to gather examples of your past experience that reflect critical competencies required for the position. The AR is based on Selective Evaluation (SE) statements that were developed and validated by Subject Matter Experts (SMEs) currently serving in the same role. These prompts are tailored to assess performance relevant to the responsibilities and qualifications at the GS-13 level.



What You Need to Do - You will be asked to respond to several prompts, each representing a key competency (e.g., scientific writing, stakeholder engagement, analytical review). For each: - Provide one specific example from your professional experience. Use the guiding questions to help shape your response but you do not need to answer each question individually. Your response should be a narrative that clearly communicates what you did, how you did it, and what the outcome was.




Response Format - Word Limit: Up to 500 words per response. Style: Use full sentences and narrative form; bullet points are discouraged. Structure: We recommend using the STAR format (Situation, Task, Action, Result) to organize your response clearly and effectively.




Important Notes - Your responses must be supported by your resume. Ensure that any accomplishments or roles you describe are reflected in your work history. Responses will be evaluated for clarity, relevance, and level of complexity aligned with the GS-13 position. You will be scored based on the quality of your examples, not the quantity of tasks you mention. Do not include confidential or proprietary information in your responses. Tips For Success - Choose real, past examples-avoid hypothetical scenarios. Focus on your individual contributions, even if you worked as part of a team. Be as specific as possible when describing your actions and their impact. Think about the results: What changed, improved, or was accomplished because of your efforts?
