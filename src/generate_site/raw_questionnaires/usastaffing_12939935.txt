 A. I have the ability to perform the most difficult and complex Tools and Parts Attendant duties which include interpreting and applying warehouse plans and methods to perform warehousing techniques such as receiving, storing, issuing and re-warehousing materials. I am skilled in determining storage identification or stock location methods considering factors such as temperatures, humidity, height and weight limits, space availability and turnover. I am considered an expert and work with a high degree of independence.
 B. I have the ability to perform the full range of Tools and Parts Attendant duties which includes interpreting and applying warehouse plans and methods to perform warehousing techniques such as receiving, storing, issuing and re-warehousing materials. I am skilled in utilizing a matching process to identify and select items for issue. I work on my own initiative under general supervision subject to occasional inspection.
 C. I have the ability to perform common Tools and Parts Attendant duties such as interpreting and applying warehouse plans and methods to perform warehousing techniques such as receiving, storing, issuing and re-warehousing materials. I can check numbers and names of items on container labels to receiving documents. I work under normal supervision of a work leader or journey-level employee who observes tasks in progress and upon completion to make sure they are properly performed.
 D. I have the ability to perform simple Tools and Parts Attendant duties such as interpreting and applying warehouse plans and methods to perform warehousing techniques such as receiving, storing, issuing and re-warehousing materials or I have received training on use of basic hand tools.
 E. I do not possess the abilities described above.
