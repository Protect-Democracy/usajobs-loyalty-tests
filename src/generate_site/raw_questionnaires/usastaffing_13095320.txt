Carefully read the following descriptions below. Select the one response below that best describes your qualifications for this position at the GS-11 grade level.  I possess one (1) full year of specialized experience equivalent to the GS-09 level in the Federal service that has equipped me with the particular knowledge, skills, and abilities to perform successfully the duties of the position, and that is typically in or related to the work of this position. Examples of specialized experience would typically include, but are not limited to: Establish and maintain relationships with Veteran to support successful goal of competitive employment in community job settings. Assesses training needs to meet client's vocational goals. Conduct job development, job search, and job carving activities to secure employment opportunities tailored to each Veteran’s strengths, interests, and abilities. Provides counseling to allow treatment to continue on schedule. Exercise sound judgment and problem-solving skills in working with employers to ensure successful employment for the Veterans and the employer. Maintain employer contacts based on individual Veteran's interest. Develop strategies that motivate Veterans to actively pursue employment opportunities and address concerns related to benefits. NOTE: Experience must be fully documented on your resume and must include job title, duties, month & year starting and ending dates AND hours worked per week.
