In making your response, consider that your past and/or current training/experience must have provided the knowledge, skill, and ability to perform as a boiler plant operator: Applying knowledge of the principles of boiler plant, chiller plant, utility systems, operation, and maintenance sufficient to locate and check for high and low pressure, water temperature and pressure and proper gas and oil pressure and take appropriate action if findings are not within specifications. Working knowledge of the Direct Digital Control (DDC) computer system sufficient to identify problems and report to the supervisor or operator in charge for corrective action. Skill in using generalized hand and power tools common to all trades sufficient to use tools such as a pipe threading machine and wheel and bearing pullers.
