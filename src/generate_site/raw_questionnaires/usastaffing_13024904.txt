 Review the budget for unused funds and coordinate with the relevant MSC to provide the product or service, since the customer is always right.
 Conduct market research to find alternative sources and provide the customer recommendations on where best to procure the product/service.

7.
This role may require tracking data and metrics to monitor progress on projects, availability of materiel, or other factors. How best should you track this data?

 Develop trackers for your personal visibility. Use information you can independently validate or have primary sources can verify for you, as enterprise systems are often out of date.
 Use tools like EBS, QLIK, or the Enterprise Dashboard to collect information. If tools do not exist in these systems, work with stakeholders and developers to explore creation of viable tools.
 Use GenAi to monitor real time status, feeding in any starting information. Ask others on the project to feed supporting information into the same tool so it has as much data as possible for anybody who wants to pull status.
 Act as, or appoint, a dedicated person to maintain status. Determine a solution early to ensure information is consolidated, like a master spreadsheet or single email thread so it can be scrolled through and reviewed, as needed.

 As a DLA Liaison Officer, you should be able to bank on your extensive understanding of DLA policies and should be able to deconflict to suit DLA’s position, ensuring Warfighter Always.
 As a senior logistics analyst and upstanding Federal employee, your sense of right and wrong should make it clear what action to take.
 Consult relevant offices like General Counsel and Legislative Affairs to determine the best way forward. Work with stakeholders and leadership to align policy and act within the law.
 Inform the customer of the conflict and recommend they modify their policy to align with DLA’s position. Provide suggestions on how best to avoid the conflict without raising any suspicions.
