The contractor is aggressively pushing for an expedited negotiation schedule, and the procuring contracting officer (PCO) is requesting your team's finalized report by the end of the week. How do you address this situation?

 Approve the POM as written to support the expedited schedule, but document the risk associated with the escalation rate in the report so the PCO is aware prior to opening negotiations.
