 I have successfully completed a course of study leading to a bachelor's or higher degree that included a major field of study in biology, ecology, environmental science, natural resource management, botany, chemistry, agriculture, or related disciplines appropriate to the position that MUST include: 1) At least 6 semester hours in aquatic subjects such as limnology, ichthyology, fishery biology, aquatic botany, aquatic fauna, oceanography, fish culture, or related courses in the field of fishery biology; AND 2) At least 12 semester hours in the animal sciences in such subjects as general zoology, vertebrate zoology, comparative anatomy, physiology, entomology, parasitology, ecology, cellular biology, genetics, or research in these fields.  I have at least one full year of specialized experience comparable in scope and responsibility to the GS-09 grade level in the Federal service (obtained in either the public or private sectors). To be creditable, specialized experience must have been equivalent to at least the next lower grade level in the normal line of progression for the occupation in the organization. Qualifying specialized experience is: 1) Planning and conducting field monitoring or biological surveys for aquatic fish species, including collecting, documenting, and managing scientific data using established protocols. 2) Designing and implementing fisheries conservation measure projects, including planning and conducting monitoring, early detection, rapid response, suppression, and habitat or species conservation activities; coordinating with partner organizations; and evaluating results to support adaptive management of aquatic resources. 3) Analyzing biological or environmental data and preparing technical reports, summaries, or recommendations to support natural resource management decisions. 4) Coordinating field activities with multiple partners, including Federal, Tribal, state, or local agencies, contractors, or research organizations, to accomplish shared resource management objectives. 5) Operating and maintaining field equipment, watercraft, vehicles, or specialized fisheries sampling gear while conducting fieldwork in remote or challenging environments and following established safety procedures. You must include hours worked per week.

You must include transcripts.
 I have successfully completed a combination of education (only excess credits beyond 36 credits of graduate education are creditable) and experience as described above which together equals 100% of the requirement.
