Assessment 1
QUALIFICATION REQUIREMENTS:
Specialized Experience GS-15:
You must have one (1) year of specialized experience at a level of difficulty and responsibility equivalent to the GS-14 grade level in the Federal service. Specialized experience for this position must include:
-    Maintaining technical authority and competence within assigned areas, subject matters, and/or programs. 
-    Directed others to implement new processes or programs after familiarizing and gaining proficiency with them.  
-    Negotiating with management to accept and implement recommendations where proposals involve organizational resources or updates to established procedures.
-    Leveraging organizational and management systems to analyze divergent programs to develop processes that impact organizational workflow.
-    Coordinating assignments internally and between organizational areas/divisions, including special cross-functional programs and projects as a principal advisor.
-    Providing technical guidance and advice on novel, controversial, or complex tax administration issues. 
-    Conducting the final review and approval for management on technical projects, policies, or processes that require executive-level approval.
-    Representing within RAAS and with other IRS business units on technical, administrative, and operational matters.
-    Adjust to shifting priorities while maintaining focus on commitments.
