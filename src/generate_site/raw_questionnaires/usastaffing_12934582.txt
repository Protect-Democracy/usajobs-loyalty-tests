These Knowledge, Skills, and Abilities (KSAs) reflect the critical competencies needed for successful performance in this position:

GS-11 KSAs:
Skill in enforcing federal, state, and local laws, regulations, relevant court decisions that relate to public lands.
Knowledge of methods of investigation, obtaining and maintaining a chain of custody, interviewing and interrogation techniques. Skill in applying law enforcement patrol techniques and practices.
Ability to detect, prevent, or curtail illegal and prohibited activities of a potentially destructive nature to public lands and their resources.
Ability to interact with others in a professional manner to address a variety of client groups on law enforcement program objectives.
GL-09 KSAs:
Skill in the full range of advanced law enforcement techniques and procedures: including patrol operations, law enforcement, and criminal investigations.
Knowledge of federal, state, and local laws, regulations, relevant court decisions that relate to the management of public lands and their natural, cultural. and human resources.
Skill in managing and resolving conflicts, grievances, confrontations, or disagreements in a constructive manner to minimize negative personal impact.
Skill in working and dealing with people and in establishing and maintaining close working relationships with other agencies and other law enforcement authorities in neighboring jurisdictions.
GL-07 KSAs
Knowledge of the rules of evidence and methods used to maintain the evidence chain of custody (e.g., Federal Rules of Criminal Procedure) and of the elements needed to establish probable cause and reasonable suspicion sufficient to conduct searches and make arrests and seizures under the various laws and regulations enforced.
Knowledge of investigative principles and techniques sufficient to perform investigations, identify methods and patterns of criminal operations, use available sources of information, interviewing, and interrogation techniques.
Ability to communicate orally with a variety of audiences including agency employees, the public, and other agencies
Ability to communicate in writing with a variety of audiences including agency employees, the public, and other agencies.
