Assessment 1
QUALIFICATION REQUIRMENTS: To qualify for this position, you must meet the qualification requirements outlined below:

SPECIALIZED EXPERIENCE FOR IR-01:
You must have one (1) year of specialized experience at a level of difficulty and responsibility equivalent to the next lower level within the payband or GS grade in the normal line of progression in the Federal service that demonstrates the required managerial skills and technical competence required to perform the job. To be qualified for this position your experience must include:
Experience advocating for clients to effect resolution of tax controversy matters;
Experience applying tax laws demonstrated by career experience in accounting, financial or legal management positions or served in a management position that includes tax administrative responsibilities;
Experience working with operating policies, procedures, and processes of a large organization;
Experience collaborating with multiple organizations or stakeholders to resolve complex issues, negotiate solutions, implement program initiatives, or achieve organizational objectives
Experience communicating different tax issues, policies, practices and laws in a variety of situations and different geographic areas, including advocating for a tax position that may be considered contrary to the traditional tax interpretation;
Experience evaluating complex issues with consideration for taxpayer rights, organizational objectives, social and economic impacts, congressional interests, and public perception.
