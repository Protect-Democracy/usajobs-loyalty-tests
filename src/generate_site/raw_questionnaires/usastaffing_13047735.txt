For this position, specialized experience is defined as the following: Using data analysis and statistical modeling to translate complex information in concise visualizations and recommendations. Applying broad mathematical, statistical, econometric and other scientific methods to develop key performance indicators and metrics. Developing and improving organizational program analysis through quantitative methods. Evaluating technologies and systems and recommending enhancements to optimize effectiveness, discontinuance of development, or new options for future deployment.
