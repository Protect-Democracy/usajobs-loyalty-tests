21.
Work in this position is both inside and outside in all kinds of weather. When inside, they are frequently exposed to drafts, changing temperatures, and noise which is difficult to talk above. When outside, they sometimes work in bad weather, in mud or snow, and in wet or icy areas. Both inside and outside, workers are exposed to irritations and discomfort from dust, dirt, noise, grease, heat, and fumes. They typically work on parts and systems, which are dirty, oily or greasy.
