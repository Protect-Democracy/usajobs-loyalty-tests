SPECIALIZED EXPERIENCE:
GS-9 Level: You must have one year of specialized experience at a level of difficulty and responsibility equivalent to the GS-7 grade level in the Federal service. Specialized experience for this position includes:
Dealing with Business Operating Division policies, and functional responsibilities in the Telephone Systems Analyst area to assist in analyzing problems and provide solutions;
Utilizing work processes, programs, and functions, to identify error trends in the day-to-day workflow;
Using oral and written communications techniques to assist in the preparation of routine responses and to coordinate data and information;
Working with internal and external automated systems and controls to assist with training;
Utilizing Microsoft Excel calculations to prepare a report;
OR Two full academic years of progressively higher-level graduate education in any field of study from an accredited college or university that demonstrates the knowledge, skills, and abilities necessary to do the work.

OR LL.B or J.D., if related.
OR You may qualify by a combination of experience and education.

GS-11 Level: You must have one year of specialized experience at a level of difficulty and responsibility equivalent to the GS-9 grade level in the Federal service. Specialized experience for this position includes:
