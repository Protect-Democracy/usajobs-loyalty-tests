2.
From the descriptions below, select the ONE response that BEST describes the education and/or specialized experience that you possess that demonstrates your ability to perform as a GS-09 Data Scientist.
 I have at least one full year of specialized experience equivalent to the GS-07 level in Federal service. Specialized experience is defined as experience 1.) Creating and modeling data analysis and statistical trends using data pipelines which includes data cleaning, feature engineering, design and implementation of machine learning models, including generative AI and agentic AI solutions, to support projects in Amazon Web Services (AWS) and Microsoft 365 (M365) environments. 2.) Creating visualizations for data analysis and findings using business intelligence tools, Link Analysis tools, and Machine Learning (ML) services such as Microsoft PowerBI, ArcGIS Knowledge, ArcGIS Enterprise, and Amazon SageMaker. 3.) Developing or optimizing SQL, Python, and R code related to (ML) models, queries, or database operations for efficient data access, processing, and reporting. 4.) Assisting with technical documentation by creating data dictionaries, methodologies, and utilizing version control for traceability of the entire lifecycle of data and analytical models.
 Education: Master's or equivalent graduate degree or 2 full years of progressively higher level graduate education leading to such a degree in Mathematics, statistics, computer science, data science or field directly related to the position. The degree must be in a major field of study (at least at the baccalaureate level) that is appropriate for the position.
 I have a combination of specialized experience as described in (A) and qualifying undergraduate or graduate education as described in (B) which when combined equal 100% of the requirements. Qualifying Education percentage: is calculated by first determining the total number of semester hours (undergraduate or graduate) that exceed the basic requirement (such as the 30 semester hours equivalent to a major for the 1560 series), then divide that total by the total number of education hours required for a full year of study (typically 30 semester hours, or your school's definition).
 I do not have the education or experience as described above.
