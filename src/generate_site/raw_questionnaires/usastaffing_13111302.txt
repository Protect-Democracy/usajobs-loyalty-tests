Public Affairs Specialist (Social Media & Reports), GS-1035-14
.
Specialized Experience: Applicants must have at least one year of specialized experience at the GS-13 grade level in the Federal service or equivalent to the GS-13 grade level in the private sector developing and implementing public affairs or congressional communications for an organization. This experience includes developing and clearing communications on complex or sensitive issues; managing projects involving leadership, subject matter experts, and multiple deadlines; and responding to media or congressional inquiries, advising leadership, or coordinating external outreach. Examples include : Preparing and clearing news releases, talking points, congressional correspondence, testimony, reports, articles, web content, and organizational social media content; managing report production and review; and coordinating media or congressional responses and outreach. Routine posting, clerical formatting, or personal social media use alone does not demonstrate specialized experience unless the work reflects the complexity, responsibility, and communications duties described above.
