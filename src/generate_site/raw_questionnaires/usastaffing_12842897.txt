5.
This position requires regular and recurring work and camping in rugged mountainous backcountry at high elevations for extended periods of time. Selectee(s) must be able to hike by foot in the backcountry at high elevations for extended periods of time while carrying backpacks, tools, and supplies weighing at least 50 lbs. The selectee must be able to camp for several days in succession.
