QUALIFICATION REQUIREMENTS:

SPECIALIZED EXPERIENCE: GS-14 Level:
You must have one year of specialized experience at a level of difficulty and responsibility equivalent to the GS-13 grade level in the Federal service. Specialized experience for this position must include:
Managing multiple assignments involving operational oversight, workload monitoring, risk identification, milestone tracking, quality review, training coordination, issue resolution, and follow-up actions to ensure timely completion of program objectives;
Experience with public and/or business administration or public policy;
Using oral and written communication skills to present plans and proposals to negotiate, and gain cooperation in reconciling conflicting interests or views;
Experience with automated systems and business tools to compile, validate, analyze, and report program data, including spreadsheets, tracking tools, quality review reports, training records, workload inventories, correspondence/case data, and dashboards used to support program oversight and management decisions and develop new approaches and/or refined existing techniques;
Experience with preparing written reports, spreadsheets, statistical tables, and flowcharts; Examples may include assignments where you performed the full range of project management principles, program management, business practices, methods, and techniques; conducting analysis of issues broad in nature, and/or of strategic importance;
