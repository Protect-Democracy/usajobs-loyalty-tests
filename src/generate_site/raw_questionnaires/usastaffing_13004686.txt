Reemployment Priority List (RPL) for Washington, DC - The RPL is the mechanism the Department of Health and Human Services (HHS) uses to give reemployment consideration to former competitive service employees separated by reduction-in-force (RIF) or fully recovered from a compensable injury after more than one year.

Do you wish to be considered under the Reemployment Priority List (RPL) as a former HHS employee who has been separated by RIF or due to a compensable injury? If so, have you met all the following criteria? 

Separated Under RIF:
Registered for the RPL program in same series and grade as the vacancy, and
Occupied a position in the same local commuting area as the vacancy (Washington, DC), and
Occupied a position at or above the grade of the announced position and with no greater promotion potential, and
Have a current/most recent performance rating of at least "fully successful" or equivalent.
Separated Due to a Compensable Injury:
Registered for the RPL program in same series and grade as the vacancy, and
Occupied a position in the same local commuting area as the vacancy (Washington, DC), and
Must either have accepted a position at a lower grade or pay level in lieu of separation or have been separated because of a compensable injury or disability, and
Must have fully recovered more than 1 year after compensation began; and
Must have received notification from the Office of Workers' Compensation Programs, Department of Labor, that injury compensation benefits have ceased or will cease.
For more information about RPL eligibility and required documentation, visit 5 CFR 330 Subpart B. 

Proof is required:

You must submit the following documentation listed below to support your claim if you were separated due to RIF:
Your most recent SF-50 (Notification of Personnel Action), or equivalent, as stated in the job announcement that shows position occupied (block #34 of your SF-50); tenure; grade and step; salary; and full performance level.
Your SF-50 showing Separation - RIF. If that is not available yet, then official documentation of your Reduction in Force (RIF) separation notice or a proposed removal/separation notice.
Your Reduction in Force (RIF) separation notice or a proposed removal/separation notice. and
A copy of your latest performance appraisal with final rating score as at least fully successful or equivalent.  If a performance appraisal is not available, a copy of your RIF notice showing your last final rating score as at least fully successful or equivalent, or a recent Within Grade Increase SF-50 with a remark indicating fully successful performance.

You must submit the following documentation listed below to support your claim if you were separated due to a Compensable Injury:

Your SF-50, which shows you've been separated, or the SF-50 that shows you accepted a position at a lower grade or pay level in lieu of separation.
