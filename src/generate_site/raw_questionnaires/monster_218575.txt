The Core Focus of the ECQs are cross-cutting and should be addressed within your two-page resume.&nbsp; The following are the foundation for success in each of the ECQs below: Constitutional fidelity and service to the American people; Efficient resource use, cost-saving, process modernization; Technical excellence and high-quality performance; Team leadership, accountability, performance culture; and Meeting and exceeding mission-aligned goals. ECQ 1 - This core qualification encompasses the ability to uphold the Constitution and American governing ideals - a foundation for federal leadership. ECQ 2 - This core qualification involves the ability to demonstrate effective resource management, budget discipline, waste reduction, and leveraging technology or process improvements. ECQ 3 - This core qualification stresses the delivering of exceptional quality work through technical skill, knowledge, and reliable performance. ECQ 4 - This ECQ involves the ability to inspire and guide teams toward mission objectives while fostering accountability, performance, and adaptability. ECQ 5 - This core qualification involves consistently delivering outcomes aligned with organizational goals and the broader mission set by leadership. MANDATORY TECHNICAL QUALIFICATIONS:&nbsp; All candidates must address the following Mandatory Technical Qualifications within their two-page resume. MTQ 1- Demonstrated expert knowledge of federal communications policy and regulatory frameworks affecting consumers of telecommunications and broadband services, including experience developing, implementing, or overseeing policies and programs that protect consumer rights, address service accessibility, and promote access to communications services; and ability to interpret and apply statutes, regulations, and Commission orders related to consumer protection, disability access, and telecommunications service obligations. MTQ 2 - Demonstrated ability to lead large-scale consumer engagement initiatives and programs , including experience overseeing programs related to accessibility of communications technologies, public education campaigns, consumer complaint resolution, and stakeholder outreach.; and ability to collaborate effectively with advocacy organizations, state and local governments, and/or industry stakeholders to advance consumer-focused policy objectives. MTQ 3 - Demonstrated executive leadership experience managing complex programs within a federal regulatory or public policy environment, including leading multidisciplinary teams; managing significant operational resources; establishing strategic priorities to support organizational mission objectives; &nbsp; advising senior leadership; and coordinating across other internal organizational unit and with external partners to implement consumer-focused initiatives and ensure effective delivery of public services.
