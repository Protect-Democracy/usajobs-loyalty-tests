1.
Ability to serve as the unit's expert intelligence analyst on all intelligence matters, including intelligence processes, support requirements, technical aspects of weapons systems, and terrorist operational methods.


2.
Ability to gain and maintain Global Situational Awareness (GSA) in order to quickly and accurately advise commanders/decision-makers on possible enemy courses of action.


3.
Ability to research, evaluate, integrate, and analyze all source data in the preparation of clear, concise, in depth intelligence analytical products focusing on the threats to air operations, including the operational concepts of military forces and trends with potential military implications.


4.
Ability to prepare and manage the budgeting of fiscal and personnel resources for the intelligence function.


5.
Ability to coordinate with military, federal, state, and local officials to ensure terrorist threats are identified and mitigated at home station and all deployed locations.
