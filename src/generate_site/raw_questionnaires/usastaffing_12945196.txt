Assessment 1
Specialized experience for this GS-14 position is one year of experience at the GS-13 level, or equivalent, that is directly related to the position which has equipped the candidate with the knowledge, skills, and abilities to successfully perform the duties of the position. Specialized experience includes:
- Establishing or enforcing enterprise architecture principles, standards, or reference models; AND
- Reviewing system designs, modernization proposals, or IT investments for architectural alignment; AND
- Developing current-state, target-state, or transition architectures; AND
- Integrating cybersecurity, interoperability, or data architecture requirements into enterprise designs; AND
- Advising leadership on enterprise risks such as fragmentation, technical debt, duplicative systems, vendor lock-in, or security vulnerabilities ; AND
- Briefing stakeholders/executive leadership on architecture, modernization, or enterprise IT strategy.
