 I have experience performing the work of a Painter Worker under normal supervision. I have experience in surface repairs to match surrounding areas, and knowledge of standard methods and techniques best suited to clean and prepare surfaces, such as degreasing, sanding, wire brushing, scraping, spackling, puttying, and in applying prepared solvents to remove scale, rust dirt, and old paint from surfaces so that coasting materials will adhere properly. I have experience in coating methods, techniques, devices and materials, how they are used to get a variety of finish results on various kinds of surfaces, and ability to determine those which are best suited to the surface finish required. Completed projects independently with no in-progress check and final product review by a supervisor.
 Ability to independently apply coating materials to various surfaces by any of the accepted methods which include brushing, rolling, spraying, cutting-in, overflowing and troweling to insure a smooth-textured and decorative appearance and meet thickness and level requirements. Is skilled in special finishing techniques such as mottling, marbling, and graining. In various surface preparations including steam cleaning, precision masking, and chemical pre-treatment, and in matching, tinting, toning, and blending coating materials with various additives. Evidence of pertinent safety training and uses all required safety equipment, clothing, and precautions to protect self and others. Completed projects independently with little in-progress checks and final product review by a supervisor.
 Ability to satisfactorily accomplish coating work including camouflage work by standard coating methods such as brushing, rolling, and spraying, apply other related methods and techniques as cutting-in techniques and dip and overflow methods so that surfaces are fully coated and free from drips and runs, and to read and follow directions that explain how to apply and use prepared solvents and thin, mix, and stir coating materials. Skilled in standard finish processes such as hammer tone, wrinkle, and non-skid finishes which are applied as per set process. Has had safety training in this or related occupation. Can recognize obvious hazards, follow safety directions and precautions and not endanger self or co-workers. Completed projects were spot checked by a supervisor during and at the time of completion.
