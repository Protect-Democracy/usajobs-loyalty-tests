Position Requirements
Please review the position requirements and acknowledge by selecting Yes/No to each question.


1.
I have reviewed and demonstrated possession of the following technical qualifications on my 2-page resume.

TQ1. Demonstrated ability to comprehensively plan, direct, and execute a comprehensive financial management program for a federal Power Marketing Administration (PMA) or a large electric transmission utility. This includes expert application of fiscal law, policies, regulations, principles, and standards to financial management activities; skilled analysis and evaluation of budget, program issues, financial data, and reports using advanced business tools, cost/economic analysis, and performance metrics for actionable recommendations; and proficiency in applying financial analysis, forecasting, and planning methods to ensure alignment of financial strategies with Agency-wide goals.

TQ2. Demonstrated expert-level experience in federal financial management, including budget formulation and execution, and/or equivalent expertise within a large electric transmission utility, particularly with FERC Uniform System of Accounts or comparable non-jurisdictional accounting. This encompasses managing, allocating, and monitoring financial resources in strict compliance with federal laws (e.g., Reclamation laws) and regulations, ensuring transparency, robust internal controls, efficient goal attainment, and timely repayment of obligations. Crucially, candidates must possess expert-level engagement with and support acquisition from oversight bodies such as OMB and U.S. Congress, and regulatory bodies like FERC or state public utility/energy commissions.
