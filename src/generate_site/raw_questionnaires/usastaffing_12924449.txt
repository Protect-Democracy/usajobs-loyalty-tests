 I have the ability to perform the full range of Masonry Worker tasks such as maintenance, repair and construction of stone masonry walls and closely related structures; mix soils, water and amendments for mortars; removing damaged portions of walls for repair of damage, stabilization and original fabric; implementing prescribed treatments (capping, veneering, reappointing, resetting, new laying, etc.); use and maintain tools and equipment related to trail structures and masonry, inspection of stone masonry and perform masonry condition assessments recognizing hazards and resources damage.
