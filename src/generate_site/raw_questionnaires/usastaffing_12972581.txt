1.
Select the one statement that best describes your highest level of experience as it relates to your ability to perform the duties of the Electronic Integrated Systems Mechanic, WG-2610-12.
 I have the ability to perform the most difficult and complex Electronic Integrated Systems Mechanic tasks which include: performing trouble shooting analysis and final alignment of multisystem complexes consisting of several complete integrated systems; repairing, overhauling, rebuilding, aligning, and adjusting complete multi-systems such as the electronics package in a highly automated combat vehicle where target acquisition and tracking, weapons control, and other complex functions are performed by numerous systems which are extensively interconnected with data feedback loops. I am skilled in determining proper sequence of operations and starting point in sequential operations in order to pinpoint areas of malfunction. I am considered an expert and work with a high degree of independence.
 I have the ability to perform the full range of Electronic Integrated Systems Mechanic tasks which include: overhauling, repairing, rebuilding, troubleshooting, adjusting, installing, testing, inspecting, fabricating, maintaining, and integrating operable subsystems into a functional system; analyzing indications obtained during operation, relating them to the technical data available on the individual subsystems and various data and function exchanges between the subsystems; determining type and location of malfunction and performing repairs or turning the defective sub-systems over to lower grade employees for repair. I am skilled in monitoring the operation of complex interrelated subsystems, analyzing operating trends, proposing preventive maintenance down time to assure continued operating capability, and recommending units for overhaul or engineering evaluation for disposal; rebuilding, overhauling, installing, troubleshooting, repairing, modifying, calibrating, aligning, and maintaining integrated electronic systems. I work on my own initiative under general supervision subject to occasional inspection.
 I have the ability to perform common Electronic Integrated Systems Mechanic tasks which include: repairing and maintaining integrated electronic systems. I can demonstrate how to troubleshoot and repair electronic warfare or multiple integrated electronic systems composed of several systems which are closely interrelated and interdependent. I work under normal supervision of a work leader or journey-level employee who observes tasks in progress and upon completion to ensure they are properly performed.
 I have the ability to perform simple Electronic Integrated Systems Mechanic duties which include: assisting with repairing electronic integrated systems or I have received training on basic electronic integrated systems such as electronic circuits and signals. I require close supervision when performing these duties.
 I do not possess the abilities described above.
