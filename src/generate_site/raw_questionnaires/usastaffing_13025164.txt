QUALIFICATION REQUIREMENTS:  To qualify for this position, you must meet the qualification requirements outlined below:

SPECIALIZED EXPERIENCE IR-08: To be minimally qualified for this position you must have one (1) year of specialized experience at a level of difficulty and responsibility equivalent to the next lower level within the payband or GS grade in the normal line of progression in the Federal service that demonstrates the required managerial skills and technical competence required to perform the job. To be qualifying for this position your experience must include:
Experience examining taxpayer returns, accounts, and supporting financial documentation to identify discrepancies, validate claimed refundable credits, and determine taxpayer eligibility.
Experience applying provisions of the Internal Revenue Code, related tax regulations, and Internal Revenue Manual procedures to analyze and resolve tax-return or taxpayer-account issues and determine appropriate case actions.
Experience processing examination cases by identifying missing, incomplete, or inconsistent information; initiating follow-up actions; documenting findings; and preparing cases for resolution or closure.
Experience using tax-administration case-processing systems and databases to research taxpayer information, update accounts, input transaction codes, track case status, and maintain case documentation.
Experience evaluating examination cases against program criteria, statutory requirements, procedural requirements, and quality standards to determine compliance and resolve refundable-credit issues.
