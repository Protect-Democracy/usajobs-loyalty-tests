The Warden needs a comprehensive psychological evaluation to determine housing placement, security level considerations, and treatment needs. You have full access to psychological testing materials and must prepare a complete psychological report within 72 hours.

 Recommend treatment interventions including behavioral techniques for anger management, cognitive intervention for depressive symptoms, group counseling for substance abuse in the residential drug treatment program, and impulse control training to address the personality disorder traits, documenting your recommendations in the treatment plan based on your review of the assessment findings and clinical presentation in the correctional environment.
 Provide treatment by recommending cognitive-behavioral therapy techniques for the depression and suicidal ideation, biofeedback and relaxation techniques for stress management, suicide prevention strategies with crisis intervention protocols, drug treatment programming through the residential substance abuse program, and anger management interventions, while identifying this as an appropriate case for referral to psychiatry for potential psychotropic medication to augment the psychological interventions.

The shift commander has secured the area and is requesting your immediate assistance to resolve the crisis before the situation escalates further or the inmate injures himself.

Which action represents the most effective and appropriate crisis response approach in this situation?

 Immediately respond to C-Block and position yourself at a safe distance from the cell where you can communicate with the inmate, use calm verbal de-escalation techniques to establish rapport and assess his mental status, work to defuse the immediate suicide risk by addressing the auditory hallucinations and validating his emotional distress without reinforcing the command hallucinations, consult with the shift commander and medical staff about emergency medication options if verbal intervention is insufficient, direct custody staff on how to approach the situation to minimize triggering aggressive behavior, coordinate with the crisis response team on tactical considerations while maintaining psychological intervention, and continue verbal crisis management until the inmate surrenders the object and agrees to mental health evaluation and treatment.
