QUALIFICATION REQUIREMENTS: To qualify for this position, you must meet the qualification requirements outlined below:SPECIALIZED EXPERIENCE GS-13: To be eligible for this position at this grade level, you must meet the following requirements. In addition to the basic requirements, you must have one (1) year of specialized experience at a level of difficulty and responsibility equivalent to the GS-12 grade level in the Federal service.Specialized experience for this position includes:
Experience serving as an analyst performing technical and analytical financial management assignments, including reviewing financial management program operations, and providing guidance on financial processes, procedures, or requirements.
Experience applying financial management policies, procedures, and practices to prepare reports, conduct financial studies, research accounting operations, or review financial systems.
Experience analyzing financial management programs, processes, or systems to identify operational issues, document findings, and recommend improvements to financial management procedures.
Experience researching financial management program issues and applying applicable policies or procedures to resolve problems related to assigned programs.
Experience conducting studies or reviews, preparing written findings, and communicating financial management recommendations through reports, briefings, or presentations to management officials.
