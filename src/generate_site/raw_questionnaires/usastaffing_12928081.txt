My creditable specialized experience includes: Understanding visual functions including computer graphics, computer modeling, engineering design, and analyses software, digital photography, graphic illustration, digital editing software and hardware, multimedia production, graphics/exhibits, computer software and hardware; Applying methods and techniques of communicating information through visual means and ability to apply the principles of visual design to present subject matter information in a variety of digital forms/formats that will convey the intended message to specific audiences; Using qualitative and quantitative techniques to conduct studies/research and recommend solutions for selection/application and production of current and future technologies while ensuring the most cost-effective utilization available; Understanding project management processes sufficient to initiate, plan, execute, monitor, control, and close projects.
