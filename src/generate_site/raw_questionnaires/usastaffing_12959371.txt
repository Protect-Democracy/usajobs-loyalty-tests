1.
Minimal Qualifications:

Skill and Knowledge: The incumbent must possess journeyman level skills and knowledge of all common construction hand and power tools, electrical/electronic testing equipment including: pipe threaders, pipe wrenches, electric arc welders, oxyacetylene torches, hand and power snakes, hydraulic conduit benders, leak detectors, meggers, phase rotation meters, power quality analyzers, and multimeters. They are knowledgeable of the uses of special materials that will withstand a variety of environments and is knowledgeable of special safety precautions involving handling of hazardous materials such as toxic materials or asbestos. Must be skilled in cutting, threading, bending and installation of rigid pipe and other conduit and piping systems.

The incumbent must have a minimum of four years work experience and a thorough understanding of boiler operation, as well as the safety devices incorporated in the system to protect the boiler and plant personnel in the event of boiler malfunction.
