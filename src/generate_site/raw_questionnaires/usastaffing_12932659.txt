Are you applying based upon the Veterans Opportunity to Work (VOW) to Hire Heroes Act of 2011?
 
Many service members begin their civilian job search prior to being released from Active Duty service, and may not have a DD-214 form to submit along with their resume. VOW allows certain Active Duty service members to be treated as preference eligibles in the competitive or excepted service, even though the service member has not been discharged or released from Active Duty. Under the VOW Act, tentative preference will be granted to a active duty service member who meets the following:Submit a certification (in lieu of a DD-214 form) along with their job application materials. (Agencies will verify the individual meets the definition of 'preference eligible' under 5.U.S.C. 2108 prior to appointment).
A "certification" is any written document from the Armed Forces that certifies the service member is expected to be discharged or released from Active Duty service in the Armed Forces within 120 days after the certification is submitted by the applicant.
The certification letter should be on letterhead of the appropriate military branch of service and contain (1) the military service dates including the expected discharge or release date; and (2) the Character of Service.Note:  If the certification has expired; you must provide documentation (e.g., a copy of the DD form 214) that demonstrates the service member is a preference eligible per 5 U.S.C. 2108, in your application package before veterans' preference can be awarded.

To Learn more about VOW, please go to: https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/veterans-employment-initiative/vet-guide

Interprets instructions and specifications related to mobile equipment operation.  2.  Proficiency in driving light delivery vehicles.  3. Proficiency in handling heavy weights up to 80 lbs. and loads.  Applicants must have experience and/or training of sufficient scope and quality to independently perform the duties typically Without More Than Normal Supervision.
 I am able to perform the most difficult and complex of a Motor Vehicle Operator, KX-5703-05 duties. As part of my regular duties, I independently perform as a journeyperson and/or assist others, including journeypersons and work leads. I perform operator maintenance and daily inspections, such as checking fuel, oil, water, battery liquid, lights, horn, windshield wipers and fluids; and ensures that vehicles are properly serviced and equipped in accordance with established office rules and regulations. I am able to retrieve work from internal customers and at specific locations within the company, carries various weigts up two (2) tons of manufacturer’s rated capacity and retrieves signatures, using scanners, from recipients of packages delivered. I am often consulted by other journeypersons. I am considered an expert and work with a high level of independence.
 I am able to perform the full range of Motor Vehicle Operator, KX-5703-05, duties on my own initiative with only general supervision from journey-level Leads. As part of my regular duties, I am able to perform and make minor emergency repairs to vehicles when necessary, duties on my own initiative with only general supervision from journey-level Leads, drive various sizes and types of vehicles for which qualified and licensed as required by the workload and verifies and corrects address labels if necessary, work on my own initiative, under general supervision, subject to occasional inspection.
 I am able to perform common duties of a Motor Vehicle Operator, KX-5703-05, I perform common duties/tasks to assist in the training of new drivers and verifies and corrects address labels if necessary.
