From the descriptions below, select the appropriate response based on your level of experience, education, and/or training, that you fully possess and that demonstrates your ability to perform the Tractor Operator duties. (SCREEN-OUT)

 I have the ability to perform the most difficult and complex tractor operator tasks. I have worked as a Tractor Operator operating farm-type tractors and attachments that include three-point hitch and trail type mowers used for mowing along road shoulders in high traffic areas, wet and/or slick hillsides and along steep and uneven slopes. I have performed preventive and routine maintenance as well as repairs on these types of tractors and attachments. I am considered an expert and work with a high degree of independence.
 I have the ability to perform the full range of tractor operator tasks. I can safely operate farm-type tractors and associated attachments that include three-point hitch mounted rotary type mowers to complete such work as plowing, mowing, and planting on rolling hills, mowing along road shoulders in moderate traffic areas and slopes. I work on my own initiative under general supervision subject to occasional inspection.
 I have the ability to perform common farm-type tractor operator tasks. I have the ability to safely operate a tractor type belly mower and attachments (such as sickle mowers, rakes hailers, etc.) used for mowing on mostly level terrain.
 I have the ability to perform simple tractor operator tasks, or I have the ability to operate such vehicles as pickup trucks, delivery vans, lawn and garden equipment, etc.
