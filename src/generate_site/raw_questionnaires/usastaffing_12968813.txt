Assessment 1
1.
Select the response below that best describes your skill and ability to perform the work of an Automotive Worker WG-5823-08 without more than normal Supervision. Duties include performing automotive and equipment repairs, using common tools of the trade in environments that are often less than favorable, requiring work in awkward and uncomfortable positions, and performed frequently while standing for long periods of times. The work involves frequently lifting, handling and carrying parts and equipment weighing 40 pounds and occasionally moving items weighing up to 50 pounds with assistance or equipment. Uses diagnostic skills to establish procedures for repairing electrical, fuel, steering/suspension, driveline, braking and other issues causing failure in automotive systems. Candidates will demonstrate skills in the basic tools common to the occupation of the Automotive Worker and skills in the operation of equipment common to the trade. Candidates will possess the skills to independently accomplish routine repair and maintenance duties. Work is performed in a work that caused continued exposure to dirt, dust, grease, and the potential for burns, shocks, chemical irritations, bruises, cuts and strains. The incumbent must be able to work safely and expected to wear all PPE necessary to prevent on the job injuries. Please provide evidence of this skill and ability in your resume.
