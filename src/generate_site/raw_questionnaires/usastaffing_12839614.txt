From the descriptions below, select the appropriate response based on your current level of experience and/or training that demonstrates your ability to perform the duties of an Animal Packer (Custodial), WG-5001-06 without more than normal supervision.
Animal Packer-Custodial workers at this level perform the following duties: Perform minor maintenance such as clearing clogged drains, painting, replacing toilet seats and installing dispensers. Hike to backcountry toilet facilities to process waste and recycling, while leading llamas or other pack animals to pack tools and supplies in and solid waste out. Use and maintain custodial equipment and operate vehicles and pickup trucks to transport supplies, materials, and pack animals.
