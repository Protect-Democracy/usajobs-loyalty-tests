Assessment 1

QUALIFICATION REQUIREMENTS:  To qualify for this position, you must meet the qualification requirements outlined below:

GS-14 SPECIALIZED EXPERIENCE: You must have one year of specialized experience at a level of difficulty and responsibility equivalent to the GS-13 grade level in the Federal Service. Specialized experience for this position must include:
developing and managing performance metrics, tracking mechanisms, and reporting systems to evaluate the effectiveness of programs, and leveraging reporting tools and data visualization platforms to present complex information clearly and effectively.
communicating effectively with data governance bodies, Information Technology, and key stakeholders to ensure data integrity, accuracy, and compliance with Service-wide standards and developing briefings and talking points for executives based on data from multiple report and data sources.
applying a range of laws, policies, and regulations to recommend improvements to program operations (i.e. data analysis, position analysis, technology) to support operations and external stakeholders including TIGTA.
providing technical guidance to staff, reviewing and integrating training initiatives across offices, branches, and sections,. and developing comprehensive action plans and schedules for short and long-range project implementation, ensuring alignment with Service goals, priorities, and policies.
coordinating project objectives and recommendations with key line and/or staff functions of the Service by evaluating progress against milestones, adjusting strategies, formulating corrective actions to mitigate risk, allocating project resources and recommending resource realignment to address emerging priorities.
