Assessment 1
Specialized Experience for the GS-15, you must have one year (full 52 weeks) of specialized experience at a level of difficulty and responsibility equivalent to the GS-14 grade level in the Federal service. Specialized experience for this position is defined as: 
Managing and administering a travel management program, including planning, coordinating, and overseeing domestic and international travel operations, travel systems, and relocation programs to ensure compliance with the Federal Travel Regulation (FTR), applicable laws, regulations, and agency policies; AND
Developing, implementing, and evaluating travel policies, operational procedures, internal controls, performance measures, and process improvements to enhance program effectiveness, regulatory compliance, financial accountability, customer service, and operational efficiency; AND
Managing travel systems, financial interfaces, contracts, and vendor performance; analyzing travel program operations and financial data; and collaborating with agency leadership, the General Services Administration (GSA), financial management officials, and other stakeholders to resolve issues, improve travel operations, and support organizational objectives.
