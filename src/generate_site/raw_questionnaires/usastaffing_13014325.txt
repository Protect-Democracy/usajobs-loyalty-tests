 I have worked as a maintenance worker performing and leading others in trail rehabilitation. This includes work such as building, maintaining and repairing trail tread and drainage structures, constructing dry and wet stone multi-coursed/tiered rock walls, turnpiking, cribbing, and foot bridges, removing windfalls, felling trees, and utilizing block, tackle, and winches for moving and placing heavy objects. I am proficient at installing/repairing/cleaning water bars- rock and wood, turnpiking, and repairing trail tread through leveling with fill. I am proficient with cross cuts and chainsaws. I can lift and carry objects weighing more than 50 pounds and hike up to 20 miles a day over uneven terrain.
 I have worked as a maintenance worker performing trail work with limited supervision. I have performed routine trail maintenance functions such as water bar cleaning, clearing trails of windfalls, leveling tread, and brushing with minimal supervision. I am proficient using a chainsaw in felling timber, cutting, bucking, and limbing trees.
 I have performed maintenance worker tasks on a trail crew or group responsible for carrying out work on trails with no more than normal supervision. I have constructed rock walls, water bars, and retainer bars. I have used chainsaws to cut wood or limb trees.
