Assessment 1
1.
 From the description below, choose the statement that best describes the highest level of experience you possess to perform the duties of a Painter, WE-4102-2, without more than normal supervision.
 I have the ability to perform the most difficult and complex Painter duties. I can paint, decorate, and refinish interior and exterior surfaces that may require a protective or decorative coating. I can evaluate surface conditions and select a preparation technique consistent with the type and condition, the coating material to be applied, and the exposure and expected condition of use. I can perform lead paint abatement that requires a state certification. I can perform such tasks with a high level of independence.
 I have the ability to perform a full range of Painter duties. I can paint, decorate, and refinish interior and exterior surfaces that may require a protective or decorative coating. I can evaluate surface conditions and select a preparation technique consistent with the type and condition, the coating material to be applied, and the exposure and expected condition of use. I can perform such tasks under general supervision subject to occasional inspection.
 I have the ability to perform common Painter duties. I can paint, decorate, and refinish interior and exterior surfaces that may require a protective or decorative coating. I can evaluate surface conditions and select a preparation technique consistent with the type and condition, the coating material to be applied, and the exposure and expected condition of use. I can perform such tasks without more than normal supervision.
 I have the ability to perform common painting tasks such as painting, decorating, and refinishing interior and exterior surfaces that may require a protective or decorative coating. I can perform such tasks under close supervision of a work-leader or journey-level painter who observes tasks in progress and upon completion - making sure they are properly performed.
 I am unable to perform common painting tasks.
