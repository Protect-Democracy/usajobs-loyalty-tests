I work on my own initiative under general supervision subject to occasional inspection
 I have the ability to perform common Machine Tool Operator tasks, to include operating a variety of standard machine tools such as lathes, boring mills, milling machines, and grinding machines. I can operate CNC machines in the automatic mode using pre-programmed feeds and speeds, making only minor adjustments for tool and workpiece size differences. I work under normal supervision of a work leader or journey-level employee who observes tasks in progress and upon completion to make sure they are properly performed.
