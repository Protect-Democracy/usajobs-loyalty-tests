QUALIFICATION REQUIRMENTS: To qualify for this position, you must meet the qualification requirements outlined below:

SPECIALIZED EXPERIENCE:
GS-14 LEVEL: You must have at least one year of specialized experience at a level of difficulty and responsibility equivalent to the GS-13 grade level in the Federal service. Specialized experience for this position must include: 
Experience with a wide range of analytical and evaluative methods and techniques, both qualitative and quantitative; 
Experience in Collection program(s) that require understanding of tax compliance programs to apply to projects and studies concerning the efficiency and effectiveness of operations and means to improve Collection program(s); 
Evaluating complex projects and studies and identifying problem areas, trends, deficiencies, and other similar factors affecting Collection operations to make recommendations to improve efficient utilization of resources; 
Experience with tax compliance programs to identify, develop corrective action plan, and propose and implement resolutions of technical Collection issues; 
Coordinating the identification, research, development, and resolution of technical collection issues;
Using oral and written communication techniques to present findings, deliver briefings, and negotiate solutions to disputed recommendations.
