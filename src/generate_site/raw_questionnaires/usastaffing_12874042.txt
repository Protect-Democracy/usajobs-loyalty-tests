1.
Knowledge of Army Supply Policies and Procedures. Comprehensive understanding of Army supply regulations and policies, including AR 710-2 (Supply Policy Below the National Level) and AR 735-5 (Property Accountability Policies). Knowledge of logistics systems such as GCSS-Army and AESIP. Familiarity with Army Force Management processes, including MTOE (Modified Table of Organization and Equipment) and TDA (Table of Distribution and Allowances) structures.


2.
Knowledge of Commander's Unit Status Report (CUSR) Metrics. Knowledge in CUSR metrics and reporting requirements, including personnel, equipment, and readiness data. Ability to analyze and interpret C-level ratings and readiness metrics to identify trends and areas for improvement such as maintenance readiness.


3.
Skill in MTOE/TDA Analysis. Ability to analyze MTOE and TDA documents to ensure proper alignment of personnel, equipment, and mission requirements. Skill in identifying discrepancies between authorized and on-hand equipment and personnel, and recommending corrective actions. Ability to conduct force structure analysis to support operational planning and readiness.


4.
Skill in Data Analysis and Reporting. Proficiency in collecting, analyzing, and interpreting supply and readiness data to support decision-making. Ability to generate detailed reports and briefings for senior leaders, including readiness trends, equipment shortages, and operational impacts. Experience using tools such as Microsoft Excel, Power BI, and Army logistics systems for data visualization and reporting.


5.
Knowledge of Logistics Operations. Understanding of logistics operations, including supply chain management, transportation, and maintenance. Ability to coordinate with subordinate units, higher headquarters, and external agencies to ensure timely and accurate supply support. Knowledge of Army sustainment operations and the integration of logistics into operational planning.


6.
Skill in Problem Solving and Critical Thinking. Ability to identify and resolve supply chain and readiness issues, including equipment shortages, property accountability discrepancies, and operational gaps. Skill in developing innovative solutions to improve supply management processes and enhance unit readiness.


7.
Ability to Communicate Effectively. Strong verbal and written communication skills to provide clear guidance, briefings, and reports to senior leaders and subordinate units. Ability to collaborate with diverse stakeholders, including logistics personnel, commanders, and external agencies, to achieve mission objectives. Experience in developing training materials and conducting workshops to improve unit-level logistical operations.


8.
Ability to Work Under Pressure. Ability to perform effectively in high-stress environments, such as during readiness reporting deadlines or operational planning cycles. Skill in prioritizing tasks and managing competing demands to ensure mission success.
