Specialized experience for this position includes: Experience using computer systems, applications, and software to perform routine work assignments Experience identifying basic computer or system issues and assisting with troubleshooting or resolving problems. Experience reviewing or entering data and maintaining information in automated systems or spreadsheets. Experience assisting with administrative or system support activities, cush as security requests, invoice processing, or customer/vendor contacts. Experience assisting with the development of user guides or training materials and providing basic assistance to system users. SPECIALIZED EXPERIENCE: GS-8 Level: You must have one year of specialized experience at a level of difficulty and responsibility equivalent to the GS-7 grade level in the Federal service. Specialized experience for this position includes: Experience using computer systems, applications, and software programs to perform work assignments Experience assisting with identifying and resolving routine computer system or application issues. Experience reviewing or entering data and identifying discrepancies or problems. Experience using spreadsheets or other software applications to maintain, reconcile, or analyze information. Experience assisting customer or system users and providing guidance on established procedures. Experience preparing or updating user guides, instructions, or training materials. SPECIALIZED EXPERIENCE: GS-9 Level: You must have one year of specialized experience at a level of difficulty and responsibility equivalent to the GS-8 grade level in the Federal service. Specialized experience for this position includes: Experience applying knowledge of the features, characteristics and interrelationships of computer equipment, applications and utility programs Experience applying analytical and diagnostic methods and procedures to identify and resolve computer issues. Experience analyzing data to identify and troubleshoot problems encountered on computer systems. Experience using IRS applications such as ISRP, RPS, and SCRIPS. Experience in using Commercial off-the-shelf (COTS) software applicants to perform administrative or technical work. Experience performing security review processes security administration functions. Experience performing data entry, invoice reconciliation, spreadsheet analysis and customer/vendor coordination. Experience developing user guides or training material and providing training or technical assistance to system users.
