The Reemployment Priority List (RPL) is a mechanism used by the Department of Health and Human Services (HHS) to provide reemployment consideration to former competitive service employees who were separated by reduction-in-force (RIF) or who have fully recovered from a compensable injury after more than one year.

Do you wish to be considered under the Reemployment Priority List (RPL) as a former HHS employee who has been separated by RIF or due to a compensable injury?

If so, have you met all the following criteria? 

Separated Under RIF:
Registered for the RPL program, and
Occupied a position in the same local commuting area as the vacancy, and
Occupied a position at or above the grade of the announced position and with no greater promotion potential, and
Have a current/most recent performance rating of at least "fully successful" or equivalent.
Separated Due to a Compensable Injury:
Registered for the RPL program, and
Occupied a position in the same local commuting area as the vacancy, and
Must either have accepted a position at a lower grade or pay level in lieu of separation or have been separated because of a compensable injury or disability, and
Must have fully recovered more than 1 year after compensation began; and
Must have received notification from the Office of Workers' Compensation Programs, Department of Labor, that injury compensation benefits have ceased or will cease.
For more information about RPL eligibility and required documentation, visit 5 CFR 330 Subpart B. 

Documentation is required.

You must submit the following documentation to support your claim if you were separated due to RIF:
Your most recent SF-50 (Notification of Personnel Action), or equivalent, as stated in the job announcement that shows position occupied (block #34 of your SF-50); tenure; grade and step; salary; and full performance level.
Your SF-50 showing Separation - RIF. If that is not available yet, then official documentation of your Reduction in Force (RIF) separation notice or a proposed removal/separation notice.
Your latest performance rating including  your rating score.
OR

You must submit the following documentation to support your claim if you were separated due to a Compensable Injury:
Your most recent SF-50 (Notification of Personnel Action), or equivalent, as stated in the job announcement that shows position occupied (block #34 of your SF-50); tenure; grade and step; salary; and full performance level.
Your SF-50, which shows you've been separated, or the SF-50 that shows you accepted a position at a lower grade or pay level in lieu of separation.
Notification from the Office of Workers' Compensation Programs, Department of Labor, that injury compensation benefits have ceased or will cease.
Your latest performance rating and rating of record (if separate documents) showing your rating score.
