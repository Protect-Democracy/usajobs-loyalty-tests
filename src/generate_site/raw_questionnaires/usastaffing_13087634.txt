DO NOT submit the following SF-50s, since they will not demonstrate that you meet time-in-grade: Cash or time-off award SF-50s; or, General Adjustments with an effective date less than a year old. If you are a US Citizen Personnel Service Contractor, you MUST submit documentation to verify your employment. If you do not submit documentation of your Personnel Service Contract with your application materials, your application will be disqualified from further consideration.

Preferences
1.
Has your background or work history involved a connection to intelligence activities? "Intelligence activity" includes any activities or specialized training involving or related to the clandestine collection of information, or the analysis or dissemination of such information, intended for use by the United States Government or any foreign government in formulating or implementing political or military policy in regard to other countries. This includes, but is not limited to, any involvement in covert actions designed to influence events in foreign countries. Some examples include employment with: An intelligence agency; or, A division of an agency involved in intelligence activities; or, An instrumentality of the United States Government or any foreign government, a substantial part of whose mission involves intelligence activities. This includes government contracting companies and other non-governmental organizations. Please note: Applicants who have a background or work history involving a connection to intelligence activities will be required to submit a completed Intelligence Background Questionnaire upon request. Failure to meet this requirement will result in disqualification from further consideration. An applicant's Intelligence Background Questionnaire will be reviewed by the Peace Corps Office of General Counsel and the applicant may be deemed ineligible for employment for a minimum of 10 years from the last day of disqualifying employment. Please see the "Additional Information" section for more information on Peace Corps Manual Section 611 requirements and restrictions.
