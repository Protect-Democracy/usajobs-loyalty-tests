SPECIALIZED EXPERIENCE To qualify for the GS-14 level, you must possess at least one (1) full year of specialized experience equivalent in the level of difficulty and responsibility to that of the next lower grade level (GS-13) in Federal Service or equivalent private/public sector experience. For this position, specialized experience is defined as the following: Developing policies and programs relating to the medical or health sciences field; Serving as a recognized expert and consultant on programs that impact the well-being of the public; Protecting an operational workforce by applying knowledge and guidance relating to plant physiology, infections, pests, vectors, genes, pathology, diagnostics emerging threats, invasive species, biosecurity and/or biodefense programs and systems; and Developing and assessing strategic and operational plans related to health, food, and animal agriculture preparedness and resilience.
