This position is being filled under the Field Unit Local Hiring Authority [P.L. 6938 Section 127]. This appointing authority permits direct appointment of qualified individuals maintaining a permanent and exclusive residence in the vicinity of a field unit into competitive service positions. Review the location(s) below to be considered for Field Local Hiring.

Field Unit Local Hiring is defined as the following counties:  Gallatin County, MT; Park County, MT; Park County, WY, Sublette County, WY, Teton County, WY or Fremont County, ID
