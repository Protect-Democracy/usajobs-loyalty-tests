Assessment 1

QUALIFICATION REQUIRMENTS: To qualify for this position, you must meet the qualification requirements outlined below:

SPECIALIZED EXPERIENCE GS-15: To be eligible for this position, you must have one (1) year of specialized experience at a level of difficulty and responsibility equivalent to the GS-14 grade level in the Federal service.
Specialized experience includes:

Experience analyzing and synthesizing information from multiple sources to identify organizational issues, trends, risks, dependencies, impacts, and courses of action.
Experience conducting organizational studies and assessments involving multiple functional areas and developing findings, alternatives, and recommendations to support management decision-making.
Experience evaluating organizational programs, initiatives, and operations to assess effectiveness, performance, implementation progress, systemic issues, and improvement needs.
Experience interpreting, integrating, and applying Federal laws, regulations, policies, directives, and management guidance to address organizational and program issues.
Experience assessing resource implications, operational impacts, implementation challenges, organizational risks, and potential consequences associated with proposed actions and program decisions.
Experience planning and coordinating projects, studies, and organizational initiatives involving multiple organizations or functional areas, competing priorities, and interdependent requirements.
