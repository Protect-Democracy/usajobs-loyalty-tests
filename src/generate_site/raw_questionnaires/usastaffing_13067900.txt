You have been tasked with developing a comprehensive solution that addresses both the clinical outcomes and the budgetary concerns while ensuring the program integrates effectively with other health services.

Which action represents the most effective and appropriate program management approach in this situation?

 Contact outside medical facilities to coordinate care for inmates with the most severe diabetes cases, facilitate communication with specialty physicians to obtain treatment recommendations, and provide patient education during admission and orientation about the facility's diabetes management services and expectations.
