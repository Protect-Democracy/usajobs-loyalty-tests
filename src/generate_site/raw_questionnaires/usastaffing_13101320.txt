Specialized experience for the GS-09: You must have one (1) year of specialized experience that has equipped you with the particular competencies/knowledge, skills and abilities to successfully perform the duties of the position. To be creditable, specialized experience must have been equivalent to at least the GS-07 level in the Federal service, public or other private sectors. Specialized experience for this position includes the following: Experience assisting in the review process prior to production proofs or final products at various quality levels of printing to increasing levels of difficulty from basic documents to Advanced Adobe Software Suite designs and other digital printing; AND Experience in processing, writing and reviewing contract orders.; AND Experience working with a team in project management that could include developing project milestones and deadlines, identifying contractual requirements and providing analytical information.; AND Experience working with printed products which range from one or more spot colors to 4-color process printing. OR master's or equivalent graduate degree or 2 full years of progressively higher level graduate education leading to such a degree or LL.B. or J.D., if related. OR COMBINATION OF EXPERIENCE AND EDUCATION: You may qualify by a combination of experience and education. Only graduate level education in excess of the first 18 semester hours may be combined with experience.
