Communicating with both internal and external stakeholders to coordinate, plan and/or organize operations to meet deadlines or streamline processes; AND
Researching, assembling and summarizing data from multiple sources to prepare reports and other forms of correspondence; AND
Demonstrating customer service skills, such as conflict resolution and timely responses to inquiries.
