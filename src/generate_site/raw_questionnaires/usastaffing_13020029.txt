To qualify for this Contract Specialist position you must demonstrate: (1) the Individual Occupational Requirement (IOR) for the federal GS-1102 series AND (2) at least 52 weeks of Specialized Experience.* (See exception for GS-11 below.)


1.
IOR: Indicate how you meet the IOR for this position.

 I successfully completed a course of study in an accredited college or university resulting in a bachelor's degree. *You must provide all relevant transcripts to support this claim.
 In lieu of a bachelor’s degree, I successfully completed at least 24 semester hours in any combination of the following fields: accounting, business, finance, law, contracts, purchasing, economics, industrial management, marketing, quantitative methods or organization and management. *You must provide all relevant transcripts to support this claim.
 I do not meet the Individual Occupational Requirement (IOR) for this series.


SPECIALIZED EXPERIENCE for GS-11 must have been gained in a federal position at or above GS-09 OR (if your position is on another federal pay scale OR outside of federal service) by performing duties at a level of responsibility and difficulty equivalent to GS-09 or above.

Your resume must include: (1) your name and contact information, (2) employer name and position held, (3) full start and stop dates (month/day/year) for each position, (4) full-time or part-time hours worked per week, (5) your grade and pay scale (for federal positions) and (6) a clear description of the duties you perform that relate to the experience required for this position. If you no longer hold a position, it must show an ending date. If your resume doesn't clearly show this information, you will be found ineligible.
