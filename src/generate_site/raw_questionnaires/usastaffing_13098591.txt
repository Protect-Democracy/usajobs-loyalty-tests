Related experience includes: Operating low-pressure fire tube boilers while protecting property, equipment and systems from damage and preventing the loss of human life. Maintaining an accurate and complete log of conditions and events. Using electronic and pneumatic controls, charts, recorders, meters, gauges, and computer-generated data to determine adjustments or corrections necessary for proper boiler operations or changing load requirements. Maintaining and analyzing the boiler water treatment program. Making independent decisions and judgments regarding troubleshooting techniques, and modification and repair procedures. Using tools such as manometers, multi-meters, combustion analyzer, test gauges, infrared thermometers, and dead-weight testers. Responsibility to carry out the majority of steam fitting, plumbing, painting, and housekeeping duties in the boiler plant. Perform minor electrical troubleshooting and repairs.
