MINIMUM QUALIFICATION REQUIREMENTS: Maintenance Mechanic WG-4749-10


1.
From the descriptions below, select the one statement that best describes the highest level of experience that you possess and which demonstrates your ability to perform the duties of a Maintenance Mechanic, WG-4749-10 without more than normal supervision. Your resume must support the response that you select.
 I am able to perform complex Maintenance Mechanic tasks to include installing, maintaining, repairing and performing quality preventive maintenance and modifying complex heating, ventilation, air conditioning and refrigeration systems and support equipment. At this level, I am able to provide technical recommendations for methods, tools, and materials used, layout all work to be completed and ensure proper selection of materials and manpower for the completion of projects. Work orders are completed with a high degree of independence subject to occasional inspection.
 I am able to perform the full range of Maintenance Mechanic tasks to include installing, maintaining, repairing and performing quality preventive maintenance and modifying complex heating, ventilation, air conditioning and refrigeration systems and support equipment. At this level, I am able to plan and layout all work to be completed and ensure proper selection of tools and complete work orders on my own initiative. Work orders are completed under limited supervision subject to occasional inspection.
 I am able to perform common Maintenance Mechanic tasks to include installing, maintaining, repairing and performing quality preventive maintenance on heating, ventilation, air conditioning and refrigeration systems. At this level, I am able to identify and ensure proper selection of tools and complete work orders on my own initiative with a high level of independence. Work orders are completed under normal supervision of a work leader or journey level maintenance mechanic and subject to inspection upon completion to make sure they are properly performed.
 I am able to perform simple Maintenance Mechanic tasks on heating, ventilation, air conditioning and refrigeration systems. At this level, I am familiar with tools used for projects and work is completed under the supervision of higher-grade maintenance mechanics.
 I have no experience or training in Maintenance Mechanic.
