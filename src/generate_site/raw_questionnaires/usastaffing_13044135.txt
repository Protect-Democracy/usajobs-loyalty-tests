Documentation verifying the widow or widower's marriage to the member of the armed forces (e.g., a marriage license or other legal documentation verifying marriage); and
A statement certifying that the individual seeking to use the authority is the un-remarried widow or widower of the service member.

This includes the ability to analyze complex legal, regulatory, compliance, enforcement, and policy issues; assess the impact of proposed or existing regulations, advisory opinions, guidance, or enforcement approaches; and provide authoritative advice and recommendations to senior agency leadership on matters affecting plan participants, beneficiaries, sponsors, service providers, and other stakeholders.
 I do not have the demonstrated experience related to the above Mandatory Technical Qualification.
 I have working knowledge of ERISA and benefits sufficient to handle routine retirement and health/welfare matters. I have helped analyze issues and supported recommendations, working under senior staff direction rather than advising leadership on complex issues.
 I have strong working knowledge of ERISA and benefits practices. I have independently analyzed complex regulatory and compliance issues, weighed the impact of rules and guidance, and regularly advised managers and senior officials on benefit plan matters.
 I have expert-level knowledge of ERISA and benefits practices across all plan types. I have tackled the most complex or first-of-their-kind issues, advised senior leadership strategically, and helped shape policy and regulatory approaches at an organizational or national level.
