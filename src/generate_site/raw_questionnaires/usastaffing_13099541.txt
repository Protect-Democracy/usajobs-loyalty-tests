1.
In the DHS Cybersecurity Service, employees in the Leadership Track perform a range of leadership roles. Some employees are just starting to oversee the technical work of small teams, while other employees have several years of experience managing cybersecurity teams, programs, and organizations. Select the response that best describes your leadership experience. Please ensure that your resume clearly reflects cybersecurity technical work experience. To ensure that your resume reflects the cybersecurity technical work experience, DHS is seeking, please review our Resume Tips application portal , and the DHS Cybersecurity Service technical capabilities at http://dhscs.usajobs.gov/Jobs to determine whether you need to update your resume to ensure DHS can accurately credit the cybersecurity technical work experience you claim. Note: If none of these responses seem accurate, you may have applied via the wrong career track. Contact cybersecurityservice@hq.dhs.gov for assistance before you proceed.
 New to Leadership: I have several years of technical cybersecurity work experience as an individual contributor, and I am interested in transitioning to a leadership role.
 Technical/Team Lead: I have been responsible for managing and reviewing the technical work of lower-level cybersecurity employees
 Manager: I have served in leadership roles (typically for up to 3 years) where I have been responsible for reviewing the technical work—and managing the administrative supervision of—subordinate employees.
 Senior Manager: I am a seasoned cybersecurity manager with 5+ years of experience managing cybersecurity programs, projects, or organizations as well as the cybersecurity work of employees or teams.
