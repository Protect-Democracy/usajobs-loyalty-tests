Assessment 1
1.
From the descriptions below, select the appropriate response based on your level of experience, education, and/or training, that you fully possess and that demonstrates your ability to perform the duties of this position without more than normal supervision. The duties of this position consist of:   
- Performing preventative maintenance and repairs on buildings, grounds, structures, fixtures, and utilities
- Maintaining exterior areas by mowing grass, trimming trees and shrubbery, removing snow, collecting litter, and performing other landscaping duties
- Operating and performing routine maintenance checks on tools, vehicles, and equipment such as mowers, chainsaws, plows, tractors, and hand tools
- Apply pesticides and herbicides to eradicate weeds, insects, rodents, and other pests from facilities and grounds.
 
 I do not have experience, education, or training related to the duties of this position.
 I have assisted with or performed the duties described on a limited basis or under close supervision, such as helping with grounds maintenance or equipment use, but I do not yet perform these duties independently.
 I have the ability to perform some of the duties described, such as basic grounds maintenance and use of common tools and equipment, but I require regular supervision or assistance to complete assignments and have limited experience with repairs or pesticide application.
 I have the ability to perform most of the duties described, including grounds maintenance, operation of tools and equipment, and assisting with preventative maintenance and repairs. I perform these tasks under normal supervision and occasionally require guidance on more complex assignments.
 I have the ability to independently perform the full range of duties described, including performing preventative maintenance and basic repairs to buildings, grounds, and related systems; maintaining exterior areas through mowing, trimming, and snow removal; operating and performing routine maintenance on tools and equipment; and applying pesticides and herbicides in accordance with established procedures. I am able to complete assignments without more than normal supervision and am relied upon to handle routine problems and work assignments.
