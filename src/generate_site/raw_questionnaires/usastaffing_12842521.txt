I am skilled in: 1) Installing, modifying, renovating, and repairing high-temperature water and high-pressure piping systems, including hydraulic, nitrogen, oxygen, steam-heating, and steam-generating systems. 2) Replacing sections of pipe, repairs steam traps and relief and control valves, and performs other maintenance tasks to ensure optimal system performance.3) Measuring, cutting, bending, and threading pipe and tile, as well as caulking and sealing elbows, union joints, tile pipe, faucets, and shower drains. 4) Installing equipment such as sewage, water, oil, and gas distribution systems, water closets, tubs, fire sprinkler systems, and showers. I work on my own initiative under general supervision subject to occasional inspection.
