I am considered an expert and work with a high degree of independence.
 I have the ability to perform the full range Cemetery Caretaker tasks including operating the controls of equipment such as forklifts, pick-up trucks, and small tractors with various attachments; grading of burial areas; compaction the soil of gravesites; erecting tents and setting up chairs for ceremonial purposes, cleaning and setting of group markers, headstones and footstones; and performing standard preventative maintenance. I work on my own initiative under general supervision subject to occasional inspection.
 I have the ability to perform some Cemetery Caretaker tasks including manipulating hand and foot controls to stop, start, and maneuver riding mowers, small tractors and pickup trucks which tow pre-adjusted grounds maintenance equipment; following safety rules and regulations for moving, loading/unloading vehicles; applying fertilizer evenly, in preparing soil mixtures, and in planting seeds and shrubs and able to spot obvious plant diseases and blights, and unusual or poor plant growth; . I work under supervision of a work leader or journey-level employee who reviews my work for completion.
 I have the ability to perform common Cemetery Caretaker tasks including digging and squaring holes with tools such as pneumatic tampers, picks, and shovels; cutting grass with heavy walking-type power mowers; operating hand or gasoline driven pumps to empty water from holes; setting and aligning masonry. I work under close supervision of a work leader or journey-level employee who observes tasks in progress and upon completion to make sure they are properly performed.
