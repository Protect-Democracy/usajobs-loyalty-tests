 Office of the Chief Financial Officer (CFO) - administers guidance and oversight for all financial matters in support of the Secret Service investigative and protective missions by managing budgets and financial information, tracking agency resources, and assisting with administrative tasks related to financial operations. Majors that align with CFO: Finance and Accounting.
 Office of Protective Operations (OPO) - plans, directs, coordinates, and implements the Secret Service’s protective policies, programs, and operations by managing budgets, property, vehicles, administrative duties, and special projects that enable efficient day-to-day operations for protective divisions. Majors that align with OPO: Criminal Justice, Finance, and Business Administration.
 Office of the Chief Information Officer (CIO) - utilizes technology to help the Secret Service protect people, investigate threats, and support major national events. CIO helps ensure employees have the reliable tools they need to carry out a critical national mission by managing secure communications, computer systems, software, data, IT projects and providing on-site technical support. Majors that align with CIO: Business Administration, Human Resources, and Public Administration. (If interested in a more technical developmental position, please apply to the Student Trainee – Information Technology position.)
 Office of Technical Operations (TEC) - supports the Secret Service mission by applying technology, technical expertise, and protective capabilities to help maintain safe and secure environments for protected persons, facilities, and events. TEC assesses evolving risks and develops practical, technology-based solutions that support the agency’s protective operations. Majors that align with TEC: Engineering, Biology, Chemistry and Criminal Justice.
 Office of Human Resources (HUM) - provides comprehensive human capital services through our specialized divisions such as Staffing and Recruitment, Benefits and Payroll, Workforce Planning, Applicant Engagement, HR Policy and Accountability, or Safety, Health and Environmental. Collectively, these functions support talent acquisition, workforce effectiveness, employee well-being, and organizational readiness. Majors that align with HUM: Human Resource Management, Public Administration, Industrial Organization, and Psychology.
 Office of Administration (ADM) - supports the mission of the Secret Service by providing logistics, security, and workforce-resilience services that keep personnel protected, prepared, and ready to serve. ADM helps enable protective and investigative operations nationwide by managing facilities and vehicles, safeguarding information, and supporting employee well-being. Majors that align with ADM: Emergency Management, Data Analytics, and Business Administration.
 Office of Equal Employment Opportunity and Conflict Resolution (ECR) - advances a supportive and collaborative workplace through agency-wide constructive dispute resolution, promoting fair and professional standards, and ensuring full compliance with federal equal employment opportunity guidelines. Majors that align with ECR: Human Resources Management and Business Management
 Office of Intergovernmental and Legislative Affairs (IGL) - supports the agency’s mission by advancing agency legislative priorities and maintaining active congressional engagement, assisting staff with daily legislative tracking, preparing briefing binders, tracking incoming congressional inquiries, and drafting routine correspondence. Majors that align with IGL: Political Science, Public Policy, and International Affairs
 Office of the Chief (HQD) - supports all divisions of the Secret Service Police by providing administrative support such as coordinating travel; assisting with policy updates; preparing briefings; and maintaining files in accordance with records-management requirements. Majors that align with HQD: Criminal Justice, Data Analytics, Government and Homeland Security.
