These Knowledge, Skills, and Abilities (KSAs) reflect the critical competencies needed for successful performance in this position:
GL-07 KSAs:
Knowledge of federal, state, and local laws, regulations, and relevant court decisions related to the management of public lands and their natural, cultural, and human resources.
Knowledge of the rules of evidence, methods for maintaining a proper chain of custody, and the legal elements of probable cause and reasonable suspicion needed to support searches, arrests, and seizures under applicable laws and regulations.
Knowledge of investigative principles and techniques sufficient to conduct investigations; recognize methods and patterns of criminal activity; effectively use available information sources; and apply appropriate interviewing and interrogation techniques.
Ability to communicate orally with a variety of audiences including agency employees, the public, and other agencies.
Ability to communicate in writing with a variety of audiences including agency employees, the public, and other agencies.
