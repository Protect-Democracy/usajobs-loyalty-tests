1.
Knowledge of Logistics Management Principles and Supply Chain Operations: Comprehensive knowledge of end-to-end logistics management principles, including procurement, distribution, inventory control, transportation, warehousing, and asset management. Ability to analyze and optimize complex supply chain operations to ensure efficient and cost-effective delivery of materials, equipment, and services in support of organizational mission requirements.


2.
Ability to Plan, Direct, and Coordinate Large-Scale Logistics Programs: Demonstrated ability to develop, implement, and oversee comprehensive logistics programs involving multiple stakeholders, significant resources, and competing priorities. Skill in strategic planning, resource allocation, and program management to ensure logistics operations meet organizational objectives while maintaining compliance with federal regulations and policies.


3.
Ability to Lead Teams and Communicate with Senior Leadership: Proven ability to lead, mentor, and supervise multidisciplinary logistics teams. Excellent oral and written communication skills to prepare executive-level briefings, reports, and recommendations for senior leadership. Skill in building collaborative relationships with internal and external stakeholders, including other federal agencies, contractors, and industry partners.


4.
Knowledge of Budget Formulation and Financial Management: Comprehensive knowledge of federal budget processes, financial management systems, and fiscal controls. Ability to develop, justify, and execute multi-million dollar logistics budgets, conduct cost analyses, track expenditures, and ensure optimal use of appropriated funds while maintaining compliance with financial regulations and anti-deficiency act requirements.


5.
Expertise in Emergency Response and Contingency Logistics Planning: Advanced knowledge of contingency planning, crisis management, and emergency logistics operations. Ability to develop and execute rapid deployment strategies, establish emergency supply chains, coordinate disaster response logistics, and maintain operational readiness under high-pressure conditions to support mission-critical requirements.
