Specialized Experience for the GS-14, you must have one year (full 52 weeks) of specialized experience at a level of difficulty and responsibility equivalent to the GS-13 grade level in the Federal service. Specialized experience for this position is defined as: 1. Developing or implementing agency-wide recruitment and talent acquisition strategies to address workforce requirements, including mission-critical and hard-to-fill positions; AND 2. Interpreting and applying federal staffing and recruitment laws, regulations, and hiring authorities. Examples may include providing technical guidance on staffing policies and procedures, researching regulatory requirements, resolving recruitment and staffing issues, or recommending improvements to existing practices; AND 3. Leading recruitment initiatives and establishing strategic partnerships with internal and external stakeholders to expand talent pipelines and support organizational hiring objectives; AND 4. Evaluating recruitment programs using workforce data, performance measures, or hiring trends to identify opportunities for process improvement and program effectiveness; AND 5. Providing strategic advice and recommendations to senior leadership regarding recruitment and workforce planning matters such as recruitment policies, talent acquisition initiatives, and long-range workforce hiring needs.
