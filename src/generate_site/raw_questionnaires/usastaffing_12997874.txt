Thank you for your interest in the Chief Technology Officer position with the Social Security Administration. This position is a Senior Level (SL) position in the federal government. SL employees are in non-executive positions whose duties are broad and complex enough to be classified above the GS-15 level. SL positions involve highly specialized and complex work. Please answer each of the following questions to determine your eligibility for this position. If your application does not demonstrate possession of the required qualifications for this position, your application will be ineligible for consideration.
