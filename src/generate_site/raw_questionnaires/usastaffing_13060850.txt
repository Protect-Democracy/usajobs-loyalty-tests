Professional References - Please Read Carefully

In accordance with VA Notice 22-13, pre-employment reference checks are a required part of the VA selection process and must be completed before a tentative job offer can be extended.

Please provide FOUR (4) professional references.

At least TWO (2) of the four references MUST be current or former supervisors:

One current supervisor (or your most recent supervisor if you are not currently employed); and
One additional current or former supervisor.

If you are unable to provide two supervisory references, you may substitute an employment-based professional reference who directly observed your work and can speak to your professional work products, performance, aptitude, and conduct. Please do not substitute a coworker or other professional reference when a supervisory reference is available.

For EACH of the four references, provide:

First and last name
Professional relationship to you (e.g., current supervisor, former supervisor, coworker who directly observed your work)
Current phone number
Email address

Incomplete reference information may delay the hiring process. By providing this information, you are granting permission for the VA to contact the references listed. No additional authorization is required. Hiring officials may request alternate or additional references as needed.
