I am considered an expert and work with a high degree of independence.
 I have the ability to perform the full range of maintenance mechanic tasks to include repair work and operation as electrician, air conditioning mechanic, heating equipment mechanic, plumber, and solar heating system mechanic. I am skilled in 1) Servicing power sources, tightening connections, making splices, insulating exposed wires, and cleaning/lubricating moving parts.; 2) Repairing and replacing water regulators, boosters, generators, oil and air supplies and controls, alarms, safety devices, etc.; 3) Installing and modifying potable water disinfectant systems including working with chemicals, analyzers and other components such as digital mixing valves, circulation pumps and other anti-scald devices.; 4) Interpreting readings from charts and meters to determine whether to increase or lower fire in boiler, whether to start or stop boiler feed pumps, and to determine how long these are to operate. I work on my own initiative under general supervision subject to occasional inspection.
 I have the ability to perform common Maintenance Mechanic tasks to include repair work and operation as electrician, air conditioning mechanic, heating equipment mechanic, plumber, and solar heating system mechanic. I am skilled in 1) Operating hand and power tools; Uses electrical tools, tools of the trade such tapes, rules, hacksaws, and power pipe threaders, cutters, packing and caulking irons and pipe wrenches.; 2) Removing and replacing fixtures and controls to make repairs such as tightening connections, soldering loose wire threads, and skills to rearrange old or install new outlets, test equipment.; 3) Performing preventive maintenance such as greasing/oiling motors, replacing belts and filters, setting controls, regulators, switches, and starters, replacing parts, repairing refrigerant leaks, and replacing refrigerant. I work under close supervision of a work leader or journey-level employee who observes tasks in progress and upon completion to make sure they are properly performed.
 I have the ability to perform simple repair or maintenance work or I have received training on basic repair or maintenance work.
