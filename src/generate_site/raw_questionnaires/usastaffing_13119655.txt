GS-8 Grade Level Requirement: In addition to meeting the basic requirements for the occupation, applicants must also meet the grade level requirements. Employees at this grade level serve as the full performance level ICTs. The ICT performs more complex medicine assignments within the full range of duties allotted and is responsible for assisting licensed personnel with the delievery of care and examinations to patients with complex, specialized, or potentially life-threatening situations. The ICT administers advanced life support care in accordance with established medical standards, which may include CPR, airway management, and drug treatments. ICTs at this level independently perform more highly technical skills that are typically performed in an ambulatory care setting, such as, but not limited to, skin procedures (punch biopsy, callus removal, wound closure with suture/staples, nail care), gastrointestinal procedures (nasogastric tube placement), musculoskeletal (splinting, ambulation aid sizing), cardiovascular (ultrasound guided IV placement), and otolaryngology (epistaxis control, ear irrigation). Applicants must have one year of experience comparable to the next lower grade level (GS-7) which demonstrates the knowledge, skills, and abilities related to the position to be filled. Creditable Experience. To be creditable, the experience must have required the use of knowledge, skills, and abilities (KSAs) associated with an ICT. Experience is only creditable if it was acquired by a Veteran who served in a medical occupation while serving as a member of the U.S. Armed Forces (for example, Combat Medical Specialist, Hospital Corpsman, or Health Service Technician) OR performed the work of an ICT in a clinical/health care setting (for example, Paramedic or Advanced Emergency Medical Technician) and is either directly related to the position to be filled or a related field. Experience satisfying this requirement may be paid or non-paid employment as an ICT in the health care field. In addition to meeting the experience or educational requirements for this grade level, the candidate must fully demonstrate the following KSAs: (a) Knowledge of complex integrated health care and its interactions with other healthcare systems. (b) Skill in applying emergency techniques through the systematic application of care and determining the approach that will most effectively sustain the patient's life.(c) Skill in completing formal screenings and making recommendations to clinical team in support of patient evaluation.(d) Skill in recognizing and detecting relevant diagnostic signs and symptoms of illness or injury and alerting health care providers based on findings of health observations. e) Skill in performing minor procedures to achieve desired therapeutic outcomes.
