Assessment 1
SPECIALIZED EXPERIENCE FOR GS-11: To be eligible for this position at this grade level, you must have one (1) year of specialized experience at a level of difficulty and responsibility equivalent to the GS-9 grade level in the Federal service. Specialized experience includes:
Applying quantitative and qualitative techniques to support administrative functions like analyzing facts, drawing conclusions, and devising alternatives to project areas.
Conducting studies and evaluations with limited supervision and developing recommendations for program improvements.
Utilizing management principles and processes to perform project management duties to coordinate and integrate project objectives and recommendations.
Applying oral and written communication techniques to present plans and proposals to negotiate changes to project areas.
Supporting management by conducting analytical studies through extracting and interpreting data from information database systems for writing queries and formulas for reports.
Using initiative and judgement to improve program efficiency through systems or software tools (e.g., Microsoft SharePoint Designer or comparable software).
Providing Visual Basic updates via MS SharePoint Designer or comparable software for project/program area.
OR you may substitute education for specialized experience as follows: A Ph.D. or equivalent doctoral degree from an accredited college or university in a field of study that demonstrates the knowledge, skills, and abilities necessary to do the work of this position.
OR three (3) full years of progressively higher-level graduate education leading to a Ph.D. or equivalent doctoral degree in a field of study that demonstrates the knowledge, skills, and abilities necessary to do the work of this position.
OR LL.M. in a field of study that demonstrates the knowledge, skills, and abilities necessary to do the work of this position.
OR you may qualify with at least one (1) year of combined qualifying education and specialized experience as defined above.



SPECIALIZED EXPERIENCE GRADE 12: To be eligible for this position at this grade level, you must have one (1) year of specialized experience at a level of difficulty and responsibility equivalent to the GS-11 grade level in the Federal service. Specialized experience must include:
Developing and implementing action plans and project schedules for short- and long-term accomplishments, ensuring alignment with organizational goals, priorities, and policies.
Independently performing operational reviews and program evaluations and providing recommendations to improve program effectiveness and achieve established goals and objectives.
Determining and justifying project resource requirements and negotiating with managers or stakeholders to obtain necessary support and resources.
Applying analytical and evaluative methods to assess program performance, identify trends, and develop recommendations for process improvements and organizational efficiency.
Utilizing evaluative methods to conduct projects or studies, including establishing criteria to measure accomplishments, progress, and program effectiveness.
Preparing and delivering briefings, reports, and presentations to communicate findings, support decision-making, and influence outcomes.
Applying oral and written communication techniques to present plans, deliver briefings, and negotiate solutions to complex or disputed recommendations.
Conducting analytical studies by extracting and interpreting data from information systems to develop reports and support data-driven decisions.
Experience leading or coordinating analytical projects or studies involving multiple stakeholders or functional areas.
