From the descriptions below, select the appropriate response based on your level of experience, education, and/or training, that you fully possess and that demonstrates your ability to perform the Painter (Explosives Worker) duties without more than normal supervision.

 I have the ability to perform the most difficult and complex preparation and painting of a variety of surfaces to include interior or exterior conventional ammunition items, guided missiles, missile containers, ammunition canisters, or crates. I am skilled in the application of prime -to meet thickness, texture, and appearance requirements, preparing surfaces to be painted, such as removing scale, rust, grease, dirt and old paint, applying markings to munitions using stencils, rubber stamps or spray guns, and performing maintenance on paint equipment. I am considered an expert and work with a high degree of independence.
 I have the ability to perform the full range of preparation and painting of a variety of surfaces to include interior or exterior conventional ammunition items, guided missiles, missile containers, ammunition canisters, or crates. I am skilled in applying markings to ammunition or guided missiles using stencils, rubber stamps or spray guns, and performing maintenance on paint equipment. I work on my own initiative under general supervision subject to occasional inspection.
 I have the ability to perform some tasks including preparing and painting a variety of surfaces to include interior or exterior items, applying prime, intermediate, and final coats to meet thickness, texture, and appearance requirements. I can prepare surfaces to be painted, such as removing scale, rust, grease, dirt, and old paint, apply markings to ammunition or guided missiles using stencils, rubber stamps or spray guns, and operate and performing maintenance on paint equipment. I work under normal supervision of a work leader or journey-level employee who reviews my work for completion.
 I have the ability to perform common preparation and painting of a variety of surfaces to include interior or exterior items. I can prepare surfaces to be painted, such as removing scale, rust, grease, dirt and old paint. I work under close supervision of a work leader or journey-level employee who observes tasks in progress and upon completion to make sure they are properly performed.
 I have the ability to perform simple duties. I have received training on preparing surfaces to be painted, such as removing scale, rust, grease, dirt and old paint, applying markings, and operating paint equipment.
