Assessment 1
Please read each question and all response options carefully before making your selection. Select the response that best describes your experience. Your resume and supporting application materials must support the qualifications and experience you claim in your responses. If your application materials do not support your responses, you may be found ineligible for further consideration. For purposes of this assessment, journey-level experience means having the trade knowledge and skill to independently perform assigned preservation work using established methods. This includes determining how to carry out the work, selecting and using appropriate tools and materials, and completing the work without close technical instruction, step-by-step guidance, or on-the-job training.
