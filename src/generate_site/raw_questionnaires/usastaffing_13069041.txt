Assessment 1
Specialized Experience for the GS-13, you must have one year (full 52 weeks) of specialized experience at a level of difficulty and responsibility equivalent to the GS-12 grade level in the Federal service. Specialized experience for this position is defined as experience: 

- Providing senior-level administrative management support to executives or senior leadership by advising on and coordinating a broad range of administrative operations, policies, and programs.; AND
- Coordinating travel for senior officials, including functions such as preparing travel authorizations, arranging travel logistics, coordinating with internal and external stakeholders, or ensuring compliance with applicable travel policies and procedures.; AND
- Serving as a liaison with internal and external stakeholders to coordinate executive meetings, events, protocol activities, or high-level engagements involving senior officials.
