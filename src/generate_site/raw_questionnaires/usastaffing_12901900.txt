Applicants must have one year of specialized experience equivalent to at least the next lower grade GS-11 in the healthcare field performing progressively responsible analytical, administrative and clinical management duties. This work may have been performed in an operating health care facility or a higher organizational echelon with advisory or directional authority over such facilities.
