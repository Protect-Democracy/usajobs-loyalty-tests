There are times where interactions or projects with the customer do not go as smoothly as we'd like. When this occurs, what is the best course of action?
 Forward leadership the details of the failure, with comprehensive documentation of where issues occurred and who is at fault. Include as much documentation as possible to ensure necessary action can be taken.

 Focus attention on what DLA did well and ensure any fault can be correlated to a customer action to help diffuse responsibility.
