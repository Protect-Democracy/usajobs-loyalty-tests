Thank you for your interest in an Air Conditioning Equipment Mechanic position with the Department of the Army. We will evaluate your resume and your responses to this assessment questionnaire to determine if you are among the best qualified for this position. Your responses are subject to verification. 1.
From the descriptions below, select the appropriate response based on your level of experience, education, and/or training, that you fully possess and that demonstrates your ability to perform Air Conditioning Equipment Mechanic duties at the WG-08 grade level without more than normal supervision.

 I have the ability to perform the most difficult and complex Air Conditioning Equipment Mechanic tasks. I am skilled in replacing and repairing compressors and related components on industrial air conditioning units and systems. Servicing and repairing evaporators, condensers, and receivers; re-packing valves, replacing electric motors, belts and pulleys, lubricating moving parts, replacing and/or cleaning filters; bleeding systems and replacing refrigerants; testing for leaks and for excessive load conditions; reconditioning, modifying and fabricating parts. I am skilled in installing and relocating industrial size equipment. I am considered an expert and work with a high degree of independence.
 I have the ability to perform the full range of Air Conditioning Equipment Mechanic tasks. I am skilled in installing and recognizing the causes of faulty equipment. Making repairs on a variety of industrial air conditioning units and systems such as reach-in and walk-in refrigerators; cold-storage and cold-room equipment; freezers, water coolers, dehumidifiers, heating equipment, air conditioning units and systems, ventilating systems, and related equipment. I am skilled in Diagnosing and locating malfunctions; disassembling, repairing, replacing, and/or adjusting thermostats, pressure-stats, humidistats, relays, switches, DDC controllers and other automatic or electronic control devices. I work on my own initiative under general supervision subject to occasional inspection.
 I have the ability to perform common Air Conditioning Equipment Mechanic tasks. I can work from oral instructions, work orders, and/or manufacturer's manuals; support senior journeymen mechanics on assignments involving units and systems that are larger and more complex; assist in the removal and/or installation of equipment; and use testing devices including a variety of meters and gauges. I work under normal supervision of a work leader or journey-level employee who observes tasks in progress and upon completion to make sure they are properly performed.
 I have the ability to perform simple Air Conditioning Equipment Mechanic tasks, or I have received training on operating hand tools common to the general refrigeration and air conditioning mechanical trades.
 I do not possess the abilities described above.

From the descriptions below, select the appropriate response based on your level of experience, education, and/or training, that you fully possess and that demonstrates your ability to perform Air Conditioning Equipment Mechanic duties at the WG-09 grade level without more than normal supervision.

 I have the ability to perform the most difficult and complex Air Conditioning Equipment Mechanic tasks. I am skilled in working from oral instructions, work orders, and manufacturer's manuals using testing devices including a variety of meters and gauges; determining causes of faulty equipment; repairing, reconditioning, modifying, and relocating components and equipment; installing and maintaining a variety of commercial domestic air conditioning/heating systems and coolant units; disassembling, cleaning, repairing, soldering, replacing, modifying, reassembling, and adjusting a variety of parts and components; independently installing and/or repairing components of the more complex systems. I work on my own initiative under general supervision subject to occasional inspection.
 I have the ability to perform common Air Conditioning Equipment Mechanic tasks. I can operate multi-meters, dial indicators, megger, regulated pressure gauges, leak detector, refrigerant tables, special purpose thermometers, etc. I can locate malfunctions; replace and/or adjust thermostats, pressure-stats, humidistats, relays, switches, DDC controllers or other automatic or electronic control devices. I can repair units and systems that are larger and more complex.
