Assessment 1
Specialized Experience: Applicants must have at least one year of specialized experience at the GS-13 grade level in the Federal service or equivalent to the GS-13 grade level in the private sector formulating, justifying, and executing  budgets, portfolios, and investments at the organizational level while using expertise and experience that also accounts for OMB policies and guidance,; consulting and advising stakeholders in the planning, programming, budgeting and execution of budgets; providing authoritative advice and guidance on budget strategies; leveraging expert understanding of OMB budget governance and policies, and preparing and coordinating executive-level briefings related to the formulation or execution of a budget program.

Examples include: deciphering accounting information for use in budget execution; preparing budget estimates, exhibits, backup tables, written justifications, and executive-level summaries; managing or monitoring  high-value projects  or investments with specified budgets, timelines, and performance goals; developing OMB budget submissions included Congressional Budget Justification and Apportionment documentation, and analyzing budget execution, spend plans, obligations, contract renewals, or cost-benefit information  to support the budget formulation decisions.
