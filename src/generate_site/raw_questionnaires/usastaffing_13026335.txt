2.
Select the appropriate answer to each of the following questions based on your experience or education that demonstrates your ability to perform the duties of this position.
 I have one year of specialized experience equivalent to at least the GS-9 grade level. The experience involves the recognition, evaluation, corrective actions, and elimination of environmental conditions in the workplace that causes sickness, impaired health, or illness.  This experience demonstrates a professional knowledge of the theory and application of the principles of industrial hygiene and closely related sciences such as physics and engineering controls. Such work involved experience in all of the following areas: the acquisition of quantitative and qualitative data, and the measurement of exposures for a variety of chemical, physical, and biological stresses; the analysis of the data acquired and the prediction of probable effects of exposures on the health and well-being of workers; and the selection and recommendation of appropriate controls, including management, medical, engineering, education or training, and personal protective equipment. I possess one year of specialized experience which equipped me with the knowledge, skills, and abilities to perform the duties of the position. Specialized experience at or equivalent to the GS-9 level includes providing independent Industrial Hygiene services such as written industrial hygiene reports, researching safety, radiation, exposure to agents, respiratory protection program, asbestos control, water safety and testing, accident review infection control and prevention, waste management, construction safety, sterile processing or other areas as related to industrial hygiene.
 I have three years of progressively higher level graduate education leading to a Ph.D. degree or Ph.D. or equivalent doctoral degree (TRANSCRIPT REQUIRED)
 I have a combination of successfully completed specialized experience and graduate level education in excess of 2 full years of progressively higher level graduate education . (TRANSCRIPT REQUIRED)
