To qualify at the GS-13 level, you must have one year of specialized experience at a level of difficulty and responsibility equivalent to the GS-12 grade level in the federal service or equivalent. Examples of specialized experience for this position include experience in performance auditing, inspections, or evaluations that demonstrate knowledge of accounting and auditing principles, methods, and techniques. Experience must include applying auditing standards such as GAGAS and QSIE; planning audits (including research, risk assessments, and development of audit steps); collecting and analyzing data; and preparing work papers that document findings.
