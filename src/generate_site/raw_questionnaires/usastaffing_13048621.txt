Assessment 1
QUALIFICATION REQUIREMENTS: To qualify for this position, you must meet the qualification requirements outlined below:

SPECIALIZED EXPERIENCE: To be minimally qualified for this position you must have at least one (1) year of specialized experience equivalent to the next lower level payband or GS grade in the normal line of progression. Specialized experience would include one year of substantive supervisory or program management experience at the GS-13 or GS-14 level or equivalent level manager or comparable management or leadership experiences outside of Federal service. To be qualifying for this position your experience should be sufficient to demonstrate:
Examining documents, records, accounting reports, and processes to ensure compliance with accounting and auditing principles, laws, and regulation
Providing technical briefing to leadership on matters involving statutory provisions regarding accounting and auditing principles laws and regulation.
Partnering with internal and/or external stakeholders and implemented program improvements to increase voluntary compliance (i.e., policy, tax forums, headquarters, etc.).
Identifiying and developed fraud trends and/or areas of noncompliance.
Providing technical guidance on communications, liaison activities, public outreach initiatives, and communication program requirements to support consistent program implementation.
Conducting comprehensive compliance reviews and examinations of municipal debt portfolios to ensure strict adherence to federal statutory provisions under the Internal Revenue Code.
Leveraging advanced knowledge of public finance to audit the key structural elements of municipal bonds, verifying the tax status of qualified issuers and evaluating the impact of private business use and purpose investments on the retention of tax-exempt status.
Coordinating projects involving multiple organizations or stakeholders by facilitating meetings, resolving project issues, monitoring project milestones, and communicating project status and recommendations.
Implementing process improvement initiatives by developing or applying project management methodologies, performance measures, or process improvement techniques to improve operational efficiency and service delivery.
