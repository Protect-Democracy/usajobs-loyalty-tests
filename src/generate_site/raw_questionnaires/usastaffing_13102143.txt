MANDATORY ENTRY AGE (MEA) Applicants for this position must be at least 21 years of age, AND have not reached their 37th birthday prior to the closing date of this job opportunity announcement and upon appointment to this position. The maximum entry age of 37 applies to this position. This is a primary position covered by enhanced retirement provisions which stipulate mandatory retirement at age 57 after 20 years of service (5 U.S.C. 3307 for Federal Law Enforcement positions). Acceptable exceptions : 1.) An applicant has prior Federal service in a primary covered position sufficient to complete 20 years of service on or before the mandatory retirement age of 57; OR 2.) An applicant is a qualified veterans' preference eligible, in which case the MEA is waived and their corresponding retirement age may be higher than 57 after completing 20 years of service; OR 3.) An applicant is in receipt of an official Maximum Entry Age Waiver Memorandum approved by the National Park Service, Director of Law Enforcement AND is under the age of 40 years. Please note: - If you are claiming to meet the exception for the mandatory maximum entry age based on prior Federal service in a primary covered position sufficient to complete 20 years of service on or before the mandatory retirement age of 57, you must include the appropriate SF-50s with your application that clearly document the periods during which you served in a primary covered position. - If you are claiming to meet the exception as a qualified veterans' preference eligible, you must include the appropriate veterans' preference documentation with your application. - If you are claiming to meet the exception because you are in receipt of an official Maximum Entry Age Waiver Memorandum approved by the National Park Service, Director of Law Enforcement, you must include the completed/signed waiver with your application. ** If you do not submit the required documentation for any acceptable exceptions listed above, you will be deemed ineligible for failing to meet the mandatory entry age requirements.
