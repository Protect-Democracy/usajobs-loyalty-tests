1.
Applicants must have at least one full year (52 weeks) of specialized experience which is in or directly related to the line of work of this position. Specialized experience must demonstrate experience in ALL areas defined below:
Leading and coordinating large-scale Agile delivery across multiple Agile Release Trains (ARTs) within a Solution Train, applying principles of the Scaled Agile Framework.
Driving alignment between solution delivery, strategic objectives, and Lean Portfolio Management priorities to ensure end-to-end value realization.
Managing cross-ART dependencies, risks, and impediments, and facilitating resolutions to enable integrated solution delivery.
Facilitating Solution Train events (e.g., PI Planning, Pre- and Post-PI Planning, and Inspect & Adapt) to ensure synchronization and continuous improvement.
Leading data-driven continuous improvement efforts using flow, quality, and delivery metrics to optimize performance and delivery outcomes.
