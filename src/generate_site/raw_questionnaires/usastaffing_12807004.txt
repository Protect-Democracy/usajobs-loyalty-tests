I have performed these duties with some direction, oversight, and/or regular inspections. I am able to troubleshoot to solve problems and accomplish work assignments in a safe manner. I have knowledge of most technical trade terminology, tools, and equipment in at least one trade mentioned above. I possess a strong work ethic, dexterity and am able to perform strenuous activity for long periods of time.
 I have been trained in the tasks and fields listed above and can perform most of the common tasks under the supervision of a journey-level work leader who observes task in progress and upon completion to ensure they are properly performed. I possess a strong work ethic, dexterity and am able to perform strenuous activity for long periods of time.
 I am able to perform some of the simple tasks listed above and have some training or experience, but I am willing to learn what I do not know. I possess a strong work ethic, dexterity and am able to perform strenuous activity for long periods of time.
