Assessment 1
Specialized experience for the GS-12: You must have one year of specialized experience at a level of difficulty and responsibility at or equivalent to the GS-11 grade level in the Federal service. Specialized experience for this position
includes:
Accomplishment of IT-related project assignments that require a wide range of knowledge of IT support, deployment and customer service requirements and techniques pertinent to providing IT customer support. This experience is generally demonstrated by analyzing alternative approaches and advising management of any major IT issues, problems and concerns; AND
Experience independently providing various information technology customer and support services. Examples of how this experience is generally demonstrated include:
Imaging, managing and deploying customer workstations, peripherals and applications;
Assessing, submitting, tracking, and managing projects through full change and release management life cycle;
Installing, troubleshooting, and updating certified Commercial-Off-the-Shelf (COTS) and Government-Off-the-Shelf (GOTS) software on workstations;
Performing workstation and application related security reviews and identify actions required;
Generating and analyzing software inventory reports;
Working with management and stakeholders to ensure customer requirements can be met;
Providing technical, project or task leadership, with minimal supervision.
