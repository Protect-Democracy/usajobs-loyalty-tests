GS-0486-07 Wildlife Biologist Min Quals
1.
The GS-0486 Wildlife Biology series has a basic education requirement. Please indicate below how you meet this requirement:
 I possess a degree in biological science that included: (1) at least 9 semester hours in such wildlife subjects as mammalogy, ornithology, animal ecology, wildlife management, or research courses in the field of wildlife biology; and (2) at least 12 semester hours in zoology in such subjects as general zoology, invertebrate zoology, vertebrate zoology, comparative anatomy, physiology, genetics, ecology, cellular biology, parasitology, entomology, or research courses in such subjects (Excess courses in wildlife biology may be used to meet the zoology requirements where appropriate.); and (3) at least 9 semester hours in botany or the related plant sciences.
 I possess a combination of education and experience equivalent to a major in biological science (i.e., at least 30 semester hours), with at least (1) 9 semester hours in wildlife subjects; (2) 12 semester hours in zoology; and (3) 9 semester hours in botany or related plant science, as shown in A above, plus appropriate experience or additional education.
 I do not meet the individual occupational requirements for the GS-0486 Wildlife Biology series.
