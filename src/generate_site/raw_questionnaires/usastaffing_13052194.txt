1.
From the descriptions below, select the appropriate response based on your level of experience, education, and/or training, that you fully possess and that demonstrates your ability to perform Industrial Equipment Mechanic duties without more than normal supervision.

 I have the ability to perform the most difficult and complex tasks including: Independently planning and laying out work for the installation, overhaul, and maintenance of a wide variety of special purpose, one-of-a-kind, or extensively modified industrial machinery and real property (e.g., sewer, water, and boiler plant equipment); Diagnosing complex mechanical, hydraulic, and pneumatic failures—often without the aid of maintenance manuals—and distinguishing these from electrical/electronic failures; Fabricating needed repair parts using lathes and milling machines; Designing and installing machinery foundations, and performing advanced WG-5210-8 Rigging duties (including calculating breaking strains, safe working loads, and splicing cables/slings). I am considered an expert and work with a high degree of independence.
 I have the ability to perform the full range of tasks including: Layout, install, align, overhaul, repair and maintain various types of industrial plant machinery, real property, and systems (mechanical, hydraulic, pneumatic); Disassemble and repair machinery and systems, and determine the nature of repairs using knowledge of mechanical principles, blueprints, and floor plans; Install foundations for machinery using cement and steel, connect power sources (steam, air, fuel), and perform functional tests; Perform standard rigging duties to lift and move heavy equipment, as well as incidental electrical wiring checks and welding. I work on my own initiative under general supervision subject to occasional inspection.
 I have the ability to perform common tasks including: Installing, maintaining, or repairing general industrial plant machinery, equipment, and structural foundations; Interpreting basic specifications to assist in detecting malfunctions and making routine adjustments; Operating ordinary hand, pneumatic, or electric tools (e.g., grinders, sanders) to complete assigned mechanical tasks. I work under normal supervision of a work leader or journey-level employee who observes tasks in progress and upon completion to make sure they are properly performed.
 I have the ability to perform simple tasks or I have received training on this type of work, however, I know little or nothing about the duties identified in "A", "B", or "C" above.
