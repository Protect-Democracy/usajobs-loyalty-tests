Recent Graduate Program Eligibility This position is part of the Federal Pathways Recent Graduates Program. To be eligible, you must meet one of the requirements below. Select the one response that best describes you. Recent graduate : Within the last 2 years , you completed all requirements for one of the following from a qualifying educational institution: a qualifying associate's, bachelor's, master's, professional, or doctoral degree; a vocational or technical degree or certificate; or a qualifying career or technical education program. The 2 years are counted from the date you finished all requirements, such as your last course, exam, or clinical hours. They are not counted from the date your diploma was issued, or your graduation ceremony was held. Your application must be received no later than 2 years after that date. A qualifying educational institution is generally one accredited by an accrediting body recognized by the U.S. Department of Education. OR Veteran with delayed eligibility : You are a veteran, as defined in 5 U.S.C. 2108, and military service kept you from applying during some or all of your 2-year eligibility period. In that case: you have a full 2 years from your release or discharge from active duty to apply; but you must apply within 6 years of completing your degree, certificate, or program. Example: You finished your bachelor's degree in May 2022 while still on active duty and were discharged in March 2025. You are eligible to apply until March 2027, which is 2 years after discharge and still within 6 years of finishing your degree.

Minimum Qualifications: Human Resources Assistant, GS-0203-05 To qualify for this position at the GS-05 grade level, you must meet one of the requirements below. Select the one response that best describes how you meet the minimum qualifications. Specialized experience : One year of specialized experience equivalent to at least the GS-04 grade level in the Federal service. Specialized experience is experience that gave you the knowledge, skills, and abilities to successfully perform the duties of this position. For this position, specialized experience is defined as: Reviewing documents, records, or transactions to verify completeness, accuracy, and compliance with established procedures; Maintaining information in automated systems or databases to support accurate recordkeeping; Applying policies, procedures, instructions, or guidelines to process administrative actions in accordance with organizational requirements; and Identifying discrepancies in records, data, or documentation to support corrective actions and maintain data integrity. Experience may have been gained in Federal, state, local, military, private-sector, nonprofit, or volunteer work, paid or unpaid. One year means 12 months of full-time work (40 hours per week / 2,087 hours a year), or the equivalent in part-time work. OR Education : Successful completion of four years of education above the high school level leading to a bachelor's degree (120 semester hours or 180 quarter hours), or a completed bachelor's degree. OR Combination : A combination of specialized experience and education beyond the second year of college. Only education in excess of the first 60 semester hours (90 quarter hours) can be combined with experience at the GS-05 grade level.
 I have at least one year of specialized experience equivalent to the GS-04 grade level, as described above.
 I have successfully completed four years of education above the high school level leading to a bachelor's degree (120 semester hours or 180 quarter hours), or I have a bachelor's degree.
 I have a combination of specialized experience and education beyond the first 60 semester hours (90 quarter hours) that equals at least 100% of the requirement, as described above.
