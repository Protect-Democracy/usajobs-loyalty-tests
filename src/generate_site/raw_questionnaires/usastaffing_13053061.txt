Quality Ranking Factor This position requires someone with a social science, psychology, and/or practitioner background in the following areas: Behavioral Threat Assessment Integration Drive Integration: Lead the integration of behavioral science best practices into NTER initiatives and the Master Trainer Program curriculum. Research & Analysis: Apply behavioral and social science research principles, concepts, and methods to execute targeted research tasks (e.g., data collection, literature reviews, and scientific literature synthesis). Curriculum Modernization: Consult on and assist with curriculum updates, incorporating feedback from Master Trainers and cutting-edge BTAM research. Impact Reporting: Help articulate and communicate program impact by drafting comprehensive reports, presentations, and materials demonstrating programmatic outcomes to leadership. Stakeholder Engagement & Strategic Partnerships Represent NTER: Serve as a representative for the NTER Program Office with key stakeholders across I&A, the DHS Intelligence Enterprise, the Intelligence Community, and the Homeland Security Enterprise. Partnership Development: Proactively build and maintain new strategic partnerships with academic, public, and private stakeholders outside of DHS to advance threat identification and mitigation methods. Interagency Coordination: Participate in specialized working groups, coordinate with I&A analytic centers, and align threat assessment and violence prevention programs within DHS and the broader Intelligence Community. Technical Assistance: Provide direct technical support and advisory services on threat assessment methodologies to F/SLTT stakeholders. Briefings & Presentations: Design and deliver high-impact presentations and briefings at professional conferences, meetings, and stakeholder engagements.
