Assessment 1
SPECIALIZED EXPERIENCE INSTRUCTIONS

Specialized experience is expertise that prepared you to do the work of the job for which you are applying.  It must be equal to at least the next lower grade level in this line of work.

We can only credit experience you clearly describe in your resume.  This experience can come from a federal, state, local, or private sector job, or military service.  Education or training alone does not count, you must show that you actually performed the duties described in each question below.

We prorate part-time and intermittent work based on a 2,087-hour full-time work year. For example, if you worked 20 hours a week (half-time) for one year, we credit you with six months of full-time experience. You must list the hours you worked per week for each job on your resume. If you don't list your hours, we cannot credit that period of experience.

You must meet all of the specialized experience statements below to qualify for this position.  If your resume does not support one or more statements, you will be found not qualified, even if you meet the other statements.

Review your resume before you answer the questions below.  Make sure it clearly shows the experience needed to support your answers.


1.
GS-14 Specialized Experience Self-Assessment Question

In order to be qualified for this position, candidates must have one year of specialized experience equivalent to or higher than the GS-13 grade level in the Federal service. Specialized experience is defined as:
Evaluating the policies, procedures, or other critical aspects of a defined contribution retirement or employee benefits program to identify areas for improvement and provide recommendations that enhanced program efficiency and participant outcomes;
Leading project or functional teams responsible for executing complex programs and projects, ensuring effective resource allocation, stakeholder engagement, and achievement of organizational objectives; and
Monitoring the performance of contracted service providers ensuring alignment with organizational goals and program requirements, coordinating with stakeholders to ensure timely delivery of products and services.

Select the response that best describes your experience:
 Yes, I possess at least one-year specialized experience equivalent to or higher than the GS-13 grade level described above.
 No, I do not possess the specialized experience described above.
