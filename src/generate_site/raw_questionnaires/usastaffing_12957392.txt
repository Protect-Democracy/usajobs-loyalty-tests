- Providing second-level supervision over Utility Systems Repairer-Operator Supervisors responsible for the operation, maintenance, and repair of high-pressure boiler systems, chilled water systems, and co-generation equipment
- Planning, directing, and controlling plant operations across multiple shifts through subordinate supervisors to ensure safe, reliable, and efficient utility services
- Assigning and reviewing work, establishing priorities, and evaluating performance of subordinate supervisors to ensure effective execution of plant operations
- Monitoring system performance and directing corrective actions to maintain continuous and stable plant operations
Ensuring compliance with established procedures, manufacturer specifications, and applicable regulatory requirements
- Coordinating plant operations with internal organizations and external utility providers to support operational requirements and continuity of service
