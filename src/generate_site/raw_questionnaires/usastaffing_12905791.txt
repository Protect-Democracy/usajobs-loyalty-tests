 I have the ability to perform the most difficult and complex duties of a Painter. I independently perform common and unique task including: matching, tinting, texturing and blending coating materials. I use special finishing techniques by using coating materials that are prepared in special ways. I am able to build up, shape, and level surfaces with synthetic resins, plastics, patching compounds, and drywall plaster. I am able to read and interpret written instructions and specifications. I plan my work sequences and track labor hours, tools, and materials costs. I select proper tools and otherwise carry out assignments through to completion. My work is reviewed in terms of overall results achieved. I am consulted by other painters because of my expertise in applying materials so that surfaces meet match, texture, decorative, sheen, and level requirements. I am considered an expert and work with a high degree of independence.
 I have the ability to perform the full range duties as a Painter. I have experience in mixing, matching, tinting and blending coating materials. I am able to apply a smooth finish to surfaces that meet the proper mil thickness requirements. My work is performed using a variety of tools and equipment. My work is assigned in broad general instructions covering the scope of the task assigned. I work on my own initiative under general supervision subject to occasional inspection.
 I have the ability to perform common duties as a Painter. I have experience in painting various surfaces ensuring for a smooth surface. I can mix, match, and tint paint. I apply fillers to rough surfaces. I check requirements written instructions; and use a variety of tools. I work under normal supervision of a work leader or journey-level employee who observes tasks in progress and upon completion to make sure they are properly performed.
 I have the ability to perform simple duties as a Painter or I have received training on the use of basic hand tools.
