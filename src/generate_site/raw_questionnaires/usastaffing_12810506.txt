Assessment 1
For the GS-14: You must have one year of specialized experience at a level of difficulty and responsibility equivalent to the GS-13 grade level in the Federal service. Specialized experience for this position is defined as experience in ALL of the following:
- Experience developing custom code in Structured Query Language (SQL) and Python programming languages that perform Extract Transform and Load (ETL) procedures to intake financial information from SharePoint, Oracle, and/or other financial feeder systems; AND
- Experience utilizing government financial regulations and accounting best practices to automate cost allocations, populate financial agreement forms, and calculate associated billing through financial systems (i.e. custom Python and SQL code); AND
- Experience developing custom labor projections in SQL combining data from human resources and financial data sets that duplicates the US Tax Code and/or other governmental pay adjustments; AND
- Experience developing data collection in SharePoint to capture financial information not available in the financial system or associated feeder systems.
