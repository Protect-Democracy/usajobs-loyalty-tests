Which statement best describes your ability to perform the work of a Preservationist, WG-4701-10, without more than normal supervision?
Note- Your resume and application materials must support the experience you select. Failure to provide supporting information may result in a lower rating or removal from further consideration.
 I have the ability to independently perform the full range of difficult and complex Preservationist work without more than normal supervision. I provide technical recommendations on methods, tools, and materials; plan and lay out work assignments; and determine the materials and workforce needed to complete projects. I have experience performing carpentry, masonry, roofing, and painting work on buildings. I have repaired, reconstructed, restored, or rehabilitated wood structures and building components such as frameworks, rafters, doors, windows, floors, staircases, trusses, beams, trim, and roofing. I have experience performing both rough and finish carpentry, restoring historic properties, and reading construction documents, sketches, and working drawings to complete preservation and restoration projects.
 I have the ability to perform the full range of Preservationist work with a high degree of independence. I have experience performing many of the duties described in Option A, including carpentry, masonry, roofing, and painting work on buildings and structures. I am able to plan and lay out work assignments, select and use appropriate tools and materials, maintain and repair trade tools and equipment, and read construction documents, sketches, and working drawings to complete assigned projects.
 I have the ability to perform Preservationist work under normal supervision. I have experience performing carpentry, masonry, roofing, and painting work on buildings and structures. I can use, clean, and maintain trade tools and equipment, follow written instructions, and read basic sketches and working drawings. My work is reviewed by a supervisor or higher-level employee.
 I do not have the experience or training required to perform Preservationist duties.
