Specialized Experience:

GS-13 Level:
Specialized Experience:
To qualify for the GS-13 level, you must possess at least one (1) year of specialized experience equivalent to the GS-12 grade level in the Federal government or public/private sector equivalent experience. For this position, specialized experience is defined as:
In-depth ability to effectively communicate and coordinate with and advise management officials, program managers and specialists, contractor personnel, and representatives of other agencies; to reconcile differing or conflicting viewpoints; to organize information and present statements of position; and, to write succinctly and clearly.
Understands and applies knowledge of collection strategies, management processes, systems and tools. Incumbent effectively employs CI/HUMINT collection strategies and processes to inform the USIC of potential CI threats to the DHS and USG.
Maintains a thorough understanding of DHSs responsibility and appropriate roles within the Intelligence Community, in order to build interagency cooperation and facilitate the coordination of resources. Coordinates with Federal, state, and municipal civilian law enforcement counterparts for intelligence fusion and situational awareness of terrorist groups and FIS activities for both general awareness and for potential DHS jurisdiction.
Ability to communicate moderately complex ideas both written and verbally in order to formulate and present findings, briefings, project papers, status reports, and correspondence to foster understanding and acceptance of findings and recommendations.
Strong customer service and interpersonal skills in order to maintain effective working relationships with both internal and external customers.
Advanced knowledge of DHS I&A homeland security intelligence mission and the role of both raw and finished intelligence reporting in the relationship between DHS, State/Local governments and the Intelligence Community.
