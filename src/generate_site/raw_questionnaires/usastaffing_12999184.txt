Assessment 1

Specialized Experience for the GS-09 grade level is defined as one year of experience at the GS-07 grade level, or equivalent, that is typically in or related to the position and which equipped the candidate with the particular knowledge, skills, and abilities to successfully perform the duties of this position. Specialized experience for this position is defined as experience:

Utilizing a wide range of systems or databases to search beyond readily accessible material to locate, analyze, index, and manage records; AND
Performing record maintenance and preservation in adherence with archival principles, procedures and record keeping practices; AND
Providing customer service and guidance with record research, reference and/or access services.


OR

You may substitute education for specialized experience as follows: Master's or equivalent graduate degree; OR 2 full years of progressively higher-level graduate education leading to such a degree or LL.B. or J.D., if related;

OR

You may qualify by a combination of experience and education. Options for qualifying based on a combination will be identified in the online questions. The total percentage of experience at the required grade level compared to the requirement, as well as the percentage of completed education compared to the requirement must equal at least 100 percent. Only graduate level education in excess of the first 18 semester hours (27 quarter hours) may be combined to be considered for qualifying education.
