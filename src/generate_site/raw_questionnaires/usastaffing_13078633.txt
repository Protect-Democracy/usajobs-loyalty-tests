You may be eligible for ICTAP if: You are a current federal employee who meets the definition of a "surplus" or "displaced employee—you've received official notice that your job is no longer needed or that you will lose your job by Reduction-in-Force (RIF); AND The agency you're applying to is accepting applications from outside of their workforce; AND The job you're applying to is in the local commuting area; AND You are applying to a position that does not have greater promotion potential than the position from which you were separated from; AND You meet the qualifications and other requirements of the job you're applying for.
