 I have the ability to perform the most difficult and complex Crane Operator such as: operating mobile boom cranes of various boom lengths, angles and positions in areas in which maneuverability is restricted to load/unload/handle material which includes combat vehicles, equipment, containers, lumber and loose materials and making lift capacity judgments while changing positions to make precise load placements. I am skilled in working from hand signals and radio transmissions when load is not in the range of vision; working as a rigger in crane operations planning, laying out, assembling, repairing, installing weight handling gear and rigging required in lifting, moving and positioning heavy equipment and machinery; operating a variety of heavy equipment including graders or dozers and construction equipment; and receiving work from written instructions, drawings and sketches. I am considered an expert and work with a high degree of independence.
 I have the ability to perform the full range of Crane Operator duties such as: operating mobile boom cranes to load/unload/handle material which includes combat vehicles, equipment, containers, lumber and loose materials; using hand signals and radio transmissions when required; and crane operations planning, laying out, assembling, repairing, installing weight handling gear and rigging required in lifting, moving and positioning heavy equipment and machinery. I am skilled in operating a variety of heavy equipment including graders or dozers and construction equipment. and receiving work from written instructions, drawings and sketches. I work on my own initiative under general supervision subject to occasional inspection.
 I have the ability to perform common Crane Operator duties such as: operating a variety of cranes with different lifting capacities to move large items in congested areas and confined spaces and operating a variety of heavy mobile equipment, such as backhoes and dozers in confined areas. and planning, selecting, and installing rigging gear for crane operations. I work under normal supervision of a work leader or journey-level employee who observes tasks in progress and upon completion to make sure they are properly performed.
