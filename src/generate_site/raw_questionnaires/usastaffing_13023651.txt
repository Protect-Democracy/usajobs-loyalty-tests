2.
SPECIALIZED EXPERIENCE GS-13
To qualify for the GS-13 level, you must possess at least one (1) full year of specialized experience equivalent in the level of difficulty and responsibility to that of the next lower grade level (GS-12) in Federal Service or equivalent private/public sector experience. For this position, specialized experience is defined as the following:
Reviewing complex procurement actions and identifying compliance issues, risks, and opportunities for improvement;
Conducting and evaluating market research;
Developing guidance, procedures, reports, dashboards, or other products that improve acquisition processes; AND
Applying knowledge of statutory and regulatory requirements when making procurement decisions.

3.
SPECIALIZED EXPERIENCE GS-14
To qualify for the GS-14 level, you must possess at least one (1) full year of specialized experience equivalent in the level of difficulty and responsibility to that of the next lower grade level (GS-13) in Federal Service or equivalent private/public sector experience. For this position, specialized experience is defined as the following:
Applying qualitative and quantitative analytical methods to assess program effectiveness, identify trends and risks, and develop recommendations for improving acquisition outcomes;
Developing and modifying policies related to acquisition; 
Evaluating acquisition strategies, assessing compliance, and providing authoritative guidance on small business considerations; AND
Analyzing, interpreting, and applying requirements to complex acquisition programs based on knowledge of Federal acquisition laws, regulations, and policies.
