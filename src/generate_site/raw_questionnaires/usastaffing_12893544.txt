In addition to the above Basic Requirements, to qualify for this position at the GS-09 grade level, you must possess at least one of the following minimum qualifications by close of the announcement: one year of specialized experience equivalent to at least the GS-07 grade level which is directly related to the work of this position, and which has equipped the applicant with the knowledge, skills, and abilities to successfully perform the duties of the position. For this position, specialized experience includes: use ecological principles, natural systems ecology, and natural resources management; ecologist tests and implements scientific monitoring protocols to identify, evaluate, and do long-term monitoring of ecosystem components, such as flora and fauna, and ecological processes; provide ecological studies support; collection of natural resource field data; identify animal and plant species; utilize technical instruments, manuals, and protocols; conducts scientifically credible protection, mitigation, and restoration projects; knowledge of biological ecosystems including soils, aquatic systems, native flora, and fauna, and their associated habitat requirements in order to effectively implement scientifically credible inventory, monitoring, mitigation, and protection programs; knowledge of data collection techniques, including installation, operation, and maintenance of testing and sampling equipment; collection, data entry and database management, and specialized software programs essential to resource
management activities.
