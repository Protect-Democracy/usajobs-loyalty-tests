What risk does this present to Operational Support and Contingency objectives?

10.
A major threat intelligence feed and the vendor report a critical, actively exploited zero-day vulnerability affecting a core component of your system. Internal policy mandates remediation of critical vulnerabilities within 14 days, but the official vendor patch will not be released for three weeks. What is the ISSO's correct course of action?

 Disconnect the entire enterprise system from the network immediately without informing the business or system owners.
