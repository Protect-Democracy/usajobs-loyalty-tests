Assessment 1
For the GS-11, specialized experience is one (1) year of experience at the GS-09 level, or equivalent that is directly related to the position and which has equipped the candidate with the particular knowledge, skills, and abilities to successfully perform the duties of the position. Specialized experience includes researching and applying policy and procedures in order to assist or participate in conducting claims research analysis and making recommendations to improve work processes and programs.
Examples of such experience could include:
- Processing ACH (Automated Clearing House) non-receipt claims for variety of agencies; OR
- Assisting or leading projects in a customer service environment; OR
- Gathering supporting information to analyze workflow; assist in changing workflow, and work assignments; OR
- Communicating effectively with internal and external customers at all levels.
OR
You may substitute education for specialized experience as follows: Ph.D. or equivalent doctoral degree or 3 full years of progressively higher level graduate education leading to such a degree.
or
LL.M., if related
- You may qualify by a combination of experience and education. Only graduate level education in excess of the first 36 semester hours (54 quarter hours) may be combined to be considered for qualifying education.
