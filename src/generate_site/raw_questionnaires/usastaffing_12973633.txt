The Office of Human Resources and Administration, Office of the Chief Human Capital Officer, is seeking seasoned  HR Specialists to provide leadership and support for key enterprise initiatives. This role is for managerial professionals with strong technical expertise who can take on complex HR program areas with minimal onboarding. Selected individuals will leverage their advanced knowledge to oversee national HR projects, guide policy development, and promote consistency and excellence across HR programs. This assignment is well-suited for leaders prepared to operate with a high degree of independence in a dynamic, mission-driven environment.
