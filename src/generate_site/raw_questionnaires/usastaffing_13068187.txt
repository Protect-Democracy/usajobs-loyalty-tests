 I have the ability to perform the most difficult and complex tasks such as I have comprehensive journeyman level knowledge and practical skill across multiple mechanical, electrical, and construction trades. I can independently plan, lay out, install, troubleshoot, and overall electrical infrastructure up to 500 volts, power generation equipment, commercial kitchen appliances, and lightning protection systems using complex schematics and wiring diagrams. I have extensive knowledge of refrigeration cycles, EPA standards for refrigerant recovery and charging, and HVAC preventative maintenance. I can calculate dimensions, fabricate and modify complex piping networks (water, sewer, steam, bottled gas) as well as service lift stations and backflow preventers. I have journeymen level structural carpentry, masonry and metal roofing skills. I can operate heavy construction machinery e.g. backhoes, loaders, dump trucks with plows to clear terrain and maintain perimeter security. I am considered an expert and work with a high degree of independence.
 I have the ability to perform the full range of tasks such as routine and moderately complex work in multiple trades with some independence. I provide troubleshooting, planning, and system modifications with higher level confirmation. Installs circuits, conduit, basic electrical devices; performs routine repairs; reads simple wiring diagrams. Performs standard HVAC maintenance and minor repairs; assists with diagnostics on more complex systems. Installs and repairs domestic plumbing lines and performs basic pipefitting work. Performs basic carpentry, masonry, drywall, and painting with some independence. Operates light to medium construction equipment; supports snow removal and grounds tasks. Recognizes standard malfunctions and selects appropriate tools for typical tasks. Still requires oversight for advanced repairs, major system layouts, and code sensitive work. I work on my own initiative under general supervision subject to occasional inspection.
 I have the ability to perform some tasks that I can work at least two trades such as electrical, HVAC, plumbing, or carpentry trades under supervision of a work leader or journey-level employee who reviews my work for completion. I can follow detailed instructions and use standard tools. Handle basic electrical repairs, switches, outlets, simple fixtures and assist with more complex systems. Perform routine HVAC maintenance, filters, belts, cleaning coils, and assist with minor repairs. Conduct basic plumbing tasks, cutting pipe, simple domestic water/drain repairs. Perform simple carpentry, painting, patching, and masonry under direction. Operate light equipment and may receive introductory training on larger machinery. Requires guidance for troubleshooting and trade method selection. Work leader or journey-level employee reviews my work for completion.
 I have the ability to perform common tasks such as: Performs basic, routine maintenance tasks in one or more trades such as electrical, plumbing, HVAC, carpentry, or grounds/utility support. Assists higher grade workers by carrying tools and materials, preparing work sites, and performing simple steps of larger projects. Completes straightforward repairs such as replacing switches, outlets, light fixtures, simple valves, filters, belts, gaskets, or worn hardware. Conducts basic troubleshooting using established checklists; identifies obvious problems and reports findings to a higher grade mechanic. Performs simple HVAC tasks such as cleaning coils, replacing filters, checking thermostat operation, or assisting with simple component swaps. Assists in basic plumbing duties such as clearing minor clogs, tightening fittings, replacing traps, or helping install simple fixtures. Completes entry level carpentry tasks including patching drywall, repairing trim, minor framing assistance, and basic painting or finishing. Operates common tools and small equipment safely; may use basic power tools under supervision. Follows detailed work instructions, safety requirements, and technical guidance. I work under close supervision of a work leader or journey-level employee who observes tasks in progress and upon completion to make sure they are properly performed.
 I have the ability to perform simple repair tasks, using hand tools and following directions or I have received training on maintenance repairs.
