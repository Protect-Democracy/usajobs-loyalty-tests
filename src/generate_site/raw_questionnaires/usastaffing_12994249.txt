Specialized Experience
You must possess the specialized experience equivalent to the next lower grade level in federal service (GS-14) or public or private sector equivalent. For this position, specialized experience is defined as:
Provide leadership and strategic direction for the planning, execution, and continuous assessment of complex counterintelligence operations, collection, and investigations ensuring the integration of technical resources to proactively detect, deter, and neutralize foreign intelligence threats and insider risks targeting the organization's sensitive assets and threats to SLTTP partners.
Lead the development, implementation, and enforcement of comprehensive investigative protocols and methodologies ensuring all activities are conducted in strict compliance with applicable legal, regulatory, and policy requirements, while maximizing operational effectiveness and the integrity of counterintelligence investigations.
Represent the Counterintelligence Program in high-level interagency forums and strategic partnerships, advocating for counterintelligence priorities, negotiating collaborative agreements, and ensuring effective coordination with senior officials across government.
Support the DHS Counterintelligence Director (DCID) in setting and executing the strategic vision for the counterintelligence program, translating organizational priorities into actionable plans, and ensuring the effective allocation of resources to meet mission objectives and respond to evolving national security threats.
Act as the principal advisor to the DCID, providing expert recommendations on counterintelligence policy, program priorities, and resource management, and ensuring the Director's intent is operationalized across all program activities.
NOTE: Your resume must explicitly indicate how you meet this requirement, otherwise you will be found ineligible.
