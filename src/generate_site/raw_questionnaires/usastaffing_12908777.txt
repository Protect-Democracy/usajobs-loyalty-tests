Select the responses that most accurately demonstrate your ability to perform the duties of an Air Conditioning Equipment Mechanic, WG-5306-11. All responses are subject to verification. NOTE: Your resume must document specific duties, hours per week, and employment dates (MM/YYYY) to verify your qualifications.


1.
From the descriptions below, select the appropriate response based on your level of experience, education, or training, that demonstrates your Ability to do the work of an Air Conditioning Equipment Mechanic Leader without more than normal supervision; set work priorities, demonstrate proper work and safety procedures, installing new split and package HVAC systems; diagnosing and repairing electrical problems, replacing compressors, condenser fans, blower wheels, bearings, and other HVAC system components; use plans and blueprints to locate and repair HVAC system mechanical, electric/electronic components.  (Screen out)
 I have the ability to perform complex duties including working as a leader in a group or team. Establish deadlines and priorities and lead subordinate employees in tasks to include planning, laying out, installing, troubleshooting, repairing, maintaining, and overhauling commercial air conditioning units/systems of various cooling capacities, refrigeration systems, heat pumps, ventilation equipment, and air compressors units. I pass on to other workers instructions received by the supervisor and assign immediate tasks to be performed. I check the work in progress and, I check for compliance with the supervisor's instructions. I answer any workers' questions about procedures, policies, or instructions. I make complex repairs including installing and fitting connecting rods, crankshafts, piston rings, bearings, and bushings; overhauling valves by adjusting or replacing gaskets, springs, floats diaphragms, valve fittings, seals, and couplings; aligning motors and flywheel drives.
 I have the ability to perform the full range of experience leading subordinate employees. I lead team members by scheduling work and preventive maintenance to include repair or replacement and maintenance of major components of HVAC systems such as chilled water and split system air-cooled condensers, heat recovery systems, compressors, water distribution pumps, air handlers, fan coil units; calculate airflow, and the pressure-temperature characteristics of different systems to locate and repair faulty equipment; changing compressor oil, cleaning cooling towers, lubing motors, and pumps. I am skilled in performing a wide range of maintenance and repair tasks for various capacities of HVAC systems and components, refrigeration systems, heat pumps, air compressors, and heat reclaimers. I perform troubleshooting tasks utilizing building automated systems to diagnose problems with direct digital control as well as mechanical components.
 I have the ability to perform common lead duties, set work priorities, demonstrate proper work and safety procedures, install new split and package HVAC systems, diagnose and repair electrical problems, and replace compressors, condenser fans, blower wheels, bearings, and other HVAC system components. I use plans and blueprints to locate and repair HVAC system mechanical, and electric/electronic components.
 I have the ability to perform simple tasks related to HVAC and refrigeration systems maintenance, such as replacing filters, cleaning condensing and evaporative coils, lubing electrical and pump motors, checking refrigerant pressures, and cleaning condensation pans.
 I do not possess the abilities described above.
