 I have the ability to perform the most difficult and complex Heating Repairer duties. I am skilled in installing, troubleshooting, and repairing a variety of small and domestic heating units and systems, to include oil and gas fired hot air furnaces, low pressure hot water and steam boilers, and other equipment with comparable arrangements of heat sources, controls, and circulating methods. Trace malfunctions, replace safety devices, thermostats, heating element switches magnetic coils. Disassemble small electrical motors and replace bearings and brushes, frayed wires; and clean, grease and reassemble. Perform tests to locate leaks on low-pressure gas lines. Make repairs to gas lines including installation of new piping. Perform preventive maintenance to heating units and systems before the heating season starts to insure that all units are in proper working order. Replace major portions of the heating, install, adjust, and repair auxiliary equipment such as motors, blower, pilots, igniters, and thermostat controls. I am considered an expert and work with a high degree of independence.
