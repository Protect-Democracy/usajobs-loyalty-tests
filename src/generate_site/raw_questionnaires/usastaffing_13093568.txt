1.
Select each role for which your existing background, experience, and skill set provide a strong foundation. Astronaut training will prepare you for a specific mission assignment; however, not every astronaut will train for every role. Your selections below will be informational only to NASA's Rating Panel and will not affect your eligibility to apply for Astronaut Candidacy.
 Commercial LEO Destination Commander--Duties include: Lead a multinational crew aboard a commercial habitat/laboratory; Direct safe habitat operations, research activities, and mission priorities; Coordinate NASA, international-partner, and commercial objectives
 LEO Spacecraft Commander/Pilot--Duties include: Command/pilot a complex spacecraft; Lead rendezvous, docking, undocking, re-entry, and related flight operations; Direct the crew during nominal and off-nominal situations
 Artemis Spacecraft Commander/Pilot--Duties include: Same roles/responsibilities as a LEO Spacecraft Commander/Pilot but on lunar missions; Lead long-duration, higher-risk operations with greater crew autonomy; Rendezvous and docking with a lunar lander
 Lunar Lander Commander/Pilot--Duties include: Command/pilot a lunar lander during approach, descent, landing, and low-altitude operations; Operate automated systems and assume manual control if required; Execute landing, abort, surface-departure, and other contingency procedures
 Lunar Surface Base or Expedition Commander--Duties include: Lead a lunar surface crew, habitat, or expedition; Direct construction, logistics, surface operations, and scientific objectives; Coordinate exploration, field science, robotics, and extravehicular activities; Direct the crew during nominal and off-nominal situations
