5.
Must possess at least 12 months experience or training which has provided knowledge of mobile equipment construction and how all components and parts are installed and determine the total extent of damage and repair alternatives. Experience in the use of gas, arc, and welding equipment to cut out damaged or deteriorated areas, remove rusted nuts and bolts, strengthen brackets and braces, weld simple cuts and breaks, and apply heat to bend, reshape damaged areas. Experience in the use of trade equipment. Experience applying body filler to build up depressed areas, and the skill to smooth by grinding and sanding, until the contour is uniform with the adjoining areas. Experience applying primers paint with brush or spray gun, gravity feed and HVLP (high volume low pressure) equipment. Experience in mathematical calculations and scribe patterns in the fabrication of wood or metal parts having straight lines or irregular curves.

1.
Ability to determine the methods, equipment, materials, and techniques required to repair damaged mobile equipment bodies and sheet metal components, or provide replacements.


2.
Knowledge and ability to apply different types of body filler applications, and the skill to smooth by grinding and sanding, until the contour is uniform with adjoining areas.


3.
Skill in using hammers, dolly blocks, spoons, bending irons, mallets, sanders, grinders, files, and hydraulic jacks to remove dents and creases in mobile equipment body components.


4.
Knowledge of basic electrical systems to remove, splice, insulate, solder, replace, or cut defective wiring on mobile equipment.


5.
Knowledge of metal body construction to determine the extent of damage and how various components and parts are installed, i.e., bolted, soldered, riveted, or welded.


6.
Ability to determine which welding process is needed when repairing automotive, heavy equipment, or military vehicle body sheet metal and structural components.
