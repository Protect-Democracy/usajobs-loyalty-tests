Assessment 1
Selective Factor: Wildlife Handling (turtle or other animal handling experience)


1.
This position requires at least three months of experience assisting or leading sea turtle programs or other programs which required animal handling. Required wildlife experience includes safely handling and working around wildlife, and collecting data and samples from wildlife. Must be able to collect morphologic data on nesting sea turtles during night and day operations after receiving on-the-job-training, this includes reading and applying flipper tags, collecting carapace measurements, collecting biological samples and conducting nest excavations. This experience must be documented in your resume.

 I have at least three months of experience assisting or leading sea turtle programs or other programs which required animal handling.
 I do not meet any of the requirements outlined above.


Selective Factor: Motorboat Operation


2.
This position requires operation of small vessels up to 28 feet in length in open water environments. You must be able to demonstrate the ability to safely navigate a small vessel in remote locations with exposure to the ocean from all sides. Rough seas, frequent thunderstorms, and high winds can make vessel operation challenging. Vessel operations including beaching and live boat operations with SCUBA divers is a common requirement. This experience must be documented in your resume.

 I currently possess certification of completion of the Department of the Interior Motorboat Operator Certification Course (MOCC) or an acceptable training program which meets the MOCC standard and have substantial demonstrated experience operating small vessels up to 28 feet in length.
 I do not currently possess certification of completion of Motorboat Operator Certification Course but have previously operated small vessels up to 28 feet in length as part of my job duties.
 I have safely operated small vessels up to 28 feet in length for personal use.
