Assessment 1
 For the GS-15, you must have one year of specialized experience at a level of difficulty and responsibility equivalent to the GS-14 level in the Federal service. Specialized experience is defined as
experience assessing organizational compliance of the Integrity electronic financial disclosure filing system with related laws, regulations and policies, and making recommendations for improvement for non-compliant program areas.

Some examples of this experience may include:
- Evaluating compliance policies and procedures.
- Reporting program level information regarding the Integrity electronic financial disclosure filing system to internal and external leadership on the status of compliance efforts and programs.
- Planning and conducting compliance reviews, identifying non-compliant situations, and recommending remediation.
