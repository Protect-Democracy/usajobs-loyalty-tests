Assessment 1
Please answer the questionnaire below.


Specialized Experience - Must have at least 1-year specialized experience equivalent to at least the next lower grade, GS-07. Experience analyzing, developing, evaluating to improve supply programs, policies, procedures, techniques, etc. Experience involving the control of supply stocks and accounting for equipment. Knowledge of supply functions, cataloging, maintenance, and disposal procedures. Experience in cost estimates, budgets, or funding management. Experience conducting extensive searches for required information; reconstruct records for complex supply transactions; and/or provide supply operations support. Experience requiring person-to-person contacts to convey information and ability to compile reports, letters, memoranda, etc. Formulates and recommends operating procedures to achieve optimum support to the logistics program on matters pertaining to property accountability. Develops and provides data for property accountability of supported units/activities. Directs and provides written technical guidance to unit/activity representatives involving property book procedures and functions. Analyzes results of property book reconciliations to determine if systemic problems exist. Analyze trends for effectiveness and efficiency.
