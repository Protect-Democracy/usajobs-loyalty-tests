My experience includes thorough knowledge of all components of air operations concepts, principles and methodology, and be able to apply knowledge to develop new methods, approaches, or procedures to ensure mission planning is accurate, complete and above reproach. Knowledge and understanding of all aspects relating to offensive/defensive employment of unit's multiple aircraft is also necessary. Incumbents must have an operational knowledge of DoD, DAF, FAA and ICO regulations and directives, and DoD, DAF, and joint doctrine as they apply to air operations, compliance in aircraft operation, safety of flight, and CONUS/OCONUS agency and command relationships. The position utilizes technical expertise and experience when dealing with extremely broad and complex special air operations missions and must be capable of integrating fixed wing aircraft movements to achieve a tasked objective. This includes the development of aircrew mission packages, refueling requirements, and mission flight following procedures.
