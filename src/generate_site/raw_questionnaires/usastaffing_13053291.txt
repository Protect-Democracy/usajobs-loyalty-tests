7.
Medical Records Technician (HIT), GS-7 (Full Performance Level). To qualify for the GS7, you must have
Experience. One year of creditable experience equivalent to the next lower grade level (GS-6)
AND

Demonstrated Knowledge, Skills, and Abilities. In addition to the experience above, the candidate must demonstrate all of the following KSAs:
Ability to judge whether medical records contain sufficient information for regulatory requirements; are acceptable as legal documents; are adequate for continuity of patient care; and support education and research needs. This includes the ability to take appropriate actions if medical record contents are not adequate, accurate, timely, and/or reliable;
 Skill in using appropriate software and data applications in order to analyze and record documentation trends;
 Skill in communicating with individuals or groups who may be uncooperative, or skeptical, in order to obtain the desired effect, such as gaining compliance with established policies and regulations;
Ability to manage priorities and coordinate work in order to complete duties in an accurate and timely fashion. This includes the ability to follow-up on pending issues and an understanding of the impact of incomplete work;
Ability to work independently to accomplish a variety of responsibilities including setting priorities, organizing and adjusting workflows, and utilizing problem-solving techniques in the accomplishment of work; and
Knowledge of qualitative and/or quantitative methods for assessment and improvement of medical record functions.
Ability to review, analyze, abstract, compile, maintain, and extract information from the health record. Check for completeness and accuracy of the health record, and monitor and report for regulatory compliance. Knowledge of the theories, principles, and techniques of medical record documentation practices in inpatient and outpatient records is utilized to analyze records for adequacy, omissions, and/or inconsistencies, timeliness and completeness;
Must have knowledge of VHA Handbook 1907.1, JCAHO standards, Medical Staff Bylaws, Rules and Regulations, FOIA, Privacy Act, Medicare's Conditions of Participation and other resources related to record completion and confidentiality of records in order to protect the privacy of the patient and compare the documentation with acceptable standards;
 Must have knowledge of the internal organization of patient's record such as proper forms, and appropriate filing sequence in order to identify and bring any deficiencies to the attention of the appropriate clinical or administrative action for correction action. Must examine each record in its entirety from a variety of approaches to ensure the integrity of the record as a legal document and ensure that all JCAHO and VA/OIG regulatory requirements are fulfilled;
A specialized knowledge of theories, principles and techniques of medical record documentation in an acute care inpatient facility. Some specific required knowledge's are as follows:
Knowledge in medical terminology
Medical abbreviations and symbols
Knowledge of medical record documentation requirements required by JCAHO, HCFA and other accrediting bodies
Limited knowledge of anatomy and physiology
Ability to interpret and use medical record references related to documentation
Understand that certain symptoms and diagnoses in the record will require a specific treatment and that certain results will follow.
Incumbent must use a great deal of organizational and time management skills. Must have the ability to set work priorities and accomplish work within specified deadlines. Must have the ability to apply statistical formulae for mathematical calculations;
Skill in reading handwritten entries in the medical record, often illegible, recorded by health care professionals is necessary in order to perform duties completely and accurately. Possesses the ability to access patient medical information via CPRS and VISTA to analyze incomplete information;
Knowledge and skill in VISTA applications such as CPRS, TIU, Appointment Management, Surgery Package, consult tracking and others as developed within the electronic record;
Must have skill in the use of medical record references;
Knowledge of clinical and administrative functions as they related to the care and treatment of inpatients and outpatients, flow of the medical record, and the internal organization, in order to appropriately review records, communicate with the providers of care, and report findings of the reviews to the appropriate individuals;
Ability to use a personal computer and associated VISTA and commercial software to review reference material, analyze data, prepare reports, and receive training;
Demonstrated skills in communication, tact, diplomacy, and good judgment are necessitated to enable the incumbent to function effectively with medical center staff.
