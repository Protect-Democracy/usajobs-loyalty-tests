2.
Applicants must meet the following Screen-Out element, Job Element #73: ABILITY TO LEAD OR SUPERVISE. These duties include: 1) Setting up, operating, and maintaining multi-color offset sheet-fed presses (up to 7 units with split fountains) and screen-printing presses; 2) Leading press operators or printing personnel; and 3) Reading and interpreting printing instructions such as job tickets, press specifications, paper stock, ink requirements, and finishing instructions.

Qualifications will first be evaluated against the prescribed screen out element, which usually appears as question 1 in the online questionnaire. If your knowledge and ability in the SCREEN OUT ELEMENT below is not sufficient, you will receive no further consideration. Applicants must have experience and/or training of sufficient scope and quality to independently perform the duties typically required of an Offset Press Operator Supervisor.

From the choices listed below, please select the best (ONE) response which demonstrates your Ability to Lead or Supervise as an Offset Press Operator Supervisor based on your current level of experience or training.

 I have the ability to perform the most difficult and complex offset press operations. I set up, operate, and maintain multi-color offset sheet-fed presses (Including presses with split fountains and multiple units), making precise adjustments to equipment, sequencing, timing, and movement of materials between machines or workstations. I lead and direct press operator or printing personnel, assign work, monitor production, and ensure quality and safety standards are met. I independently interpret job tickets, press specifications, paper stock, ink requirements, and finishing instructions, and I troubleshoot and correct complex press and production problems. I am considered an expert and work with a high degree of independence.
 I have the ability to perform the full range of offset press operations. I set up and operate multi-color offset presses, make adjustments to maintain quality and production standards, and perform routine operator maintenance using standard and specialized tools. I provide guidance to other press operators and assist with coordinating workflow. I read and interpret job tickets and print specifications and work under general supervision subject to occasional review.
 I have the ability to operate offset presses for standard printing jobs and perform basic set up and adjustments under the direction of a work leader or journey-level operator. I can follow job tickets and written instructions and perform limited troubleshooting. I do not regularly lead other employees.
 I have the ability to perform simple press operation tasks or have received training on basic offset press equipment, tools, and materials, but I require close supervision and do not perform full set up or lead other workers.
 I do not possess training or work experience related to this position.
