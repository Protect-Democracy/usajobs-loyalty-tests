From the descriptions below, select the appropriate response based on your level of experience, education, and/or training, that you fully possess and that demonstrates your ability to perform Materials Expediter Leader duties without more than normal supervision.

 I have the ability to perform the most difficult and complex Materials Expediter Leader duties. I am consulted by other employees or in difficult situations regarding the performance of the following duties: reviewing work for effectiveness and maintaining an orderly flow of parts and materials. I am considered an expert and work with a high degree of independence.
 I have the ability to perform the full range of Materials Expediter Leader duties. I am skilled in recommending reclamation action taken on salvage parts when they are listed on the critical shortage list. Prepare transfer order for reclamation items indicating work to be done on production orders. I work on my own initiative under general supervision to occasional inspection.
 I have the ability to perform common Materials Expediter Leader duties by serving as a working leader of three (3) or more employees in accomplishing trades and labor work. I reroute parts to relieve overloaded work areas in order to improve processing time. I route parts which require no additional assembly directly to the assembly line. I use the Logistic Modernization Program (LMP) for time and attendance reporting, production schedules, expenditures reports, parts availability, reporting non-conforming or damaged parts and reporting shop equipment failures. I work under normal supervision of a work leader or journey-level employee who observes in progress and upon completion to make sure they are properly performed.
 I have the ability to perform simple Materials Expediter Leader duties, or I have received training on the use of basic hand tools and how to handle hazardous materials/waste.
