1.
Knowledge of Army supply regulations, policies, and procedures (e.g., AR 710-2, AR 735-5, DA PAM 710-2-1) to maintain property accountability and support Property Book operations.



2.
Knowledge of property accountability principles, hand receipt management, inventory procedures, and financial liability requirements.


3.
Knowledge of automated logistics systems, including Global Combat Support System-Army (GCSS-Army), to process transactions, update records, and track equipment.


4.
Knowledge of supply classifications, cataloging procedures, and turn-in, lateral transfer, and fielding processes for expendable, durable, and non-expendable items.




5.
Skill in reviewing, reconciling, and maintaining accurate property records to ensure 100% accountability.


6.
Skill in using automated logistics systems and databases to input, manage, and retrieve supply information.



7.
Skill in evaluating supply discrepancies and preparing adjustment documents and supporting paperwork.


8.
Skill in coordinating with units, supply personnel, and external agencies to complete supply transactions and resolve issues.



9.
Skill in planning and executing inventories, to include cyclic, sensitive item, and annual requirements.


10.
Ability to communicate effectively both orally and in writing to explain supply procedures, provide guidance, and prepare documentation.


11.
Ability to work independently, manage multiple supply actions, and meet deadlines while maintaining accuracy and attention to detail.



12.
Ability to interpret and apply regulations, policies, and standard operating procedures to daily supply operations.



13.
Ability to train and assist unit supply personnel in proper property accountability processes and documentation requirements.


14.
Ability to maintain organized, audit-ready files and records.
