SPECIALIZED EXPERIENCE: GS-12 - Must have at least one year of specialized experience equivalent to the GS-11 grade level demonstrating comprehensive knowledge and technical expertise in collecting, analyzing, and interpreting complex data, federal laws, Department of Defense (DoD), Department of the Air Force (DAF), and National Guard Bureau (NGB) regulations. This experience must include serving as a Subject Matter Expert (SME) or advisor to commanders on Inspector General (IG) programs, including the Commander's Inspection Program (CCIP), Unit Self-Assessment Program (USAP), and Complaints Resolution Program (CRP); utilizing advanced diagnostic databases (such as MICT, IGEMS, IGEMS-C, and ACTS) to identify organizational risk, manage waiver processes, and conduct risk-based sampling; and planning, organizing, and executing multi-faceted inspections, readiness exercises, and focus groups. Additionally, the applicant must demonstrate exceptional interpersonal and written communication skills to conduct complex investigations, interview witnesses, resolve Fraud, Waste, and Abuse (FWA) or Whistleblower Reprisal complaints, draft official Reports of Investigation (ROI), and deliver clear, unimpeded oral briefings to senior leadership, base personnel, and external oversight agencies on sensitive matters of public trust.
