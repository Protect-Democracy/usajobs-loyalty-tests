 I have the ability to perform the most difficult and complex Sheet Metal Mechanic duties including performing the planning, fabricating, assembling and installing of sheet metal articles for buildings and structures; laying out work according to building plans, building codes, specifications, blueprints, drawings, sketches, and work orders; welding to meet specifications; and operating motor vehicle up to 5 tons, forklift lifting up to 30,000 pounds and man-lift up to 60 feet. I am skilled in performing oxygen and acetylene welding and cutting duties related to sheet metal maintenance, installation, removal, modification and repair of buildings and structures; and devising templates and patterns for the purpose of planning projects, layouts and modifications. I am considered an expert and work with a high degree of independence.
 I have the ability to perform the full range of Sheet Metal Mechanic duties including the erection of component parts to prefabricated steel buildings; repairing of metal doors and their component parts; fabricating, assembling, and installing of a variety of articles of sheet metal such as: gutters, ducts, safety guards, shelves, counter and table tops, parts for air conditioning, and heating ducts of building and structures; and laying out and cutting materials from a variety of sheet metal stock including brass, mill steel, stainless steel, copper and aluminum. I am skilled in bending and forming metal parts into desired shapes; assembling and fastening fabricated parts by soldering, spot welding, electric welding or acetylene welding; and making modifications to metallic structures. I work on my own initiative under general supervision subject to occasional inspection.
 I have the ability to perform common Sheet Metal Mechanic duties which includes 1) interpret blueprints, drawings, sketches, and work orders to perform maintenance or repairs to metal; 2) determining the proper techniques, materials, machines, and parts that are required for repair or maintenance of sheet metal; 3) lay out and cut sheet metal materials for various shapes, accounting for seams, joints, laps, and shrinkage; AND 4) Bend and form metal parts into required shapes using hand or power tools and equipment. I work under normal supervision of a work leader or journey-level employee who observes tasks in progress and upon completion to make sure they are properly performed.
