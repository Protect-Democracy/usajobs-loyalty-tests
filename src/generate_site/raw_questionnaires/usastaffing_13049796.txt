The employee will serve as a Gardener at the VA Sierra Nevada Health Care System, in Reno, NV. The primary purpose of the position is to provide ground keeping and gardening throughout the facility as assigned. The employee is expected to have skills in gardening and groundkeeper maintenance and upkeep. Duties include, but may not be limited to the following: • Performs a full scale of ground keeping tasks to include seasonal planting, fertilizing, transplanting and pruning. • Operates a variety of hand and power garden tools such as fertilizer, spreader, portable sparer, hedge trimmers, weed eaters, pruning and shears. • Operates, stores and maintains equipment such as commercial pressure, power lawn mowers, edger trimmer, pruners and weed eaters. • Provides corrective action for plant management, insect and disease control. • Performs pressure washing driveways, building, exteriors, sidewalks, exterior railing and other miscellaneous landscape features. • Operates highly spiraled carpet cleaning equipment, scrubs, strips, waxes and polishes floors using heavy powered scrubbers, buffers, pressure washers, etc. • Drives forklifts as needed to transport heavy loads such as soil, plants, pallet and ice melts. • Demonstrates knowledge of the deployment, mixing, application and current laws and guideline of restricted use pesticides. • Maintains landscape in a healthy, disease-free and attractive condition. • Performs trench digging, minor sprinkler head/risers repair work, raking and other duties associated with normal lawn and flower bed maintenance. • Performs other related duties as assigned. (This experience must be documented on your resume.) Physical Effort: The work requires frequent walking, standing, pushing of carts, bending, crawling and stooping. The employee must be able to lift heavy objects such as fertilizer, salt, plant material and maneuver loaded wheelbarrow. Often lift and handle materials weighing over 23 kilograms (50 pounds) and carry chemical solutions and equipment weighing up to 18 kilograms (40 pounds) for extended periods. Working Conditions: The majority of work is done outdoors. In working outside, the employee is subject to discomfort from long periods in the hot sun and sometimes to chilly or rainy weather. Being exposed to dirt, dust, mud and sometimes to chemical sprays and dusts, they may wear protective clothing such as gloves, safety shoes and boots. There is chance of minor injuries such as bruises, cuts and scrapes and occasional possibility of broken bones from falls. During periods of extreme weather, such as heavy rain or severe heat, the employee may be given assignments to assist other members of the section in moving materials and equipment, housekeeping and other tasks which may be outside the range of groundkeeper.
