1.
From the description below, select the appropriate response based on your level of experience, education, and/or training, that you fully possess and that demonstrates your ability to perform Electronic Mechanic duties without more than normal supervision.
 I have the ability to perform the most difficult and complex Electronic Mechanic duties. I am skilled in troubleshooting, installing, repairing and maintaining complex electronics equipment and complete operational systems consisting of numerous complex integral components which require knowledge of a wide range of electronic principles and practices. Equipment maintained is complex and complicated by unique circuitry, solid state devices, microprocessors, computer controls and interaction between circuits and compact design. Systems at this degree of complexity are closed circuit color TV studios, communications security (COMSEC) systems, computerized avionics/airborne navigation systems, fire control systems for guns, missiles and other weapons, air traffic control search radar systems and electronic countermeasures systems. Circuit theory is used on complex electrical systems to understand the operation of individual circuits and the possible interaction of other circuits which create a malfunction. I am considered an expert and work with a high degree of independence.
 I have the ability to perform the full range of Electronics Mechanic duties. I am skilled in troubleshooting, installing, repairing and maintaining moderately complex electronics equipment and operational systems working from manufacturer's specifications, schematics, block diagrams, and technical orders to determine the operation of circuits, locate the points from which to take readings, and make preliminary visual inspections and operational tests to determine components causing malfunctions or invalid readings; make required repairs by replacing damaged, missing, burnt or faulty items; use a variety of test equipment to diagnose problems in electronic equipment and troubleshooting electronic equipment to ensure proper operation. I read blueprints, drawings and schematics to accomplish the work. I work on my own initiative under general supervision subject to occasional inspection.
 I have the ability to perform common Electronics Mechanic duties. I can independently install, modify, overhaul, maintain, troubleshoot and repair electronics equipment of moderate complexity such as video surveillance equipment and/or fire alarm equipment systems. . I work under close supervision of a work leader or journey-level employee who reviews work upon completion to make sure they are properly performed.
 I have the ability to perform simple Electronics Mechanic tasks such as repairing simple electronic parts, or I have received training on this type of work.
 I do not possess the abilities described above.
