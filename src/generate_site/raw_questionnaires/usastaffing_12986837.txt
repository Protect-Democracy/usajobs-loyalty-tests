 I have performed the following duties under normal supervision: 1) making plumbing repairs that can be accomplished by removing, cleaning, replacing, packing and sealing defective parts of utility, supply and disposal systems such as dirty traps, sections of broken tile pipe, and leaky drains; 2) completing needed repairs and hook up to installed systems such things as water heaters, disposal units and faucets; 3) using basic  mathematics (e.g., addition, subtraction, division, multiplication and simple fractions) to perform repairs; and 4) using basic tools and equipment such as tapes, rules, hacksaws, hand and power pipe threaders and cutters, packing and caulking irons and pipe wrenches.
