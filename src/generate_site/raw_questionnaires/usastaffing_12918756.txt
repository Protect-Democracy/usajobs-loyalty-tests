 I have the ability to perform the most difficult and complex Small Arms Repairer duties. I am skilled in conducting the final inspection on the assembly, alignment, shimming, and adjustment of larger and more complex weapons such as multibarrel machine guns and recoilless rifles; making adjustments to small arms close to tolerances; using precision gages and measuring instruments to determine when parts are worm or damaged past acceptable specification; providing training to others on the how to repair simple and complex weapons; and generating data reports. . I am considered an expert and work with a high degree of independence.
 I have the ability to perform the full range of Small Arms Repairer duties. I am skilled in reading specifications and blueprints to assemble weapons and locating the cause of improper functioning; conducting simulated operation or test firing; using simple machine tools to alter parts; and disassembling, repairing, replacing components on small arms; and assisting in providing training to others on how to repair small arms weapons. I work on my own initiative under general supervision subject to occasional inspection.
 I have the ability to perform common Small Arms Repairer duties. I can read specifications and blueprints to assemble weapons and locate the cause of improper functioning; conduct simulated operation or test firing; use simple machine tools to alter parts; and disassemble, repair, and replace components on small arms. I work under close supervision of a work leader or journey-level employee who observes tasks in progress and upon completion to make sure they are properly performed.
