Assessment 1
1.
To qualify for a Wildland Firefighter (Fire Lookout), GS-0456-04 - You must meet at least one of the following:
 I have six months of general work experience, which can be any work experience, AND 6 months of specialized work experience equivalent to the GS-03 level duties. Examples of specialized work experience may include the following: Relay information accurately and timely via radio, telephone or text message, conducted radio relay operations for routine or emergency radio traffic for operational activities such as wildland fire operations, emergency services and law enforcement and relayed information to co-workers and supervisors within a team setting under stressful and adverse operating conditions.
 I have completed a 2-year course of study above high school, from an accredited college or university, that included at least 12 semester hours in any combination of courses such as forestry, agriculture, crop or plant science, range management or conservation, wildlife management, watershed management, soil science, natural resources (except marine fisheries and oceanography), outdoor recreation management, civil or forest engineering, or wildland fire science. No more than 3 semester hours in mathematics is creditable.
 I possess a combination of the education and work experience described above. IMPORTANT-PLEASE NOTE: If you are using education to qualify you must document your degree in your resume. If your degree is not in one of the directly-related fields listed, you must submit a legible copy of your transcripts that identifies for each course the college or university, semester or quarter hours earned, grade received, and date completed. We cannot determine eligibility without this information. It is the responsibility of the applicant to show how he/she satisfies the course requirements. If your course titles are unclear as to the subject emphasis to satisfy the above requirements, you should also provide additional information such as a narrative course content description. (A copy of your official transcripts will be required before entrance on duty, if selected).
 I do not meet the education requirements described above.
